## 💡 GATE AE Strategy for Structures

1. **Thin-walled sections (shear flow, shear center, torsion):** Aircraft structural core; high weightage.
2. **Mohr's Circle & Failure Theories:** High yield, clear mathematical formulas.
3. **Structural Dynamics:** SDOF and 2-DOF vibrations are explicitly Core in GATE 2027.
4. **Castigliano's Theorems:** Essential for deflection of trusses and indeterminate structures.
