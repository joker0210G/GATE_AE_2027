## 🔵 CORE TOPICS (≥90% of questions)

### 1. Linear Algebra
- [ ] [[02 - SUBJECTS/Engineering Mathematics/01 - Linear Algebra]] 🔲
  - Vector algebra
  - Matrix algebra: operations, transpose, inverse, determinants
  - Systems of linear equations, rank of a matrix
  - Eigenvalues and eigenvectors

### 2. Calculus
- [ ] [[02 - SUBJECTS/Engineering Mathematics/02 - Calculus]] 🔲
  - **Single variable:** limits, continuity, differentiability, chain rule, maxima and minima, integration
  - **Several variables:** partial derivatives, gradient, divergence and curl, directional derivatives
  - Line integrals, surface integrals, volume integrals
  - **Integral theorems:** Stokes, Gauss (Divergence), Green

### 3. Differential Equations (ODEs + PDEs)
- [ ] [[02 - SUBJECTS/Engineering Mathematics/03 - ODEs and PDEs]] 🔲
  - First order linear ODEs
  - Higher order linear ODEs with constant coefficients
  - Classification of PDEs (elliptic, parabolic, hyperbolic)
  - **Separation of variables** solution to:
    - Wave equation
    - Laplace equation (Laplace's PDE, not Laplace Transform!)
    - Heat equation

### 4. Numerical Methods
- [ ] [[02 - SUBJECTS/Engineering Mathematics/04 - Numerical Methods]] 🔲
  - Root finding: Bisection method, Newton-Raphson method
  - Basic numerical differentiation
  - Numerical integration: Trapezoidal rule, Simpson's rule
  - Linear regression and least squares method
  - Linear interpolation

---

## 🟡 SPECIAL TOPICS (≤10% of questions — study after mastering Core)

### 5. Fourier Series
- [ ] [[02 - SUBJECTS/Engineering Mathematics/05 - Fourier Series]] 🔲 *(Special)*
  - Fourier series representation of periodic functions
  - Even and odd functions; half-range expansions

### 6. Complex Variables
- [ ] [[02 - SUBJECTS/Engineering Mathematics/06 - Complex Variables]] 🔲 *(Special)*
  - Complex numbers, Argand plane
  - Analytic functions; Cauchy-Riemann equations

### 7. Probability & Statistics
- [ ] [[02 - SUBJECTS/Engineering Mathematics/07 - Probability and Statistics]] 🔲 *(Special)*
  - Bayes theorem
  - Mean, median, mode; variance
  - Binomial, Normal, Poisson distributions

## 💡 GATE AE Strategy for Math

1. **Linear Algebra first** — eigenvalues appear in structural dynamics, flight dynamics
2. **Calculus second** — gradient/divergence/curl appears in fluid mechanics too
3. **ODEs + PDEs** — wave/heat/Laplace equations are frequently asked; PDEs via separation of variables
4. **Numerical Methods** — always 1–2 easy direct questions; don't skip
5. **Special Topics** — do these in Week 5 only; 1 question max from each
6. ⚠️ **Do NOT study Laplace Transforms** for GATE AE — it's not in the syllabus!
7. **Target: 11+/13 marks** from Mathematics section
