## 💡 GATE AE Strategy for Math

1. **Linear Algebra first** — eigenvalues appear in structural dynamics, flight dynamics
2. **Calculus second** — gradient/divergence/curl appears in fluid mechanics too
3. **ODEs + PDEs** — wave/heat/Laplace equations are frequently asked; PDEs via separation of variables
4. **Numerical Methods** — always 1–2 easy direct questions; don't skip
5. **Special Topics** — do these in Week 5 only; 1 question max from each
6. ⚠️ **Do NOT study Laplace Transforms** for GATE AE — it's not in the syllabus!
7. **Target: 11+/13 marks** from Mathematics section
