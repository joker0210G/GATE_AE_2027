> 🎯 **GATE Weightage:** 2–4 marks per year. The wave equation is **Hyperbolic** ($\Delta > 0$) and models acoustic waves, structural vibrations, pressure disturbances in compressible flow, and electromagnetic waves. Separation of Variables is the **standard GATE solution method**.

---

## 📌 Part I: The Wave Equation — Statement & Physical Meaning

### 1D Wave Equation (Standard GATE Form)

$$\boxed{\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial t^2} = c^2 \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}, \quad 0 < x < L, \; t > 0}$$

where:
- $u(x,t)$ = displacement/pressure at position $x$ and time $t$
- $c$ = wave speed (m/s) — constant
- $L$ = length of domain (string length, pipe length, etc.)

**Classification:** $A = c^2$, $B = 0$, $C = -1$ → $\Delta = 4c^2 > 0$ → **Hyperbolic** ✓

---

### 2. Required Data (Initial + Boundary Conditions)

Since the wave equation is **2nd order in time**, it needs **TWO initial conditions**:
- $u(x,0) = f(x)$ — **Initial displacement** (shape at $t=0$)
- $\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}(x,0) = g(x)$ — **Initial velocity**

**Standard Boundary Conditions (BCs):**

| BC Type | Condition | Physical Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Dirichlet (Fixed ends)** | $u(0,t) = 0$, $u(L,t) = 0$ | String fixed at both ends |
| **Neumann (Free ends)** | $u_x(0,t) = 0$, $u_x(L,t) = 0$ | Free (no slope) at ends |
| **Mixed** | $u(0,t) = 0$, $u_x(L,t) = 0$ | Fixed at left, free at right |

---

## 🧠 Part II: Solution by Separation of Variables — Step-by-Step

### Step 1: Assume Product Solution

$$\boxed{u(x,t) = X(x) \cdot T(t)}$$

Substitute into the wave equation:

$$X(x) T''(t) = c^2 X''(x) T(t)$$

Divide both sides by $c^2 X(x) T(t)$:

$$\frac{T''(t)}{c^2 T(t)} = \frac{X''(x)}{X(x)} = -\lambda$$

(Left side depends only on $t$, right side only on $x$ → both equal the same constant $-\lambda$)

---

### Step 2: Solve the Two ODEs

This splits into **two ODEs**:

**Spatial ODE:**
$$X''(x) + \lambda X(x) = 0$$

**Temporal ODE:**
$$T''(t) + \lambda c^2 T(t) = 0$$

---

### Step 3: Apply BCs to Spatial ODE — Find Eigenvalues

**For Fixed-Fixed BCs** ($u(0,t)=0$, $u(L,t)=0$):
This means $X(0) = 0$ and $X(L) = 0$.

**Case $\lambda > 0$** (set $\lambda = \mu^2 > 0$):
General solution: $X(x) = A\cos\mu x + B\sin\mu x$

Apply $X(0) = 0 \implies A = 0 \implies X(x) = B\sin\mu x$

Apply $X(L) = 0 \implies B\sin\mu L = 0$

For non-trivial solution ($B \ne 0$): $\sin\mu L = 0 \implies \mu L = n\pi$

$$\boxed{\mu_n = \frac{n\pi}{L}, \quad n = 1, 2, 3, \ldots}$$

**Eigenvalues:** $\lambda_n = \mu_n^2 = \frac{n^2\pi^2}{L^2}$

**Eigenfunctions:** $X_n(x) = \sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)$

> ⚡ **Cases $\lambda = 0$ and $\lambda < 0$ give only trivial solutions $X=0$ for fixed BCs — discard them!**

---

### Step 4: Solve Temporal ODE

With $\lambda_n = \frac{n^2\pi^2}{L^2}$, the temporal ODE becomes:

$$T''(t) + \frac{n^2\pi^2 c^2}{L^2} T(t) = 0$$

General solution (pure imaginary roots $\pm i\frac{n\pi c}{L}$):

$$\boxed{T_n(t) = a_n \cos\left(\frac{n\pi c}{L}t\right) + b_n \sin\left(\frac{n\pi c}{L}t\right)}$$

---

### Step 5: Write Complete Solution (Superposition)

$$\boxed{u(x,t) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left[a_n \cos\left(\frac{n\pi c}{L}t\right) + b_n \sin\left(\frac{n\pi c}{L}t\right)\right] \sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)}$$

---

### Step 6: Apply Initial Conditions — Find Fourier Coefficients

**IC 1:** $u(x,0) = f(x)$:
$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n \sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right) = f(x)$$

This is a **Fourier Sine Series**! By orthogonality:
$$\boxed{a_n = \frac{2}{L}\int_0^L f(x)\sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right) dx}$$

**IC 2:** $\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}\bigg|_{t=0} = g(x)$:
$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} b_n \cdot \frac{n\pi c}{L} \sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right) = g(x)$$

$$\boxed{b_n = \frac{2}{n\pi c}\int_0^L g(x)\sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right) dx}$$

---

## 🧠 Part III: Special Case — Zero Initial Velocity $g(x) = 0$

**Most common in GATE!** When $g(x) = 0$ (string released from rest):

$$b_n = 0 \quad \text{(all zero)}$$

$$\boxed{u(x,t) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n \cos\left(\frac{n\pi c}{L}t\right) \sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)}$$

$$a_n = \frac{2}{L}\int_0^L f(x)\sin\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right) dx$$

---

## 🧠 Part IV: d'Alembert's Solution (Alternative — High Yield!)

For the **infinite** or **semi-infinite** domain wave equation with ICs but NO boundary conditions, use **d'Alembert's formula**:

$$\boxed{u(x,t) = \frac{f(x+ct) + f(x-ct)}{2} + \frac{1}{2c}\int_{x-ct}^{x+ct} g(s)\,ds}$$

**Physical Interpretation:**
- $f(x+ct)$: wave traveling to the **LEFT** with speed $c$
- $f(x-ct)$: wave traveling to the **RIGHT** with speed $c$
- $g(x)$ contribution: integral of initial velocity

**Special case** $g(x)=0$ (zero initial velocity):
$$\boxed{u(x,t) = \frac{f(x+ct) + f(x-ct)}{2}}$$

> ⚡ **d'Alembert is FASTER for GATE MCQs** on infinite domain — no Fourier series needed!

---

## 🧠 Part V: Natural Frequencies & Normal Modes

### Natural Frequencies

The $n$-th mode oscillates at angular frequency $\omega_n$:

$$\boxed{\omega_n = \frac{n\pi c}{L} \text{ rad/s}, \quad f_n = \frac{n c}{2L} \text{ Hz}}$$

**Fundamental frequency** ($n=1$): $f_1 = \frac{c}{2L}$  
**Harmonics:** $f_n = n \cdot f_1$

### Normal Modes (Standing Waves)

Each term $u_n(x,t) = \sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)\cos\!\left(\frac{n\pi c}{L}t\right)$ is a **standing wave** (normal mode):

| Mode | Name | Nodes | Shape |
|---|---|---|---|
| $n=1$ | Fundamental / 1st harmonic | 0 internal | $\sin(\pi x/L)$ |
| $n=2$ | 2nd harmonic / 1st overtone | 1 internal | $\sin(2\pi x/L)$ |
| $n=3$ | 3rd harmonic / 2nd overtone | 2 internal | $\sin(3\pi x/L)$ |

**Aerospace Application: Structural Resonance**  
If forcing frequency matches a natural frequency $f_n$ → **resonance** → unbounded amplitude → catastrophic structural failure (Tacoma Narrows, wing flutter!).

---

## 🧠 Part VI: Boundary Conditions — Other Cases

### Case 2: Free-Free BCs ($u_x(0,t) = 0$, $u_x(L,t) = 0$)

Spatial ODE: $X''(x) + \lambda X(x) = 0$ with $X'(0) = X'(L) = 0$

Eigenfunctions: $X_n(x) = \cos\!\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)$, $n = 0, 1, 2, \ldots$

> Include $n=0$: $X_0 = 1$ (constant mode — rigid body translation)

$$u(x,t) = \frac{a_0}{2} + \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \left[a_n\cos\!\left(\frac{n\pi c}{L}t\right) + b_n\sin\!\left(\frac{n\pi c}{L}t\right)\right]\cos\!\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right)$$

Fourier coefficients use **cosine series**:
$$a_n = \frac{2}{L}\int_0^L f(x)\cos\!\left(\frac{n\pi x}{L}\right) dx$$

---

### Case 3: Fixed-Free BCs ($u(0,t) = 0$, $u_x(L,t) = 0$) — Organ Pipe / Quarter-Wave

Eigenfunctions: $X_n(x) = \sin\!\left(\frac{(2n-1)\pi x}{2L}\right)$, $n = 1, 2, 3, \ldots$

Eigenvalues: $\mu_n = \frac{(2n-1)\pi}{2L}$

Natural frequencies: $f_n = \frac{(2n-1)c}{4L}$

> ⚡ **Aerospace use:** Closed-open acoustic duct (one end closed, one open) — only **odd harmonics** present! Fundamental frequency = $c/(4L)$ instead of $c/(2L)$.

---

## ⚡ AIR-1 Speed Tricks & Mnemonics

### 🚀 Trick 1: Eigenvalues from BCs — Direct Memory
| BC Type | Eigenfunction | Eigenvalue $\lambda_n$ | Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fixed-Fixed | $\sin(n\pi x/L)$ | $(n\pi/L)^2$ | $nc/2L$ |
| Free-Free | $\cos(n\pi x/L)$ | $(n\pi/L)^2$ | $nc/2L$ |
| Fixed-Free | $\sin((2n-1)\pi x/2L)$ | $((2n-1)\pi/2L)^2$ | $(2n-1)c/4L$ |

**"Fixed = Sine, Free = Cosine!"** — Dirichlet BC at an end → sine; Neumann BC at an end → cosine.

---

### 🚀 Trick 2: d'Alembert — Fastest for Infinite Domain
For infinite string with $g(x) = 0$ (most GATE MCQs):
$$u(x,t) = \frac{f(x+ct) + f(x-ct)}{2}$$

No integration needed! Just shift $f(x)$ left and right by $ct$ and average.

---

### 🚀 Trick 3: Fourier Coefficient Check — Odd/Even Symmetry
- If $f(x)$ is an **odd function** on $[-L, L]$ → all cosine coefficients zero → only sine series
- If $f(x)$ is an **even function** on $[-L, L]$ → all sine coefficients zero → only cosine series
- Saves computing half the integrals!

---

### 🚀 Trick 4: Resonance Condition
If any external forcing at frequency $\omega = \omega_n = n\pi c/L$ → **resonance** → amplitude grows linearly with $t$ → structural failure mode.

---

### 🧠 Mnemonic: "ST-OCT" for Separation of Variables Steps
1. **S**eparate: assume $u = X(x)T(t)$
2. **T**wo ODEs: one for $X$, one for $T$
3. **O**btain eigenvalues: apply BCs to $X$ ODE
4. **C**ombine: superposition $u = \sum X_n T_n$
5. **T**une coefficients: Fourier series + ICs

## 📝 Fully Solved GATE Example

### Example: Vibrating String (GATE PYQ Type — 4 Marks)

**Problem:** Solve the wave equation $u_{tt} = 4u_{xx}$ ($c=2$) for $0 < x < \pi$, $t > 0$ with:
- $u(0,t) = 0$, $u(\pi,t) = 0$ (fixed-fixed)
- $u(x,0) = \sin x + \frac{1}{2}\sin 2x$ (initial displacement)
- $u_t(x,0) = 0$ (released from rest)

**Solution:**

**Step 1 — Eigenvalues** ($L = \pi$, fixed-fixed):  
$X_n(x) = \sin(nx)$, $\mu_n = n$, $n = 1, 2, 3, \ldots$

**Step 2 — Temporal functions** (since $c=2$, $\omega_n = n\pi c/L = 2n$):  
$T_n(t) = a_n\cos(2nt) + b_n\sin(2nt)$

**Step 3 — Apply $g(x)=0$**: $b_n = 0$ for all $n$.

**Step 4 — General solution**:
$$u(x,t) = \sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n\cos(2nt)\sin(nx)$$

**Step 5 — Apply $u(x,0) = \sin x + \frac{1}{2}\sin 2x$**:
$$\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} a_n\sin(nx) = \sin x + \frac{1}{2}\sin 2x$$

By matching coefficients (Fourier sine series, $L=\pi$):
$$a_1 = 1, \quad a_2 = \frac{1}{2}, \quad a_n = 0 \text{ for } n \ge 3$$

**Step 6 — Final Solution**:

$$\boxed{u(x,t) = \cos(2t)\sin x + \frac{1}{2}\cos(4t)\sin 2x}$$

> ⚡ **Speed Trick:** When $f(x)$ is already given as a finite sine series, you can read off the $a_n$ values **directly** without computing any integrals!

## 📐 Master Summary: Wave Equation Solution

| Item | Formula |
|---|---|
| PDE | $u_{tt} = c^2 u_{xx}$, $0<x<L$, $t>0$ |
| Classification | Hyperbolic ($B^2-4AC = 4c^2 > 0$) |
| General solution (fixed-fixed) | $u = \sum_{n=1}^\infty [a_n\cos(\omega_n t)+b_n\sin(\omega_n t)]\sin(n\pi x/L)$ |
| Angular frequency | $\omega_n = n\pi c/L$ |
| Natural frequency | $f_n = nc/2L$ Hz |
| $a_n$ (from $f(x)$) | $\frac{2}{L}\int_0^L f(x)\sin(n\pi x/L)\,dx$ |
| $b_n$ (from $g(x)$) | $\frac{2}{n\pi c}\int_0^L g(x)\sin(n\pi x/L)\,dx$ |
| d'Alembert (infinite domain) | $u = \frac{f(x+ct)+f(x-ct)}{2} + \frac{1}{2c}\int_{x-ct}^{x+ct}g(s)\,ds$ |
| Eigenfunctions (Fixed) | $\sin(n\pi x/L)$ |
| Eigenfunctions (Free) | $\cos(n\pi x/L)$ |
| Eigenfunctions (Fixed-Free) | $\sin((2n-1)\pi x/2L)$ |

## ⚠️ Common Exam Traps & Pitfalls

> 🔴 **Trap 1: Confusing $\omega_n$ and $f_n$ for Fixed-Free vs Fixed-Fixed**  
> Fixed-Fixed (both ends closed or both open): $f_n = \frac{nc}{2L}$ → ALL harmonics (n=1,2,3…)  
> Fixed-Free (one closed, one open — organ pipe!): $f_n = \frac{(2n-1)c}{4L}$ → ODD harmonics only!

> 🔴 **Trap 2: Missing Zero-Velocity IC Shortcut**  
> When $g(x) = 0$ (released from rest), ALL $b_n = 0$ immediately. Don't compute the integral — just set all $b_n = 0$.

> 🔴 **Trap 3: Normalising the Fourier Coefficient Factor**  
> The formula is $\frac{2}{L}\int_0^L \ldots$ — the factor is $\frac{2}{L}$ not $\frac{1}{L}$. The factor 2 comes from the orthogonality of sine functions: $\int_0^L \sin^2(n\pi x/L)\,dx = L/2$.

> 🔴 **Trap 4: d'Alembert Valid Only for Infinite / Semi-Infinite Domain**  
> d'Alembert's formula is for an infinite or semi-infinite string WITHOUT spatial boundary conditions. For a finite string $[0,L]$ with BCs, use Fourier series (separation of variables).
