> 🎯 **GATE Weightage:** 3–5 marks per year. Newton-Raphson is the **most heavily tested root-finding method in GATE**. Known for its **Quadratic Convergence** ($p=2$), it doubles the number of correct decimal places at every iteration.

---

## 📌 Part I: Geometric Derivation & Master Formula

### Geometric Concept
At an initial guess $x_n$, replace the curve $y = f(x)$ with its **tangent line** at $(x_n, f(x_n))$. The $x$-intercept of this tangent line becomes the next approximation $x_{n+1}$.

```
           y ^            Tangent at (xₙ, f(xₙ))
             |            /
             |   f(x)    /
             |   _--*-- / 
      -------+--+------+--------► x
             | α  xₙ₊₁ xₙ
```

Equation of tangent line at $(x_n, f(x_n))$:
$$y - f(x_n) = f'(x_n)(x - x_n)$$

Set $y = 0$ (finding the $x$-intercept $x = x_{n+1}$):
$$0 - f(x_n) = f'(x_n)(x_{n+1} - x_n) \implies x_{n+1} - x_n = -\frac{f(x_n)}{f'(x_n)}$$

$$\boxed{x_{n+1} = x_n - \frac{f(x_n)}{f'(x_n)}}$$

---

### Alternative Derivation via Taylor Series
Expand $f(\alpha) = 0$ around $x_n$ using Taylor series:
$$f(\alpha) = f(x_n) + (\alpha - x_n)f'(x_n) + \frac{(\alpha - x_n)^2}{2!}f''(x_n) + \cdots = 0$$

Truncating after first derivative:
$$f(x_n) + (\alpha - x_n)f'(x_n) \approx 0 \implies \alpha \approx x_n - \frac{f(x_n)}{f'(x_n)}$$

---

## 🧠 Part II: Order of Convergence Analysis (HIGH YIELD!)

### Quadratic Convergence ($p = 2$)

Let error $e_n = x_n - \alpha$ (where $f(\alpha) = 0$).

Substituting $x_n = \alpha + e_n$ into the Newton-Raphson formula gives:

$$\boxed{e_{n+1} \approx \frac{f''(\alpha)}{2 f'(\alpha)} \, e_n^2}$$

$$\boxed{e_{n+1} = C \cdot e_n^2 \quad \text{where} \quad C = \left|\frac{f''(\alpha)}{2f'(\alpha)}\right|}$$

- **Order of Convergence:** $p = 2$ (**Quadratic Convergence**)
- **Precision doubling:** The number of correct decimal places **doubles** with each iteration!  
  *(If $e_0 = 10^{-1} \implies e_1 \approx 10^{-2} \implies e_2 \approx 10^{-4} \implies e_3 \approx 10^{-8}$)*

---

### Sufficient Condition for Convergence

Newton-Raphson is guaranteed to converge in an interval containing root $\alpha$ if:

$$\boxed{\left| \frac{f(x) \cdot f''(x)}{[f'(x)]^2} \right| < 1}$$

---

## 🧠 Part III: Failure Modes & Traps of Newton-Raphson

While extremely fast, Newton-Raphson can fail under specific conditions:

| Failure Mode | Mathematical Condition | Graphic Result | Remedial Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Stationary Point** | $f'(x_n) = 0$ | Division by zero! Tangent is horizontal (parallel to $x$-axis). | Choose different initial guess $x_0$. |
| **2. Inflection Point** | $f''(x_n) = 0$ near root | Tangent line oscillates wildly or diverges. | Choose $x_0$ closer to root. |
| **3. Oscillatory Cycle** | $x_0 \to x_1 \to x_0 \to x_1$ | Endless loop between two points. | Change initial guess $x_0$. |
| **4. Multiple Roots** | $f'(\alpha) = 0$ (Root of multiplicity $m > 1$) | Convergence slows down to **Linear** ($p=1$)! | Use Modified Newton-Raphson. |

---

### ⚡ Modified Newton-Raphson for Multiple Roots

If $\alpha$ is a root of multiplicity $m$ (i.e. $f(x) = (x-\alpha)^m g(x)$ with $g(\alpha) \ne 0$):

$$\boxed{x_{n+1} = x_n - m \frac{f(x_n)}{f'(x_n)}}$$

Multiplying by $m$ **restores Quadratic Convergence** ($p=2$)!

---

## 🧠 Part IV: High-Speed Special Formulas (GATE Goldmines!)

These pre-derived formulas appear directly in GATE exam questions — memorize them!

### 1. Square Root Formula ($\sqrt{N}$)
To find $x = \sqrt{N}$, solve $f(x) = x^2 - N = 0$:
$f'(x) = 2x \implies x_{n+1} = x_n - \frac{x_n^2 - N}{2x_n}$

$$\boxed{x_{n+1} = \frac{1}{2}\left(x_n + \frac{N}{x_n}\right)}$$

---

### 2. Reciprocal Formula ($1/N$) — Division-Free Algorithm!
To find $x = \frac{1}{N}$, solve $f(x) = \frac{1}{x} - N = 0$:
$f'(x) = -\frac{1}{x^2} \implies x_{n+1} = x_n - \frac{1/x_n - N}{-1/x_n^2} = x_n + x_n(1 - N x_n)$

$$\boxed{x_{n+1} = x_n (2 - N x_n)}$$

> 🚀 **Used in computer hardware to calculate division using only multiplication & subtraction!**

---

### 3. Cube Root Formula ($\sqrt[3]{N}$)
To find $x = N^{1/3}$, solve $f(x) = x^3 - N = 0$:

$$\boxed{x_{n+1} = \frac{1}{3}\left(2x_n + \frac{N}{x_n^2}\right)}$$

---

### 4. Inverse Square Root Formula ($1/\sqrt{N}$)
To find $x = \frac{1}{\sqrt{N}}$, solve $f(x) = \frac{1}{x^2} - N = 0$:

$$\boxed{x_{n+1} = \frac{x_n}{2}\left(3 - N x_n^2\right)}$$

---

## ⚡ AIR-1 Topper Speed Tricks & Mnemonics

### 🚀 Trick 1: Square Root 2-Step Accuracy Magic
To calculate $\sqrt{N}$ (e.g. $\sqrt{10}$):
Choose initial guess $x_0 \approx \sqrt{N}$ (e.g., $x_0 = 3$).
1. $x_1 = \frac{1}{2}\left(3 + \frac{10}{3}\right) = \frac{19}{6} \approx 3.16667$
2. $x_2 = \frac{1}{2}\left(3.16667 + \frac{10}{3.16667}\right) = \mathbf{3.1622776}$ (Exact to 7 decimal places in 2 steps!)

---

### 🚀 Trick 2: Error Propagation Fast Calculation
If error at step $n$ is $e_n = 0.02$ and constant $C = \left|\frac{f''}{2f'}\right| = 1.5$:
$$e_{n+1} = 1.5 \times (0.02)^2 = 1.5 \times 0.0004 = \mathbf{0.0006}$$
Calculate next error in 5 seconds!

---

### 🚀 Trick 3: Instant Derivative Check ($f'(x_0) \ne 0$)
Before doing any NR calculation in an exam, check $f'(x_0)$.  
If $f'(x_0) = 0 \implies$ N-R method **fails at first step** $\implies$ Select "Method Fails" option in MCQ!

---

### 🧠 Mnemonic: "**TAN**" for Newton-Raphson
- **T**angent line substitution
- **A**ccelerated quadratic convergence ($p=2$)
- **N**o convergence if derivative is zero ($f'=0$)

## 📝 Step-by-Step Solved GATE Examples

### Example 1: Standard Newton-Raphson Iteration (GATE 2-Marker)
**Problem:** Find the root of $f(x) = x^3 - x - 1 = 0$ after 1 iteration starting with initial guess $x_0 = 1.5$.

**Solution:**
1. $f(x) = x^3 - x - 1 \implies f(1.5) = (1.5)^3 - 1.5 - 1 = 3.375 - 2.5 = 0.875$
2. $f'(x) = 3x^2 - 1 \implies f'(1.5) = 3(1.5)^2 - 1 = 3(2.25) - 1 = 5.75$
3. Apply formula:
   $$x_1 = x_0 - \frac{f(x_0)}{f'(x_0)} = 1.5 - \frac{0.875}{5.75} = 1.5 - 0.15217 = \mathbf{1.3478}$$

$$\boxed{x_1 = 1.3478}$$

---

### Example 2: Reciprocal Formula (GATE PYQ Type)
**Problem:** The Newton-Raphson iteration formula for finding the reciprocal of a number $N = 7$ is $x_{n+1} = x_n (2 - 7 x_n)$. If initial guess $x_0 = 0.1$, find $x_1$.

**Solution (Using Speed Formula 2):**
$$x_1 = x_0 (2 - 7 x_0) = 0.1 (2 - 7 \times 0.1) = 0.1 (2 - 0.7) = 0.1 \times 1.3 = \mathbf{0.13}$$

$$\boxed{x_1 = 0.13}$$

*(Notice $1/7 \approx 0.1428$. Starting at $0.1$, step 1 gets to $0.13$!)*

---

### Example 3: Quadratic Error Constant Calculation (GATE 2-Marker)
**Problem:** For the equation $f(x) = x^2 - 5 = 0$, find the error constant $C$ in the relation $e_{n+1} = C e_n^2$ near the root $\alpha = \sqrt{5}$.

**Solution:**
1. $f(x) = x^2 - 5 \implies f'(x) = 2x \implies f''(x) = 2$
2. Evaluate at root $\alpha = \sqrt{5}$:
   $f'(\sqrt{5}) = 2\sqrt{5}$, $f''(\sqrt{5}) = 2$
3. Error constant formula:
   $$C = \left| \frac{f''(\alpha)}{2 f'(\alpha)} \right| = \left| \frac{2}{2(2\sqrt{5})} \right| = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{5}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{20}} \approx \mathbf{0.2236}$$

$$\boxed{C = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{5}} \approx 0.2236}$$

---

### Example 4: Order of Convergence MCQ (GATE 1-Marker)
**Problem:** If $\alpha$ is a simple root of $f(x)=0$, the order of convergence of the Newton-Raphson method is:  
(a) $1$  
(b) $1.618$  
(c) $2$  
(d) $3$

**Solution:**  
For a simple root ($f'(\alpha) \ne 0$), Newton-Raphson has **Quadratic Convergence** ($p=2$).

$$\boxed{\text{Correct Answer: (c) } 2}$$

## 📐 Master Summary Table: Newton-Raphson Method

| Property | Formula / Value |
|---|---|
| **Master Iteration Formula** | $x_{n+1} = x_n - \frac{f(x_n)}{f'(x_n)}$ |
| **Order of Convergence ($p$)** | $2$ (Quadratic) for simple root |
| **Error Propagation Formula** | $e_{n+1} \approx \frac{f''(\alpha)}{2f'(\alpha)} \, e_n^2$ |
| **Sufficient Convergence Condition** | $\left| \frac{f(x)f''(x)}{[f'(x)]^2} \right| < 1$ |

| **Reciprocal ($1/N$)** | $x_{n+1} = x_n (2 - N x_n)$ |

## ⚠️ Common Exam Traps & Pitfalls

> 🔴 **Trap 1: Sign Error in Iteration Formula**  
> It is $x_{n+1} = x_n \mathbf{-} \frac{f(x_n)}{f'(x_n)}$ (MINUS sign!). A common mistake is writing $+ \frac{f}{f'}$.

> 🔴 **Trap 2: Forgetting Derivative $f'(x)$ Evaluation**  
> Ensure $f'(x_n)$ is evaluated at the CURRENT estimate $x_n$, NOT at $x_0$ or the previous step!

> 🔴 **Trap 3: Multiple Root Order Drop**  
> If $f(\alpha) = 0$ AND $f'(\alpha) = 0$, standard Newton-Raphson order drops to **Linear ($p=1$)**. To keep $p=2$, multiply the correction term by multiplicity $m$.

> 🔴 **Trap 4: Division-Free Reciprocal Formula Error**  
> For $1/N$, the formula is $x_n(2 - Nx_n)$, NOT $x_n(1 - Nx_n)$. Double check the constant $2$!
