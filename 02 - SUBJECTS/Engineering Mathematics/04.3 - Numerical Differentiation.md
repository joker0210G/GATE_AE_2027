## 📌 Part I: Taylor Series Derivation of Finite Difference Schemes

Let $f(x)$ be a smooth function. The Taylor series expansions around $x_0$ with step size $h$ are:

$$f(x_0 + h) = f(x_0) + h f'(x_0) + \frac{h^2}{2!} f''(x_0) + \frac{h^3}{3!} f'''(x_0) + \cdots \quad \text{--- (Eq. 1)}$$

$$f(x_0 - h) = f(x_0) - h f'(x_0) + \frac{h^2}{2!} f''(x_0) - \frac{h^3}{3!} f'''(x_0) + \cdots \quad \text{--- (Eq. 2)}$$

---

## 🧠 Part II: First Derivative Approximations

### 1. Forward Difference Scheme (1st Order Accurate)

Rearranging Eq. 1 for $f'(x_0)$:

$$\boxed{f'(x_0) = \frac{f(x_0 + h) - f(x_0)}{h} + O(h)}$$

- **Formula:** $f'_0 \approx \frac{y_1 - y_0}{h}$
- **Truncation Error:** $E(h) = -\frac{h}{2} f''(\xi) = \mathbf{O(h)}$ (Linear error reduction: halving $h$ halves error).

---

### 2. Backward Difference Scheme (1st Order Accurate)

Rearranging Eq. 2 for $f'(x_0)$:

$$\boxed{f'(x_0) = \frac{f(x_0) - f(x_0 - h)}{h} + O(h)}$$

- **Formula:** $f'_0 \approx \frac{y_0 - y_{-1}}{h}$
- **Truncation Error:** $E(h) = \frac{h}{2} f''(\xi) = \mathbf{O(h)}$

---

### 3. Central Difference Scheme (2nd Order Accurate — HIGH YIELD!)

Subtracting Eq. 2 from Eq. 1:
$$f(x_0 + h) - f(x_0 - h) = 2h f'(x_0) + \frac{2h^3}{3!} f'''(x_0) + \cdots$$

$$\boxed{f'(x_0) = \frac{f(x_0 + h) - f(x_0 - h)}{2h} + O(h^2)}$$

- **Formula:** $f'_0 \approx \frac{y_1 - y_{-1}}{2h}$
- **Truncation Error:** $E(h) = -\frac{h^2}{6} f'''(\xi) = \mathbf{O(h^2)}$ (Quadratic error reduction: halving $h$ quarters error!).

> ⚡ **Central Difference is 2nd order accurate $O(h^2)$ because odd-power terms in Taylor series cancel out!**

---

### 4. 3-Point High-Accuracy Forward & Backward Formulas ($O(h^2)$)

When boundary data prevents using central differences:

**3-Point Forward Difference ($O(h^2)$):**
$$\boxed{f'(x_0) \approx \frac{-3 f(x_0) + 4 f(x_0 + h) - f(x_0 + 2h)}{2h}}$$

**3-Point Backward Difference ($O(h^2)$):**
$$\boxed{f'(x_0) \approx \frac{3 f(x_0) - 4 f(x_0 - h) + f(x_0 - 2h)}{2h}}$$

---

## 🧠 Part III: Second Derivative Approximations

Adding Eq. 1 and Eq. 2:
$$f(x_0 + h) + f(x_0 - h) = 2f(x_0) + h^2 f''(x_0) + \frac{2h^4}{4!} f^{(4)}(x_0) + \cdots$$

Solving for $f''(x_0)$:

$$\boxed{f''(x_0) = \frac{f(x_0 + h) - 2f(x_0) + f(x_0 - h)}{h^2} + O(h^2)}$$

- **Formula:** $f''_0 \approx \frac{y_1 - 2y_0 + y_{-1}}{h^2}$
- **Stencil Coefficients:** $[1, -2, 1] / h^2$
- **Truncation Error:** $E(h) = -\frac{h^2}{12} f^{(4)}(\xi) = \mathbf{O(h^2)}$

---

## 🧠 Part IV: Differentiation from Finite Difference Tables

For data given at equal intervals $x_0, x_1, x_2, \ldots$ with spacing $h$:

### Forward Difference Operator $\Delta$
- $\Delta y_0 = y_1 - y_0$
- $\Delta^2 y_0 = \Delta y_1 - \Delta y_0 = y_2 - 2y_1 + y_0$
- $\Delta^3 y_0 = y_3 - 3y_2 + 3y_1 - y_0$

### Derivatives at the Start ($x = x_0$) via Newton's Forward Formula:

$$\boxed{f'(x_0) = \frac{1}{h} \left( \Delta y_0 - \frac{\Delta^2 y_0}{2} + \frac{\Delta^3 y_0}{3} - \frac{\Delta^4 y_0}{4} + \cdots \right)}$$

$$\boxed{f''(x_0) = \frac{1}{h^2} \left( \Delta^2 y_0 - \Delta^3 y_0 + \frac{11}{12}\Delta^4 y_0 - \cdots \right)}$$

---

### Derivatives at the End ($x = x_n$) via Newton's Backward Formula:

$$\boxed{f'(x_n) = \frac{1}{h} \left( \nabla y_n + \frac{\nabla^2 y_n}{2} + \frac{\nabla^3 y_n}{3} + \frac{\nabla^4 y_n}{4} + \cdots \right)}$$

$$\boxed{f''(x_n) = \frac{1}{h^2} \left( \nabla^2 y_n + \nabla^3 y_n + \frac{11}{12}\nabla^4 y_n + \cdots \right)}$$

---

## 🧠 Part V: Total Error & Optimal Step Size $h$

Total numerical error = **Truncation Error** (decreases as $h \to 0$) + **Round-off Error** (increases as $h \to 0$ due to division by small $h$).

$$\text{Total Error } E_{total} \approx \frac{M h}{2} + \frac{2\epsilon_{round}}{h}$$

### Optimal Step Size $h_{opt}$ for 1st-Order Scheme:
$$\boxed{h_{opt} = 2 \sqrt{\frac{\epsilon_{round}}{M}}}$$

### Optimal Step Size $h_{opt}$ for Central Difference Scheme:
$$\boxed{h_{opt} = \left( \frac{3 \epsilon_{round}}{M_3} \right)^{1/3}}$$

---

## ⚡ AIR-1 Speed Tricks & Mnemonics

### 🚀 Trick 1: Central Difference Supremacy ($O(h^2)$)
Always default to **Central Difference** $\frac{y_1 - y_{-1}}{2h}$ unless evaluating at domain boundaries where $y_{-1}$ or $y_1$ is unavailable!

---

### 🚀 Trick 2: Second Derivative 1-2-1 Stencil Memory
Memorize the stencil for $f''(x)$:  
$$\frac{1}{h^2} \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -2 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$$
Multiply points $[y_{-1}, y_0, y_1]$ by $[1, -2, 1]$ and divide by $h^2$. Done in 3 seconds!

---

### 🚀 Trick 3: Symmetry Cancels Odd Error Terms Rule
If a stencil is **symmetric** around $x_0$ (like central differences), all odd-order derivative error terms cancel $\implies$ accuracy order jumps from $O(h)$ to **$O(h^2)$**!

---

### 🚀 Trick 4: Newton's Forward Derivative Sign Pattern
- $f'(x_0)$: Alternating signs $+ \Delta - \frac{\Delta^2}{2} + \frac{\Delta^3}{3} - \frac{\Delta^4}{4} \dots$
- $f'(x_n)$ (Backward): ALL positive signs $+ \nabla + \frac{\nabla^2}{2} + \frac{\nabla^3}{3} + \frac{\nabla^4}{4} \dots$

---

### 🧠 Mnemonic: "**FBC**" Scheme Summary
- **F**orward: $\frac{y_1 - y_0}{h}$, Error $O(h)$ (uses $x_0, x_1$)
- **B**ackward: $\frac{y_0 - y_{-1}}{h}$, Error $O(h)$ (uses $x_{-1}, x_0$)
- **C**entral: $\frac{y_1 - y_{-1}}{2h}$, Error $O(h^2)$ (uses $x_{-1}, x_1$)

## 📝 Step-by-Step Solved GATE Examples

### Example 1: Comparison of Differentiation Schemes (GATE 2-Marker)
**Problem:** Estimate $f'(2)$ for $f(x) = x^3$ using step size $h = 0.1$ with:
(a) Forward Difference, (b) Backward Difference, (c) Central Difference. Compare with exact value.

**Exact Value:**  
$f'(x) = 3x^2 \implies f'(2) = 3(2)^2 = \mathbf{12}$.

**Points:**  
$x_0 = 2, f(2) = 8$  
$x_1 = 2.1, f(2.1) = (2.1)^3 = 9.261$  
$x_{-1} = 1.9, f(1.9) = (1.9)^3 = 6.859$

**(a) Forward Difference:**
$$f'(2) \approx \frac{f(2.1) - f(2)}{0.1} = \frac{9.261 - 8}{0.1} = \frac{1.261}{0.1} = \mathbf{12.61} \quad (\text{Error} = +0.61)$$

**(b) Backward Difference:**
$$f'(2) \approx \frac{f(2) - f(1.9)}{0.1} = \frac{8 - 6.859}{0.1} = \frac{1.141}{0.1} = \mathbf{11.41} \quad (\text{Error} = -0.59)$$

**(c) Central Difference:**
$$f'(2) \approx \frac{f(2.1) - f(1.9)}{2(0.1)} = \frac{9.261 - 6.859}{0.2} = \frac{2.402}{0.2} = \mathbf{12.01} \quad (\text{Error} = +0.01)$$

> ⚡ **Notice:** Central difference error ($0.01$) is **60 times smaller** than Forward/Backward error ($\approx 0.6$)!

---

### Example 2: Second Derivative Approximation (GATE PYQ Type)
**Problem:** Given tabular data:

| $x$ | $1.0$ | $1.2$ | $1.4$ |
|---|---|---|---|
| $y$ | $2.00$ | $2.44$ | $2.96$ |

Estimate $f''(1.2)$ using central difference.

**Solution (Using Speed Trick 2):**
$h = 0.2$, $x_0 = 1.2$, $y_{-1} = 2.00, y_0 = 2.44, y_1 = 2.96$.

$$f''(1.2) \approx \frac{y_1 - 2y_0 + y_{-1}}{h^2} = \frac{2.96 - 2(2.44) + 2.00}{(0.2)^2}$$
$$f''(1.2) \approx \frac{2.96 - 4.88 + 2.00}{0.04} = \frac{0.08}{0.04} = \mathbf{2.00}$$

$$\boxed{f''(1.2) = 2.00}$$

---

### Example 3: Tabular Data Derivative via Difference Table (GATE 2-Marker)
**Problem:** Given experimental data:

| $x$ | $0$ | $1$ | $2$ | $3$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| $y$ | $1$ | $2$ | $5$ | $10$ |

Find $f'(0)$ using Newton's Forward Difference formula.

**Solution:**
Step size $h = 1$.  
Forward difference table:
- $y_0 = 1, y_1 = 2, y_2 = 5, y_3 = 10$
- $\Delta y_0 = 2 - 1 = 1$
- $\Delta y_1 = 5 - 2 = 3 \implies \Delta^2 y_0 = 3 - 1 = 2$
- $\Delta y_2 = 10 - 5 = 5 \implies \Delta^2 y_1 = 5 - 3 = 2 \implies \Delta^3 y_0 = 2 - 2 = 0$

Apply formula:
$$f'(0) = \frac{1}{h} \left( \Delta y_0 - \frac{\Delta^2 y_0}{2} + \frac{\Delta^3 y_0}{3} \right) = \frac{1}{1} \left( 1 - \frac{2}{2} + 0 \right) = 1 - 1 = \mathbf{0}$$

$$\boxed{f'(0) = 0}$$

*(Note: Data fits $y = x^2 + 1 \implies f'(x) = 2x \implies f'(0) = 0$ exactly!)*

## 📐 Master Reference Table: Numerical Differentiation

| Scheme | Derivative | Formula | Order of Accuracy |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Forward Difference** | $f'(x_0)$ | $\frac{f(x_0+h) - f(x_0)}{h}$ | $O(h)$ |
| **Backward Difference** | $f'(x_0)$ | $\frac{f(x_0) - f(x_0-h)}{h}$ | $O(h)$ |
| **Central Difference** | $f'(x_0)$ | $\frac{f(x_0+h) - f(x_0-h)}{2h}$ | $O(h^2)$ |
| **Second Derivative Central** | $f''(x_0)$ | $\frac{f(x_0+h) - 2f(x_0) + f(x_0-h)}{h^2}$ | $O(h^2)$ |
| **3-Point Forward** | $f'(x_0)$ | $\frac{-3f(x_0) + 4f(x_0+h) - f(x_0+2h)}{2h}$ | $O(h^2)$ |
| **3-Point Backward** | $f'(x_0)$ | $\frac{3f(x_0) - 4f(x_0-h) + f(x_0-2h)}{2h}$ | $O(h^2)$ |
| **Newton Forward $f'(x_0)$** | $f'(x_0)$ | $\frac{1}{h}\left(\Delta y_0 - \frac{\Delta^2 y_0}{2} + \frac{\Delta^3 y_0}{3} - \cdots\right)$ | Exact for polynomials |
| **Newton Backward $f'(x_n)$** | $f'(x_n)$ | $\frac{1}{h}\left(\nabla y_n + \frac{\nabla^2 y_n}{2} + \frac{\nabla^3 y_n}{3} + \cdots\right)$ | Exact for polynomials |

## ⚠️ Common Exam Traps & Pitfalls

> 🔴 **Trap 1: Denominator Factor in Central Difference vs. Forward/Backward**  
> Central Difference denominator is **$2h$** (NOT $h$!). Forward/Backward denominator is **$h$**.  
> Second derivative central denominator is **$h^2$**.

> 🔴 **Trap 2: Order of Accuracy Confusion ($O(h)$ vs. $O(h^2)$)**  
> 2-point Forward/Backward are **$O(h)$**. Central difference is **$O(h^2)$**.  
> If $h$ is halved ($h \to h/2$):
> - Forward/Backward error halves ($\text{Error} \to \text{Error}/2$).
> - Central difference error quarters ($\text{Error} \to \text{Error}/4$).

> 🔴 **Trap 3: Signs in Newton's Forward vs Backward Formulas**  
> - Forward formula has **alternating signs**: $+ \Delta - \frac{\Delta^2}{2} + \frac{\Delta^3}{3} - \frac{\Delta^4}{4} \ldots$
> - Backward formula has **all positive signs**: $+ \nabla + \frac{\nabla^2}{2} + \frac{\nabla^3}{3} + \frac{\nabla^4}{4} \ldots$
