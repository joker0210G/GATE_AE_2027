## 📌 Executive Summary & GATE Significance

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The orientation of an aircraft relative to the oncoming free-stream air velocity vector ($\vec{V}_\infty$) is uniquely specified by two aerodynamic angles:
> 1. **Angle of Attack ($\alpha$)**: Pitch angle of relative wind relative to aircraft symmetry plane ($X_b - Z_b$):
>    $$\bbox[10px,border:2px solid #2196F3,background-color:#0d1117]{ \tan\alpha = \frac{w}{u} \implies \alpha = \arctan\left(\frac{w}{u}\right) \approx \frac{w}{u} \text{ (rad)} }$$
> 2. **Sideslip Angle ($\beta$)**: Yaw angle of relative wind relative to aircraft symmetry plane ($X_b - Z_b$):
>    $$\bbox[10px,border:2px solid #E91E63,background-color:#0d1117]{ \sin\beta = \frac{v}{V} \implies \beta = \arcsin\left(\frac{v}{V}\right) \approx \frac{v}{V} \text{ (rad)} }$$
> where $(u, v, w)$ are aircraft body-frame velocity components and $V = \sqrt{u^2 + v^2 + w^2}$ is true airspeed.

### Why does GATE AE care?
1. **Kinematic Velocity Resolution:** GATE AE tests converting body-axis velocity components $(u,v,w)$ to speed $V$, angle of attack $\alpha$, and sideslip angle $\beta$.
2. **Induced Angle of Attack ($\alpha_i$):** Calculating 3D wing section downwash ($\alpha_i = C_L / \pi AR e$) and effective angle of attack ($\alpha_{eff} = \alpha - \alpha_i$).
3. **Static Stability Derivatives:** $\alpha$ governs longitudinal pitch stability ($C_{m_\alpha}$), while $\beta$ governs directional yaw stability ($C_{n_\beta}$) and lateral roll stability ($C_{l_\beta}$).

## 🔵 Core Concept 1: Velocity Vector Resolution & Definitions

```
                     AIRCRAFT BODY-AXIS VELOCITIES
                     
                                 X_b (Longitudinal / Forward)
                                     ^
                                     |  u (Forward Velocity)
                                     |
              v (Right Velocity)     |
    Y_b <----------------------------+
  (Lateral / Right)                  |
                                     |
                                     v
                                 Z_b (Normal / Downward)
                                     w (Downward Velocity)
```

### 1. Velocity Components & Total Airspeed ($V$)
Let $(u, v, w)$ be the linear velocity components of the relative wind expressed in body-fixed axes:
- $u$: Velocity along longitudinal axis $X_b$ (positive forward).
- $v$: Velocity along lateral axis $Y_b$ (positive towards right wing).
- $w$: Velocity along vertical body axis $Z_b$ (positive downward).

The total true airspeed magnitude $V$ is:
$$\bbox[10px,border:2px solid #2196F3,background-color:#0d1117]{ V = \sqrt{u^2 + v^2 + w^2} }$$

---

### 2. Exact Transformation Equations
Expressing velocity components in terms of Airspeed $V$, Angle of Attack $\alpha$, and Sideslip Angle $\beta$:

$$\bbox[12px,border:3px solid #E91E63,background-color:#0d1117]{ u = V \cos\alpha \cos\beta }$$

$$\bbox[12px,border:3px solid #E91E63,background-color:#0d1117]{ v = V \sin\beta }$$

$$\bbox[12px,border:3px solid #E91E63,background-color:#0d1117]{ w = V \sin\alpha \cos\beta }$$

---

### 3. Small Angle Approximations ($\alpha, \beta \ll 1 \text{ rad}$)
For un-stalled normal flight ($\alpha, \beta < 15^\circ \implies \cos\alpha \approx 1, \cos\beta \approx 1$):
$$\bbox[8px,border:1px solid #FF9800,background-color:#0d1117]{ u \approx V }$$
$$\bbox[8px,border:1px solid #FF9800,background-color:#0d1117]{ \alpha \approx \frac{w}{u} \approx \frac{w}{V} \text{ (in radians)} }$$
$$\bbox[8px,border:1px solid #FF9800,background-color:#0d1117]{ \beta \approx \frac{v}{V} \text{ (in radians)} }$$

## 🔵 Core Concept 2: Angle of Attack Classifications ($\alpha, \alpha_{abs}, \alpha_{eff}, \alpha_i$)

```
                       ANGLE OF ATTACK TYPES
                                 |
    +------------------+---------+---------+------------------+
    |                  |                   |                  |
Geometric Alpha    Zero-Lift Alpha     Absolute Alpha     Induced & Effective
  (Chord Line)       (alpha_L=0)        (Zero-Lift Line)    (3D Downwash Tilt)
```

### 1. Geometric Angle of Attack ($\alpha$)
Measured between the **physical chord line** of the airfoil/wing and the relative wind vector $\vec{V}_\infty$.

### 2. Zero-Lift Angle of Attack ($\alpha_{L=0}$)
The specific geometric angle of attack at which the airfoil produces **ZERO Lift ($C_L = 0$)**.
- **Symmetric Airfoil:** $\alpha_{L=0} = 0^\circ$.
- **Positively Cambered Airfoil:** $\alpha_{L=0} < 0^\circ$ (typically $-2^\circ$ to $-4^\circ$). Generates positive lift even at $\alpha = 0^\circ$!
- **Negatively Cambered Airfoil:** $\alpha_{L=0} > 0^\circ$.

---

### 3. Absolute Angle of Attack ($\alpha_{abs}$)
Measured from the **Zero-Lift Line ($ZLL$)** of the airfoil:
$$\bbox[12px,border:3px solid #2196F3,background-color:#0d1117]{ \alpha_{abs} = \alpha - \alpha_{L=0} }$$

Lift Coefficient in terms of Absolute Alpha:
$$C_L = a_0 \cdot \alpha_{abs} = a_0 (\alpha - \alpha_{L=0})$$
where $a_0 = \frac{dC_L}{d\alpha}$ is the 2D lift-curve slope ($a_0 = 2\pi \approx 6.28 \text{ rad}^{-1}$ for thin airfoils).

---

### 4. Induced Angle of Attack ($\alpha_i$) & Effective Angle of Attack ($\alpha_{eff}$) for 3D Wings

On a 3D finite wing, wingtip vortices induce a downward velocity component called **Downwash ($w_i$)**, tilting the local relative wind downward by the **Induced Angle of Attack ($\alpha_i$)**:

```
                       3D WING DOWNWASH GEOMETRY
                       
      Relative Wind V_inf ------------------------>  Chord Line
                            \  alpha_i  /
                             \         /
                              \       /  alpha_eff
                               v     v
                         Local Relative Wind V_local
```

#### Formulas for 3D Finite Wing:
$$\bbox[10px,border:2px solid #9C27B0,background-color:#0d1117]{ \alpha_i = \frac{w_i}{V_\infty} = \frac{C_L}{\pi AR e} \text{ (in radians)} }$$

$$\bbox[12px,border:3px solid #E91E63,background-color:#0d1117]{ \alpha_{eff} = \alpha - \alpha_i = \alpha - \frac{C_L}{\pi AR e} }$$

where $AR = \frac{b^2}{S}$ is Aspect Ratio and $e$ is Oswald span efficiency factor ($e \le 1.0$).

## ⚡ AIR-1 Mnemonics & Speed Tricks

### 🧠 Mnemonic 1: "A-W-U" (Alpha Formula)
- **A**lpha $= \mathbf{w / u}$ (Downward velocity $w$ divided by Forward velocity $u$).

### 🧠 Mnemonic 2: "B-V-V" (Beta Formula)
- **B**eta $= \mathbf{v / V}$ (Rightward velocity $v$ divided by Total Airspeed $V$).

### ⚡ Speed Trick 1: Degree-to-Radian 5-Second Conversion
In $\alpha_i = \frac{C_L}{\pi AR e}$, the output is in **RADIANS**!
To convert to **DEGREES**:
$$\mathbf{\alpha_i (^\circ) = \frac{57.3 \times C_L}{\pi AR e}}$$

## 📝 Solved GATE & Exam-Standard Examples

### Example 1: Velocity Component Resolution for $\alpha$ and $\beta$ (GATE Classic)
**Problem:** An aircraft is flying through turbulent air with body-axis velocity components:
- Forward velocity $u = 120.0 \text{ m/s}$
- Lateral rightward velocity $v = 12.0 \text{ m/s}$
- Downward velocity $w = 10.5 \text{ m/s}$

Calculate:
1. True Airspeed $V$
2. Angle of attack $\alpha$ in degrees
3. Sideslip angle $\beta$ in degrees

**Solution:**
1. **True Airspeed ($V$):**
   $$V = \sqrt{u^2 + v^2 + w^2} = \sqrt{120^2 + 12^2 + 10.5^2} = \sqrt{14400 + 144 + 110.25} = \sqrt{14654.25} = \mathbf{121.055 \text{ m/s}}$$

2. **Angle of Attack ($\alpha$):**
   $$\tan\alpha = \frac{w}{u} = \frac{10.5}{120.0} = 0.0875$$
   $$\alpha = \arctan(0.0875) = 5.0006^\circ \approx \mathbf{5.00^\circ}$$

3. **Sideslip Angle ($\beta$):**
   $$\sin\beta = \frac{v}{V} = \frac{12.0}{121.055} = 0.099128$$
   $$\beta = \arcsin(0.099128) = 5.688^\circ \approx \mathbf{5.69^\circ}$$

---

### Example 2: Effective Angle of Attack on 3D Finite Wing
**Problem:** A rectangular wing with Aspect Ratio $AR = 8.0$ and Oswald efficiency $e = 0.90$ flies at a geometric angle of attack $\alpha = 8.0^\circ$. The airfoil has zero-lift angle $\alpha_{L=0} = -2.0^\circ$ and 2D lift slope $a_0 = 2\pi \text{ rad}^{-1}$. Calculate:
1. 3D wing lift curve slope $a$
2. Lift coefficient $C_L$
3. Induced angle of attack $\alpha_i$ in degrees
4. Effective angle of attack $\alpha_{eff}$ in degrees

**Solution:**
1. **3D Lift Curve Slope ($a$):**
   $$a = \frac{a_0}{1 + \frac{a_0}{\pi AR e}} = \frac{2\pi}{1 + \frac{2\pi}{\pi \times 8 \times 0.90}} = \frac{6.2832}{1 + \frac{2}{7.2}} = \frac{6.2832}{1 + 0.2778} = \frac{6.2832}{1.2778} = \mathbf{4.917 \text{ rad}^{-1}}$$

2. **Absolute Angle of Attack ($\alpha_{abs}$):**
   $$\alpha_{abs} = \alpha - \alpha_{L=0} = 8.0^\circ - (-2.0^\circ) = 10.0^\circ = 10.0 \times \frac{\pi}{180} = \mathbf{0.17453 \text{ rad}}$$

3. **Lift Coefficient ($C_L$):**
   $$C_L = a \cdot \alpha_{abs} = 4.917 \times 0.17453 = \mathbf{0.8582}$$

4. **Induced Angle of Attack ($\alpha_i$):**
   $$\alpha_i = \frac{C_L}{\pi AR e} = \frac{0.8582}{\pi \times 8 \times 0.90} = \frac{0.8582}{22.6195} = \mathbf{0.03794 \text{ rad}}$$
   $$\alpha_i (^\circ) = 0.03794 \times 57.2958 = \mathbf{2.17^\circ}$$

5. **Effective Angle of Attack ($\alpha_{eff}$):**
   $$\alpha_{eff} = \alpha - \alpha_i = 8.0^\circ - 2.17^\circ = \mathbf{5.83^\circ}$$

## ⚠️ Common GATE Traps & Exam Pitfalls

> [!CAUTION]
> 1. **$\tan\alpha = w/u$ vs $\sin\alpha = w/V$ Trap:** Angle of attack is defined by $\tan\alpha = w/u$ (ratio of normal to longitudinal body velocity), NOT $w/V$. Sideslip angle is defined by $\sin\beta = v/V$!
> 2. **Radian vs Degree Unit Trap:** In $\alpha_i = C_L / (\pi AR e)$, the result is strictly in **RADIANS**. Remember to multiply by $57.3^\circ$ when subtracting from geometric $\alpha$ given in degrees!
> 3. **Zero-Lift Angle Sign Trap:** For a positively cambered airfoil, $\alpha_{L=0}$ is **NEGATIVE** (e.g., $-3^\circ$). Therefore, $\alpha_{abs} = \alpha - (-3^\circ) = \alpha + 3^\circ$!
> 4. **Aspect Ratio Effect:** High aspect ratio wings ($AR \to \infty$) have $\alpha_i \to 0$, so $\alpha_{eff} \approx \alpha$. Low aspect ratio wings suffer large induced downwash $\alpha_i$.

## 🔄 Revision & Master Alpha & Beta Reference Card

```
====================================================================================
               ANGLE OF ATTACK (alpha) & SIDESLIP (beta) REFERENCE CARD
====================================================================================
1. EXACT DEFINITIONS:
   - Total Airspeed:    V = sqrt(u^2 + v^2 + w^2)
   - Angle of Attack:   tan(alpha) = w / u  --> alpha = arctan(w/u) ≈ w/u (rad)
   - Sideslip Angle:    sin(beta)  = v / V  --> beta  = arcsin(v/V) ≈ v/V (rad)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. BODY VELOCITY RESOLUTION:
   - u = V * cos(alpha) * cos(beta)
   - v = V * sin(beta)
   - w = V * sin(alpha) * cos(beta)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ALPHA TYPES:
   - Absolute Alpha:   alpha_abs = alpha - alpha_L=0
   - Induced Alpha:    alpha_i   = CL / (pi * AR * e)   [in radians]
   - Effective Alpha:  alpha_eff = alpha - alpha_i
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. AIRFOIL ZERO-LIFT ANGLES:
   - Symmetric:        alpha_L=0 = 0°
   - Pos. Cambered:    alpha_L=0 < 0°  (e.g., -2° to -4°)
   - Neg. Cambered:    alpha_L=0 > 0°
====================================================================================
```
