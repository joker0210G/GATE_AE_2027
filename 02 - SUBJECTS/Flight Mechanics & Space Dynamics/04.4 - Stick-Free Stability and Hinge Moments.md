## 🧠 MASTER MENTAL MODEL — What is Stick-Free Longitudinal Stability?

> **CORE CONCEPT:** In **Stick-Free Static Stability**, the pilot **releases the control column (stick)**. The elevator is no longer held at a rigid angle; instead, it is free to rotate about its hinge line until the net aerodynamic **Hinge Moment is ZERO ($C_{h_e} = 0$)**.

```
  STICK-FIXED: Elevator locked rigidly by pilot/actuator
               Elevator deflection δ_e = constant (does NOT float with AoA)
               Neutral Point = h_n

  STICK-FREE:  Control stick released by pilot
               Elevator FLOATS freely to balance aerodynamic hinge moments (C_h = 0)
               Floating angle δ_e,float = -(C_h_α / C_h_δe) · α_t
               Neutral Point = h_n*  (ALWAYS FORWARD OF h_n: h_n* < h_n)
```

### Why is Stick-Free Stability More Critical?
When an atmospheric gust increases the aircraft's angle of attack ($\alpha \uparrow$), the local tail angle of attack increases ($\alpha_t \uparrow$). 

1. Aerodynamic pressure on the elevator creates a **trailing-edge UP moment** ($C_{h_\alpha} < 0$).
2. The free elevator **floats UP** ($\delta_{e,\text{float}} < 0$).
3. Floating UP **reduces the effective lift generated by the horizontal tail**!
4. Less tail lift $\implies$ **reduced restoring pitching moment** $\implies$ **Stick-Free Neutral Point shifts FORWARD ($h_n^* < h_n$)**!

> 🎯 **GOLDEN DESIGN RULE:**
> An aircraft must be designed to be stable under **Stick-Free conditions** ($h < h_n^*$), because if the pilot lets go of the stick, the aircraft must remain self-correcting!

## 📐 SECTION 1 — Hinge Moment Mechanics & Derivatives ($C_{h_e}$)

### 1.1 Dimensional Hinge Moment ($H_e$)

The aerodynamic moment acting on the elevator about its hinge line is:

$$\boxed{H_e = q_t S_e c_e C_{h_e}}$$

Where:
- $q_t = \frac{1}{2} \rho_t V_t^2 = \eta_t q_\infty$ = Dynamic pressure at horizontal tail
- $S_e$ = Area of elevator aft of hinge line
- $c_e$ = Mean chord of elevator aft of hinge line
- $C_{h_e}$ = Non-dimensional Elevator Hinge Moment Coefficient

---

### 1.2 Non-Dimensional Hinge Moment Coefficient ($C_{h_e}$)

Linearizing $C_{h_e}$ with respect to tail angle of attack ($\alpha_t$), elevator deflection ($\delta_e$), and trim tab deflection ($\delta_t$):

$$\boxed{C_{h_e} = C_{h_0} + C_{h_\alpha} \alpha_t + C_{h_{\delta_e}} \delta_e + C_{h_{\delta_t}} \delta_t}$$

```
                       C_h_α < 0                           C_h_δe < 0
             (Floating Tendency)                  (Restoring Tendency)
                     
                  Tail AoA ↑                          Elevator Down (+δ_e)
                      │                                        │
                      ▼                                        ▼
             Elevator Floats UP                      Restoring Moment DOWN
            (Trailing Edge UP)                       (Trailing Edge UP)
```

#### Detailed Breakdown of Hinge Moment Derivatives:

| Derivative | Mathematical Definition | Typical Sign | Physical Meaning & Role |
|------------|------------------------|--------------|-------------------------|
| **$C_{h_0}$** | $\left.\frac{\partial C_{h_e}}{\partial \alpha_t}\right|_{\alpha_t=\delta_e=0}$ | $\approx 0$ (for symmetric) | Residual hinge moment at zero AoA and deflection |
| **$C_{h_\alpha}$** | $\frac{\partial C_{h_e}}{\partial \alpha_t}$ | **$< 0$ (Negative)** | Change in hinge moment per unit change in tail AoA. Negative sign means AoA increase floats elevator UP. |
| **$C_{h_{\delta_e}}$** | $\frac{\partial C_{h_e}}{\partial \delta_e}$ | **$< 0$ (Negative)** | Change in hinge moment per unit elevator deflection. Negative sign acts as aerodynamic stiffness restoring elevator toward neutral. |
| **$C_{h_{\delta_t}}$** | $\frac{\partial C_{h_e}}{\partial \delta_t}$ | **$< 0$ (Negative)** | Trim tab hinge moment effectiveness. Used by pilot to trim stick force to zero ($F_s = 0$). |

## 📐 SECTION 2 — Elevator Floating Angle ($\delta_{e,\text{float}}$) & Free Elevator Factor ($f$)

### 2.1 Derivation of Floating Angle ($\delta_{e,\text{float}}$)

When the stick is free, the elevator floats until $C_{h_e} = 0$. Setting $C_{h_e} = 0$:

$$C_{h_0} + C_{h_\alpha} \alpha_t + C_{h_{\delta_e}} \delta_{e,\text{float}} + C_{h_{\delta_t}} \delta_t = 0$$

Solving for $\delta_{e,\text{float}}$ (assuming zero trim tab $\delta_t = 0$):

$$\boxed{\delta_{e,\text{float}} = -\frac{C_{h_0}}{C_{h_{\delta_e}}} - \left(\frac{C_{h_\alpha}}{C_{h_{\delta_e}}}\right) \alpha_t}$$

---

### 2.2 Free Elevator Factor ($f$)

The rate of change of elevator floating angle with respect to tail angle of attack is defined as the **Free Elevator Factor ($f$)**:

$$\boxed{f = \frac{d\delta_{e,\text{float}}}{d\alpha_t} = -\frac{C_{h_\alpha}}{C_{h_{\delta_e}}}}$$

Since both $C_{h_\alpha} < 0$ and $C_{h_{\delta_e}} < 0$:

$$\boxed{f = -\frac{-|C_{h_\alpha}|}{-|C_{h_{\delta_e}}|} = -\frac{|C_{h_\alpha}|}{|C_{h_{\delta_e}}|} < 0}$$

> 💡 **KEY PHYSICAL INSIGHT:**
> Because $f < 0$, an **increase in tail angle of attack ($\Delta \alpha_t > 0$) causes the elevator to float UP ($\Delta \delta_e < 0$)**.
> This floating motion opposes the tail lift generation!

---

## 📐 SECTION 3 — Stick-Free Tail Lift Slope ($a_t^*$) & Pitch Stiffness ($C_{m_\alpha}^*$)

### 3.1 Stick-Free Tail Lift Curve Slope ($a_t^*$)

The lift coefficient of the horizontal tail with elevator deflection is:

$$C_{L_t} = a_t \alpha_t + a_t \tau_e \delta_e$$

Substituting the floating elevator angle $\delta_{e,\text{float}} = f \cdot \alpha_t = -\left(\frac{C_{h_\alpha}}{C_{h_{\delta_e}}}\right) \alpha_t$:

$$C_{L_t} = a_t \alpha_t + a_t \tau_e \left(-\frac{C_{h_\alpha}}{C_{h_{\delta_e}}}\right) \alpha_t = a_t \left(1 - \frac{C_{h_\alpha}}{C_{h_{\delta_e}}} \tau_e\right) \alpha_t$$

Defining the **Stick-Free Horizontal Tail Lift Curve Slope ($a_t^*$)**:

$$\boxed{a_t^* = \frac{d C_{L_t}}{d \alpha_t} = a_t \left(1 - \frac{C_{h_\alpha}}{C_{h_{\delta_e}}} \tau_e\right) = a_t (1 + f \tau_e)}$$

Where:
- $a_t$ = Stick-Fixed Tail Lift Slope ($\text{rad}^{-1}$)
- $\tau_e$ = Elevator flap effectiveness factor ($\approx 0.4 - 0.6$)
- $f = -\frac{C_{h_\alpha}}{C_{h_{\delta_e}}} < 0$

> 🛡️ **CRITICAL RELATIONSHIP:**
> Since $\frac{C_{h_\alpha}}{C_{h_{\delta_e}}} > 0$ and $\tau_e > 0$:
> $$\boxed{a_t^* < a_t \quad \text{ALWAYS!}}$$
> The floating elevator **reduces the tail's effective lift-curve slope** by $10\% - 30\%$!

---

### 3.2 Stick-Free Pitch Stiffness Derivative ($C_{m_\alpha}^*$)

Substituting $a_t^*$ into the total aircraft pitching moment slope equation:

$$\boxed{C_{m_\alpha}^* = C_{L_{\alpha,\text{wb}}} \left(h - h_{ac,\text{wb}}\right) - \eta_t V_H a_t^* \left(1 - \frac{d\epsilon}{d\alpha}\right)}$$

Comparing Stick-Fixed vs. Stick-Free pitch stiffness:

$$C_{m_\alpha}^* - C_{m_\alpha} = \eta_t V_H a_t \tau_e \left(1 - \frac{d\epsilon}{d\alpha}\right) \left(\frac{C_{h_\alpha}}{C_{h_{\delta_e}}}\right) > 0$$

$$\boxed{C_{m_\alpha}^* > C_{m_\alpha} \quad (\text{Less negative } \implies \text{LESS STABLE!})}$$

---

## 📐 SECTION 4 — Stick-Free Neutral Point ($h_n^*$) & Static Margin ($K_n^*$)

### 4.1 Derivation of Stick-Free Neutral Point ($h_n^*$)

Setting $C_{m_\alpha}^* = 0$:

$$C_{L_{\alpha,\text{wb}}} \left(h_n^* - h_{ac,\text{wb}}\right) - \eta_t V_H a_t^* \left(1 - \frac{d\epsilon}{d\alpha}\right) = 0$$

Solving for $h_n^*$:

$$\boxed{h_n^* = h_{ac,\text{wb}} + \frac{\eta_t V_H a_t^*}{C_{L_\alpha}} \left(1 - \frac{d\epsilon}{d\alpha}\right)}$$

Or expressing in terms of $h_n$ (Stick-Fixed Neutral Point):

$$\boxed{h_n^* = h_n - \frac{\eta_t V_H a_t \tau_e}{C_{L_\alpha}} \left(1 - \frac{d\epsilon}{d\alpha}\right) \left(\frac{C_{h_\alpha}}{C_{h_{\delta_e}}}\right)}$$

$$\boxed{h_n^* < h_n \quad \text{(Stick-Free NP is ALWAYS FORWARD of Stick-Fixed NP!)}}$$

```
  LEMAC                                                                TE
  ───┼─────────────┼───────────────┼──────────────────┼─────────────────┼───> x
     0           AC_wb            h_n*               h_n               1.0
                  (0.25)       Stick-Free         Stick-Fixed
                              Neutral Point      Neutral Point
```

---

### 4.2 Stick-Free Static Margin ($K_n^*$)

The **Stick-Free Static Margin ($K_n^*$)** is the distance between the Stick-Free Neutral Point and the CG:

$$\boxed{K_n^* = h_n^* - h}$$

$$\boxed{C_{m_\alpha}^* = -C_{L_\alpha} \cdot K_n^*}$$

Since $h_n^* < h_n$:

$$\boxed{K_n^* < K_n \quad \text{(Stick-Free Static Margin is SMALLER than Stick-Fixed Static Margin)}}$$

| Parameter | Stick-Fixed Condition | Stick-Free Condition | Relationship |
|-----------|----------------------|----------------------|--------------|
| **Control State** | Elevator locked rigidly ($\delta_e = \text{const}$) | Elevator floats freely ($C_{h_e} = 0$) | — |
| **Tail Lift Slope** | $a_t$ | $a_t^* = a_t \left(1 - \frac{C_{h_\alpha}}{C_{h_{\delta_e}}}\tau_e\right)$ | $a_t^* < a_t$ |
| **Pitch Stiffness** | $C_{m_\alpha}$ | $C_{m_\alpha}^*$ | $C_{m_\alpha} < C_{m_\alpha}^* < 0$ |
| **Neutral Point** | $h_n$ | $h_n^*$ | $\mathbf{h_n^* < h_n}$ |
| **Static Margin** | $K_n = h_n - h$ | $K_n^* = h_n^* - h$ | $\mathbf{K_n^* < K_n}$ |

## 📐 SECTION 5 — Control Stick Force ($F_s$) & Stick Force Gradient ($\frac{dF_s}{dV}$)

### 5.1 Mechanical Linkage & Stick Force Definition ($F_s$)

The force exerted by the pilot on the control column to maintain a specific flight condition is:

$$\boxed{F_s = G \cdot H_e = G \cdot q_t S_e c_e C_{h_e}}$$

Where:
- $G = \frac{d\delta_e}{ds}$ = Mechanical gearing ratio ($\text{rad/m}$ or $\text{rad/ft}$ of stick movement)
- $H_e$ = Elevator aerodynamic hinge moment ($\text{N}\cdot\text{m}$)
- $F_s > 0$ $\implies$ Pilot **PULLS** stick back (nose up)
- $F_s < 0$ $\implies$ Pilot **PUSHES** stick forward (nose down)

---

### 5.2 Trim Tab Mechanics & Zero Stick Force ($F_s = 0$)

In steady flight, a pilot uses a **Trim Tab** ($\delta_t$) to make the net hinge moment zero ($C_{h_e} = 0 \implies F_s = 0$) at the desired cruising speed.

```
       ELEVATOR DOWN (+δ_e)               TRIM TAB UP (-δ_t)
       Generates Nose-Down Moment          Generates Downward Hinge Moment
            ┌──────────────────┐               ┌───┐
            │                  └───────────────┤   │
            └──────────────────┐               └───┘
                               └───────────────────> Flow
```

Setting $C_{h_e} = 0$ gives the required trim tab deflection:

$$\boxed{\delta_{t,\text{trim}} = -\frac{C_{h_0} + C_{h_\alpha}\alpha_t + C_{h_{\delta_e}}\delta_{e,\text{trim}}}{C_{h_{\delta_t}}}}$$

> 💡 **CRITICAL TRIM TAB RULE:**
> To hold a surface deflected in one direction, the trim tab deflects **IN THE OPPOSITE DIRECTION**!
> Example: To trim UP elevator ($\delta_e < 0$), trim tab deflects DOWN ($\delta_t > 0$).

---

### 5.3 Analytical Equation for Stick Force ($F_s$)

Substituting $\delta_{e,\text{trim}}$ into the stick force equation yields the standard GATE formula:

$$\boxed{F_s = G q_\infty S_e c_e \eta_t \left(\frac{C_{h_{\delta_e}}}{C_{m_{\delta_e}}}\right) C_{L_\alpha} K_n^* \left(C_L - C_{L,\text{trim}}\right)}$$

---

### 5.4 Stick Force Gradient with Airspeed ($\frac{dF_s}{dV}$)

Expressing $C_L = \frac{2W}{\rho_\infty S V^2}$ and differentiating $F_s$ with respect to velocity $V$:

$$\boxed{\frac{dF_s}{dV} = -2 G S_e c_e \eta_t \left(\frac{C_{h_{\delta_e}}}{C_{m_{\delta_e}}}\right) \left(\frac{W}{S}\right) K_n^* \left(1 - \frac{V^2}{V_{\text{trim}}^2}\right)}$$

For a stable aircraft ($K_n^* > 0$):

```
  Stick Force (F_s)
     ^
PULL |   \  <--- Slower than trim (V < V_trim): Pilot PULLS stick (F_s > 0)
(+F_s|    \
     |     \   Trim Speed (V = V_trim, F_s = 0)
  ───┼──────\─────────────────────────> Airspeed (V)
     |       \
PUSH |        \ <--- Faster than trim (V > V_trim): Pilot PUSHES stick (F_s < 0)
(-F_s|         v
```

> 🎯 **FAA / DGCA REGULATORY REQUIREMENT:**
> - To fly **slower** than trim speed ($V < V_{\text{trim}}$), pilot must **PULL** back ($F_s > 0$).
> - To fly **faster** than trim speed ($V > V_{\text{trim}}$), pilot must **PUSH** forward ($F_s < 0$).
> - The slope $\frac{dF_s}{dV}$ is directly proportional to the **Stick-Free Static Margin ($K_n^*$)**!

## 📐 SECTION 6 — Maneuver Point ($h_m, h_m^*$) & Maneuver Margin ($K_m, K_m^*$)

### 6.1 Curved Maneuvering Flight & Pitch Rate ($q$)

During a pitch maneuver (such as a level turn or pull-up loop with load factor $n = L/W > 1$), the aircraft rotates about its y-axis with pitch rate $q$:

$$q = \frac{g (n - 1)}{V_\infty}$$

This pitch rate creates an additional angle of attack at the horizontal tail:

$$\Delta \alpha_{t,q} = \frac{q l_t}{V_\infty} = \frac{g l_t (n - 1)}{V_\infty^2}$$

This additional tail AoA creates an extra stabilizing pitch damping moment, shifting the effective neutral point **AFT**!

---

### 6.2 Stick-Fixed Maneuver Point ($h_m$) & Maneuver Margin ($K_m$)

The **Stick-Fixed Maneuver Point ($h_m$)** is the CG position at which elevator deflection per $g$ is zero ($\frac{d\delta_e}{dn} = 0$):

$$\boxed{h_m = h_n + \frac{\rho_\infty g l_t}{2 (W/S)} \left[\eta_t V_H a_t \frac{l_t}{\bar{c}} - C_{m_q}\right] > h_n}$$

The **Stick-Fixed Maneuver Margin ($K_m$)**:

$$\boxed{K_m = h_m - h > K_n}$$

---

### 6.3 Stick-Free Maneuver Point ($h_m^*$) & Maneuver Margin ($K_m^*$)

The **Stick-Free Maneuver Point ($h_m^*$)** is the CG position at which control stick force per $g$ is zero ($\frac{dF_s}{dn} = 0$):

$$\boxed{h_m^* = h_n^* + \frac{\rho_\infty g l_t}{2 (W/S)} \left[\eta_t V_H a_t^* \frac{l_t}{\bar{c}} - C_{m_q}^*\right] > h_n^*}$$

The **Stick-Free Maneuver Margin ($K_m^*$)**:

$$\boxed{K_m^* = h_m^* - h}$$

---

### 6.4 Stick Force Per g ($\frac{dF_s}{dn}$)

The stick force required to pull a load factor increment of $1g$ ($\Delta n = 1$) is:

$$\boxed{\frac{dF_s}{dn} = -G S_e c_e \eta_t \left(\frac{C_{h_{\delta_e}}}{C_{m_{\delta_e}}}\right) \left(\frac{W}{S}\right) C_{L_\alpha} K_m^*}$$

---

### 6.5 Master Chordwise Hierarchy of Critical Points

$$\boxed{h_{ac,wb} < h_n^* < h_n < h_m^* < h_m}$$

```
  LEMAC                                                                TE
  ───┼───────┼───────────┼───────────┼───────────┼───────────┼──────────┼───> x
     0     AC_wb        h_n*        h_n         h_m*        h_m        1.0
                         NP          NP          MP          MP
                       (Free)      (Fixed)     (Free)      (Fixed)
```

## 📐 SECTION 7 — Aerodynamic Balancing of Control Surfaces

To keep pilot stick forces within manageable human limits ($F_s < 200\text{ N}$) without hydraulic boosters, aerodynamic balancing techniques are used to reduce $C_{h_{\delta_e}}$ and $C_{h_\alpha}$:

```
  1. OVERHANG BALANCE                   2. HORN BALANCE
     Hinge line set back into surface      Extension ahead of hinge line at tip
        ┌───────┬──────────────┐               ┌──────────┐
        │ Nose  │   Elevator   │               │ Horn Tip │   Elevator
        └───────┴──────────────┘               └────┬─────┴──────────────┐
                ▲ Hinge                             │  Hinge
                                                    ▼ Line
```

| Method | Description | Aerodynamic Effect |
|--------|-------------|--------------------|
| **Overhang Balance** | Hinge line moved rearward into control surface chord | Reduces $|C_{h_{\delta_e}}|$ by creating counter-moment ahead of hinge |
| **Horn Balance** | Triangular/rectangular extension at control tip ahead of hinge | Shielded or unshielded horn reduces $|C_{h_\alpha}|$ and $|C_{h_{\delta_e}}|$ |
| **Internal Balance** | Flexible seal inside internal vented cavity ahead of hinge | Pressure differential across seal opposes hinge moment |
| **Beveled Trailing Edge** | Wedged shape trailing edge increasing boundary layer pressure | Reduces $|C_{h_{\delta_e}}|$ |
| **Balance Tab** | Linked mechanically to deflect **OPPOSITE** to primary surface | Automatically reduces stick force during deflection |
| **Anti-Servo Tab** | Linked mechanically to deflect in **SAME** direction as surface | Increases stick force (used on all-moving stabilators for feel) |

## 🔑 SECTION 8 — Mnemonics, Shortcuts & Tricks

### 8.1 Master Rule: "Free Elevator Floats UP, Pulls NP Forward"
> **"Floating elevator drops tail lift, making stick-free neutral point MOVE FORWARD ($h_n^* < h_n$)!"**

### 8.2 Master Point Hierarchy Mnemonic: "AC < NP-Free < NP-Fixed < MP-Free < MP-Fixed"
> **"A-N-N-M-M"** (Aerodynamic Center $\to$ NP Free $\to$ NP Fixed $\to$ MP Free $\to$ MP Fixed).

### 8.3 5-Second Stick-Free Tail Slope Shortcut
$$\boxed{a_t^* = a_t \left(1 - \frac{C_{h_\alpha}}{C_{h_{\delta_e}}} \tau_e\right)}$$

### 8.4 5-Second Neutral Point Shift Shortcut
$$\boxed{\Delta h_n = h_n - h_n^* = \frac{\eta_t V_H a_t \tau_e}{C_{L_\alpha}} \left(1 - \frac{d\epsilon}{d\alpha}\right) \left(\frac{C_{h_\alpha}}{C_{h_{\delta_e}}}\right)}$$

## 🔢 SECTION 9 — Solved GATE-Style Numerical Examples

### Example 1 — Complete Stick-Free Neutral Point & Static Margin Calculation

**Problem:** An aircraft has the following measured parameters:
- Wing-body aerodynamic center $h_{ac,wb} = 0.25$
- Horizontal tail volume ratio $V_H = 0.55$, Tail efficiency $\eta_t = 0.90$
- Wing lift curve slope $C_{L_\alpha} = 4.5\text{ rad}^{-1}$, Tail lift curve slope $a_t = 3.8\text{ rad}^{-1}$
- Downwash gradient $\frac{d\epsilon}{d\alpha} = 0.35$
- Elevator effectiveness $\tau_e = 0.50$
- Elevator hinge moment derivatives: $C_{h_\alpha} = -0.30\text{ rad}^{-1}$, $C_{h_{\delta_e}} = -0.60\text{ rad}^{-1}$
- Current CG location $h = 0.30$

**Calculate:**
1. Stick-Fixed Neutral Point $h_n$.
2. Stick-Free Tail Lift Slope $a_t^*$.
3. Stick-Free Neutral Point $h_n^*$.
4. Stick-Free Static Margin $K_n^*$.

**Solution:**

**Step 1: Stick-Fixed Neutral Point $h_n$**
$$h_n = h_{ac,wb} + \frac{\eta_t V_H a_t}{C_{L_\alpha}} \left(1 - \frac{d\epsilon}{d\alpha}\right)$$
$$h_n = 0.25 + \frac{0.90 \times 0.55 \times 3.8}{4.5} (1 - 0.35)$$
$$h_n = 0.25 + \frac{1.881}{4.5} \times 0.65 = 0.25 + 0.418 \times 0.65 = 0.25 + 0.2717 = 0.5217$$
$$\boxed{h_n = 0.5217 \quad (52.17\% \text{ MAC})}$$

**Step 2: Stick-Free Tail Lift Slope $a_t^*$**
$$a_t^* = a_t \left(1 - \frac{C_{h_\alpha}}{C_{h_{\delta_e}}} \tau_e\right) = 3.8 \left(1 - \frac{-0.30}{-0.60} \times 0.50\right)$$
$$a_t^* = 3.8 \left(1 - 0.50 \times 0.50\right) = 3.8 \times (1 - 0.25) = 3.8 \times 0.75 = 2.85\text{ rad}^{-1}$$
$$\boxed{a_t^* = 2.85\text{ rad}^{-1}}$$

**Step 3: Stick-Free Neutral Point $h_n^*$**
$$h_n^* = h_{ac,wb} + \frac{\eta_t V_H a_t^*}{C_{L_\alpha}} \left(1 - \frac{d\epsilon}{d\alpha}\right)$$
$$h_n^* = 0.25 + \frac{0.90 \times 0.55 \times 2.85}{4.5} (1 - 0.35)$$
$$h_n^* = 0.25 + \frac{1.41075}{4.5} \times 0.65 = 0.25 + 0.3135 \times 0.65 = 0.25 + 0.203775 = 0.4538$$
$$\boxed{h_n^* = 0.4538 \quad (45.38\% \text{ MAC})}$$

**Step 4: Stick-Free Static Margin $K_n^*$**
$$K_n^* = h_n^* - h = 0.4538 - 0.30 = 0.1538$$
$$\boxed{K_n^* = +15.38\% \text{ MAC} \quad (\text{Statically Stable ✅})}$$

*(Notice $h_n^* = 0.4538 < h_n = 0.5217$ — a forward shift of $6.79\% \bar{c}$!).*

### Example 2 — Elevator Floating Angle Calculation

**Problem:** An aircraft is flying at a tail angle of attack $\alpha_t = 6^\circ = 0.1047\text{ rad}$.
Given: $C_{h_0} = 0$, $C_{h_\alpha} = -0.25\text{ rad}^{-1}$, $C_{h_{\delta_e}} = -0.50\text{ rad}^{-1}$.
Find the elevator floating angle $\delta_{e,\text{float}}$ in degrees.

**Solution:**
$$\delta_{e,\text{float}} = -\left(\frac{C_{h_\alpha}}{C_{h_{\delta_e}}}\right) \alpha_t$$
$$\delta_{e,\text{float}} = -\left(\frac{-0.25}{-0.50}\right) \times 6^\circ = -(0.50) \times 6^\circ = -3.0^\circ$$

$$\boxed{\delta_{e,\text{float}} = -3.0^\circ \quad (\text{Trailing edge FLOATS UP by } 3^\circ)}$$

### Example 3 — Control Stick Force in Pull-Up Maneuver

**Problem:** A fighter aircraft requires a stick force per $g$ of $\frac{dF_s}{dn} = 50\text{ N/g}$. If the pilot executes a $4g$ maneuver ($n=4.0$), what is the total stick force required from the trim condition?

**Solution:**
$$\Delta n = n - 1 = 4.0 - 1.0 = 3.0g$$
$$F_s = \left(\frac{dF_s}{dn}\right) \cdot \Delta n = 50\text{ N/g} \times 3.0g = 150\text{ N}$$

$$\boxed{F_s = +150\text{ N} \quad (\text{Pilot must PULL back with } 150\text{ N force})}$$

## ⚠️ SECTION 10 — Critical GATE Exam Traps

### Trap 1: Signs of $C_{h_\alpha}$ and $C_{h_{\delta_e}}$
> Both $C_{h_\alpha}$ and $C_{h_{\delta_e}}$ are **NEGATIVE**.
> Ratio $\frac{C_{h_\alpha}}{C_{h_{\delta_e}}} = \frac{-|C_{h_\alpha}|}{-|C_{h_{\delta_e}}|} > 0$ is **POSITIVE**.
> Therefore, $a_t^* = a_t \left(1 - \frac{C_{h_\alpha}}{C_{h_{\delta_e}}} \tau_e\right) < a_t$ ALWAYS.

### Trap 2: Stick-Free vs. Stick-Fixed Neutral Point Order
> Remember: $\mathbf{h_n^* < h_n}$.
> If a GATE problem gives $h_n^* = 0.40$, then $h_n$ CANNOT be $0.35$; it MUST be larger than $0.40$ (e.g. $0.45$).

### Trap 3: Trim Tab Deflection Direction
> Trim tab deflects **OPPOSITE** to primary control surface!
> - To hold UP elevator ($\delta_e < 0$), trim tab deflects DOWN ($\delta_t > 0$).
> - To hold DOWN elevator ($\delta_e > 0$), trim tab deflects UP ($\delta_t < 0$).

### Trap 4: Maneuver Point Position
> Maneuver points ($h_m, h_m^*$) are ALWAYS **AFT** of static neutral points ($h_n, h_n^*$).
> Order: $h_{ac,wb} < h_n^* < h_n < h_m^* < h_m$.
