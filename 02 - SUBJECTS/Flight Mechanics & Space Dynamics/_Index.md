## 🔵 CORE TOPICS — Flight Mechanics (≥90% of questions)

### 1. Standard Atmosphere & Aircraft Basics
- [ ] [[02 - SUBJECTS/Flight Mechanics & Space Dynamics/01 - Standard Atmosphere & Aircraft Basics]] 🔲
  - Properties of standard atmosphere (ISA model)
  - Classification of aircraft
  - Airplane configuration and various parts
  - **Pressure altitude**; equivalent, calibrated, indicated air speeds (IAS/CAS/EAS/TAS)
  - **Primary flight instruments:**
    - Altimeter, Air speed indicator, Vertical speed indicator, Turn-bank indicator

### 2. Aerodynamic Forces & Controls
- [ ] [[02 - SUBJECTS/Flight Mechanics & Space Dynamics/02 - Aero Forces and Controls]] 🔲
  - Aerodynamic forces and moments
  - Angle of attack; sideslip angle
  - High-lift devices (flaps, slats, slots)
  - Roll, pitch & yaw controls (aileron, elevator, rudder)

### 3. Airplane Performance
- [ ] [[02 - SUBJECTS/Flight Mechanics & Space Dynamics/03 - Airplane Performance]] 🔲
  - CL-alpha curve, drag polar
  - Take-off and landing distances/speeds
  - Steady climb and descent; Absolute and service ceiling
  - Range and endurance (Breguet equations)
  - Load factor, turning flight
  - V-n diagram (flight envelope)
  - Winds: head wind, tail wind, cross wind effects

### 4. Static Stability & Control
- [ ] [[02 - SUBJECTS/Flight Mechanics & Space Dynamics/04 - Static Stability]] 🔲
  - Stability and control derivatives
  - **Longitudinal static stability:** stick fixed and free
  - Horizontal tail position and size; neutral point, static margin
  - **Directional stability:** vertical tail position and size
  - **Lateral stability:** wing dihedral, sweep & position
  - Hinge moments; stick forces

### 5. Rigid Body Dynamics
- [ ] [[02 - SUBJECTS/Flight Mechanics & Space Dynamics/05 - Rigid Body Dynamics]] 🔲
  - Linear momentum balance for rigid bodies
  - Angular momentum balance for rigid bodies

## 💡 GATE AE Strategy for FM & Space Dynamics

1. **Static Stability** is the most asked FM topic — master all derivatives (Cm-alpha, Cn-beta, Cl-beta)
2. **Performance** — Breguet range equation, V-n diagram are frequently asked
3. **Flight instruments** — ISA altimeter, airspeed types (IAS/CAS/EAS) are easy marks
4. **Kepler's laws + escape velocity** — 1–2 direct questions; formulaic, easy to master
5. **Dynamic modes (Special)** — Study longitudinal modes only lightly; focus on core first
6. **Hohmann transfers (Special)** — Very formula-driven, can get 1 easy mark
7. **Target: 13+/17 marks** from this combined section
