## 💡 GATE AE Strategy for FM & Space Dynamics

1. **Static Stability** is the most asked FM topic — master all derivatives (Cm-alpha, Cn-beta, Cl-beta)
2. **Control Derivatives** — elevator trim ($C_{m_{\delta_e}}$) and aileron strip theory ($C_{l_{\delta_a}}$) appear in GATE numericals
3. **Longitudinal Static Stability (Stick-Fixed)** — master $h_n$ derivation, static margin $K_n$, elevator trim curve slope $d\delta_e/dC_L$
4. **Stick-Free Stability & Hinge Moments** — master elevator floating angle $\delta_{e,\text{float}}$, stick-free neutral point $h_n^*$, and stick force gradients
5. **Performance** — Breguet range equation, V-n diagram are frequently asked
6. **Flight instruments** — ISA altimeter, airspeed types (IAS/CAS/EAS) are easy marks
7. **Kepler's laws + escape velocity** — 1–2 direct questions; formulaic, easy to master
8. **Target: 13+/17 marks** from this combined section
