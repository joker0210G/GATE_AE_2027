## 📌 Executive Summary & GATE Significance

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Aircraft Endurance ($E$)** is the maximum time an aircraft can remain airborne for a given fuel mass consumption ($W_0 \to W_1$):
> 1. **Breguet Endurance Equation for Jet Aircraft:**
>    $$\bbox[12px,border:3px solid #2196F3,background-color:#0d1117]{ E_{\text{jet}} = \frac{1}{c_t} \left(\frac{C_L}{C_D}\right) \ln\left(\frac{W_0}{W_1}\right) = \frac{1}{c_t} \left(\frac{L}{D}\right) \ln\left(\frac{W_0}{W_1}\right) }$$
> 2. **Breguet Endurance Equation for Propeller Aircraft:**
>    $$\bbox[12px,border:3px solid #E91E63,background-color:#0d1117]{ E_{\text{prop}} = \frac{\eta_p}{c_p} \sqrt{2 \rho_\infty S} \left( \frac{C_L^{3/2}}{C_D} \right) \left[ \frac{1}{\sqrt{W_1}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{W_0}} \right] }$$
> where $c_t$ is TSFC ($1/\text{s}$), $c_p$ is power SFC ($\text{N}/(\text{W}\cdot\text{s})$), $W_0$ is gross initial weight, and $W_1$ is final empty weight.

### Why does GATE AE care?
1. **Master Range vs. Endurance Duality:** GATE AE tests the classic cross-pairing: **Propeller Range uses the EXACT SAME formula as Jet Endurance!** Both require flying at $(L/D)_{\max}$ with logarithmic weight term $\ln(W_0/W_1)$.
2. **Propeller Endurance Optimum:** Deriving propeller endurance maximized at $(C_L^{3/2}/C_D)_{\max}$ (Minimum Power Required $P_{R,\min}$).
3. **Unit Transformations:** Converting TSFC ($c_t$) and time $E$ between seconds, minutes, and hours.

## 🔵 Core Concept 1: Kinematics & Fuel Consumption Rates for Endurance

Endurance is the time integral of fuel weight consumption:

$$dt = -\frac{dW}{\text{Fuel Burn Rate}}$$

$$E = \int_0^E dt = -\int_{W_0}^{W_1} \frac{dW}{\text{Fuel Burn Rate}}$$

---

### 1. Jet Engine Endurance Derivation
For jet engines, fuel weight burn rate is $\frac{dW}{dt} = -c_t T = -c_t D$.
Substituting $D = \frac{W}{L/D}$ in steady level flight ($T=D, L=W$):

$$dt = -\frac{1}{c_t D} dW = -\frac{1}{c_t} \left(\frac{L}{D}\right) \frac{dW}{W}$$

Integrating from $W = W_0$ to $W = W_1$:

$$\bbox[12px,border:3px solid #2196F3,background-color:#0d1117]{ E_{\text{jet}} = \frac{1}{c_t} \left(\frac{C_L}{C_D}\right) \ln\left(\frac{W_0}{W_1}\right) }$$

#### Optimum Condition for Maximum Jet Endurance:
To maximize time in air for a jet aircraft, maximize $\frac{C_L}{C_D}$:

$$\bbox[12px,border:3px solid #E91E63,background-color:#0d1117]{ C_L = C_{L,(L/D)_{\max}} = \sqrt{\frac{C_{D,0}}{K}} \implies \mathbf{Parasite \ Drag = Induced \ Drag} \ (C_{D,0} = K C_L^2) }$$

- **Optimum Speed for Jet Endurance:**
  $$V_{\text{endur, jet}} = V_{(L/D)_{\max}} = \mathbf{1.000 \cdot V_{(L/D)_{\max}}}$$

---

### 2. Propeller Engine Endurance Derivation
For propeller engines, fuel weight burn rate is $\frac{dW}{dt} = -c_p P = -c_p \left(\frac{D V}{\eta_p}\right)$.
Substituting $V = \sqrt{\frac{2 W}{\rho_\infty S C_L}}$ and $D = \frac{W}{C_L/C_D}$:

$$\frac{dW}{dt} = -\frac{c_p}{\eta_p} \sqrt{\frac{2}{\rho_\infty S}} \left(\frac{C_D}{C_L^{3/2}}\right) W^{3/2}$$

Rearranging for $dt$:

$$dt = -\frac{\eta_p}{c_p} \sqrt{\frac{\rho_\infty S}{2}} \left( \frac{C_L^{3/2}}{C_D} \right) W^{-3/2} dW$$

Integrating from $W = W_0$ to $W = W_1$:

$$\bbox[12px,border:3px solid #E91E63,background-color:#0d1117]{ E_{\text{prop}} = \frac{\eta_p}{c_p} \sqrt{2 \rho_\infty S} \left( \frac{C_L^{3/2}}{C_D} \right) \left[ \frac{1}{\sqrt{W_1}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{W_0}} \right] }$$

#### Optimum Condition for Maximum Propeller Endurance:
To maximize time in air for a propeller aircraft, maximize **$\frac{C_L^{3/2}}{C_D}$** (Minimum Power Required $P_{R,\min}$):

$$\bbox[12px,border:3px solid #9C27B0,background-color:#0d1117]{ C_L = C_{L,P_{R,\min}} = \sqrt{\frac{3 C_{D,0}}{K}} = \sqrt{3} \cdot C_{L,(L/D)_{\max}} }$$

$$\mathbf{Induced \ Drag = 3 \times Parasite \ Drag} \ (K C_L^2 = 3 C_{D,0})$$

- **Optimum Speed for Propeller Endurance:**
  $$V_{\text{endur, prop}} = V_{P_{R,\min}} = \mathbf{0.760 \cdot V_{(L/D)_{\max}}}$$

## 🔵 Core Concept 2: The Master Range vs. Endurance Duality Matrix

This is the **single most important reference table** for performance optimization in GATE Aerospace Engineering!

```
                  MASTER RANGE vs ENDURANCE OPTIMIZATION MATRIX
                  
    Performance Parameter       Propeller Aircraft              Jet Aircraft
    -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
    MAXIMUM RANGE (R_max)       (L/D)_max                       (C_L^(1/2) / C_D)_max

    -----------------------------------------------------------------------------------

```

> **The Golden Duality Rule:**
> - **Propeller Range** and **Jet Endurance** are aerodynamic twins! Both achieve their maximum at **$(L/D)_{\max}$** with speed $V = 1.000 V_{(L/D)_{\max}}$ and log weight ratio $\ln(W_0/W_1)$!

## ⚡ AIR-1 Mnemonics & Speed Tricks

### 🧠 Mnemonic 1: "PROP RANGE = JET ENDURANCE = (L/D)max"
- Both require flying at $(L/D)_{\max}$ with speed $V = 1.00 \cdot V_{(L/D)_{\max}}$ and $\ln(W_0/W_1)$!

### 🧠 Mnemonic 2: "PROP ENDURANCE USES 3/2 POWER, JET RANGE USES 1/2 POWER"
- Propeller Endurance $\propto (C_L^{3/2}/C_D)_{\max}$ at speed $0.76 V_m$.
- Jet Range $\propto (C_L^{1/2}/C_D)_{\max}$ at speed $1.32 V_m$.

### ⚡ Speed Trick 1: 5-Second Jet Endurance Formula
$$E_{\text{jet}} = \frac{1}{c_t} \left(\frac{L}{D}\right)_{\max} \ln\left(\frac{W_0}{W_1}\right)$$
- If $TSFC$ $c_t = 0.50 \text{ hr}^{-1} = 1.389 \times 10^{-4} \text{ s}^{-1}$, $(L/D)_{\max} = 16, W_0/W_1 = 1.25$:
  $$E_{\text{jet}} = \frac{16 \times \ln(1.25)}{1.389 \times 10^{-4}} = \frac{16 \times 0.22314}{1.389 \times 10^{-4}} = \frac{3.5703}{1.389 \times 10^{-4}} = \mathbf{25,704 \text{ s}} = \mathbf{7.14 \text{ hours}}.$$

## 📝 Solved GATE & Exam-Standard Examples

### Example 1: Jet Aircraft Maximum Endurance (GATE Classic 2-Marker)
**Problem:** A jet fighter aircraft has a gross weight $W_0 = 80,000 \text{ N}$ and empty weight $W_1 = 60,000 \text{ N}$. The engine thrust specific fuel consumption is $c_t = 0.70 \text{ hr}^{-1} = 1.9444 \times 10^{-4} \text{ s}^{-1}$ and maximum lift-to-drag ratio is $(L/D)_{\max} = 14.0$.
Calculate the maximum endurance $E_{\max}$ in seconds and hours.

**Solution:**
1. **Identify Optimum Condition:**
   For a jet aircraft, maximum endurance occurs at **$(L/D)_{\max}$**:
   $$E_{\text{jet}} = \frac{1}{c_t} \left(\frac{L}{D}\right)_{\max} \ln\left(\frac{W_0}{W_1}\right)$$

2. **Calculate Weight Ratio:**
   $$\frac{W_0}{W_1} = \frac{80,000}{60,000} = 1.33333$$
   $$\ln\left(\frac{W_0}{W_1}\right) = \ln(1.33333) = \mathbf{0.28768}$$

3. **Calculate Endurance ($E$):**
   $$E = \frac{1}{1.9444 \times 10^{-4}} \times 14.0 \times 0.28768 = 5142.9 \times 4.0275 = \mathbf{20,713 \text{ s}}$$

4. **Convert to Hours:**
   $$E = \frac{20,713}{3600} = \mathbf{5.75 \text{ hours}}$$

---

### Example 2: Propeller Aircraft Maximum Endurance
**Problem:** A propeller-driven reconnaissance drone has a weight $W_0 = 10,000 \text{ N}$ and empty weight $W_1 = 6,400 \text{ N}$, wing area $S = 10.0 \text{ m}^2$, drag polar $C_D = 0.020 + 0.040 C_L^2$, propeller efficiency $\eta_p = 0.80$, and power SFC $c_p = 6.0 \times 10^{-7} \text{ N}/(\text{W}\cdot\text{s})$. Flying at sea level density $\rho_0 = 1.225 \text{ kg/m}^3$:
1. Calculate optimum $C_L$ and $(C_L^{3/2}/C_D)_{\max}$ for maximum propeller endurance.
2. Calculate maximum endurance $E$ in hours.

**Solution:**
1. **Optimum $C_L$ for Max Propeller Endurance:**
   $$C_{D,0} = 0.020, \quad K = 0.040$$
   $$C_{L, P_R} = \sqrt{\frac{3 C_{D,0}}{K}} = \sqrt{\frac{3 \times 0.020}{0.040}} = \sqrt{1.50} = \mathbf{1.22475}$$

2. **Optimum $C_D$:**
   $$C_D = 4 C_{D,0} = 4 \times 0.020 = \mathbf{0.080}$$

3. **Maximum $(C_L^{3/2}/C_D)_{\max}$ Factor:**
   $$\left(\frac{C_L^{3/2}}{C_D}\right)_{\max} = \frac{(1.22475)^{1.5}}{0.080} = \frac{1.3554}{0.080} = \mathbf{16.9425}$$

4. **Weight Difference Factor:**
   $$\left[ \frac{1}{\sqrt{W_1}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{W_0}} \right] = \left[ \frac{1}{\sqrt{6400}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{10000}} \right] = \frac{1}{80} - \frac{1}{100} = 0.0125 - 0.0100 = \mathbf{0.0025 \text{ N}^{-1/2}}$$

5. **Calculate Endurance ($E$):**
   $$E_{\text{prop}} = \frac{\eta_p}{c_p} \sqrt{2 \rho_0 S} \left(\frac{C_L^{3/2}}{C_D}\right)_{\max} \left[ \frac{1}{\sqrt{W_1}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{W_0}} \right]$$
   $$\sqrt{2 \rho_0 S} = \sqrt{2 \times 1.225 \times 10.0} = \sqrt{24.5} = 4.94975$$
   $$E_{\text{prop}} = \frac{0.80}{6.0 \times 10^{-7}} \times 4.94975 \times 16.9425 \times 0.0025$$
   $$E_{\text{prop}} = 1,333,333.33 \times 4.94975 \times 16.9425 \times 0.0025 = \mathbf{279,539 \text{ s}}$$
   $$\text{In Hours:} \quad E = \frac{279,539}{3600} = \mathbf{77.65 \text{ hours}}$$

## ⚠️ Common GATE Traps & Exam Pitfalls

> [!CAUTION]
> 1. **Propeller vs Jet Endurance Condition Swap:**
>    - Jet Endurance: Maximized at **$(L/D)_{\max}$** ($C_{D,0} = K C_L^2$).
>    - Propeller Endurance: Maximized at **$(C_L^{3/2}/C_D)_{\max}$** ($K C_L^2 = 3 C_{D,0}$).
> 2. **Golden Twin Trap:** Propeller Range uses $(L/D)_{\max}$, whereas Propeller Endurance uses $(C_L^{3/2}/C_D)_{\max}$! Do NOT confuse them.
> 3. **Propeller Endurance Weight Term:** Propeller Endurance uses $\left[ \frac{1}{\sqrt{W_1}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{W_0}} \right]$, NOT $\ln(W_0/W_1)$! (Notice final weight $W_1$ comes FIRST inside the bracket because $W_1 < W_0 \implies \frac{1}{\sqrt{W_1}} > \frac{1}{\sqrt{W_0}}$!).
