## 🧠 MASTER MENTAL MODEL — Central Force Motion & Newton's Law of Gravitation

> **CORE CONCEPT:** A **Central Force Field** is a force $\vec{F}(\vec{r})$ acting on a body that is always directed along the radius vector $\vec{r}$ pointing toward or away from a fixed center of attraction $O$, with magnitude depending solely on distance $r = |\vec{r}|$:
>
> $$\vec{F}(\vec{r}) = -\frac{G M m}{r^2} \hat{r} = -\frac{\mu m}{r^3} \vec{r}$$
>
> Where $\mu = G(M + m) \approx G M$ is the **Gravitational Parameter** of the central primary body ($\text{m}^3/\text{s}^2$ or $\text{km}^3/\text{s}^2$).

```
                    TWO-BODY CENTRAL FORCE GEOMETRY
                               Position r(t)
                                 Satellite (m)
                                    ● 
                                   ╱│
                                  ╱ │ Velocity V(t)
                                 ╱  ▼
                                ╱   
                               ╱  Flight Path Angle γ
                              ╱ ⦞ ─────> Local Horizon
                             ╱
                            ● Center of Attraction O (Primary Body M >> m)
```

## 📐 SECTION 1 — Reduction of Two-Body Problem to Relative Motion

For two spherical masses $M$ (Earth/Sun) and $m$ (satellite) subject only to mutual gravitational force:

1. Equation of motion for mass $m$: $m \ddot{\vec{r}}_m = -\frac{G M m}{r^3} (\vec{r}_m - \vec{r}_M)$
2. Equation of motion for mass $M$: $M \ddot{\vec{r}}_M = -\frac{G M m}{r^3} (\vec{r}_M - \vec{r}_m)$
3. Subtracting the two equations yields the **Relative Equation of Motion**:

$$\boxed{\ddot{\vec{r}} + \frac{\mu}{r^3} \vec{r} = 0}$$

Where $\vec{r} = \vec{r}_m - \vec{r}_M$ is the relative position vector of the satellite with respect to the primary body center, and $\mu = G(M + m) \approx GM$.

### Primary Body Gravitational Parameters ($\mu$):
- **Earth ($\mu_\oplus$):** $3.986004 \times 10^{14} \text{ m}^3/\text{s}^2 = 398,600\text{ km}^3/\text{s}^2$
- **Sun ($\mu_\odot$):** $1.327 \times 10^{11} \text{ km}^3/\text{s}^2$
- **Moon ($\mu_\text{moon}$):** $4.903 \times 10^3 \text{ km}^3/\text{s}^2$

## 📐 SECTION 2 — Three Universal Conservation Laws of Central Force Motion

Central force motion is governed by three strict conservation principles:

```
  1. CONSERVATION OF ANGULAR MOMENTUM (h = const)  ──> Motion is Strictly Planar (2D)!
  2. CONSERVATION OF SPECIFIC ENERGY (E = const)    ──> Speed is Fixed for Fixed Altitude!
  3. CONSERVATION OF AREAL VELOCITY (dA/dt = h/2)   ──> Kepler's Second Law!
```

---

### 2.1 Conservation of Specific Angular Momentum ($\vec{h}$)

Taking the vector cross product of position $\vec{r}$ with the relative motion equation $\ddot{\vec{r}} + \frac{\mu}{r^3}\vec{r} = 0$:

$$\vec{r} \times \ddot{\vec{r}} + \frac{\mu}{r^3} (\vec{r} \times \vec{r}) = 0 \implies \vec{r} \times \ddot{\vec{r}} = 0$$

Notice that $\frac{d}{dt}(\vec{r} \times \vec{v}) = \dot{\vec{r}} \times \vec{v} + \vec{r} \times \ddot{\vec{r}} = 0 + 0 = 0$.

$$\boxed{\vec{h} = \vec{r} \times \vec{v} = \text{Constant Vector}}$$

#### Key Physical Consequences of $\vec{h} = \text{const}$:
1. **Planar Motion:** Since $\vec{h}$ is a constant vector perpendicular to both $\vec{r}$ and $\vec{v}$, the orbital path lies in a **fixed 2D plane** in space!
2. **Polar Scalar Expression:**
   $$h = |\vec{r} \times \vec{v}| = r \cdot v_\theta = r^2 \dot{\theta} = \text{constant}$$
3. **Flight Path Angle ($\gamma$) Relation:**
   $$\boxed{h = r \cdot v \cdot \cos\gamma = \text{constant}}$$
   Where $\gamma$ is the **Flight Path Angle** (angle between velocity vector $\vec{v}$ and the local horizontal line perpendicular to $\vec{r}$).

---

### 2.2 Conservation of Specific Mechanical Energy ($\mathcal{E}$)

Taking the dot product of velocity $\vec{v} = \dot{\vec{r}}$ with $\ddot{\vec{r}} + \frac{\mu}{r^3}\vec{r} = 0$:

$$\vec{v} \cdot \ddot{\vec{r}} + \frac{\mu}{r^3} (\vec{r} \cdot \dot{\vec{r}}) = 0 \implies \frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{v^2}{2}\right) + \frac{\mu}{r^2} \dot{r} = 0 \implies \frac{d}{dt}\left(\frac{v^2}{2} - \frac{\mu}{r}\right) = 0$$

$$\boxed{\mathcal{E} = \frac{v^2}{2} - \frac{\mu}{r} = \text{Constant Specific Mechanical Energy } (\text{J/kg or m}^2/\text{s}^2)}$$

Where:
- $\frac{v^2}{2}$ = Specific Kinetic Energy ($\text{J/kg}$)
- $-\frac{\mu}{r}$ = Specific Gravitational Potential Energy ($\text{J/kg}$)

---

### 2.3 Constant Areal Velocity (Kepler's Second Law)

The area $dA$ swept out by the radius vector $\vec{r}$ in time $dt$ is $dA = \frac{1}{2} r (r d\theta) = \frac{1}{2} r^2 \dot{\theta} dt$.

$$\boxed{\frac{dA}{dt} = \frac{1}{2} r^2 \dot{\theta} = \frac{h}{2} = \text{Constant}}$$

> 📜 **KEPLER'S SECOND LAW:** A line segment joining a planet and the Sun sweeps out equal areas during equal intervals of time.

---

## 📐 SECTION 3 — Derivation of Orbit Trajectory Equation $r(\theta)$

Substituting polar coordinates into $\ddot{\vec{r}} + \frac{\mu}{r^3}\vec{r} = 0$ using change of variables $u = 1/r$:

$$\frac{d^2 u}{d\theta^2} + u = \frac{\mu}{h^2}$$

This is a second-order linear differential equation for a simple harmonic oscillator with constant driving force $\frac{\mu}{h^2}$. Its general solution is:

$$u(\theta) = \frac{1}{r(\theta)} = \frac{\mu}{h^2} \left[ 1 + e \cos(\theta - \theta_0) \right]$$

Setting perigee at true anomaly $\theta_0 = 0$:

$$\boxed{r(\theta) = \frac{p}{1 + e \cos\theta} = \frac{h^2 / \mu}{1 + e \cos\theta}}$$

Where:
- $r(\theta)$ = Radial distance from primary center at true anomaly $\theta$ ($\text{m}$ or $\text{km}$).
- $p = \frac{h^2}{\mu}$ = **Semi-Latus Rectum** ($\text{m}$ or $\text{km}$).
- $e$ = **Orbital Eccentricity** (dimensionless).
- $\theta$ = **True Anomaly** (polar angle measured from perigee / periapsis).

---

## 📐 SECTION 4 — The Vis-Viva Equation (Master Velocity Formula)

The specific mechanical energy $\mathcal{E}$ is directly related to the semi-major axis $a$ of the conic orbit:

$$\boxed{\mathcal{E} = -\frac{\mu}{2 a}}$$

Substituting $\mathcal{E} = \frac{v^2}{2} - \frac{\mu}{r} = -\frac{\mu}{2 a}$ yields the **Vis-Viva Equation**:

$$\boxed{v^2 = \mu \left(\frac{2}{r} - \frac{1}{a}\right)}$$

$$\boxed{v = \sqrt{\mu \left(\frac{2}{r} - \frac{1}{a}\right)}}$$

> 🎯 **THE MOST IMPORTANT FORMULA IN SPACE DYNAMICS:**
> The Vis-Viva equation allows instant calculation of orbital speed $v$ at any radial distance $r$ for an orbit of semi-major axis $a$!

## 🔑 SECTION 6 — Mnemonics, Shortcuts & Tricks

### 6.1 Vis-Viva 5-Second Shortcut Matrix
- **Circular Orbit Speed ($a = r$):**
  $$\boxed{v_{\text{circ}} = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{r}}}$$
- **Escape Speed / Parabolic Speed ($a \to \infty$):**
  $$\boxed{v_{\text{esc}} = \sqrt{\frac{2\mu}{r}} = \sqrt{2} \cdot v_{\text{circ}} \approx 1.414 \cdot v_{\text{circ}}}$$
- **Hyperbolic Excess Speed ($v_\infty$):**
  $$\boxed{v_\infty^2 = v_{\text{burn}}^2 - v_{\text{esc}}^2 = \frac{\mu}{|a|}}$$

### 6.2 Semi-Latus Rectum Shortcut
$$\boxed{p = \frac{h^2}{\mu} = a(1 - e^2)}$$

### 6.3 Flight Path Angle Shortcut at Periapsis / Apoapsis
> At **Perigee ($\theta = 0$)** and **Apogee ($\theta = 180^\circ$)**, velocity is purely perpendicular to radius vector $\implies \gamma = 0^\circ \implies \cos\gamma = 1 \implies \mathbf{h = r_p v_p = r_a v_a}$!

## 🔢 SECTION 7 — Solved GATE-Style Numerical Examples

### Example 1 — Speed & Energy in Low Earth Orbit (LEO)

**Problem:** A satellite is in a circular Low Earth Orbit at altitude $h_{\text{alt}} = 600\text{ km}$ above Earth ($R_\oplus = 6378\text{ km}, \mu_\oplus = 3.986 \times 10^5 \text{ km}^3/\text{s}^2$).

Calculate:
1. Orbital radius $r$.
2. Circular orbital speed $v_{\text{circ}}$ in $\text{km/s}$.
3. Specific mechanical energy $\mathcal{E}$ in $\text{kJ/kg}$.
4. Escape speed $v_{\text{esc}}$ from this altitude.

**Solution:**

**1. Orbital Radius ($r$):**
$$r = R_\oplus + h_{\text{alt}} = 6378 + 600 = 6978\text{ km}$$

**2. Circular Speed ($v_{\text{circ}}$):**
$$v_{\text{circ}} = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{r}} = \sqrt{\frac{398,600}{6978}} = \sqrt{57.1224} = 7.5579\text{ km/s}$$

$$\boxed{v_{\text{circ}} \approx 7.56\text{ km/s}}$$

**3. Specific Mechanical Energy ($\mathcal{E}$):**
$$\mathcal{E} = -\frac{\mu}{2 a} = -\frac{398,600}{2 \times 6978} = -28.561\text{ km}^2/\text{s}^2 = -28,561\text{ kJ/kg}$$

$$\boxed{\mathcal{E} \approx -28.56\text{ MJ/kg}}$$

**4. Escape Speed ($v_{\text{esc}}$):**
$$v_{\text{esc}} = \sqrt{2} \times v_{\text{circ}} = 1.4142 \times 7.5579 = 10.688\text{ km/s}$$

$$\boxed{v_{\text{esc}} \approx 10.69\text{ km/s}}$$

---

### Example 2 — Vis-Viva Velocity Calculation at Perigee & Apogee

**Problem:** An elliptical Earth orbit has perigee radius $r_p = 7,000\text{ km}$ and apogee radius $r_a = 15,000\text{ km}$. ($\mu_\oplus = 398,600\text{ km}^3/\text{s}^2$).

Calculate:
1. Semi-major axis $a$ and eccentricity $e$.
2. Perigee speed $v_p$ and apogee speed $v_a$ using Vis-Viva equation.
3. Specific angular momentum $h$.

**Solution:**

**1. Semi-major axis ($a$) and Eccentricity ($e$):**
$$a = \frac{r_p + r_a}{2} = \frac{7000 + 15000}{2} = \frac{22000}{2} = 11,000\text{ km}$$

$$e = \frac{r_a - r_p}{r_a + r_p} = \frac{15000 - 7000}{22000} = \frac{8000}{22000} = 0.3636$$

$$\boxed{a = 11,000\text{ km}, \quad e \approx 0.3636}$$

**2. Perigee Speed ($v_p$):**
$$v_p = \sqrt{\mu \left(\frac{2}{r_p} - \frac{1}{a}\right)} = \sqrt{398,600 \times \left(\frac{2}{7000} - \frac{1}{11000}\right)}$$
$$\frac{2}{7000} - \frac{1}{11000} = 0.000285714 - 0.000090909 = 0.000194805$$
$$v_p = \sqrt{398,600 \times 0.000194805} = \sqrt{77.6493} = 8.8119\text{ km/s}$$

$$\boxed{v_p \approx 8.81\text{ km/s}}$$

**3. Apogee Speed ($v_a$):**
$$v_a = \sqrt{398,600 \times \left(\frac{2}{15000} - \frac{1}{11000}\right)}$$
$$\frac{2}{15000} - \frac{1}{11000} = 0.000133333 - 0.000090909 = 0.000042424$$
$$v_a = \sqrt{398,600 \times 0.000042424} = \sqrt{16.9102} = 4.1122\text{ km/s}$$

$$\boxed{v_a \approx 4.11\text{ km/s}}$$

**4. Specific Angular Momentum ($h$):**
$$h = r_p \cdot v_p = 7000 \times 8.8119 = 61,683.3\text{ km}^2/\text{s}$$

$$\boxed{h \approx 61,683\text{ km}^2/\text{s}}$$

## ⚠️ SECTION 8 — Critical GATE Exam Traps

### Trap 1: Altitude vs. Orbital Radius
> Standard GATE problem statement gives **Altitude $h_{\text{alt}}$**.
> You MUST ADD Earth's radius: $r = R_\oplus + h_{\text{alt}}$! Using altitude directly in Vis-Viva formula results in a zero-mark error.

### Trap 2: Flight Path Angle ($\gamma$) in Angular Momentum
> $h = r \cdot v \cdot \cos\gamma$. Do NOT assume $\cos\gamma = 1$ everywhere! $\gamma = 0^\circ$ ONLY at perigee ($\theta=0$) and apogee ($\theta=180^\circ$).

### Trap 3: Units of Gravitational Parameter ($\mu$)
> Pay strict attention to units of $\mu$:
> - $\mu = 3.986 \times 10^{14}\text{ m}^3/\text{s}^2 \implies$ distances in **meters (m)**, speed in **m/s**.
> - $\mu = 398,600\text{ km}^3/\text{s}^2 \implies$ distances in **kilometers (km)**, speed in **km/s**.
