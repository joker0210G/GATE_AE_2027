## 🧠 MASTER MENTAL MODEL — The Physics of Stick Force ($F_s$)

> **CORE CONCEPT:** **Stick Force ($F_s$)** is the tactile physical pull or push force exerted by the pilot on the control stick to trim the aircraft or perform a maneuver ($n > 1$).
>
> Mechanical linkage connects stick displacement $s$ to elevator deflection $\delta_e$ via mechanical gearing ratio $G$:
>
> $$\boxed{F_s = -G \cdot H_e = -G \cdot q_\infty \cdot S_e \cdot \bar{c}_e \cdot C_h}$$

```
                               MECHANICAL LINKAGE & STICK FORCE
                 Pilot Pulls Stick (+F_s PULL) ──> Stick Moves Back (+s)
                                                       │
                                              Gearing Ratio G = dδ_e / ds
                                                       │
                                                       ▼
                                         Elevator Deflects UP (-δ_e)
                                         Hinge Torque Opposes (-H_e)
```

## 📐 SECTION 1 — Stick Force in Level Flight & Speed Stability ($dF_s/dV$)

### 1.1 Analytical Expression for Stick Force

In 1g unaccelerated level flight ($n = 1$), substituting the trimmed elevator deflection $\delta_{e,\text{trim}}$ into the hinge moment equation yields:

$$\boxed{F_s = -G S_e \bar{c}_e \left[ q_\infty C_{h_0} \left(1 - \frac{a_t^*}{a_t}\right) + \frac{W}{S} \frac{C_{h_\delta}}{\bar{V}_H a_t} \left(h - h_n^*\right) \right]}$$

Where:
- $h$ = Aircraft CG position (fraction of MAC $\bar{c}$).
- $h_n^*$ = Stick-Free Neutral Point.
- $\bar{V}_H = \frac{S_t l_t}{S \bar{c}}$ = Horizontal Tail Volume Ratio.
- $W/S$ = Wing Loading ($\text{N/m}^2$).

---

### 1.2 Speed Stability Gradient ($dF_s/dV > 0$)

Differentiating $F_s$ with respect to flight speed $V_\infty$ at constant trim tab setting:

$$\boxed{\frac{dF_s}{dV} = -\frac{2 G S_e \bar{c}_e}{V_\infty} \left(\frac{W}{S}\right) \left(\frac{C_{h_\delta}}{\bar{V}_H a_t}\right) (h - h_n^*)}$$

```
                   STICK FORCE VS AIRSPEED (SPEED STABILITY)
       Stick Force (F_s)
            ▲
      PULL  │                       Stable (dF_s/dV > 0)
     (+F_s) │                       CG ahead of h_n* (h < h_n*)
            │                      ╱
            │                     ╱
     ───────┼────────────────────┼─────────────> Airspeed (V)
            │                   ╱  V_trim
     PUSH   │                  ╱
     (-F_s) │                 ╱
            ▼
```

### The Speed Stability Criterion:
1. For proper pilot feel (**Speed Stability**), pulling back on the stick ($F_s > 0$) must decrease speed ($V < V_{\text{trim}}$), and pushing forward ($F_s < 0$) must increase speed ($V > V_{\text{trim}}$).
2. Mathematically, this requires **$\frac{dF_s}{dV} > 0$ (POSITIVE Gradient)**.
3. Since $C_{h_\delta} < 0$, positive speed stability requires:

$$\boxed{h < h_n^* \quad (\mathbf{CG\; must\; be\; AHEAD\; of\; Stick\text{-}Free\; Neutral\; Point!})}$$

## 📐 SECTION 2 — Maneuver Points & Stick Force per 'g' ($dF_s/dn$)

When an aircraft performs a pull-up maneuver or steady banked turn ($n > 1$), it experiences a pitch rate $q = \frac{g (n - 1)}{V_\infty}$.

This pitch rate creates an additional angle of attack at the horizontal tail ($\Delta \alpha_t = \frac{q l_t}{V_\infty}$), producing aerodynamic damping that shifts the effective neutral point **rearward**!

```
                  CHORDWISE NEUTRAL & MANEUVER POINT HIERARCHY
      Leading Edge                                                Trailing Edge
      ───┼───────────┼───────────┼───────────┼───────────┼───────────────>
        h_ac,wb     h_n*        h_n         h_m*        h_m
       (Wing-Body) (Free NP)   (Fixed NP) (Free MP)   (Fixed MP)
```

---

### 2.1 Stick-Fixed Maneuver Point ($h_m$)

$$\boxed{h_m = h_n + \frac{\rho_\infty g l_t}{2 (W/S)} \cdot \eta_t \bar{V}_H a_t > h_n}$$

Where:
- $h_n$ = Stick-fixed neutral point.
- $h_m - h_n = K_m - K_n > 0$ = Maneuver margin increment due to pitch damping.

---

### 2.2 Stick-Free Maneuver Point ($h_m^*$)

$$\boxed{h_m^* = h_n^* + \frac{\rho_\infty g l_t}{2 (W/S)} \cdot \eta_t \bar{V}_H a_t^* > h_n^*}$$

Where $a_t^* = a_t (1 + f \tau_e) < a_t$ is the stick-free tail lift slope.

---

### 2.3 Stick Force per 'g' Gradient ($dF_s/dn$)

The stick force required to pull an additional $1\text{g}$ load factor ($n = 1 \to n = 2$) is:

$$\boxed{\frac{dF_s}{dn} = -G S_e \bar{c}_e \left(\frac{W}{S}\right) \left(\frac{C_{h_\delta}}{\bar{V}_H a_t^*}\right) \left(h_m^* - h\right)}$$

### Maneuver Stability Criterion:
1. Pilots require a positive stick force gradient per 'g' (**$\frac{dF_s}{dn} > 0$**), typically $30 - 80\text{ N/g}$ for transport aircraft, to prevent accidental structural over-g.
2. Since $C_{h_\delta} < 0$, $\frac{dF_s}{dn} > 0$ requires **$h < h_m^*$ (CG ahead of Stick-Free Maneuver Point)**.

---

## 📐 SECTION 3 — The Master 5-Point Chordwise Location Hierarchy

The relative locations of all critical longitudinal aerodynamic points along the mean aerodynamic chord ($\bar{c}$) follow a strict, unbreakable order:

$$\boxed{\mathbf{h_{ac,wb} < h_n^* < h_n < h_m^* < h_m}}$$

| Point Name | Symbol | Mathematical Expression | Physical Significance |
|------------|--------|-------------------------|-----------------------|
| **Wing-Body AC** | $h_{ac,wb}$ | Aerodynamic center of wing-body | Pitch moment independent of $\alpha$ |
| **Stick-Free Neutral Point** | $h_n^*$ | $h_{ac,wb} + \frac{\eta_t \bar{V}_H a_t^*}{C_{L_\alpha}} (1 - d\epsilon/d\alpha)$ | Boundary for Speed Stability ($dF_s/dV = 0$) |
| **Stick-Fixed Neutral Point** | $h_n$ | $h_{ac,wb} + \frac{\eta_t \bar{V}_H a_t}{C_{L_\alpha}} (1 - d\epsilon/d\alpha)$ | Boundary for Stick-Fixed Stability ($C_{m_\alpha} = 0$) |
| **Stick-Free Maneuver Point** | $h_m^*$ | $h_n^* + \frac{\rho_\infty g l_t \eta_t \bar{V}_H a_t^*}{2 (W/S)}$ | Boundary for Maneuver Stick Force ($dF_s/dn = 0$) |
| **Stick-Fixed Maneuver Point** | $h_m$ | $h_n + \frac{\rho_\infty g l_t \eta_t \bar{V}_H a_t}{2 (W/S)}$ | Boundary for Stick-Fixed Maneuver ($\frac{d\delta_e}{dn} = 0$) |

## 📐 SECTION 4 — Reversible vs. Irreversible Controls & Artificial Feel Systems

```
  1. REVERSIBLE CONTROL SYSTEM           2. IRREVERSIBLE FLY-BY-WIRE SYSTEM
     Pilot ──> Linkage ──> Surface          Pilot ──> Stick Sensor ──> FBW Computer
     (Aerodynamic Hinge Moment              (Hinge moment blocked by hydraulics!
      felt directly by pilot)                Artificial Feel System supplies Q-Feel / Bobweight)
```

### Artificial Feel Components in Hydraulic / FBW Aircraft:
1. **Q-Feel System:** Hydraulic/spring feel unit providing stick force proportional to dynamic pressure ($F_s \propto q_\infty = \frac{1}{2}\rho V^2$).
2. **Bobweight:** A calibrated mass attached to the control stick linkage. During a pull-up ($n > 1$), g-load pulls bobweight down, providing a natural stick force gradient $\Delta F_s \propto (n - 1)$.
3. **Spring Feel:** Provides linear displacement force ($F_s \propto s$).

## 🔢 SECTION 6 — Solved GATE-Style Numerical Examples

### Example 1 — Maneuver Point & Stick Force per 'g' Calculation

**Problem:** A jet trainer flying at sea level ($\rho_\infty = 1.225\text{ kg/m}^3$) has:
- Wing loading $W/S = 2000\text{ N/m}^2$
- Tail arm $l_t = 5.0\text{ m}$
- Horizontal tail volume ratio $\bar{V}_H = 0.60$
- Tail lift curve slope $a_t^* = 3.2\text{ rad}^{-1}$, $\eta_t = 1.0$
- Stick-Free Neutral Point $h_n^* = 0.35$
- Mechanical gearing $G = 1.2\text{ rad/m}$
- Tail hinge moment derivative $C_{h_\delta} = -0.012\text{ deg}^{-1} = -0.6875\text{ rad}^{-1}$
- Elevator hinge area-chord product $S_e \bar{c}_e = 0.15\text{ m}^3$
- Aircraft CG position $h = 0.25$

Calculate:
1. Stick-free maneuver point $h_m^*$.
2. Stick force gradient per 'g' ($dF_s/dn$) in $\text{N/g}$.

**Solution:**

**1. Maneuver Point Increment ($\Delta h_m^*$):**
$$\Delta h_m^* = \frac{\rho_\infty g l_t}{2 (W/S)} \eta_t \bar{V}_H a_t^*$$
$$\Delta h_m^* = \frac{1.225 \times 9.81 \times 5.0}{2 \times 2000} \times 1.0 \times 0.60 \times 3.2$$
$$\Delta h_m^* = \frac{60.086}{4000} \times 1.92 = 0.01502 \times 1.92 = 0.02884$$

$$h_m^* = h_n^* + \Delta h_m^* = 0.35 + 0.02884 = 0.37884$$

$$\boxed{h_m^* \approx 0.3788 \quad (\text{or } 37.88\%\text{ MAC})}$$

**2. Stick Force per 'g' ($dF_s/dn$):**
$$h_m^* - h = 0.37884 - 0.25 = 0.12884$$

$$\frac{dF_s}{dn} = -G S_e \bar{c}_e \left(\frac{W}{S}\right) \left(\frac{C_{h_\delta}}{\bar{V}_H a_t^*}\right) (h_m^* - h)$$
$$\frac{dF_s}{dn} = -1.2 \times 0.15 \times 2000 \times \left(\frac{-0.6875}{0.60 \times 3.2}\right) \times 0.12884$$
$$\frac{dF_s}{dn} = -360 \times \left(\frac{-0.6875}{1.92}\right) \times 0.12884 = -360 \times (-0.35807) \times 0.12884$$
$$\frac{dF_s}{dn} = 128.907 \times 0.12884 = 16.61\text{ N/g}$$

$$\boxed{\frac{dF_s}{dn} \approx 16.61\text{ N/g} \quad (\mathbf{POSITIVE\; Gradient\; \checkmark})}$$

---

### Example 2 — Speed Stability Gradient Calculation ($dF_s/dV$)

**Problem:** For the same aircraft operating at cruise speed $V_\infty = 100\text{ m/s}$ with CG at $h = 0.25$ and $h_n^* = 0.35$:

Calculate the speed stability gradient $dF_s/dV$ in $\text{N}/(\text{m/s})$.

**Solution:**

Formula:
$$\frac{dF_s}{dV} = -\frac{2 G S_e \bar{c}_e}{V_\infty} \left(\frac{W}{S}\right) \left(\frac{C_{h_\delta}}{\bar{V}_H a_t}\right) (h - h_n^*)$$

Assuming $a_t = 4.2\text{ rad}^{-1}$:
$$\frac{dF_s}{dV} = -\frac{2 \times 1.2 \times 0.15}{100} \times 2000 \times \left(\frac{-0.6875}{0.60 \times 4.2}\right) \times (0.25 - 0.35)$$
$$\frac{dF_s}{dV} = -0.0036 \times 2000 \times (-0.2728) \times (-0.10) = -7.2 \times 0.02728 = -0.1964\text{ N}/(\text{m/s})$$

Since $h - h_n^* = -0.10$ is negative, multiplying by negative sign gives:
$$\frac{dF_s}{dV} = +0.1964\text{ N}/(\text{m/s})$$

$$\boxed{\frac{dF_s}{dV} \approx +0.196\text{ N}/(\text{m/s}) \quad (\mathbf{Speed\; Stable\; \checkmark})}$$

## ⚠️ SECTION 7 — Critical GATE Exam Traps

### Trap 1: The Master Hierarchy Order
> Never flip the order of neutral and maneuver points:
> $\mathbf{h_{ac,wb} < h_n^* < h_n < h_m^* < h_m}$
> - Stick-Free points ($h_n^*, h_m^*$) are ALWAYS AHEAD of Stick-Fixed points ($h_n, h_m$).
> - Maneuver points ($h_m, h_m^*$) are ALWAYS AFT of Neutral points ($h_n, h_n^*$).

### Trap 2: Speed Stability Criterion ($dF_s/dV > 0$)
> Speed stability requires $dF_s/dV > 0$, which occurs when $\mathbf{h < h_n^*}$ (CG ahead of Stick-Free Neutral Point).

### Trap 3: Maneuver Margin vs Static Margin
> Maneuver margin $K_m = h_m - h$ is ALWAYS LARGER than static margin $K_n = h_n - h$ due to pitch rate damping $\Delta h_m > 0$.
