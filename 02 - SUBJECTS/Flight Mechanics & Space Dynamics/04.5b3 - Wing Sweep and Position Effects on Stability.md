## 🧠 MASTER MENTAL MODEL — Multi-Axis Impact of Sweep & Placement

> **CORE CONCEPT:** Wing Sweep Angle ($\Lambda$) and Wing Vertical Position (High / Mid / Low) are not just aesthetic choices; they exert a **profound, coupled aerodynamic influence across ALL THREE MOTION AXES**:
> 1. **PITCH (Longitudinal $C_{m_\alpha}$):** Sweep shifts AC aft and causes tip stall / pitch-up. High wing provides low-CG pendulum stability.
> 2. **ROLL (Lateral $C_{l_\beta}$):** Swept wing produces strong $C_L$-dependent dihedral effect ($C_{l_\beta,\Lambda} \propto -C_L$). High wing adds $+2^\circ - +3^\circ$ effective dihedral; low wing subtracts $-2^\circ - -3^\circ$.
> 3. **YAW (Directional $C_{n_\beta}$):** Swept wing provides positive restoring yaw stiffness ($\Delta C_{n_\beta,\Lambda} \propto C_L^2 \tan^2\Lambda > 0$).

```
                      CROSS-AXIS STABILITY MATRIX
      ┌──────────────────┬──────────────────┬──────────────────┐
      │  PITCH (C_m_α)   │   ROLL (C_l_β)   │   YAW (C_n_β)    │
┌─────┼──────────────────┼──────────────────┼──────────────────┤
│SWEEP│ Shifts AC Aft    │ Strong Stabilizing│ Stabilizing      │
│(Λ>0)│ Pitch-Up hazard  │ Proportional C_L │ Proportional C_L^2│
├─────┼──────────────────┼──────────────────┼──────────────────┤
│HIGH │ Pendulum Stable  │ Free Dihedral    │ Slightly         │
│WING │ Low CG advantage │ (+2° to +3°)     │ Stabilizing      │
├─────┼──────────────────┼──────────────────┼──────────────────┤
│LOW  │ Requires Pitch   │ Destabilizing    │ Slightly         │
│WING │ Trim adjustment  │ (-2° to -3°)     │ Destabilizing    │
└─────┴──────────────────┴──────────────────┴──────────────────┘
```

## 📐 SECTION 1 — Wing Sweep Back ($\Lambda$) & Longitudinal Stability

### 1.1 Aerodynamic Center Shift
For a swept-back wing with quarter-chord sweep $\Lambda_{c/4}$:
1. The mean aerodynamic chord (MAC) shifts **rearward** relative to the wing root chord.
2. The wing-body aerodynamic center $h_{ac,wb}$ shifts **aft**, which increases the stick-fixed static margin ($K_n = h_n - h \uparrow$).

---

### 1.2 Tip Stall & Dangerous Pitch-Up Mechanism ($\Delta C_m > 0$)

On a swept-back wing, spanwise boundary layer flow migrates outboard toward the wingtips:
1. Thick boundary layer accumulates at the tips $\implies$ **wingtips stall first** at high angles of attack ($\alpha_{\text{tip stall}} < \alpha_{\text{root stall}}$).
2. Because the wingtips are located **aft of the aircraft CG**, the loss of tip lift causes a sudden, catastrophic loss of rearward lift.
3. This creates an uncommanded **nose-UP pitching moment ($\Delta C_{m,ac} > 0$)**, driving the aircraft further into stall ("pitch-up / Sabre dance hazard").

```
  SWEPT WING TIP STALL PITCH-UP MECHANISM
  Windward flow causes tips to stall first
           ┌───────┐
          ╱Stalled!│ <--- Loss of aft lift creates NOSE-UP pitching moment!
         ╱         └───────┐
        ╱   Active Wing    │
       ├───────────────────┤  <--- CG
```

#### Mitigations against Swept-Wing Pitch-Up:
1. **Wing Washout (Negative Twist):** Twist wingtips downward ($\theta_{\text{tip}} < \theta_{\text{root}}$) so root stalls first.
2. **Wing Fences & Saw-Tooth Leading Edges:** Block outboard spanwise boundary layer flow.
3. **Vortex Generators:** Keep boundary layer attached over outer wing panels.

## 📐 SECTION 2 — Wing Sweep Back ($\Lambda$) & Lateral Stability ($C_{l_\beta}$)

### 2.1 Lift-Dependent Dihedral Derivation

When a swept-back wing ($\Lambda > 0$) flies at sideslip $\beta > 0$ (wind from right):
1. **Windward (Right) Wing:** Effective sweep angle is reduced: $\Lambda_{\text{eff},R} = \Lambda - \beta$.
2. **Leeward (Left) Wing:** Effective sweep angle is increased: $\Lambda_{\text{eff},L} = \Lambda + \beta$.
3. Dynamic pressure normal to quarter-chord line varies as $q_n = q_\infty \cos^2 \Lambda_{\text{eff}}$.
4. Windward wing experiences higher normal dynamic pressure $\implies L_R > L_L \implies$ produces a restoring **negative rolling moment ($C_l < 0$)**:

$$\boxed{C_{l_\beta,\Lambda} = -\frac{C_L}{3} \cdot \sin(2\Lambda) \quad (\text{for } C_l \text{ in rad}^{-1}, \Lambda \text{ in rad})}$$

---

### 2.2 Key Properties of Sweep Dihedral Effect

1. **$C_L$-Dependence:** Dihedral effect from sweep is **proportional to total lift coefficient $C_L$**:
   - High speed / low AoA ($C_L \to 0$) $\implies C_{l_\beta,\Lambda} \approx 0$ (negligible dihedral effect).
   - Low speed / landing / high AoA ($C_L \uparrow\uparrow$) $\implies |C_{l_\beta,\Lambda}| \uparrow\uparrow$ (extremely strong dihedral effect!).
2. **Forward Sweep ($\Lambda < 0$):**
   - Windward wing has higher effective sweep $\implies L_R < L_L \implies C_{l_\beta,\Lambda} > 0$ (**DESTABILIZING**).
   - However, forward sweep causes root stall first (no pitch-up hazard!).

---

## 3. SECTION 3 — Wing Sweep Back ($\Lambda$) & Directional Stability ($C_{n_\beta}$)

A swept-back wing provides a positive restoring contribution to weathercock stability ($C_{n_\beta,w} > 0$):

1. In sideslip $\beta > 0$, windward wing has lower effective sweep $\implies$ higher effective aspect ratio $\implies$ **higher induced drag $C_{D,i}$**.
2. Leeward wing has higher effective sweep $\implies$ lower induced drag.
3. Drag differential creates a **nose-right restoring yawing moment ($C_n > 0$)**:

$$\boxed{\Delta C_{n_\beta,\Lambda} \approx \frac{C_L^2}{4 \pi AR} \tan^2\Lambda > 0 \quad (\mathbf{STABILIZING!})}$$

> 💡 **KEY DIRECTIONAL INSIGHT:**
> Directional stability contribution from sweep is **proportional to $C_L^2$**. It provides significant weathercock assistance at high angles of attack!

## 📐 SECTION 4 — Wing Vertical Position (High vs. Mid vs. Low Wing)

```
    HIGH WING                           MID WING                          LOW WING
    Mounted on top of fuselage          Mounted on fuselage centerline    Mounted on bottom of fuselage
        ┌──────────┐                        ┌──────────┐                      ┌──────────┐
      ──┴──────────┴──                    ──┼──────────┼──                  ──┬──────────┬──
        │ Fuselage │                        │ Fuselage │                      │ Fuselage │
        └──────────┘                        └──────────┘                      └──────────┘
```

### 4.1 Cross-Flow Interference & Dihedral Effect ($\Delta C_{l_\beta,\text{pos}}$)

When an aircraft sideslips ($\beta > 0$), flow is forced around the fuselage cylinder:

#### A. High-Wing Configuration
- Flow bowing UP over top of fuselage increases local AoA on windward wing root ($\Delta \alpha_R > 0$).
- Windward wing generates more lift $\implies$ **STABILIZING ($C_{l_\beta} < 0$)**.
- Effective Dihedral Contribution: **$\Delta \Gamma_{\text{eff}} \approx +2^\circ \text{ to } +3^\circ$**.

#### B. Low-Wing Configuration
- Flow bowing DOWN under bottom of fuselage decreases local AoA on windward wing root ($\Delta \alpha_R < 0$).
- Windward wing generates less lift $\implies$ **DESTABILIZING ($C_{l_\beta} > 0$)**.
- Effective Dihedral Contribution: **$\Delta \Gamma_{\text{eff}} \approx -2^\circ \text{ to } -3^\circ$**.
- Requires designers to add $+5^\circ \text{ to } +7^\circ$ of geometric dihedral ($\Gamma > 0$) to compensate!

#### C. Mid-Wing Configuration
- Cross-flow is symmetric $\implies \Delta \Gamma_{\text{eff}} \approx 0^\circ$. Used in acrobatic aircraft & jet fighters for zero roll-sideslip coupling.

---

### 4.2 Pendulum Stability & Low CG Advantage

In a High-Wing configuration, the heavy aircraft fuselage hangs below the wing aerodynamic center ($z_{cg} < z_{ac}$):
1. When banked ($\phi > 0$), the weight vector $W$ acting at CG creates a restoring pendulum moment about the wing lift center.
2. Enhances lateral self-righting capability ("Pendulum Effect").

## 🔢 SECTION 7 — Solved GATE-Style Numerical Examples

### Example 1 — Dihedral Effect of Wing Sweep at Two Flight Regimes

**Problem:** A jet fighter has quarter-chord sweep $\Lambda = 30^\circ$.
Calculate the wing sweep dihedral effect derivative $C_{l_\beta,\Lambda}$ at:
1. High-speed cruise ($C_L = 0.20$).
2. Low-speed landing approach ($C_L = 1.20$).

**Solution:**

Formula:
$$C_{l_\beta,\Lambda} = -\frac{C_L}{3} \sin(2\Lambda) = -\frac{C_L}{3} \sin(60^\circ) = -\frac{C_L}{3} \times 0.86603 = -0.28868 \cdot C_L$$

**1. At Cruise ($C_L = 0.20$):**
$$C_{l_\beta,\Lambda} = -0.28868 \times 0.20 = -0.0577\text{ rad}^{-1}$$
$$\boxed{C_{l_\beta,\Lambda} \approx -0.0577\text{ rad}^{-1} \quad (\text{Mild dihedral effect})}$$

**2. At Approach ($C_L = 1.20$):**
$$C_{l_\beta,\Lambda} = -0.28868 \times 1.20 = -0.3464\text{ rad}^{-1}$$
$$\boxed{C_{l_\beta,\Lambda} \approx -0.3464\text{ rad}^{-1} \quad (\text{Extremely strong dihedral effect!})}$$

### Example 2 — Sizing Geometric Dihedral for Low-Wing vs High-Wing Aircraft

**Problem:** An aircraft designer wants a total target dihedral effect equivalent to $\Gamma_{\text{target}} = +5^\circ$.
Given: Wing sweep equivalent $\Delta \Gamma_{\text{sweep}} = +2^\circ$.

Find the required geometric dihedral angle $\Gamma_{\text{geom}}$ for:
1. A High-Wing design ($\Delta \Gamma_{\text{pos}} = +2.5^\circ$).
2. A Low-Wing design ($\Delta \Gamma_{\text{pos}} = -2.5^\circ$).

**Solution:**

Formula:
$$\Gamma_{\text{target}} = \Gamma_{\text{geom}} + \Delta \Gamma_{\text{pos}} + \Delta \Gamma_{\text{sweep}}$$

**1. High-Wing Design:**
$$+5.0^\circ = \Gamma_{\text{geom}} + 2.5^\circ + 2.0^\circ \implies +5.0^\circ = \Gamma_{\text{geom}} + 4.5^\circ$$
$$\Gamma_{\text{geom}} = +5.0^\circ - 4.5^\circ = +0.5^\circ$$
$$\boxed{\Gamma_{\text{geom}} \approx 0.5^\circ \quad (\text{Virtually flat wing})}$$

**2. Low-Wing Design:**
$$+5.0^\circ = \Gamma_{\text{geom}} - 2.5^\circ + 2.0^\circ \implies +5.0^\circ = \Gamma_{\text{geom}} - 0.5^\circ$$
$$\Gamma_{\text{geom}} = +5.0^\circ + 0.5^\circ = +5.5^\circ$$
$$\boxed{\Gamma_{\text{geom}} = +5.5^\circ \quad (\text{Significant upward dihedral required})}$$

## ⚠️ SECTION 8 — Critical GATE Exam Traps

### Trap 1: $C_L$ Dependence of Sweep Dihedral Effect
> Sweep back ($\Lambda > 0$) provides dihedral effect **ONLY when producing lift ($C_L > 0$)**.
> At zero lift ($C_L = 0$), sweep contribution $C_{l_\beta,\Lambda} = 0$.

### Trap 2: Swept Wing Pitch-Up Cause
> Swept-back wings stall at the **TIPS FIRST**, not the root. Because tips are aft of CG, loss of tip lift causes a **NOSE-UP pitching moment ($\Delta C_m > 0$)**.

### Trap 3: Low Wing vs High Wing Sign
> High wing position is **STABILIZING** ($C_{l_\beta} < 0$).
> Low wing position is **DESTABILIZING** ($C_{l_\beta} > 0$).
