## 📌 Executive Summary & GATE Significance

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The **Total Take-off Distance ($S_{TO}$)** consists of three distinct phases: **Ground Roll ($S_g$)**, **Rotation ($S_r$)**, and **Air Distance ($S_c$)** to clear an obstacle height $h_{obs}$ ($35\text{ ft}$ / $10.5\text{ m}$ for commercial transport FAR-25):
> 1. **Governing Ground Roll Differential Equation:**
>    $$\bbox[10px,border:2px solid #2196F3,background-color:#0d1117]{ m V \frac{dV}{dx} = F_{\text{net}} = T - D - \mu_r (W - L) }$$
> 2. **Average Force Analytical Formula for Ground Roll ($S_g$):**
>    $$\bbox[12px,border:3px solid #E91E63,background-color:#0d1117]{ S_g = \frac{m V_{LOF}^2}{2 \bar{F}_{\text{net}}} = \frac{W V_{LOF}^2}{2 g \bar{F}_{\text{net}}} }$$
> where $\bar{F}_{\text{net}} = \left[ T - D - \mu_r (W - L) \right]_{0.707 V_{LOF}}$ is evaluated at average velocity $V_{av} = \frac{V_{LOF}}{\sqrt{2}} \approx 0.707 V_{LOF}$.

### Why does GATE AE care?
1. **Ground Roll Distance Calculations:** GATE AE frequently tests calculating $S_g$ given aircraft mass $m$, engine thrust $T(V)$, drag polar coefficients ($C_{D,0}, K$), rolling friction coefficient $\mu_r$, and liftoff speed $V_{LOF}$.
2. **Optimum Take-off Ground Roll Angle of Attack:** Deriving $C_{L,g,\text{opt}} = \frac{\mu_r}{2 K}$ to minimize ground roll drag.
3. **Parametric Scaling & Headwind Effects:** Scaling takeoff distance for changes in aircraft weight ($S_g \propto W^2$), density/altitude ($S_g \propto 1/\rho$), and headwind velocity ($S_g \propto (1 - V_w/V_{LOF})^2$).

## 🔵 Core Concept 1: Phases of Take-off & Speed Hierarchy

```
                          TAKE-OFF PHASES & SPEEDS
                          
    V = 0                  V_1           V_r       V_LOF                   V_2
   +------------------------+-------------+----------+----------------------+------------+
   | <-------------- Ground Roll S_g ----------->| Rot S_r| <-- Air Distance S_c --> |
   |                                                     |                          |
   |                                                     |                          v h_obs = 35 ft (10.5 m)
   +-----------------------------------------------------+---------------------------------------
```

### 1. Take-off Phases
1. **Ground Roll ($S_g$):** Accelerating along the runway from rest ($V=0$) to Liftoff Speed ($V_{LOF}$).
2. **Rotation Phase ($S_r$):** The pilot pulls back on the stick to increase angle of attack to liftoff orientation (typically lasts $1$ to $3$ seconds).
3. **Air / Climb Distance ($S_c$):** Accelerating and climbing from liftoff point to clear standard obstacle height $h_{obs}$ ($35 \text{ ft} / 10.5 \text{ m}$ for FAR-25 commercial or $50 \text{ ft} / 15 \text{ m}$ for FAR-23 military).

---

### 2. Take-off Speed Regulations (FAR Definitions)
- **$V_s$ (Stall Speed):** Stall speed in takeoff flap configuration ($V_s = \sqrt{\frac{2 W}{\rho_\infty S C_{L,\max}}}$).
- **$V_1$ (Decision / Engine Failure Speed):** The speed above which takeoff MUST be continued even if one engine fails.
- **$V_r$ (Rotation Speed):** Speed at which rotation is initiated ($V_r \ge 1.05 V_s$).
- **$V_{LOF}$ (Liftoff Speed):** Speed at which main landing gear leaves the runway ($V_{LOF} \approx 1.10 \text{ to } 1.20 V_s$).
- **$V_2$ (Takeoff Safety Speed):** Target speed achieved at obstacle height $h_{obs}$ ($V_2 \ge 1.20 V_s$ and $V_2 \ge 1.10 V_{mc}$).

## 🔵 Core Concept 2: Ground Roll Equation & Analytical Solutions

### 1. Force Balance Along the Runway
During ground roll, four forces act on the aircraft:
- **Thrust ($T$):** Pushes aircraft forward along runway.
- **Aerodynamic Drag ($D = \frac{1}{2}\rho V^2 S C_D$):** Opposes motion.
- **Aerodynamic Lift ($L = \frac{1}{2}\rho V^2 S C_L$):** Reduces wheel contact load.
- **Wheel Rolling Friction Force ($F_f = \mu_r N = \mu_r (W - L)$):** Opposes motion.

```
                           FORCE BALANCE ON RUNWAY
                           
                                Lift L
                                  ^
                                  |
              Thrust T            |           Drag D
            <====================[ CG ]====================>
                                  |
                                  |           Friction Force F_f = mu_r * (W - L)
                                  v           <------------
                               Weight W
```

Net accelerating force:
$$\bbox[10px,border:2px solid #2196F3,background-color:#0d1117]{ F_{\text{net}} = T - D - \mu_r (W - L) }$$

---

### 2. Exact Integral Expression for Ground Roll ($S_g$)
Using kinematics $m V \frac{dV}{dx} = F_{\text{net}}$:

$$S_g = \int_0^{S_g} dx = m \int_0^{V_{LOF}} \frac{V \, dV}{T - D - \mu_r (W - L)}$$

---

### 3. Average Force Approximation Formula (Standard GATE Method)
Because $F_{\text{net}}$ varies quadratically with speed, evaluating $F_{\text{net}}$ at an **average speed $V_{av} = \frac{V_{LOF}}{\sqrt{2}} \approx 0.707 V_{LOF}$** yields an extremely accurate closed-form solution:

$$\bbox[12px,border:3px solid #E91E63,background-color:#0d1117]{ S_g = \frac{m V_{LOF}^2}{2 \bar{F}_{\text{net}}} = \frac{W V_{LOF}^2}{2 g \bar{F}_{\text{net}}} }$$

where $\bar{F}_{\text{net}}$ is:
$$\bar{F}_{\text{net}} = \bar{T} - \bar{D} - \mu_r (W - \bar{L})$$

with $\bar{T}, \bar{D}, \bar{L}$ evaluated at $V = 0.707 V_{LOF}$.

---

### 4. Fully Expanded Ground Roll Equation
Substituting $V_{LOF}^2 = \frac{1.2^2 W}{\frac{1}{2} \rho_\infty S C_{L,LOF}}$:

$$\bbox[12px,border:3px solid #9C27B0,background-color:#0d1117]{ S_g = \frac{W^2}{g \rho_\infty S C_{L,LOF} \left[ \bar{T} - \mu_r W - \left( C_D - \mu_r C_L \right) \frac{1}{2} \rho_\infty V_{av}^2 S \right]} }$$

---

## 🔵 Core Concept 3: Optimum Ground Roll Lift Coefficient ($C_{L,g,\text{opt}}$)

To minimize ground roll distance $S_g$, we must **maximize net accelerating force $F_{\text{net}}$** during ground roll:
$$F_{\text{net}} = T - \mu_r W - \frac{1}{2}\rho V^2 S (C_D - \mu_r C_L)$$

To maximize $F_{\text{net}}$, we must **minimize $(C_D - \mu_r C_L)$**:
$$\frac{d}{dC_L} \left( C_{D,0} + K C_L^2 - \mu_r C_L \right) = 2 K C_L - \mu_r = 0$$

$$\bbox[12px,border:3px solid #E91E63,background-color:#0d1117]{ C_{L,g,\text{opt}} = \frac{\mu_r}{2 K} = \frac{\mu_r \pi AR e}{2} }$$

> [!TIP]
> **Interpretation:** During initial ground roll, holding a small positive $C_L = \frac{\mu_r}{2 K}$ reduces wheel friction $\mu_r L$ more than it adds induced drag $K C_L^2$. Once $V_{r}$ is reached, the pilot rotates to full $C_{L,\max}$!

## 🔵 Core Concept 4: Parametric Scaling Laws & Wind Effects

```
                  TAKEOFF GROUND ROLL PARAMETRIC DEPENDENCIES
                  
       +------------------------------------------------------------------+
       | Parameter                Dependency     Effect on Ground Roll S_g|
       +------------------------------------------------------------------+
       | Aircraft Weight (W)      S_g ~ W^2      Doubling W quadruples S_g!|
       | Density / Altitude (rho) S_g ~ 1/rho    High & Hot airport increases S_g|
       | Thrust (T)               S_g ~ 1/T      Higher Thrust reduces S_g|
       | Flap Area (S * C_L_max)  S_g ~ 1/C_Lmax Flaps reduce V_LOF and S_g|
       | Headwind (V_w)           Formula below  Headwind dramatically reduces S_g|
       +------------------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 1. Headwind Reduction Formula
If a steady headwind of velocity $V_w$ blows along the runway:

$$\bbox[12px,border:3px solid #2196F3,background-color:#0d1117]{ S_{g, \text{headwind}} = S_{g, 0} \left( 1 - \frac{V_w}{V_{LOF}} \right)^2 }$$

*(Notice that a headwind equal to $10\%$ of liftoff speed reduces ground roll distance by $19\%$!).*

## ⚡ AIR-1 Mnemonics & Speed Tricks

### 🧠 Mnemonic 1: "S_g PROPORTIONAL TO W SQUARED AND 1/RHO"
- $S_g \propto \frac{W^2}{\rho S C_{L,\max} T}$.

### 🧠 Mnemonic 2: "EVALUATE ACCELERATION AT 0.707 V_LOF"
- Dynamic pressure $\bar{q} = \frac{1}{2}\rho (0.707 V_{LOF})^2 = \mathbf{0.50 \cdot q_{LOF}}$!

### ⚡ Speed Trick 1: 5-Second Ground Roll Average Force Formula
$$\mathbf{S_g = \frac{m V_{LOF}^2}{2 \bar{F}_{\text{net}}}}$$
- Example: $m = 10,000 \text{ kg}, V_{LOF} = 60 \text{ m/s}, \bar{F}_{\text{net}} = 30,000 \text{ N} \implies S_g = \frac{10000 \times 3600}{2 \times 30000} = \mathbf{600 \text{ m}}$.

## 📝 Solved GATE & Exam-Standard Examples

### Example 1: Ground Roll Distance Calculation (GATE Classic 2-Marker)
**Problem:** An aircraft of mass $m = 12,000 \text{ kg}$ has a wing area $S = 30.0 \text{ m}^2$ and maximum lift coefficient $C_{L,\max} = 1.80$. The liftoff speed is $V_{LOF} = 1.15 V_s$. Flying at sea level ($\rho_0 = 1.225 \text{ kg/m}^3, g = 9.81 \text{ m/s}^2$):
1. Calculate stall speed $V_s$ and liftoff speed $V_{LOF}$.
2. If the average net accelerating force during ground roll is $\bar{F}_{\text{net}} = 32,000 \text{ N}$, calculate the ground roll distance $S_g$ in meters.

**Solution:**
1. **Calculate Stall Speed ($V_s$):**
   $$W = m \cdot g = 12,000 \times 9.81 = \mathbf{117,720 \text{ N}}$$
   $$V_s = \sqrt{\frac{2 W}{\rho_0 S C_{L,\max}}} = \sqrt{\frac{2 \times 117,720}{1.225 \times 30.0 \times 1.80}} = \sqrt{\frac{235,440}{66.15}} = \sqrt{3559.18} = \mathbf{59.66 \text{ m/s}}$$

2. **Calculate Liftoff Speed ($V_{LOF}$):**
   $$V_{LOF} = 1.15 \times V_s = 1.15 \times 59.659 = \mathbf{68.61 \text{ m/s}}$$

3. **Calculate Ground Roll Distance ($S_g$):**
   $$S_g = \frac{m V_{LOF}^2}{2 \bar{F}_{\text{net}}} = \frac{12,000 \times (68.608)^2}{2 \times 32,000} = \frac{12,000 \times 4707.03}{64,000} = \mathbf{882.57 \text{ m}}$$

---

### Example 2: Optimum Ground Roll Lift Coefficient
**Problem:** An aircraft has an aspect ratio $AR = 6.0$, Oswald efficiency $e = 0.85$, and runway rolling friction coefficient $\mu_r = 0.030$. Calculate the optimum ground roll lift coefficient $C_{L,g,\text{opt}}$ to minimize ground roll acceleration drag.

**Solution:**
1. **Calculate Induced Drag Factor ($K$):**
   $$K = \frac{1}{\pi AR e} = \frac{1}{\pi \times 6.0 \times 0.85} = \frac{1}{16.0221} = \mathbf{0.06241}$$

2. **Calculate Optimum $C_{L,g,\text{opt}}$:**
   $$C_{L,g,\text{opt}} = \frac{\mu_r}{2 K} = \frac{0.030}{2 \times 0.06241} = \frac{0.030}{0.12483} = \mathbf{0.2403}$$

---

### Example 3: Headwind Effect on Takeoff Distance
**Problem:** If the ground roll distance of an aircraft in zero wind is $S_{g,0} = 500 \text{ m}$ for $V_{LOF} = 50 \text{ m/s}$, calculate the new ground roll distance when taking off into a headwind of $V_w = 10 \text{ m/s}$.

**Solution:**
Using the headwind reduction formula:
$$S_{g,\text{headwind}} = S_{g,0} \left( 1 - \frac{V_w}{V_{LOF}} \right)^2$$
$$S_{g,\text{headwind}} = 500 \times \left( 1 - \frac{10}{50} \right)^2 = 500 \times (1 - 0.20)^2 = 500 \times (0.80)^2 = 500 \times 0.64 = \mathbf{320 \text{ m}}$$

**Insight:** A $10 \text{ m/s}$ ($20\%$) headwind reduces ground roll by **$36\%$** (from $500 \text{ m}$ down to $320 \text{ m}$)!

## ⚠️ Common GATE Traps & Exam Pitfalls

> [!CAUTION]
> 1. **Average Force Velocity Evaluation Point Trap:** When evaluating average drag $\bar{D}$ and lift $\bar{L}$ for $\bar{F}_{\text{net}}$, evaluate at $V_{av} = 0.707 V_{LOF}$, NOT at $0.50 V_{LOF}$! Because dynamic pressure scales as $V^2$, $(0.707)^2 = 0.50$, so $\bar{q} = 0.50 q_{LOF}$.
> 2. **Weight Squared Dependency:** Takeoff ground roll $S_g \propto W^2$. If aircraft weight increases by $20\%$, ground roll increases by $(1.2)^2 - 1 = \mathbf{44\%}$!
> 3. **Rolling Friction Direction:** Rolling friction force $F_f = \mu_r (W - L)$ OPPOSES thrust. Lift $L$ reduces wheel friction!
> 4. **Obstacle Height Definition:** FAR-25 commercial obstacle height is **$35 \text{ ft} = 10.5 \text{ m}$**, while FAR-23 general aviation obstacle height is **$50 \text{ ft} = 15 \text{ m}$**. Check which regulation the problem specifies!
