## 🔵 CORE TOPICS (≥90% of questions)

### 1. Basic Fluid Mechanics
- [ ] [[02 - SUBJECTS/Aerodynamics/01 - Fluid Kinematics and Conservation Laws]] 🔲
  - Streamline, streakline, pathline
  - Conservation laws: mass, linear momentum, energy (integral and differential form)
  - Dimensional analysis and dynamic similarity
  - Incompressibility conditions; Newtonian fluids

### 2. Elementary Ideas of Viscous Flows
- [ ] [[02 - SUBJECTS/Aerodynamics/02 - Viscous Flows and Boundary Layer Basics]] 🔲
  - Hagen-Poiseuille flow, Couette flow
  - Basic concepts in boundary layers, boundary layer thickness

### 3. Two-Dimensional Potential Flow Theory
- [ ] [[02 - SUBJECTS/Aerodynamics/03 - Potential Flow Theory]] 🔲
  - Elementary potential flows: sources, sinks, doublets, point vortex and their superposition
  - Bernoulli's equation

### 4. Airfoils and Wings
- [ ] [[02 - SUBJECTS/Aerodynamics/04 - Airfoils and Wings]] 🔲
  - Airfoil nomenclature; aerodynamic coefficients: lift, drag and moment
  - Kutta-Joukowski theorem
  - Thin airfoil theory, Kutta condition, starting vortex
  - Finite wing theory: induced drag, Prandtl lifting line theory
  - Critical and drag divergence Mach numbers

### 5. Compressible Flows
- [ ] [[02 - SUBJECTS/Aerodynamics/05 - Compressible Flows]] 🔲
  - Basic concepts of compressibility, 1D compressible flows, isentropic flows
  - Normal and oblique shocks
  - Prandtl-Meyer flow
  - Flow through nozzles and diffusers
