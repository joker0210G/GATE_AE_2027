## 📌 Executive Summary & GATE AE Context

Compressible flow governs high-speed aerodynamics where density variations can no longer be neglected ($\Delta\rho / \rho > 5\%$, corresponding to Mach number $M > 0.3$). In GATE AE, isentropic flow relations form the bedrock for:
1. **Speed of Sound & Mach Angle:** $a = \sqrt{\gamma R T}$, $\mu = \arcsin(1/M)$.
2. **Stagnation / Total State Ratios:** $T_0/T$, $p_0/p$, $\rho_0/\rho$ as functions of local Mach number.
3. **Characteristic State ($M=1$):** Sonic throat reference quantities $T^*$, $p^*$, $\rho^*$, $a^*$.
4. **Compressibility Rules:** Prandtl-Glauert transformation, Karman-Tsien, Laitone corrections.
5. **Dynamic Pressure Relations:** $q = \frac{1}{2}\gamma p M^2$.

## 🧠 Part I: Fundamental Thermodynamic Relations

### 1. Speed of Sound
Sound waves are infinitesimal pressure disturbances propagating isentropically ($ds = 0$):
$$a^2 = \left(\frac{\partial p}{\partial \rho}\right)_s$$

For a calorically perfect gas ($p = \rho R T$ and $p/\rho^\gamma = \text{const}$):
$$\frac{\partial p}{\partial \rho} = \gamma \frac{p}{\rho} = \gamma R T$$
$$\boxed{a = \sqrt{\gamma R T} = \sqrt{\gamma \frac{p}{\rho}}}$$

For standard air: $\gamma = 1.4$, $R = 287.05\text{ J/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}$:
$$a = \sqrt{1.4 \times 287.05 \times T} \approx 20.047 \sqrt{T}\text{ m/s}$$

---

### 2. The Mach Cone & Mach Angle
In supersonic flow ($M > 1$), disturbance waves from a point source coalesce into a conical envelope: the **Mach Cone**.
The half-angle of the Mach cone is the **Mach Angle ($\mu$)**:
$$\boxed{\sin\mu = \frac{a \Delta t}{V \Delta t} = \frac{1}{M} \implies \mu = \arcsin\left(\frac{1}{M}\right)}$$

---

## 📐 Part II: Isentropic Stagnation Relations

### 1. Stagnation Enthalpy & Temperature
For steady, adiabatic flow with no shaft work, total enthalpy is constant along a streamline:
$$h_0 = h + \frac{1}{2}V^2 = \text{const}$$

For a calorically perfect gas ($h = c_p T$ where $c_p = \frac{\gamma R}{\gamma - 1}$):
$$c_p T_0 = c_p T + \frac{1}{2}V^2 \implies \frac{T_0}{T} = 1 + \frac{V^2}{2 c_p T} = 1 + \frac{V^2}{2 \frac{\gamma R}{\gamma - 1} T}$$
Since $V^2 / (\gamma R T) = M^2$:
$$\boxed{\frac{T_0}{T} = 1 + \frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2}$$

> 💡 **Adiabatic Invariant:** In any adiabatic flow (even through irreversible shock waves!), the stagnation temperature $T_0$ remains strictly CONSTANT ($T_{01} = T_{02}$).

---

### 2. Stagnation Pressure & Density (Reversible + Adiabatic $\implies$ Isentropic)
If the flow is isentropically brought to rest ($p_0/p = (T_0/T)^{\gamma/(\gamma-1)}$ and $\rho_0/\rho = (T_0/T)^{1/(\gamma-1)}$):
$$\boxed{\frac{p_0}{p} = \left( 1 + \frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2 \right)^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}}}$$
$$\boxed{\frac{\rho_0}{\rho} = \left( 1 + \frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2 \right)^{\frac{1}{\gamma-1}}}$$

For standard air ($\gamma = 1.4$):
$$\frac{T_0}{T} = 1 + 0.2 M^2$$
$$\frac{p_0}{p} = (1 + 0.2 M^2)^{3.5}$$
$$\frac{\rho_0}{\rho} = (1 + 0.2 M^2)^{2.5}$$

---

### 3. Critical Sonic Reference Values ($M = 1.0$)
Setting $M = 1.0$ defines the starred (characteristic) state:
$$\frac{T^*}{T_0} = \frac{2}{\gamma+1} \quad (= 0.8333 \text{ for } \gamma=1.4)$$
$$\frac{p^*}{p_0} = \left(\frac{2}{\gamma+1}\right)^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}} \quad (= 0.52828 \text{ for } \gamma=1.4)$$
$$\frac{\rho^*}{\rho_0} = \left(\frac{2}{\gamma+1}\right)^{\frac{1}{\gamma-1}} \quad (= 0.63394 \text{ for } \gamma=1.4)$$

---

### 4. Dynamic Pressure in Compressible Flow
Dynamic pressure is defined as $q = \frac{1}{2}\rho V^2$.
Substituting $\rho = \frac{\gamma p}{a^2}$ and $V = M a$:
$$\boxed{q = \frac{1}{2} \rho V^2 = \frac{1}{2} \gamma p M^2}$$

The ratio of dynamic pressure to static pressure is:
$$\frac{q}{p} = \frac{\gamma}{2} M^2$$

## 🃏 Native Interactive Foldable Flashcards

> [!question]- 🃏 Flashcard: COMP-01 · Compressible Bernoulli Expansion
> **Question:** Expand the stagnation pressure ratio $p_0/p$ in powers of $M^2$ to recover incompressible Bernoulli's equation.
> > [!success]- **Answer & AIR 1 Traps:**
> > Using the binomial theorem on $\frac{p_0}{p} = (1 + \frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2)^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}}$:
> > $$p_0 = p \left[ 1 + \frac{\gamma}{2}M^2 + \frac{\gamma}{8}M^4 + \frac{\gamma(2-\gamma)}{48}M^6 + \dots \right]$$
> > $$p_0 = p + \frac{1}{2}\rho V^2 \left[ 1 + \frac{1}{4}M^2 + \frac{2-\gamma}{24}M^4 + \dots \right]$$
> > At $M \to 0$, $p_0 = p + \frac{1}{2}\rho V^2$ (Incompressible Bernoulli).  
> > At $M = 0.3$, the second term adds $\frac{1}{4}(0.3^2) = 0.0225$ ($2.25\%$ error if compressibility is ignored).

> [!question]- 🃏 Flashcard: COMP-02 · Characteristic Mach Number $M^*$
> **Question:** Define $M^*$ and express it in terms of local Mach number $M$.
> > [!success]- **Answer & AIR 1 Traps:**
> > $M^* = \frac{V}{a^*}$ where $a^* = \sqrt{\gamma R T^*}$ is the speed of sound at the sonic state ($M=1$).
> > $$(M^*)^2 = \frac{(\gamma+1)M^2}{2 + (\gamma-1)M^2}$$
> > - As $M \to 0 \implies M^* \to 0$.
> > - When $M = 1 \implies M^* = 1$.
> > - As $M \to \infty \implies M^* \to \sqrt{\frac{\gamma+1}{\gamma-1}} = \sqrt{6} \approx 2.449$ (for $\gamma=1.4$).
> > ⚠️ **AIR 1 Trap:** Unlike $M$ which can reach infinity, $M^*$ is strictly bounded by $\sqrt{\frac{\gamma+1}{\gamma-1}}$!
