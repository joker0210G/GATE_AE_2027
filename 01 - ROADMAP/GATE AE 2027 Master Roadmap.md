## 🔵 MISSION 1 — Month 1–2: BUILD
> **Goal: Make Your Strong Subjects Unbeatable**

### 🧱 Core Principles
| Principle | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Core Subjects First** | Start with your strongest/highest-weightage subjects |
| **Concept → Same-Day Questions** | Never study a concept without solving questions the same day |
| **Topic-wise PYQs** | After every topic, solve all PYQs for that topic immediately |
| **Micro Notes** | Build short, revision-ready notes as you go — not at the end |
| **Subject Test** | At the end of each subject, take a mini subject test to benchmark |
| **🚫 Lecture Marathon = No** | Do NOT re-watch lectures. Study less sources → Solve more questions |

> **TARGET:** Study Less Sources → Solve More Questions

## 🏆 AIR < 10 Formula

$$\text{AIR} < 10 = C \times P \times R \times T$$

| Letter | Full Form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **C** | Concept Clarity | Deep understanding, not surface coverage |
| **P** | PYQ Mastery | Every previous year question solved & analysed |
| **R** | Revision | Active, spaced, repeated — not passive re-reading |
| **T** | Test Analysis | Post-test diagnosis is more important than the test itself |

### ✅ What Works vs ❌ What Doesn't

| ❌ Don't Do This | ✅ Do This Instead |
|---|---|
| More Books | **More Revision** |
| More Tests | **Better Test Analysis** |
| Random Hard Work | **Repeatable System** |

> **One weak factor kills the whole product** (it's multiplication, not addition). If Revision = 0, your rank crashes even with great Concept and PYQ scores.

## 🌳 Divide & Conquer — How to Plan

> Break the impossible 6-month wall into manageable chunks.

```
6 Months (M1 → M6)
    └── Each Month has 4 Weeks (W1, W2, W3, W4)
            └── Each Week has 7 Days (D1, D2 ... D7)
```

**How to use this:**
- **Monthly goal:** What subject/mission do I complete this month?
- **Weekly goal:** What topics do I cover this week?
- **Daily goal:** What concept → PYQ → revision → test do I do TODAY?

> Never plan beyond the current week in detail. Macro plan = 6 months. Micro execute = 1 day at a time.

## 📌 Core Rules for All 6 Months
1. **🚫 No Lecture Marathon** — Read/watch once, then solve questions immediately.
2. **4 Pillars Every Day** — Hit CONCEPT + PYQ + REVISION + TEST daily without exception.
3. **Track the Right Metrics** — Log Questions ↑, Accuracy ↑, Errors ↓. Not just "hours studied".
4. **Concept → Same-Day Questions** — Every concept must be tested the day it is learned.
5. **Topic-wise PYQs are Sacred** — Solve PYQs for every topic right after studying it.
6. **30% Revision Always** — Never let a completed subject become cold (Months 3–6).
7. **Test Formula** — `TEST → FIND ERROR → FIX → RE-TEST` (not just TEST → move on).
8. **Better Test Analysis > More Tests** — Diagnosing one test deeply beats taking five blindly.
9. **Error Book** — Log every wrong answer from Month 3 onwards. This is your goldmine.
10. **No New Resources in Month 6** — Only the 4 weapons: Short Notes, PYQs, Error Book, Mocks.
11. **Divide & Conquer** — Plan monthly → weekly → daily. Never lose the daily execution.
12. **Update `AI_CONTEXT.md`** — Keep the LLM shared memory updated after every session.
