## 📒 Error Book Format (Log Every Error)

Create entries in `05 - MOCK TESTS/Error Book.md`:

```markdown
## [Date] — [Topic]

| # | Question Summary | My Answer | Correct Answer | Error Type | Root Cause |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ... | ... | ... | Conceptual / Formula / Silly / Time | ... |

**Key Learning:**
**Rule to add to Micro Notes:**
```

**Error Types:**
- 🔴 **Conceptual** — didn't understand the concept
- 🟠 **Formula** — wrong formula recalled
- 🟡 **Silly** — calculation error, sign error
- 🔵 **Time** — ran out of time, guessed wrong
