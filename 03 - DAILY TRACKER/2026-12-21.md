#### 🔑 Key Formulas & Invariants for Week 4:
1. **Strain Energy Formulations ($U$):**
   - Axial loading: $\mathbf{U = \int \frac{P^2}{2 EA} dx = \frac{P^2 L}{2 EA}}$.
   - Bending moment: $\mathbf{U = \int \frac{M^2}{2 EI} dx}$.
   - Torsion: $\mathbf{U = \int \frac{T^2}{2 GJ} dx = \frac{T^2 L}{2 GJ}}$.
   - Shear: $\mathbf{U = \int \frac{f_s V^2}{2 GA} dx}$ ($f_s = 1.2$ for rectangle).
2. **Castigliano's Theorems:**
   - **First Theorem (Force):** $\mathbf{P_i = \frac{\partial U}{\partial \Delta_i}}$.
   - **Second Theorem (Deflection for Linear Elastic Systems):**
     $$\boxed{\Delta_i = \frac{\partial U}{\partial P_i} = \int \frac{M(x)}{EI}\frac{\partial M(x)}{\partial P_i} dx + \sum \frac{P_j L_j}{E_j A_j}\frac{\partial P_j}{\partial P_i}}$$
   - **Rotational Deflection (Slope):** $\mathbf{\theta_i = \frac{\partial U}{\partial M_i} = \int \frac{M(x)}{EI}\frac{\partial M(x)}{\partial M_i} dx}$.
   - **Fictitious Load Method:** If no real force exists at the point where deflection is required, place a dummy load $Q = 0$, evaluate $\frac{\partial M}{\partial Q}$, and set $Q = 0$ after differentiation!
3. **Unit Load Method (Dummy Unit Load):**
   - Trusses: $\mathbf{\Delta = \sum \frac{n N L}{EA}}$ ($N$: real member force, $n$: force due to unit dummy load).
   - Beams: $\mathbf{\Delta = \int \frac{m M}{EI} dx}$ ($M$: real moment, $m$: moment due to unit dummy load).
