> [!faq]- 🔍 View Complete 65Q Answer Key & Worked Solutions
>
> ### SECTION 1: GENERAL APTITUDE
> **Q1: (B)** "The structural engineer, along with his assistants, is inspecting" (Subject is singular engineer).
> **Q2: (B) CURVATURE** (Kinematic response analogy).
> **Q3: (B) 25 / 144** Roots are $3, 4 \implies \frac{1}{9} + \frac{1}{16} = \frac{25}{144}$.
> **Q4: (B) 300 m** Speed $= 72\times (5/18) = 20\text{ m/s}$. Distance $= 20 \times 24 = 480\text{ m} \implies L_{\text{bridge}} = 480 - 180 = 300\text{ m}$.
> **Q5: (C) 158** Pattern: $+5, +10, +20, +40, +80 \implies 78 + 80 = 158$.
> **Q6: (B) 3** $\log_2(x^2 - 1) = 3 \implies x^2 - 1 = 8 \implies x^2 = 9 \implies x = 3$.
> **Q7: (C) 120** Union $= 350 + 250 - 120 = 480 \implies \text{Neither} = 600 - 480 = 120$.
> **Q8: (C) 15 years** $I = P$ in 5 yrs ($R = 20\%$). To become $4P$, $I = 3P \implies T = 3 \times 5 = 15\text{ years}$.
> **Q9: (B) 6** Volume cylinder $= \pi r^2(8r) = 8\pi r^3$. Volume sphere $= \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 \implies N = \frac{8}{4/3} = 6$.
> **Q10: (D) Neither follows** Standard syllogism.

> ### SECTION 2: TECHNICAL 1-MARK (Q11–Q35)
> **Q11: (B) E = 2G(1 + ν)** Standard elastic relation.
> **Q12: (D) 0.50** Incompressible limit $e = \frac{1-2\nu}{E}(\sum\sigma) = 0 \implies \nu = 0.5$.
> **Q13: (B) ν(σ_x + σ_y)** Reaction normal stress in plane strain.
> **Q14: (B) Maximum in-plane shear stress** $R = \tau_{\max,\text{in-plane}}$.
> **Q15: (B) 60.0 MPa** Ordered 3D stresses $120 \ge 40 \ge 0 \implies \tau_{\max,\text{abs}} = \frac{120-0}{2} = 60\text{ MPa}$.
> **Q16: (C) Ellipse** von Mises yield locus shape.
> **Q17: (B) 15.5% higher** $\frac{\sigma_y/\sqrt{3}}{\sigma_y/2} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}} \approx 1.155$.
> **Q18: (B) Remain plane and perpendicular** Euler-Bernoulli fundamental kinematics.
> **Q19: (B) π d³ / 32** Section modulus for solid circular section.
> **Q20: (B) Area of the M/EI diagram between A and B** Mohr's First Theorem.
> **Q21: (B) Strictly zero** Symmetry theorem for product of inertia.
> **Q22: (B) tan α_NA = I_xy / I_y** Neutral axis slope under pure $M_x$.
> **Q23: (C) τ_max = 1.50 τ_avg** Parabolic distribution in rectangular beam.
> **Q24: (B) At the intersection point** Shear flows pass directly through junction.
> **Q25: (B) Is strictly constant everywhere** Bredt-Batho 1st theorem $q = T/(2 A_m)$.
> **Q26: (D) 0.5 L** Effective length for both ends fixed.
> **Q27: (B) P² L³ / (6 EI)** Bending strain energy in cantilever with tip load.
> **Q28: (B) Corresponding generalized force P_i** Castigliano's First Theorem.
> **Q29: (B) √(k/m)** SDOF circular natural frequency.
> **Q30: (B) 2π ζ** Logarithmic decrement approximation for small damping.
