### Step 2 — Elevator Control Power & Trim Deflection (15 min)
Open `04.2 - Control Derivatives`.

1. **Elevator Control Power Derivative ($C_{m\delta_e}$):**
   - Deflection sign convention: Positive downward ($\delta_e > 0$) $\implies$ creates positive camber $\implies$ increases tail lift $\implies$ creates **NOSE-DOWN (Negative)** pitching moment:
     $$\boxed{C_{m\delta_e} \equiv \frac{\partial C_m}{\partial \delta_e} = -V_H \eta_t a_t \tau < 0}$$
     *(where $\tau \equiv \frac{\partial \alpha_t}{\partial \delta_e} \approx 0.3\text{--}0.6$ is the elevator flap effectiveness parameter).*
2. **Longitudinal Pitching Moment with Elevator:**
   $$C_m = C_{m0} + C_{m\alpha}\alpha + C_{m\delta_e}\delta_e$$
3. **Elevator Trim Deflection ($\delta_{e,\text{trim}}$):**
   Setting total pitching moment to zero ($C_m = 0$):
   $$\boxed{\delta_{e,\text{trim}} = -\frac{C_{m0} + C_{m\alpha}\alpha_{\text{trim}}}{C_{m\delta_e}}}$$

---

### Step 3 — Control Surface Hinge Moments & Floating Angle (20 min)
Open `04.4 - Stick-Free Stability and Hinge Moments`.

```
                         ELEVATOR HINGE MOMENT
                                        Trailing Edge Up (δ_e < 0)
                          Hinge Line            ▲
                          ┌─────┐       ╭───────┤ (Restoring H_e)
                  ────────┴─────┴───────┴───────▼
                                        Trailing Edge Down (δ_e > 0)
```

1. **Hinge Moment ($H_e$) & Coefficient ($C_h$):**
   $$H_e = \frac{1}{2}\rho_t V_t^2 S_e c_e C_h$$
   $$\boxed{C_h = C_{h0} + C_{h\alpha}\alpha_t + C_{h\delta_e}\delta_e + C_{h\delta_t}\delta_t}$$
   - $C_{h\alpha} \equiv \frac{\partial C_h}{\partial \alpha_t} < 0$ (Tail AoA pushes trailing edge up).
   - $C_{h\delta_e} \equiv \frac{\partial C_h}{\partial \delta_e} < 0$ (Elevator aerodynamic spring stiffness resisting deflection).
   - $C_{h\delta_t} \equiv \frac{\partial C_h}{\partial \delta_t} < 0$ (Trim tab control effectiveness).

2. **Elevator Floating Angle ($\delta_{e,\text{float}}$):**
   When the pilot lets go of the control stick (stick-free), the elevator floats freely until total hinge moment vanishes ($C_h = 0$):
   $$C_h = C_{h\alpha}\alpha_t + C_{h\delta_e}\delta_{e,\text{float}} = 0 \implies \boxed{\delta_{e,\text{float}} = -\frac{C_{h\alpha}}{C_{h\delta_e}}\alpha_t}$$
   *(Since both $C_{h\alpha} < 0$ and $C_{h\delta_e} < 0$, the ratio $\frac{C_{h\alpha}}{C_{h\delta_e}} > 0 \implies \delta_{e,\text{float}}$ opposes $\alpha_t$).*

---

### Step 4 — Stick-Free Neutral Point & Static Margin (15 min)

```
                    STICK-FIXED vs STICK-FREE NEUTRAL POINTS
                                  x'_np (Stick-Free)      x_np (Stick-Fixed)
                                       │                       │
                                       ▼                       ▼
            ───────────────────────────┼───────────────────────┼───────────► x
                        ◄─── Δx_np ────┘
                         (Forward Shift: x'_np < x_np)
```

1. **Stick-Free Tail Lift Slope ($a'_t$):**
   $$a'_t = a_t \left(1 - \tau \frac{C_{h\alpha}}{C_{h\delta_e}}\right) < a_t$$
   *(The floating elevator reduces the tail's effective lift slope and restoring capacity!).*

2. **Stick-Free Neutral Point ($x'_{np}$ or $h'_n$):**
   $$\boxed{\frac{x'_{np}}{\bar{c}} = \frac{x_{ac,wb}}{\bar{c}} + V_H \eta_t \frac{a_t}{a}\left(1 - \frac{d\epsilon}{d\alpha}\right)\left(1 - \tau \frac{C_{h\alpha}}{C_{h\delta_e}}\right)}$$
   $$\boxed{\mathbf{x'_{np} < x_{np}}}$$
   *(The stick-free neutral point is always **AHEAD** of the stick-fixed neutral point!).*

3. **Stick-Free Static Margin ($SM'$ or $K'_n$):**
   $$\boxed{SM' = \frac{x'_{np} - x_{cg}}{\bar{c}} < SM}$$
   *(An aircraft is always **LESS STABLE** stick-free than stick-fixed!).*

4. **Stick Force Gradient for Speed Stability:**
   $$\boxed{\frac{dF_s}{dV} < 0}$$
   *(Pilot must push forward on stick to fly faster than trim speed, pull back to fly slower).*

---

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Stick-Free vs. Stick-Fixed Neutral Point Shift**
> $\mathbf{x'_{np} < x_{np}}$ always! Letting go of the stick moves the neutral point FORWARD, reducing the permissible aft CG travel limit.
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Elevator Control Power Sign**
> $C_{m\delta_e} < 0$ always (negative). A positive downward elevator deflection causes a negative nose-down pitch.

### Step 3 — D5 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** The elevator control power derivative $C_{m\delta_e} \equiv \partial C_m/\partial\delta_e$ for a conventional aft-tail aircraft is:
> **(A)** Strictly positive **(B)** Strictly negative **(C)** Zero **(D)** Independent of tail volume ratio

> **Q2 (2 marks):** An aircraft has $C_{m0} = 0.05, C_{m\alpha} = -0.50\text{ rad}^{-1}$, and $C_{m\delta_e} = -1.00\text{ rad}^{-1}$. To trim the aircraft at $\alpha = 0.10\text{ rad}$, the required elevator deflection $\delta_e$ (in radians) is:
> **(A)** $0.00\text{ rad}$ **(B)** $-0.05\text{ rad}$ **(C)** $+0.05\text{ rad}$ **(D)** $-0.10\text{ rad}$

> **Q3 (1 mark):** Compared to the stick-fixed neutral point $x_{np}$, the stick-free neutral point $x'_{np}$ of an aircraft with unboosted mechanical controls is located:
> **(A)** Aft of $x_{np}$ ($x'_{np} > x_{np}$)
> **(B)** Forward of $x_{np}$ ($x'_{np} < x_{np}$)
> **(C)** Exactly at $x_{np}$
> **(D)** At the leading edge of the wing

> **Q4 (2 marks):** An elevator has hinge moment derivatives $C_{h\alpha} = -0.005\text{ deg}^{-1}$ and $C_{h\delta_e} = -0.010\text{ deg}^{-1}$. If the tail angle of attack is $\alpha_t = +4.0^\circ$, the elevator floating angle $\delta_{e,\text{float}}$ is:
> **(A)** $+2.0^\circ$ **(B)** $-2.0^\circ$ **(C)** $+4.0^\circ$ **(D)** $-4.0^\circ$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** An aircraft has stick-fixed static margin $SM = 14.0\%$. If releasing the stick shifts the neutral point forward by $\Delta x_{np}/\bar{c} = 5.5\%$, the stick-free static margin $SM'$ (in $\%$) is ___ $\%$.

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D5 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) Strictly negative**
> Downward elevator deflection ($\delta_e > 0$) increases tail lift, producing a nose-down (negative) pitching moment $\implies C_{m\delta_e} < 0$.
>
> **Q2: (A) 0.00 rad**
> $\delta_{e,\text{trim}} = -\frac{C_{m0} + C_{m\alpha}\alpha}{C_{m\delta_e}} = -\frac{0.05 + (-0.50)(0.10)}{-1.00} = -\frac{0.05 - 0.05}{-1.00} = \mathbf{0.00\text{ rad}}$.
>
> **Q3: (B) Forward of x_np (x'_np < x_np)**
> The floating elevator relieves tail aerodynamic restoring forces, moving the neutral point forward $\implies x'_{np} < x_{np}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) -2.0°**
> $\delta_{e,\text{float}} = -\frac{C_{h\alpha}}{C_{h\delta_e}}\alpha_t = -\frac{-0.005}{-0.010}(+4.0^\circ) = -0.5(+4.0^\circ) = \mathbf{-2.0^\circ}$ (Trailing edge floats UP).
>
> **Q5: 8.5**
> $SM' = SM - \Delta x_{np}/\bar{c} = 14.0\% - 5.5\% = \mathbf{8.5\%}$.
