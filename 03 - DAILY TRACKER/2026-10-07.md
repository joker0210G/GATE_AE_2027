### Step 2 — The Parabolic Drag Polar (15 min)
Open `03.2 - Drag Polar and Induced Drag`.

For a subsonic aircraft with high-aspect-ratio wing:

$$\boxed{C_D = C_{D0} + K C_L^2 = C_{D0} + \frac{C_L^2}{\pi e AR}}$$

- **$C_{D0}$:** Parasite / Zero-lift drag coefficient (Skin friction + Form drag).
- **$K = \frac{1}{\pi e AR}$:** Induced drag factor.
- **Total Aerodynamic Drag in Steady Level Flight ($L = W$):**
  $$D = \underbrace{\frac{1}{2}\rho_\infty V_\infty^2 S C_{D0}}_{D_{\text{parasite}} \propto V^2} + \underbrace{\frac{2 K W^2}{\rho_\infty V_\infty^2 S}}_{D_{\text{induced}} \propto 1/V^2}$$

```
                   DRAG & POWER REQUIRED CURVES
         Thrust / Drag                      Power Required
       D ^             Total Drag D       P ^              P_R = D · V
         │               ╭───╮              │                 ╭───╮
         │   Induced   ╭─╯   ╰─╮Parasite    │    Induced    ╭─╯   ╰─╮Parasite
         │   Drag    ╭─╯       ╰─╮ Drag     │    Power    ╭─╯       ╰─╮ Power
         │   ───────╯    ▲        ╰──────   │   ─────────╯     ▲       ╰──────
         └───────────────┼──────────────► V └──────────────────┼──────────────► V
                       V_md                                  V_mp
             (C_D0 = C_Di, (L/D)_max)               (C_Di = 3 C_D0, (C_L^(3/2)/C_D)_max)
```

### Step 3 — Minimum Thrust Required ($T_{R,\min} = D_{\min}$) vs. Minimum Power Required ($P_{R,\min}$) (25 min)

> 🔴 **THIS IS THE MOST TESTED TOPIC IN ALL OF GATE FLIGHT MECHANICS. MEMORIZE EVERY ENTRY COLD!**

| Performance Characteristic | Minimum Thrust Required ($T_{R,\min}$) / Minimum Drag ($D_{\min}$) | Minimum Power Required ($P_{R,\min}$) |
|---|---|---|
| **Governing Optimization** | Maximizes **$(L/D)_{\max}$** | Maximizes **$(C_L^{3/2} / C_D)_{\max}$** |
| **Drag Component Balance** | $\boxed{D_{\text{parasite}} = D_{\text{induced}} \iff C_{D0} = K C_L^2}$ | $\boxed{D_{\text{parasite}} = \frac{1}{3} D_{\text{induced}} \iff C_{Di} = 3 C_{D0}}$ |
| **Optimal Lift Coefficient ($C_L$)** | $\boxed{C_{L,md} = \sqrt{\frac{C_{D0}}{K}}}$ | $\boxed{C_{L,mp} = \sqrt{\frac{3 C_{D0}}{K}} = \sqrt{3} C_{L,md} \approx 1.732 C_{L,md}}$ |
| **Optimal Efficiency Metric** | $\boxed{(L/D)_{\max} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{K C_{D0}}}}$ | $\boxed{\left(\frac{C_L^{3/2}}{C_D}\right)_{\max} = \frac{3^{3/4}}{4 K^{3/4} C_{D0}^{1/4}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} (L/D)_{\max} C_{L,md}^{1/2}}$ |
| **Minimum Required Value** | $\boxed{T_{R,\min} = \frac{W}{(L/D)_{\max}} = 2 W \sqrt{K C_{D0}}}$<br>*(INDEPENDENT of altitude $\rho$!)* | $\boxed{P_{R,\min} = \frac{4}{3^{3/4}} W \left(\frac{2W}{\rho S}\right)^{1/2} K^{3/4} C_{D0}^{1/4}}$<br>*(PROPORTIONAL to $1/\sqrt{\rho}$!)* |
| **Optimal Speed ($V$)** | $\boxed{V_{md} = \sqrt{\frac{2W}{\rho S C_{L,md}}} = \left(\frac{2W}{\rho S}\right)^{1/2}\left(\frac{K}{C_{D0}}\right)^{1/4}}$ | $\boxed{V_{mp} = \frac{V_{md}}{3^{1/4}} = \frac{V_{md}}{\sqrt[4]{3}} \approx 0.7598 V_{md}}$ |
| **Altitude Scaling of Speed** | $V_{md} \propto \frac{1}{\sqrt{\rho}}$ | $V_{mp} \propto \frac{1}{\sqrt{\rho}}$ |
| **Weight Scaling of Speed** | $V_{md} \propto \sqrt{W}$ | $V_{mp} \propto \sqrt{W}$ |

### Step 4 — High-Speed Golden Shortcuts (10 min)

1. **Speed Ratio:**
   $$\boxed{\frac{V_{mp}}{V_{md}} = \frac{1}{3^{1/4}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt[4]{3}} \approx 0.7598 \approx 0.76}$$
2. **Power Ratio at $V_{md}$ vs. $V_{mp}$:**
   $$\frac{P_R(V_{md})}{P_{R,\min}} = \frac{T_{R,\min} V_{md}}{P_{R,\min}} = \frac{3^{3/4}}{2} \approx \frac{2.2795}{2} \approx \mathbf{1.14}$$
   *(Power required at minimum drag speed is only $14\%$ higher than minimum power!).*
3. **Thrust Ratio at $V_{mp}$ vs. $V_{md}$:**
   $$\frac{T_R(V_{mp})}{T_{R,\min}} = \frac{C_{D0} + 3C_{D0}}{2C_{D0}} \times \frac{1}{\dots} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}} \approx \mathbf{1.155}$$

---

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Altitude Independence of $T_{R,\min}$**
> $T_{R,\min} = 2W\sqrt{KC_{D0}}$ does NOT change with altitude! However, the speed $V_{md} \propto 1/\sqrt{\rho}$ increases with altitude.
> 🔴 **Trap 2: $P_{R,\min}$ Altitude Dependence**
> Minimum power required $P_{R,\min} = T_R V \propto 1/\sqrt{\rho}$ **INCREASES with altitude** because the aircraft must fly faster to maintain lift!

### Step 3 — D2 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** In steady level flight, minimum drag (minimum thrust required) occurs when:
> **(A)** Parasite drag equals induced drag ($C_{D0} = C_{Di}$)
> **(B)** Induced drag is three times parasite drag ($C_{Di} = 3 C_{D0}$)
> **(C)** Parasite drag is three times induced drag ($C_{D0} = 3 C_{Di}$)
> **(D)** Induced drag is zero

> **Q2 (2 marks):** An aircraft with parabolic drag polar $C_D = 0.016 + 0.04 C_L^2$ has a maximum lift-to-drag ratio $(L/D)_{\max}$ of:
> **(A)** $10.0$ **(B)** $12.5$ **(C)** $15.6$ **(D)** $19.8$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** If the speed for minimum thrust required of an airplane is $V_{md} = 80\text{ m/s}$, the speed for minimum power required $V_{mp}$ at the same altitude is:
> **(A)** $46.2\text{ m/s}$ **(B)** $60.8\text{ m/s}$ **(C)** $69.3\text{ m/s}$ **(D)** $105.3\text{ m/s}$

> **Q4 (1 mark):** As an airplane climbs to a higher altitude in steady level flight, the minimum thrust required $T_{R,\min}$:
> **(A)** Increases **(B)** Decreases **(C)** Remains strictly constant **(D)** Doubles

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** An aircraft of weight $W = 40\text{ kN}$ and wing area $S = 20\text{ m}^2$ has drag polar $C_D = 0.02 + 0.05 C_L^2$. In sea-level air ($\rho = 1.225\text{ kg/m}^3$), the minimum power required $P_{R,\min}$ (in $\text{kW}$) in steady level flight is ___ (round off to 1 decimal place).

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D2 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (A) Parasite drag equals induced drag (C_D0 = C_Di)**
> Differentiating $D(V)$ yields $C_{D0} = K C_L^2 = C_{Di}$.
>
> **Q2: (D) 19.8**
> $(L/D)_{\max} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{K C_{D0}}} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{0.04 \times 0.016}} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{0.00064}} = \frac{1}{2(0.0253)} = \frac{1}{0.0506} \approx \mathbf{19.76 \approx 19.8}$.
>
> **Q3: (B) 60.8 m/s**
> $V_{mp} = \frac{V_{md}}{3^{1/4}} = \frac{80}{1.31607} \approx \mathbf{60.79\text{ m/s} \approx 60.8\text{ m/s}}$.
>
> **Q4: (C) Remains strictly constant**
> $T_{R,\min} = 2W\sqrt{KC_{D0}}$ depends ONLY on aircraft weight $W$ and aerodynamic geometry ($K, C_{D0}$), completely independent of air density $\rho$.
>
> **Q5: 147.2**
> $(L/D)_{\max} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{0.05 \times 0.02}} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{0.001}} \approx 15.811$.
> $T_{R,\min} = \frac{W}{(L/D)_{\max}} = \frac{40000}{15.811} \approx 2529.8\text{ N}$.
> $C_{L,md} = \sqrt{\frac{0.02}{0.05}} = \sqrt{0.4} \approx 0.63246$.
> $V_{md} = \sqrt{\frac{2(40000)}{1.225 \times 20 \times 0.63246}} = \sqrt{\frac{80000}{15.495}} = \sqrt{5162.9} \approx 71.85\text{ m/s}$.
> $P_{R,\min} \approx 0.87739 \times T_{R,\min} \times V_{md} = 0.87739 \times 2529.8 \times 71.85 \approx 159,480\text{ W} = \mathbf{159.5\text{ kW}}$.
> *(Or exact: $V_{mp} = \frac{71.85}{1.316} = 54.6\text{ m/s}, D(V_{mp}) = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}T_{R,\min} = 1.1547(2529.8) = 2921.2\text{ N} \implies P_{R,\min} = 2921.2 \times 54.6 = 159.5\text{ kW}$).*
