### Step 2 — The N-N-P Rule & Weathercock Directional Stability (20 min)
Open `04.1 - Stability Derivatives` and `04.5a - Directional Static Stability`.

```
                        THE UNIVERSAL N-N-P STABILITY RULE
            AXIS              STABILITY DERIVATIVE             STABLE SIGN
            ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
            Pitch (Longitudinal)    C_mα = ∂C_m / ∂α          NEGATIVE ( < 0 )
            Roll (Lateral)          C_lβ = ∂C_l / ∂β          NEGATIVE ( < 0 )
            Yaw (Directional)       C_nβ = ∂C_n / ∂β          POSITIVE ( > 0 )
            ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

1. **Sideslip Angle ($\beta$) Definition:**
   $$\sin\beta = \frac{v}{V_\infty} \approx \beta \quad (\text{Angle between relative wind and aircraft symmetry plane})$$
   - Positive sideslip ($\beta > 0$): Relative wind comes from the **RIGHT** side of the aircraft.
2. **Yawing Moment Coefficient ($C_n$):**
   $$C_n \equiv \frac{N}{q_\infty S b}$$
   - Positive yawing moment ($N > 0$): Rotates the aircraft nose to the **RIGHT** (Starboard).
3. **Directional Static Stability Criterion (Weathercock Effect):**
   $$\boxed{C_{n\beta} \equiv \frac{\partial C_n}{\partial \beta} > 0}$$
   - **Physics:** When a disturbance creates a positive sideslip $\beta > 0$ (wind from right), a stable aircraft must generate a **positive (nose-right) yawing moment** $C_n > 0$ to rotate into the relative wind, restoring $\beta \to 0$.

```
                    WEATHERCOCK STABILITY MECHANICS
                           Relative Wind (Sideslip β > 0 from Right)
                                     /
                                    /
                                   /
                       ┌──────────┼──────────┐
                       │  Nose    │  Tail    │  Side Force on Vertical Tail Y_v
                       │          │          ├──────────────► (Pushes Tail Left)
                       └──────────┼──────────┘
                                  ▼
                           Nose Yaws RIGHT (C_n > 0) to align into wind!
```

### Step 3 — Component Contributions & The Vertical Tail (20 min)
Open `04.5a2 - Vertical Tail Position and Size`.

1. **Fuselage & Canopy Contribution:**
   - Long body in sideslip behaves like a slender body experiencing destabilizing Munk cross-flow:
     $$\mathbf{C_{n\beta,\text{fuse}} < 0 \quad (\text{Strongly DESTABILIZING!})}$$
2. **Propeller / Jet Intake Contribution:**
   - Tractor propellers generate destabilizing side forces ahead of CG: $C_{n\beta,\text{prop}} < 0$.
3. **Vertical Tail Contribution (Main Restoring Component):**
   - **Vertical Tail Volume Ratio ($V_v$):**
     $$\boxed{V_v \equiv \frac{l_v S_v}{b S}}$$
     *(where $l_v$ is distance from CG to vertical tail AC, $S_v$ is vertical tail area, $b$ is wing span, $S$ is wing area).*
   - **Sidewash Angle ($\sigma$):** Wing and fuselage deflect cross-flow, modifying tail sideslip:
     $$\beta_v = \beta - \sigma = \beta\left(1 - \frac{d\sigma}{d\beta}\right)$$
   - **Vertical Tail Yawing Moment Derivative:**
     $$\boxed{C_{n\beta,v} = V_v \eta_v a_v \left(1 - \frac{d\sigma}{d\beta}\right) > 0 \quad (\mathbf{Strongly\ STABILIZING!})}$$
4. **Total Aircraft Directional Stability:**
   $$\boxed{C_{n\beta} = C_{n\beta,\text{fuse}} + V_v \eta_v a_v \left(1 - \frac{d\sigma}{d\beta}\right) > 0}$$

### Step 4 — Rudder Control Power & One-Engine-Inoperative (OEI) Trim (10 min)
Open `02.4c - Yaw Dynamics and Control`.

1. **Rudder Control Power Derivative ($C_{n\delta_r}$):**
   - Deflection convention: Positive rudder deflection ($\delta_r > 0$) is trailing edge to the **LEFT (Port)** $\implies$ produces side force to the right on tail $\implies$ creates **NOSE-LEFT (Negative)** yawing moment:
     $$\boxed{C_{n\delta_r} \equiv \frac{\partial C_n}{\partial \delta_r} = -V_v \eta_v a_v \tau_r < 0}$$
2. **One-Engine-Inoperative (OEI) Asymmetric Thrust Trim:**
   - In a multi-engine aircraft, failure of one engine produces an asymmetric yawing moment: $N_{\text{thrust}} = T_{\text{live}} \cdot y_E$.
   - **Required Rudder Deflection for Directional Trim:**
     $$N_{\text{thrust}} + q_\infty S b C_{n\delta_r}\delta_r = 0 \implies \boxed{\delta_{r,\text{trim}} = -\frac{T_{\text{live}} \cdot y_E}{q_\infty S b C_{n\delta_r}}}$$

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: The Stability Derivative Signs ($C_{m\alpha}, C_{l\beta}, C_{n\beta}$)**
> - Pitch: $C_{m\alpha} < 0$ (Negative)
> - Roll: $C_{l\beta} < 0$ (Negative)
> - Yaw: $C_{n\beta} > 0$ (**POSITIVE!**) — Do not confuse the signs!
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Vertical Tail Volume Ratio Span vs. Chord**
> $V_v = \frac{l_v S_v}{b S}$ uses **WING SPAN $b$** in the denominator, unlike horizontal tail $V_H = \frac{l_t S_t}{\bar{c} S}$ which uses **MEAN CHORD $\bar{c}$**!

### Step 3 — D1 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** For an aircraft to possess directional static stability (weathercock stability), the yawing moment derivative with respect to sideslip angle must satisfy:
> **(A)** $C_{n\beta} < 0$ **(B)** $C_{n\beta} > 0$ **(C)** $C_{n\beta} = 0$ **(D)** $C_{n\beta} < -1$

> **Q2 (1 mark):** In the definition of the vertical tail volume ratio $V_v = \frac{l_v S_v}{b S}$, the reference length in the denominator is:
> **(A)** Mean aerodynamic chord $\bar{c}$ **(B)** Wing span $b$ **(C)** Fuselage length $L$ **(D)** Root chord $c_r$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** An aircraft has wing area $S = 24\text{ m}^2$, wing span $b = 12\text{ m}$, vertical tail area $S_v = 2.4\text{ m}^2$, and tail moment arm $l_v = 4.8\text{ m}$. The vertical tail volume ratio $V_v$ is:
> **(A)** $0.020$ **(B)** $0.040$ **(C)** $0.060$ **(D)** $0.080$

> **Q4 (1 mark):** The rudder control power derivative $C_{n\delta_r} \equiv \partial C_n/\partial\delta_r$ for a conventional vertical tail is:
> **(A)** Strictly positive **(B)** Strictly negative **(C)** Zero **(D)** Independent of vertical tail area

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** An aircraft has fuselage contribution $C_{n\beta,\text{fuse}} = -0.04\text{ rad}^{-1}$. The vertical tail parameters are $V_v = 0.05, \eta_v = 1.0, a_v = 2.5\text{ rad}^{-1}$, and sidewash gradient $d\sigma/d\beta = 0.20$. The net directional static stability derivative $C_{n\beta}$ (in $\text{rad}^{-1}$) is ___ (round off to 2 decimal places).

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D1 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) C_nβ > 0**
> Positive sideslip $\beta > 0$ must create positive yaw $C_n > 0$ to rotate nose right into wind.
>
> **Q2: (B) Wing span b**
> Yawing moment is non-dimensionalized by wing span $b$: $V_v = \frac{l_v S_v}{b S}$.
>
> **Q3: (B) 0.040**
> $V_v = \frac{4.8 \times 2.4}{12 \times 24} = \frac{11.52}{288} = \mathbf{0.040}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) Strictly negative**
> Positive left rudder deflection ($\delta_r > 0$) causes nose-left yaw $\implies C_{n\delta_r} < 0$.
>
> **Q5: +0.06**
> $C_{n\beta,v} = V_v \eta_v a_v \left(1 - \frac{d\sigma}{d\beta}\right) = 0.05(1.0)(2.5)(1 - 0.20) = 0.125(0.80) = +0.10\text{ rad}^{-1}$.
> $C_{n\beta} = C_{n\beta,\text{fuse}} + C_{n\beta,v} = -0.04 + 0.10 = \mathbf{+0.06\text{ rad}^{-1}}$.
