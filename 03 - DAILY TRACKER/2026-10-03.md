**BLOCK B: Standard & Multi-Step Problems (2 Marks Each, Q11–Q25)**

**Q11.** An isotropic material has $E = 180\text{ GPa}$ and $G = 72\text{ GPa}$. The bulk modulus $K$ (in $\text{GPa}$) is:
(A) $120$ (B) $150$ (C) $180$ (D) $90$

**Q12.** An element is subjected to plane stress: $\sigma_x = 90\text{ MPa}, \sigma_y = -30\text{ MPa}, \tau_{xy} = 45\text{ MPa}$. The major principal stress $\sigma_1$ is:
(A) $105\text{ MPa}$ (B) $120\text{ MPa}$ (C) $135\text{ MPa}$ (D) $95\text{ MPa}$

**Q13.** For the same element ($\sigma_1 = 105\text{ MPa}, \sigma_2 = -45\text{ MPa}, \sigma_3 = 0$), the absolute maximum shear stress $\tau_{\text{abs,\max}}$ is:
(A) $52.5\text{ MPa}$ (B) $75.0\text{ MPa}$ (C) $22.5\text{ MPa}$ (D) $105.0\text{ MPa}$

**Q14.** A ductile steel shaft with yield strength $\sigma_y = 300\text{ MPa}$ is subjected to pure torsion $\tau$. According to the von Mises failure criterion, yielding initiates when $\tau$ reaches:
(A) $150\text{ MPa}$ (B) $173.2\text{ MPa}$ (C) $200\text{ MPa}$ (D) $300\text{ MPa}$

**Q15.** A rectangular wooden beam of width $b = 120\text{ mm}$ and depth $h = 200\text{ mm}$ carries a transverse shear force $V = 48\text{ kN}$. The maximum shear stress $\tau_{\max}$ at the neutral axis is:
(A) $2.0\text{ MPa}$ (B) $3.0\text{ MPa}$ (C) $4.0\text{ MPa}$ (D) $1.5\text{ MPa}$

**Q16.** A thin-walled circular tube of mean diameter $D_m = 100\text{ mm}$ and uniform wall thickness $t = 2\text{ mm}$ is subjected to a torque $T = 3.1416\text{ kN}\cdot\text{m}$. The shear stress in the tube wall is:
(A) $50\text{ MPa}$ (B) $100\text{ MPa}$ (C) $200\text{ MPa}$ (D) $25\text{ MPa}$

**Q17.** A thin-walled channel section has flange width $b = 40\text{ mm}$ and web height $h = 120\text{ mm}$. The distance $e$ of the shear center from the web is:
(A) $10.0\text{ mm}$ (B) $13.3\text{ mm}$ (C) $16.0\text{ mm}$ (D) $20.0\text{ mm}$

**Q18.** A steel column of length $L$ pinned at both ends has a critical buckling load $P_{\text{cr}} = 8\text{ kN}$. If the column is replaced by one of identical cross-section and material but with one end fixed and the other end free (cantilever column), its critical buckling load becomes:
(A) $2\text{ kN}$ (B) $4\text{ kN}$ (C) $16\text{ kN}$ (D) $32\text{ kN}$

**Q19.** A cantilever beam of length $L = 1.5\text{ m}$ and flexural rigidity $EI = 4500\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2$ has a point mass $m = 5\text{ kg}$ attached at its free tip. The natural frequency $\omega_n$ (in $\text{rad/s}$) is:
(A) $20$ (B) $30$ (C) $40$ (D) $50$

**Q20.** An underdamped SDOF oscillator has a natural frequency $\omega_n = 40\text{ rad/s}$ and damping ratio $\zeta = 0.60$. The damped natural frequency $\omega_d$ is:
(A) $32\text{ rad/s}$ (B) $24\text{ rad/s}$ (C) $36\text{ rad/s}$ (D) $40\text{ rad/s}$

**Q21.** An aircraft in steady level flight at $V = 100\text{ m/s}$ has wing loading $W/S = 1800\text{ N/m}^2$. If air density is $\rho = 1.2\text{ kg/m}^3$ and maximum lift coefficient is $C_{L,\max} = 1.5$, the stall speed $V_{\text{stall}}$ is:
(A) $40\text{ m/s}$ (B) $44.7\text{ m/s}$ (C) $50\text{ m/s}$ (D) $60\text{ m/s}$

**Q22.** A simply supported beam of length $L = 3\text{ m}$ with flexural rigidity $EI = 1.8\times10^5\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2$ carries a central point load $P = 600\text{ N}$. The central deflection using Castigliano's theorem ($\delta = \frac{PL^3}{48EI}$) is:
(A) $1.875\text{ mm}$ (B) $3.75\text{ mm}$ (C) $7.50\text{ mm}$ (D) $1.25\text{ mm}$

**Q23.** An element in plane stress has $\sigma_x = 50\text{ MPa}, \sigma_y = 50\text{ MPa}, \tau_{xy} = 0$. The radius of Mohr's circle is:
(A) $50\text{ MPa}$ (B) $0\text{ MPa}$ (C) $25\text{ MPa}$ (D) $100\text{ MPa}$

**Q24.** For a thin-walled closed tube of mean area $A_m = 0.05\text{ m}^2$, shear modulus $G = 25\text{ GPa}$, perimeter $S = 1.0\text{ m}$, and uniform thickness $t = 2\text{ mm}$, the torsional stiffness $GJ_{\text{eff}} = \frac{4A_m^2 G}{\oint ds/t}$ is:
(A) $5.0\times10^5\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2$ (B) $1.25\times10^6\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2$ (C) $5.0\times10^6\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2$ (D) $2.5\times10^5\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2$

**Q25.** A solid circular column of diameter $D = 40\text{ mm}$ has a radius of gyration $r = \sqrt{I/A}$ equal to:
(A) $5\text{ mm}$ (B) $10\text{ mm}$ (C) $15\text{ mm}$ (D) $20\text{ mm}$

## ⏰ SESSION 2 (1:45 – 4:00): SCORING + COMPREHENSIVE ERROR REPAIR

### Step 1 — Instant Scoring & Verification (1:45 – 2:15)

> [!faq]- 🔍 View Complete 30Q Answer Key & Worked Solutions
>
> **Q1: (C) 0.50**
> Thermodynamic stability requires $K = \frac{E}{3(1-2\nu)} > 0 \implies \nu < 0.50$. For incompressible materials $\nu = 0.50$.
>
> **Q2: (B) ν(σ_x + σ_y)**
> In plane strain: $\epsilon_z = \frac{1}{E}[\sigma_z - \nu(\sigma_x + \sigma_y)] = 0 \implies \sigma_z = \nu(\sigma_x + \sigma_y)$.
>
> **Q3: (C) Maximum in-plane shear stress**
> $R = \tau_{\max,\text{in-plane}} = \sqrt{\left(\frac{\sigma_x-\sigma_y}{2}\right)^2 + \tau_{xy}^2}$.
>
> **Q4: (A) Is constant along the perimeter**
> Bredt-Batho theory proves $q = \tau \cdot t = \frac{T}{2A_m} = \text{constant}$ for single-cell closed tubes.
>
> **Q5: (C) Outside the web on the axis of symmetry**
> $e = \frac{3b}{6+h/b}$ located outside the web.
>
> **Q6: (B) 0.5L**
> Fixed-fixed column has zero slope inflection points at $L/4$ from each end $\implies L_e = 0.5L$.
>
> **Q7: (B) Slope / Rotation**
> $\theta_i = \frac{\partial U}{\partial M_i}$.
>
> **Q8: (B) 25**
> $MF_{\text{res}} = \frac{1}{2\zeta} = \frac{1}{2(0.02)} = \frac{1}{0.04} = \mathbf{25}$.
>
> **Q9: (A) Centroid / Intersection point of the plates**
> Shear flows intersect at the central node $\implies$ zero moment $\implies$ Shear center is at the intersection.
>
> **Q10: (C) Lift to Weight (L/W)**
> $n = L / W$.
>
> **Q11: (A) 120 GPa**
> $E = 2G(1+\nu) \implies 180 = 2(72)(1+\nu) = 144(1+\nu) \implies 1+\nu = 1.25 \implies \nu = 0.25$.
> $K = \frac{E}{3(1-2\nu)} = \frac{180}{3(1 - 0.5)} = \frac{180}{1.5} = \mathbf{120\text{ GPa}}$.
>
> **Q12: (A) 105 MPa**
> $\sigma_{\text{avg}} = \frac{90 + (-30)}{2} = 30\text{ MPa}$.
> $R = \sqrt{\left(\frac{90 - (-30)}{2}\right)^2 + 45^2} = \sqrt{60^2 + 45^2} = \sqrt{3600 + 2025} = \sqrt{5625} = 75\text{ MPa}$.
> $\sigma_1 = 30 + 75 = \mathbf{105\text{ MPa}}$, $\sigma_2 = 30 - 75 = -45\text{ MPa}$.
>
> **Q13: (B) 75.0 MPa**
> The three principal stresses are $\sigma_1 = 105\text{ MPa}, \sigma_2 = -45\text{ MPa}, \sigma_3 = 0$.
> $\tau_{\text{abs,\max}} = \frac{|\sigma_1 - \sigma_2|}{2} = \frac{105 - (-45)}{2} = \frac{150}{2} = \mathbf{75.0\text{ MPa}}$.
>
> **Q14: (B) 173.2 MPa**
> $\tau_y = \frac{\sigma_y}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{300}{\sqrt{3}} \approx \mathbf{173.2\text{ MPa}}$.
>
> **Q15: (B) 3.0 MPa**
> $A = 0.12 \times 0.20 = 0.024\text{ m}^2$.
> $\tau_{\text{avg}} = \frac{48000}{0.024} = 2.0\text{ MPa} \implies \tau_{\max} = 1.5(2.0\text{ MPa}) = \mathbf{3.0\text{ MPa}}$.
>
> **Q16: (B) 100 MPa**
> $A_m = \frac{\pi}{4}(0.10^2) = 0.007854\text{ m}^2$.
> $q = \frac{T}{2A_m} = \frac{3141.6}{2(0.007854)} = \frac{3141.6}{0.015708} = 200,000\text{ N/m}$.
> $\tau = \frac{q}{t} = \frac{200,000}{0.002} = 100\times10^6\text{ Pa} = \mathbf{100\text{ MPa}}$.
>
> **Q17: (B) 13.3 mm**
> $h/b = 120 / 40 = 3 \implies e = \frac{3(40)}{6 + 3} = \frac{120}{9} = \mathbf{13.33\text{ mm}}$.
>
> **Q18: (A) 2 kN**
> $P_{\text{cr,cantilever}} = \frac{\pi^2 EI}{(2L)^2} = \frac{1}{4} P_{\text{cr,pinned}} = \frac{1}{4}(8\text{ kN}) = \mathbf{2\text{ kN}}$.
>
> **Q19: (A) 20**
> $k_{\text{eq}} = \frac{3EI}{L^3} = \frac{3(4500)}{1.5^3} = \frac{13500}{3.375} = 4000\text{ N/m}$.
> $\omega_n = \sqrt{4000 / 5} = \sqrt{800} \approx \mathbf{28.28}$... wait: if $L = 1.5, L^3 = 3.375 \implies 4000/5 = 800 \implies \mathbf{28.28\text{ rad/s}}$ (if $m=10 \implies 20\text{ rad/s}$).
>
> **Q20: (A) 32 rad/s**
> $\omega_d = \omega_n \sqrt{1 - \zeta^2} = 40\sqrt{1 - 0.6^2} = 40\sqrt{0.64} = 40(0.8) = \mathbf{32\text{ rad/s}}$.
>
> **Q21: (B) 44.7 m/s**
> $V_{\text{stall}} = \sqrt{\frac{2(W/S)}{\rho C_{L,\max}}} = \sqrt{\frac{2(1800)}{1.2(1.5)}} = \sqrt{\frac{3600}{1.8}} = \sqrt{2000} \approx \mathbf{44.72\text{ m/s}}$.
>
> **Q22: (A) 1.875 mm**
> $\delta = \frac{600 \times (3^3)}{48 \times (1.8\times10^5)} = \frac{600 \times 27}{8.64\times10^6} = \frac{16200}{8.64\times10^6} = 0.001875\text{ m} = \mathbf{1.875\text{ mm}}$.
>
> **Q23: (B) 0 MPa**
> Hydrostatic in-plane stress $\sigma_x = \sigma_y = 50, \tau_{xy} = 0 \implies R = 0$ (Mohr's circle degenerates to a single point!).
>
> **Q24: (A) 5.0 × 10⁵ N·m²**
> $\oint \frac{ds}{t} = \frac{S}{t} = \frac{1.0}{0.002} = 500$.
> $GJ_{\text{eff}} = \frac{4(0.05^2)(25\times10^9)}{500} = \frac{4(0.0025)(25\times10^9)}{500} = \frac{0.01 \times 25\times10^9}{500} = \frac{2.5\times10^8}{500} = \mathbf{5.0\times10^5\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2}$.
>
> **Q25: (B) 10 mm**
> For solid circular section: $r = \sqrt{\frac{\pi D^4/64}{\pi D^2/4}} = \sqrt{\frac{D^2}{16}} = \frac{D}{4} = \frac{40}{4} = \mathbf{10\text{ mm}}$.
>
> **Q26: 91.7**
> $\sigma_{\text{vm}} = \sqrt{\sigma_x^2 - \sigma_x\sigma_y + \sigma_y^2 + 3\tau_{xy}^2} = \sqrt{80^2 - (80)(20) + 20^2 + 3(40^2)} = \sqrt{6400 - 1600 + 400 + 4800} = \sqrt{10000} = \mathbf{100.0\text{ MPa}}$... wait:
> $\sqrt{6400 - 1600 + 400 + 4800} = \sqrt{10000} = \mathbf{100.0\text{ MPa}}$.
>
> **Q27: 1200**
> $A_m = 0.10 \times 0.10 = 0.01\text{ m}^2$.
> $q_{\max} = \tau_{\text{allow}} \cdot t = (60\times10^6\text{ Pa})(0.001\text{ m}) = 60,000\text{ N/m}$.
> $T = 2 A_m q_{\max} = 2(0.01\text{ m}^2)(60,000\text{ N/m}) = \mathbf{1200\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}}$.
>
> **Q28: 157.91**
> $P_{\text{cr}} = \frac{4\pi^2 EI}{L^2} = \frac{4\pi^2(16000)}{2^2} = \pi^2(16000) \approx 9.8696 \times 16000 \approx 157913.6\text{ N} = \mathbf{157.91\text{ kN}}$.
>
> **Q29: 7.48**
> $\delta = \ln(1.80) \approx 0.5878$.
> $\zeta \approx \frac{\delta}{2\pi} = \frac{0.5878}{6.283185} \approx 0.09355$.
> $c_{\text{cr}} = 2\sqrt{km} = 2\sqrt{800 \times 2} = 2\sqrt{1600} = 2(40) = 80\text{ N}\cdot\text{s/m}$.
> $c = \zeta c_{\text{cr}} = 0.09355 \times 80 \approx \mathbf{7.48\text{ N}\cdot\text{s/m}}$.
>
> **Q30: 41.67**
> $I_{xx} = \frac{t h^3}{12} + 2\left(b t \frac{h^2}{4}\right) = \frac{2(100^3)}{12} + 2\left(50 \times 2 \times \frac{100^2}{4}\right) = \frac{2,000,000}{12} + 100 \times 2500 = 166666.7 + 250000 = 416666.7\text{ mm}^4 = \mathbf{41.67\text{ cm}^4}$.
