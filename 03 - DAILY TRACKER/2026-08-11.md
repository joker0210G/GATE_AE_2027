Lock these 4 traps into your head right now:

> 🔴 **Trap 1:** $\dim(P_n) = n+1$, NOT $n$

### 3:45 – 4:00: D1 Mini Quiz (5 Questions, Timed — 10 min)

Set a 10-minute timer. Attempt these **without notes**:

> **Q1 (1 mark MCQ):**
> If $\vec{a} = 2\hat{i} - \hat{j} + 2\hat{k}$ and $\vec{b} = \hat{i} + 2\hat{j} - 2\hat{k}$, find the scalar projection of $\vec{a}$ on $\vec{b}$.
> **(A)** $-\frac{4}{3}$ **(B)** $\frac{4}{3}$ **(C)** $-\frac{2}{3}$ **(D)** $2$

> **Q2 (1 mark MCQ):**
> The vectors $(1, 2, 3)$, $(4, 5, 6)$, $(7, 8, k)$ are coplanar for:
> **(A)** $k = 9$ **(B)** $k = 10$ **(C)** All $k$ **(D)** No $k$

> **Q3 (2 mark MCQ):**
> The dimension of the subspace of $4\times 4$ real skew-symmetric matrices is:
> **(A)** 4 **(B)** 6 **(C)** 8 **(D)** 10

> **Q4 (1 mark MCQ):**
> Which of the following is NOT a subspace of $\mathbb{R}^3$?
> **(A)** $\{(x,y,z) : x + y + z = 0\}$
> **(B)** $\{(x,y,z) : x^2 + y^2 = 0, z = 0\}$
> **(C)** $\{(x,y,z) : x + 2y - z = 1\}$
> **(D)** Solutions of $A\vec{x} = \vec{0}$

> **Q5 (2 mark NAT):**
> The volume of the tetrahedron formed by vectors $\vec{a} = \hat{i}+\hat{j}$, $\vec{b} = \hat{j}+\hat{k}$, $\vec{c} = \hat{k}+\hat{i}$ is ___.

**Answers (check after 10 min):**

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D1 Mini Quiz Answers
>
> **Q1 Answer: (A) $-\frac{4}{3}$**
> - **Dot Product:** $\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b} = (2)(1) + (-1)(2) + (2)(-2) = 2 - 2 - 4 = -4$
> - **Magnitude:** $\|\vec{b}\| = \sqrt{1^2 + 2^2 + (-2)^2} = \sqrt{1 + 4 + 4} = 3$
> - **Scalar Projection:** $\text{Proj}_{\vec{b}}\vec{a} = \frac{\vec{a}\cdot\vec{b}}{\|\vec{b}\|} = -\frac{4}{3}$
> 
> ⚠️ **Exam Trap:** Always recompute dot product carefully — sign errors are the #1 pitfall in projection questions.
>
> **Q2 Answer: (A) $k = 9$**
> $\det\begin{pmatrix}1&2&3\\4&5&6\\7&8&k\end{pmatrix} = 1(5k-48) - 2(4k-42) + 3(32-35)$
> $= 5k-48 - 8k+84 + 3(-3) = -3k+27 = 0 \implies k=9$
>
> **Q3 Answer: (B) 6**
> $\dim(\text{Skew-Sym }4\times4) = \frac{4(4-1)}{2} = \frac{12}{2} = 6$
>
> **Q4 Answer: (C)**
> (C) has $x+2y-z = 1 \neq 0$, so $(0,0,0)$ gives $0 \neq 1$. Zero vector not in set → ❌ NOT a subspace.
> (A) is a homogeneous plane → subspace ✅
> (B) means $x=y=0$ (origin line along z-axis) → subspace ✅
> (D) is always a subspace ✅
>
> **Q5 Answer: $\frac{1}{3}$**
> $[\vec{a}\ \vec{b}\ \vec{c}] = \det\begin{pmatrix}1&1&0\\0&1&1\\1&0&1\end{pmatrix}$
> $= 1(1-0) - 1(0-1) + 0 = 1+1 = 2$
> Volume of tetrahedron $= \frac{1}{6}|2| = \frac{1}{3}$
