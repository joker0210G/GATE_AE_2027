### Step 3 — D1 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** In thin airfoil theory, the sectional lift coefficient slope for any 2D thin airfoil is:
> **(A)** $\pi\text{ rad}^{-1}$ **(B)** $2\pi\text{ rad}^{-1}$ **(C)** $4\pi\text{ rad}^{-1}$ **(D)** $0$

> **Q2 (1 mark):** The effective length $L_e$ of a column of length $L$ with both ends fixed is:
> **(A)** $0.5 L$ **(B)** $0.707 L$ **(C)** $1.0 L$ **(D)** $2.0 L$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A satellite in a circular orbit around Earth ($\mu = 3.986\times 10^5\text{ km}^3/\text{s}^2$) has orbital radius $r = 9000\text{ km}$. The orbital velocity $v$ (in $\text{km/s}$) is:
> **(A)** $6.65\text{ km/s}$ **(B)** $7.06\text{ km/s}$ **(C)** $7.55\text{ km/s}$ **(D)** $8.12\text{ km/s}$

> **Q4 (2 marks):** An aircraft in a coordinated level turn has bank angle $\phi = 60^\circ$ and airspeed $V = 160\text{ m/s}$ ($g = 9.81\text{ m/s}^2$). The radius of turn $R$ (in meters) is:
> **(A)** $754\text{ m}$ **(B)** $1507\text{ m}$ **(C)** $2261\text{ m}$ **(D)** $3014\text{ m}$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A rocket engine has chamber pressure $P_c = 5.0\text{ MPa}$, throat area $A_t = 0.016\text{ m}^2$, and characteristic velocity $c^* = 1600\text{ m/s}$. The propellant mass flow rate $\dot{m}$ (in $\text{kg/s}$) is ___ $\text{kg/s}$.

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D1 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) 2π rad⁻¹**
> Invariant lift slope $dC_l/d\alpha = 2\pi$.
>
> **Q2: (A) 0.5 L**
> Fixed-fixed column effective length $L_e = L/2 = 0.5 L$.
>
> **Q3: (A) 6.65 km/s**
> $v = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{r}} = \sqrt{\frac{3.986\times 10^5}{9000}} = \sqrt{44.2889} \approx \mathbf{6.655\text{ km/s} \approx 6.65\text{ km/s}}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) 1507 m**
> $n = 1/\cos 60^\circ = 2.0 \implies \sqrt{n^2 - 1} = \sqrt{3} \approx 1.732$.
> $R = \frac{160^2}{9.81(1.732)} = \frac{25600}{16.99} \approx \mathbf{1506.7\text{ m} \approx 1507\text{ m}}$.
>
> **Q5: 50.0**
> $\dot{m} = \frac{P_c A_t}{c^*} = \frac{(5.0\times 10^6)(0.016)}{1600} = \frac{80000}{1600} = \mathbf{50.0\text{ kg/s}}$.
