### Step 2 — Solid Rocket Motors & Saint-Robert's Burning Law (20 min)
Open `06 - Rocket Propulsion`.

```
                    SOLID ROCKET MOTOR (SRM) ARCHITECTURE
     Motor Casing & Insulation         Propellant Grain         Choked CD Nozzle
     ┌────────────────────────┐    ┌──────────────────────┐    ╭────────────────╮
     │ Igniter                ├───►│ Burning Surface A_b  ├───►│ Throat A_t     ├──► Supersonic
     │ High-Strength Case     │    │ Linear Burn Rate ṙ   │    │ Expands Gas    │    Exhaust
     └────────────────────────┘    └──────────────────────┘    ╰────────────────╯
```

1. **Saint-Robert's Burning Rate Law (Vieille's Law):**
   $$\boxed{\dot{r} = a \cdot p_c^n}$$
   - $\dot{r}$: Linear burning rate ($\text{mm/s}$ or $\text{m/s}$).
   - $a$: Temperature-dependent burning rate constant (increases with initial grain temperature).
   - $p_c$: Chamber pressure ($\text{MPa}$ or $\text{bar}$).
   - $n$: Pressure exponent (combustion index, dimensionless).

2. **The Fundamental Combustion Stability Invariant ($n < 1.0$):**
   - Mass generation rate: $\dot{m}_{\text{gen}} = \rho_p A_b \dot{r} = \rho_p A_b a p_c^n$.
   - Mass discharge rate through choked nozzle: $\dot{m}_{\text{discharge}} = \frac{p_c A_t}{c^*}$.
   - **Stability Condition:**
     $$\frac{d\dot{m}_{\text{discharge}}}{dp_c} > \frac{d\dot{m}_{\text{gen}}}{dp_c} \implies \frac{A_t}{c^*} > n \rho_p A_b a p_c^{n-1} \implies \boxed{n < 1.0}$$
   - **Rule:** If $n \ge 1.0$, any small pressure rise $\Delta p_c > 0$ generates gas faster than the nozzle can discharge it, causing **catastrophic over-pressurization and motor explosion**! Safe SRMs use $0.2 \le n \le 0.7$.

3. **Steady-State Chamber Pressure ($p_c$):**
   Equating $\dot{m}_{\text{gen}} = \dot{m}_{\text{discharge}}$:
   $$\rho_p A_b a p_c^n = \frac{p_c A_t}{c^*} \implies p_c^{1-n} = \rho_p a c^* \left(\frac{A_b}{A_t}\right)$$
   $$\boxed{p_c = \left[\rho_p a c^* \frac{A_b}{A_t}\right]^{\frac{1}{1-n}} = \left[\rho_p a c^* K\right]^{\frac{1}{1-n}}}$$
   *(where $K \equiv A_b / A_t$ is the burning area to throat area ratio).*

### Step 3 — Grain Geometries & Liquid Engine Systems (25 min)

1. **Solid Propellant Grain Geometries & Thrust Profiles:**
   - **Progressive Burning:** Burning area increases with time ($A_b \uparrow \implies p_c \uparrow \implies F \uparrow$), e.g., cylindrical star grain with widening star points, outer tubular burning.
   - **Regressive Burning:** Burning area decreases with time ($A_b \downarrow \implies p_c \downarrow \implies F \downarrow$), e.g., end-burning solid cylinder, outer round cylinder burning inwards.
   - **Neutral Burning:** Burning area remains constant ($A_b = \text{const} \implies p_c = \text{const} \implies F = \text{const}$), e.g., internal cylindrical conduit with inhibited ends, star grain with balanced web design.

```
                    GRAIN GEOMETRIES & THRUST PROFILES
     Progressive (A_b ↑, F ↑)      Regressive (A_b ↓, F ↓)       Neutral (A_b =, F =)
           Thrust ▲                      Thrust ▲                      Thrust ▲
                  │     /                       │ \                           │ ─────────
                  │    /                        │  \                          │
                  └───┴──────►                  └───┴──────►                  └───┴──────►
                      Time                          Time                          Time
```

2. **Liquid Rocket Engine (LRE) Propellants & Feed Cycles:**
   - **Cryogenic:** $\text{LOX} / \text{LH}_2$ ($I_{\text{sp}} \approx 450\text{ s}$, ultra-high efficiency, complex cryo storage).
   - **Semi-Cryogenic:** $\text{LOX} / \text{RP-1}$ ($I_{\text{sp}} \approx 310\text{ s}$, dense, booster stages).
   - **Hypergolic:** $\text{N}_2\text{O}_4 / \text{UDMH}$ ($I_{\text{sp}} \approx 300\text{ s}$, ignites spontaneously on contact, highly storable, used in spacecraft maneuvering).
   - **Feed Cycles:**
     - Pressure-Fed: High-pressure gas pushes propellants (simple, heavy tanks).
     - Turbopump-Fed: Gas generator, Staged combustion (closed cycle, highest efficiency), Expander cycle.

3. **Combustion Instabilities:**
   - **Chugging:** Low frequency (< 400 Hz), fluid-feed system resonance.
   - **Buzzing:** Intermediate frequency (400–1000 Hz), flameholder / shock oscillation.
   - **Screeching / Whistling:** High frequency (> 1000 Hz), acoustic cavity modes inside chamber (**extremely destructive!**).

### Step 4 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Pressure Exponent $n$ Exponent in $p_c$**
> $p_c = [\rho_p a c^* K]^{\frac{1}{1-n}}$. If $n = 0.5 \implies \frac{1}{1-n} = \frac{1}{0.5} = 2$. Do NOT forget the exponent $\frac{1}{1-n}$!
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Hypergolic Igniter Requirement**
> Hypergolic propellants ignite **SPONTANEOUSLY ON CONTACT** without requiring any spark plug or pyrotechnic igniter.

### Step 3 — D3 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** In a solid rocket motor, the burning rate exponent $n$ in Saint-Robert's law ($\dot{r} = a p_c^n$) must be less than $1.0$ ($n < 1.0$) in order to:
> **(A)** Ensure subsonic nozzle flow
> **(B)** Prevent catastrophic combustion over-pressurization runaway
> **(C)** Increase specific impulse
> **(D)** Reduce grain curing time

> **Q2 (1 mark):** A hypergolic liquid propellant combination is one which:
> **(A)** Must be stored at temperatures below $20\text{ K}$
> **(B)** Requires an electric spark plug for ignition
> **(C)** Ignites spontaneously upon contact of fuel and oxidizer
> **(D)** Uses solid propellant particles suspended in a liquid

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A solid propellant has burning rate law $\dot{r} = 5.0 p_c^{0.5}\text{ mm/s}$ (with $p_c$ in $\text{MPa}$). If the burning area to throat area ratio $K = A_b / A_t$ is increased by $50\%$ (i.e. $K_2 = 1.5 K_1$), the equilibrium chamber pressure $p_c$ will increase by:
> **(A)** $22.5\%$ **(B)** $50.0\%$ **(C)** $125.0\%$ **(D)** $225.0\%$

> **Q4 (2 marks):** A solid propellant motor burns at chamber pressure $p_c = 4.0\text{ MPa}$ with $\dot{r} = 8.0\text{ mm/s}$. The propellant density is $\rho_p = 1750\text{ kg/m}^3$ and the burning area is $A_b = 0.60\text{ m}^2$. The propellant mass generation rate $\dot{m}_{\text{gen}}$ (in $\text{kg/s}$) is:
> **(A)** $4.2\text{ kg/s}$ **(B)** $8.4\text{ kg/s}$ **(C)** $12.6\text{ kg/s}$ **(D)** $16.8\text{ kg/s}$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A solid rocket propellant has burning rate constant $a = 3.0\text{ mm/(s}\cdot\text{MPa}^{0.4})$ and pressure exponent $n = 0.40$. At a chamber pressure $p_c = 6.0\text{ MPa}$, the linear burning rate $\dot{r}$ (in $\text{mm/s}$) is ___ (round off to 2 decimal places).

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D3 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) Prevent catastrophic over-pressurization runaway**
> If $n \ge 1$, mass generation outpaces choked nozzle discharge rate, causing explosive pressure rise.
>
> **Q2: (C) Ignites spontaneously upon contact**
> Hypergolic propellants (e.g. $\text{N}_2\text{O}_4/\text{UDMH}$) ignite on mixing without external ignition.
>
> **Q3: (C) 125.0%**
> $p_c \propto K^{\frac{1}{1-n}} = K^{\frac{1}{1-0.5}} = K^2$.
> $\frac{p_{c2}}{p_{c1}} = (1.5)^2 = 2.25 \implies \Delta p_c / p_{c1} = 2.25 - 1 = 1.25 = \mathbf{125.0\%}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) 8.4 kg/s**
> $\dot{m}_{\text{gen}} = \rho_p A_b \dot{r} = (1750\text{ kg/m}^3)(0.60\text{ m}^2)(0.008\text{ m/s}) = 1050 \times 0.008 = \mathbf{8.40\text{ kg/s}}$.
>
> **Q5: 6.14**
> $\dot{r} = a p_c^n = 3.0(6.0^{0.40}) = 3.0(2.0477) = \mathbf{6.143\text{ mm/s} \approx 6.14\text{ mm/s}}$.
