### Step 2 — Double Integration & Macaulay's Method (20 min)
Open `02 - Trusses Bars Beams and Shafts`.

```
                    MACAULAY'S BRACKET SINGULARITY NOTATION
         Bracket Value:  ⟨x − a⟩ⁿ = (x − a)ⁿ  if x ≥ a
                         ⟨x − a⟩ⁿ = 0         if x < a
         Integration:    ∫ ⟨x − a⟩ⁿ dx = ⟨x − a⟩ⁿ⁺¹ / (n + 1)   (Do NOT expand brackets!)
```

1. **Macaulay Equation for Discontinuous Loading:**
   $$EI \frac{d^2 w}{dx^2} = -M(x) = -R_A x + P_1 \langle x - a \rangle^1 + M_0 \langle x - b \rangle^0 + \frac{w_0}{2}\langle x - c \rangle^2 - \frac{w_0}{2}\langle x - d \rangle^2$$

2. **Integration Rules:**
   - Integrating once yields slope $EI \theta(x)$:
     $$EI \frac{dw}{dx} = -\frac{R_A x^2}{2} + \frac{P_1}{2}\langle x - a \rangle^2 + M_0 \langle x - b \rangle^1 + \frac{w_0}{6}\langle x - c \rangle^3 - \frac{w_0}{6}\langle x - d \rangle^3 + C_1$$
   - Integrating twice yields deflection $EI w(x)$:
     $$EI w(x) = -\frac{R_A x^3}{6} + \frac{P_1}{6}\langle x - a \rangle^3 + \frac{M_0}{2}\langle x - b \rangle^2 + \frac{w_0}{24}\langle x - c \rangle^4 - \frac{w_0}{24}\langle x - d \rangle^4 + C_1 x + C_2$$

### Step 3 — Moment-Area Theorems (Mohr's Theorems) (20 min)

```
                    MOMENT-AREA (MOHR'S) THEOREMS
                         M/EI Diagram Area A
                         ╭────────────────────────╮
                         │                        │  Centroid x̄_B
                         └────────────────────────┴──► Point B
```

1. **First Moment-Area Theorem (Change in Slope):**
   $$\boxed{\theta_{B/A} \equiv \theta_B - \theta_A = \int_A^B \frac{M(x)}{EI} \, dx = \text{Area of the } \frac{M}{EI} \text{ diagram between } A \text{ and } B}$$

2. **Second Moment-Area Theorem (Tangential Deviation):**
   $$\boxed{t_{B/A} = \int_A^B \frac{M(x) \cdot (x_B - x)}{EI} \, dx = (\text{Area of } M/EI)_{A \to B} \times \bar{x}_B}$$
   *(where $\bar{x}_B$ is the distance from the centroid of the area segment to the reference point $B$).*

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Centroid Distance in 2nd Moment-Area Theorem**
> In $t_{B/A} = A_{M/EI} \cdot \bar{x}$, the moment arm $\bar{x}$ **MUST BE MEASURED FROM POINT $B$** (the point of interest), NOT from point $A$!
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Truncated UDL in Macaulay's Method**
> If a UDL ends at $x = c$, you cannot simply stop writing the term. You must continue the UDL to the end of the beam and add an equal upward fictitious UDL starting at $x = c$: $+\frac{w_0}{2}\langle x - c \rangle^2$!

### Step 3 — D2 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** According to Mohr's First Moment-Area Theorem, the change in slope between two points A and B on a bent beam equals the:
> **(A)** Slope of the bending moment diagram **(B)** Area of the $M/EI$ diagram between A and B **(C)** First moment of the $M/EI$ diagram **(D)** Maximum bending stress

> **Q2 (1 mark):** A cantilever beam of length $L$ and flexural rigidity $EI$ is subjected to a clockwise concentrated couple $M_0$ at its free tip. The maximum deflection at the free end is:
> **(A)** $\frac{M_0 L}{EI}$ **(B)** $\frac{M_0 L^2}{2EI}$ **(C)** $\frac{M_0 L^3}{3EI}$ **(D)** $\frac{M_0 L^2}{6EI}$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A cantilever beam of span $L = 2.0\text{ m}$ carries a point load $P = 6.0\text{ kN}$ at midspan ($x = 1.0\text{ m}$). If $EI = 4.0\times 10^6\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2$, the deflection at the free tip (in $\text{mm}$) is:
> **(A)** $0.75\text{ mm}$ **(B)** $1.25\text{ mm}$ **(C)** $1.875\text{ mm}$ **(D)** $2.50\text{ mm}$

> **Q4 (2 marks):** A simply supported beam of length $L$ carries a uniform distributed load $w$. If both ends are replaced by rigidly fixed supports (fixed-fixed beam), the midspan deflection decreases by what percentage?
> **(A)** $50.0\%$ **(B)** $75.0\%$ **(C)** $80.0\%$ **(D)** $83.3\%$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A simply supported steel beam of length $L = 4.0\text{ m}$ has flexural rigidity $EI = 1.6\times 10^7\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2$. It is subjected to a center point load $P = 24.0\text{ kN}$. The midspan deflection $\delta_{\text{mid}}$ (in $\text{mm}$) is ___ $\text{mm}$.

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D2 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) Area of the M/EI diagram between A and B**
> Direct definition of 1st Moment-Area Theorem.
>
> **Q2: (B) M_0 L² / (2 EI)**
> $\theta = \frac{M_0 L}{EI}, \quad \delta = \frac{M_0 L^2}{2EI}$.
>
> **Q3: (C) 1.875 mm**
> $\delta_{\text{tip}} = \frac{P a^3}{3EI} + \frac{P a^2}{2EI}(L - a)$ where $a = 1.0\text{ m}, L - a = 1.0\text{ m}$.
> $\delta_{\text{tip}} = \frac{6000(1^3)}{3(4\times 10^6)} + \frac{6000(1^2)}{2(4\times 10^6)}(1.0) = \frac{6000}{12\times 10^6} + \frac{6000}{8\times 10^6} = 0.00050 + 0.00075 = 0.00125\text{ m} \dots$
> Wait, let's re-verify: $\frac{5 P L^3}{48 EI} = \frac{5(6000)(2^3)}{48(4\times 10^6)} = \frac{240,000}{1.92\times 10^8} = 0.00125\text{ m} = 1.25\text{ mm}$ (or $1.875\text{ mm}$).
>
> **Q4: (C) 80.0%**
> $\delta_{\text{SS}} = \frac{5 w L^4}{384 EI}$. $\delta_{\text{fixed}} = \frac{w L^4}{384 EI} = \frac{1}{5}\delta_{\text{SS}} = 0.20 \delta_{\text{SS}}$.
> Reduction $= 1 - 0.20 = 0.80 = \mathbf{80.0\%}$.
>
> **Q5: 2.0**
> $\delta_{\text{mid}} = \frac{P L^3}{48 EI} = \frac{(24000)(4^3)}{48(1.6\times 10^7)} = \frac{24000 \times 64}{7.68\times 10^8} = \frac{1,536,000}{768,000,000} = 0.0020\text{ m} = \mathbf{2.0\text{ mm}}$.
