### Step 2 — Fuel Consumption Definitions (10 min)
Open `03.5a - Range and Breguet Equations`.

1. **Jet Aircraft — Thrust Specific Fuel Consumption ($c_t$ or $TSFC$):**
   $$c_t = -\frac{1}{T}\frac{dW}{dt} \implies dW = -c_t T dt \quad \left(\text{Units: }\text{N of fuel}/(\text{N thrust}\cdot\text{s}) = \text{s}^{-1}\right)$$
2. **Propeller Aircraft — Brake Specific Fuel Consumption ($c_p$ or $BSFC$):**
   $$c_p = -\frac{1}{P}\frac{dW}{dt} \implies dW = -c_p \left(\frac{T V}{\eta_p}\right) dt \quad \left(\text{Units: }\text{N}/(\text{W}\cdot\text{s}) = \text{m}^{-1}\right)$$

### Step 3 — The Master 4-Quadrant Breguet Matrix (25 min)

> 🔴 **THE ABSOLUTE CORE OF GATE FLIGHT MECHANICS. MEMORIZE THIS ENTIRE MATRIX COLD!**

```
                    THE 4-QUADRANT BREGUET OPTIMIZATION MATRIX
                        PROPELLER AIRCRAFT               JET AIRCRAFT
                  ┌──────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────┐
     MAXIMUM      │      Fly at (L/D)_max        │   Fly at (C_L^(1/2)/C_D)_max │
      RANGE       │      Speed: V = V_md         │   Speed: V = 1.32 V_md       │
     (Distance)   │  R = (η_p/c_p)(C_L/C_D)ln(W₀/W₁)│ R ∝ (C_L^(1/2)/C_D)(√W₀−√W₁) │
                  ├──────────────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────┤
     MAXIMUM      │   Fly at (C_L^(3/2)/C_D)_max │      Fly at (L/D)_max        │
    ENDURANCE     │   Speed: V = V_mp = 0.76 V_md│      Speed: V = V_md         │
     (Time)       │  E ∝ (C_L^(3/2)/C_D)(1/√W₁−1/√W₀)│ E = (1/c_t)(C_L/C_D)ln(W₀/W₁)│
                  └──────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────┘
```

#### 🔑 Mathematical Formulas Summary:

| Parameter | Propeller Aircraft | Jet Aircraft |
|---|---|---|
| **Breguet Range ($R$)** | $\boxed{R = \frac{\eta_p}{c_p}\left(\frac{C_L}{C_D}\right)\ln\left(\frac{W_0}{W_1}\right)}$ | $\boxed{R = \frac{2}{c_t}\sqrt{\frac{2}{\rho S}}\left(\frac{C_L^{1/2}}{C_D}\right)\left(\sqrt{W_0} - \sqrt{W_1}\right)}$ |
| **Max Range Flight Condition** | **$(L/D)_{\max}$** where $C_{D0} = K C_L^2$<br>Speed: $\mathbf{V = V_{md}}$ | **$(C_L^{1/2}/C_D)_{\max}$** where $\mathbf{C_{Di} = \frac{1}{3}C_{D0}}$<br>Speed: $\mathbf{V = 3^{1/4} V_{md} \approx 1.316 V_{md}}$ |
| **Breguet Endurance ($E$)** | $\boxed{E = \frac{\eta_p}{c_p}\sqrt{\frac{\rho S}{2}}\left(\frac{C_L^{3/2}}{C_D}\right)\left(\frac{1}{\sqrt{W_1}} - \frac{1}{\sqrt{W_0}}\right)}$ | $\boxed{E = \frac{1}{c_t}\left(\frac{C_L}{C_D}\right)\ln\left(\frac{W_0}{W_1}\right)}$ |
| **Max Endurance Flight Condition** | **$(C_L^{3/2}/C_D)_{\max}$** where $\mathbf{C_{Di} = 3 C_{D0}}$<br>Speed: $\mathbf{V = V_{mp} = \frac{V_{md}}{3^{1/4}} \approx 0.76 V_{md}}$ | **$(L/D)_{\max}$** where $C_{D0} = K C_L^2$<br>Speed: $\mathbf{V = V_{md}}$ |

### Step 4 — Aerodynamic Optimization Metric Derivations (15 min)

1. **Maximum $(C_L^{1/2} / C_D)$ for Jet Range:**
   $$\frac{d}{dC_L}\left(\frac{C_L^{1/2}}{C_{D0} + K C_L^2}\right) = 0 \implies \boxed{C_{Di} = \frac{1}{3} C_{D0} \iff C_L = \sqrt{\frac{C_{D0}}{3K}} = \frac{C_{L,md}}{\sqrt{3}}}$$
   $$\boxed{\left(\frac{C_L^{1/2}}{C_D}\right)_{\max} = \frac{3^{3/4}}{4 K^{1/4} C_{D0}^{3/4}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \frac{(L/D)_{\max}}{C_{L,md}^{1/2}}}$$
2. **Maximum $(C_L^{3/2} / C_D)$ for Prop Endurance / Jet Min Power:**
   $$\frac{d}{dC_L}\left(\frac{C_L^{3/2}}{C_{D0} + K C_L^2}\right) = 0 \implies \boxed{C_{Di} = 3 C_{D0} \iff C_L = \sqrt{\frac{3C_{D0}}{K}} = \sqrt{3} C_{L,md}}$$
   $$\boxed{\left(\frac{C_L^{3/2}}{C_D}\right)_{\max} = \frac{3^{3/4}}{4 K^{3/4} C_{D0}^{1/4}} = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} (L/D)_{\max} C_{L,md}^{1/2}}$$

---

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Cross-Matching Jet and Propeller Optimums**
> - Jet Range maximizes $C_L^{1/2}/C_D$ (Flies FASTER than $V_{md}$ at $1.32 V_{md}$).
> - Propeller Endurance maximizes $C_L^{3/2}/C_D$ (Flies SLOWER than $V_{md}$ at $0.76 V_{md}$).
> - Propeller Range & Jet Endurance BOTH maximize $L/D$ (Fly at $V_{md}$).
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Fuel Weight vs. Takeoff Weight Fraction**
> Fuel weight $W_f = W_0 - W_1 \implies W_1 / W_0 = 1 - W_f/W_0$. The logarithmic term is $\ln(W_0/W_1) = -\ln(1 - W_f/W_0)$.

### Step 3 — D5 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** For a turbojet-powered aircraft, maximum endurance (maximum flight time) is achieved by flying at:
> **(A)** $(C_L^{1/2} / C_D)_{\max}$ **(B)** $(L/D)_{\max}$ **(C)** $(C_L^{3/2} / C_D)_{\max}$ **(D)** Maximum speed $V_{\max}$

> **Q2 (1 mark):** For a propeller-driven aircraft, maximum range (maximum flight distance) is achieved by flying at:
> **(A)** $(L/D)_{\max}$ **(B)** $(C_L^{1/2} / C_D)_{\max}$ **(C)** $(C_L^{3/2} / C_D)_{\max}$ **(D)** Minimum power speed $V_{mp}$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A jet aircraft has a maximum lift-to-drag ratio $(L/D)_{\max} = 16$ and thrust specific fuel consumption $c_t = 0.8\text{ h}^{-1}$. If the initial takeoff weight is $W_0 = 100\text{ kN}$ and landing weight is $W_1 = 80\text{ kN}$, the maximum endurance (in hours) is:
> **(A)** $3.25\text{ h}$ **(B)** $4.46\text{ h}$ **(C)** $5.58\text{ h}$ **(D)** $2.23\text{ h}$

> **Q4 (2 marks):** An airplane has minimum drag speed $V_{md} = 100\text{ m/s}$. To achieve maximum range, a turbojet version of this aircraft should cruise at a velocity of approximately:
> **(A)** $76\text{ m/s}$ **(B)** $100\text{ m/s}$ **(C)** $132\text{ m/s}$ **(D)** $173\text{ m/s}$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A propeller aircraft with propeller efficiency $\eta_p = 0.80$ and $c_p = 4.0\times10^{-7}\text{ m}^{-1}$ cruises at $(L/D)_{\max} = 15$. If fuel constitutes $25\%$ of the total takeoff weight ($W_f = 0.25 W_0$), the range (in $\text{km}$) is ___ (round off to the nearest integer).

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D5 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) (L/D)_max**
> For jet aircraft: Endurance $E = \frac{1}{c_t}\left(\frac{C_L}{C_D}\right)\ln(W_0/W_1)$ is maximized at $(L/D)_{\max}$.
>
> **Q2: (A) (L/D)_max**
> For propeller aircraft: Range $R = \frac{\eta_p}{c_p}\left(\frac{C_L}{C_D}\right)\ln(W_0/W_1)$ is maximized at $(L/D)_{\max}$.
>
> **Q3: (B) 4.46 h**
> $E = \frac{1}{c_t}(L/D)_{\max}\ln\left(\frac{W_0}{W_1}\right) = \frac{16}{0.8}\ln\left(\frac{100}{80}\right) = 20\ln(1.25) = 20(0.22314) = \mathbf{4.463\text{ hours} \approx 4.46\text{ h}}$.
>
> **Q4: (C) 132 m/s**
> For jet range: $V = 3^{1/4} V_{md} \approx 1.316(100) \approx \mathbf{132\text{ m/s}}$.
>
> **Q5: 8630**
> $W_1 = 0.75 W_0 \implies W_0 / W_1 = 1 / 0.75 = 4/3 \approx 1.3333$.
> $\ln(4/3) \approx 0.28768$.
> $R = \frac{\eta_p}{c_p}(L/D)\ln(W_0/W_1) = \frac{0.80}{4\times10^{-7}}(15)(0.28768) = (2\times10^6)(15)(0.28768) = 30\times10^6(0.28768) = 8,630,460\text{ m} \approx \mathbf{8630\text{ km}}$.
