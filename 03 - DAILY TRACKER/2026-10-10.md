### 🌊 Set A — 3D Finite Wings & Prandtl Lifting Line (FC-136 → FC-148)

> [!question]- 🃏 Flashcard: FC-136 · Helmholtz's Vortex Theorems
> **Question:** State Helmholtz's three vortex theorems and their consequence for 3D finite wings.
> > [!success]- **Answer & AIR 1 Traps:**
> > 1. Strength of vortex filament is constant along its length ($d\Gamma/ds = 0$).
> > 2. A vortex filament cannot end in a fluid; it must extend to boundaries or form a closed loop.
> > 3. Fluid particles once on a vortex filament remain on it in inviscid flow.
> > Consequence: A bound wing vortex CANNOT simply end at the wingtip — it MUST continue downstream as trailing wingtip vortices!

> [!question]- 🃏 Flashcard: FC-137 · Prandtl Horseshoe Vortex Model
> **Question:** Describe the components of Prandtl's horseshoe vortex model.
> > [!success]- **Answer & AIR 1 Traps:**
> > Consists of:
> > 1. A **bound vortex** of strength $\Gamma$ along the wing quarter-chord line from $-b/2$ to $+b/2$.
> > 2. Two **semi-infinite trailing vortex filaments** of strength $\Gamma$ shed from the wingtips ($y = \pm b/2$) extending to downstream infinity.

> [!question]- 🃏 Flashcard: FC-138 · Biot-Savart Law for Semi-Infinite Vortex
> **Question:** State the induced velocity formula for a semi-infinite straight vortex filament at perpendicular distance $r$.
> > [!success]- **Answer & AIR 1 Traps:**
> > $$w = \frac{\Gamma}{4\pi r}$$
> > ⚠️ **AIR 1 Trap:** For an infinitely long vortex filament, $w = \frac{\Gamma}{2\pi r}$. For semi-infinite (starting at wingtip and extending to $\infty$), it is exactly HALF: $\frac{\Gamma}{4\pi r}$!

> [!question]- 🃏 Flashcard: FC-139 · Downwash & Induced Angle of Attack
> **Question:** Define downwash $w(y)$ and induced angle of attack $\alpha_i(y)$, and state the relation for effective angle of attack $\alpha_{\text{eff}}$.
> > [!success]- **Answer & AIR 1 Traps:**
> > - Downwash: $w(y_0) = \frac{1}{4\pi} \int_{-b/2}^{b/2} \frac{d\Gamma/dy}{y_0 - y} \, dy$ (downward vertical velocity).
> > - Induced angle of attack: $\alpha_i(y_0) = \frac{w(y_0)}{V_\infty}$.
> > - Effective angle of attack: $\alpha_{\text{eff}}(y_0) = \alpha(y_0) - \alpha_i(y_0)$.

> [!question]- 🃏 Flashcard: FC-140 · Prandtl's Lifting Line Integro-Differential Equation
> **Question:** State Prandtl's fundamental lifting line equation.
> > [!success]- **Answer & AIR 1 Traps:**
> > $$\alpha(y_0) = \frac{2\Gamma(y_0)}{a_0 V_\infty c(y_0)} + \alpha_0(y_0) + \frac{1}{4\pi V_\infty}\int_{-b/2}^{b/2}\frac{d\Gamma/dy}{y_0 - y}\,dy$$

> [!question]- 🃏 Flashcard: FC-141 · Elliptic Circulation Distribution
> **Question:** Write the expression for an elliptic circulation distribution across a wing of span $b$ and root circulation $\Gamma_0$.
> > [!success]- **Answer & AIR 1 Traps:**
> > $$\Gamma(y) = \Gamma_0 \sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{2y}{b}\right)^2}$$
> > In Glauert angle $\theta$ ($y = -\frac{b}{2}\cos\theta$): $\Gamma(\theta) = \Gamma_0 \sin\theta$.

> [!question]- 🃏 Flashcard: FC-142 · Constant Downwash of Elliptic Wing
> **Question:** What is the downwash velocity $w$ across the span of an elliptic wing?
> > [!success]- **Answer & AIR 1 Traps:**
> > $$w = \frac{\Gamma_0}{2b} = \text{CONSTANT across entire span!}$$
> > $$\alpha_i = \frac{w}{V_\infty} = \frac{C_L}{\pi AR} = \text{CONSTANT across entire span!}$$

> [!question]- 🃏 Flashcard: FC-143 · Induced Drag of Elliptic Wing
> **Question:** State the induced drag coefficient $C_{Di}$ for an elliptic wing in terms of $C_L$ and aspect ratio $AR$.
> > [!success]- **Answer & AIR 1 Traps:**
> > $$C_{Di} = C_L \alpha_i = \frac{C_L^2}{\pi AR}$$
> > This represents the theoretical minimum induced drag for any planar wing of given span and total lift.

> [!question]- 🃏 Flashcard: FC-144 · General Monoplane Lift Equation
> **Question:** In the Fourier series $\Gamma(\theta) = 2 b V_\infty \sum_{n=1}^\infty A_n \sin(n\theta)$, express the 3D lift coefficient $C_L$ in terms of Fourier coefficients.
> > [!success]- **Answer & AIR 1 Traps:**
> > $$C_L = \pi AR A_1$$
> > Total lift depends EXCLUSIVELY on the first Fourier coefficient $A_1$.

> [!question]- 🃏 Flashcard: FC-145 · General Wing Induced Drag Factor ($\delta$)
> **Question:** Write the expression for induced drag coefficient $C_{Di}$ and factor $\delta$ for a non-elliptic wing.
> > [!success]- **Answer & AIR 1 Traps:**
> > $$C_{Di} = \frac{C_L^2}{\pi AR}(1 + \delta) = \frac{C_L^2}{\pi e AR}$$
> > where $\delta = \sum_{n=2}^\infty n \left(\frac{A_n}{A_1}\right)^2 \ge 0$, and span efficiency factor is $e = \frac{1}{1 + \delta} \le 1.0$.

> [!question]- 🃏 Flashcard: FC-146 · Symmetric Wing Fourier Vanishing Rule
> **Question:** For a symmetric wing without roll or yaw, which Fourier coefficients $A_n$ in the lifting line series are strictly zero?
> > [!success]- **Answer & AIR 1 Traps:**
> > All **EVEN** coefficients vanish identically:
> > $$A_2 = A_4 = A_6 = \dots = 0$$
> > Non-zero terms are strictly odd: $A_1, A_3, A_5, \dots$

> [!question]- 🃏 Flashcard: FC-147 · 3D Wing Lift-Curve Slope Formula
> **Question:** State the relation connecting 3D wing lift-curve slope $a$ to 2D section slope $a_0$, aspect ratio $AR$, and efficiency factor $e$.
> > [!success]- **Answer & AIR 1 Traps:**
> > $$a = \frac{a_0}{1 + \frac{a_0}{\pi e AR}}$$
> > As $AR \to \infty$, $a \to a_0 = 2\pi$. As $AR$ decreases, 3D slope decreases due to downwash.

> [!question]- 🃏 Flashcard: FC-148 · Induced Drag Variation with Speed
> **Question:** How does induced drag $D_i$ vary with flight velocity $V_\infty$ in level unaccelerated flight?
> > [!success]- **Answer & AIR 1 Traps:**
> > In level flight, $L = W \implies C_L = \frac{2W}{\rho S V_\infty^2} \propto \frac{1}{V_\infty^2}$.
> > Induced drag: $D_i = q S C_{Di} = \left(\frac{1}{2}\rho V_\infty^2 S\right) \frac{C_L^2}{\pi e AR} \propto V_\infty^2 \left(\frac{1}{V_\infty^4}\right) \propto \frac{1}{V_\infty^2}$.
> > Induced drag is inversely proportional to the SQUARE of velocity ($D_i \propto 1/V_\infty^2$)!

> [!question]- 🃏 Flashcard: FC-149 · Integration by Parts Formula & Tabular Method
> **Question:** State the integration by parts formula $\int u \, dv$ and the condition for tabular integration.
> > [!success]- **Answer & AIR 1 Traps:**
> > $$\int u \, dv = u v - \int v \, du$$
> > Tabular method is applied when one function (polynomial $P(x)$) differentiates to zero after repeated derivatives, paired with an easily integrated function ($e^{ax}, \sin ax, \cos ax$).

> [!question]- 🃏 Flashcard: FC-150 · Symmetry Properties of Definite Integrals
> **Question:** State the values of $\int_{-a}^a f(x) \, dx$ for even and odd functions, and King's property $\int_a^b f(x) \, dx$.
> > [!success]- **Answer & AIR 1 Traps:**
> > - If $f(-x) = -f(x)$ (Odd): $\int_{-a}^a f(x) \, dx = 0$.
> > - If $f(-x) = f(x)$ (Even): $\int_{-a}^a f(x) \, dx = 2 \int_0^a f(x) \, dx$.
> > - King's Property: $\int_a^b f(x) \, dx = \int_a^b f(a + b - x) \, dx$.

> [!example]- 🔥 WE-28 · Complete Analytical Solution for an Elliptic Wing
> **Problem Statement (GATE AE Level 4 NAT):**  
> An aircraft of total weight $W = 24000\text{ N}$ has an elliptic wing with wingspan $b = 14.0\text{ m}$ and aspect ratio $AR = 8.00$. It flies at sea level ($\rho_\infty = 1.225\text{ kg/m}^3$) at steady level speed $V_\infty = 60.0\text{ m/s}$.  
> 
> **Tasks:**  
> 1. Calculate the wing planform area $S$ in $\text{m}^2$ and root chord $c_0$ in meters.  
> 2. Determine the wing lift coefficient $C_L$.  
> 3. Calculate the downwash velocity $w$ in $\text{m/s}$ across the wingspan.  
> 4. Determine the induced angle of attack $\alpha_i$ in degrees.  
> 5. Compute the induced drag force $D_i$ in Newtons and induced drag coefficient $C_{Di}$.  
> 6. Calculate the peak bound circulation $\Gamma_0$ at the root in $\text{m}^2/\text{s}$.
> 
> > [!success]- **Complete Analytical Solution & TCS Calculator Keystroke Ledger:**
> > 
> > #### Step 1: Geometry
> > $$S = \frac{b^2}{AR} = \frac{14.0^2}{8.00} = \frac{196.0}{8.00} = 24.50\text{ m}^2$$
> > For an elliptic planform:
> > $$S = \frac{\pi}{4} b c_0 \implies c_0 = \frac{4 S}{\pi b} = \frac{4 \times 24.50}{\pi \times 14.0} = \frac{98.0}{43.9823} = 2.22817\text{ m}$$
> > Target Answer: **$c_0 = 2.23\text{ m}$**
> > 
> > #### Step 2: Lift Coefficient ($C_L$)
> > Dynamic pressure:
> > $$q_\infty = \frac{1}{2}\rho_\infty V_\infty^2 = \frac{1}{2} \times 1.225 \times 60.0^2 = 0.6125 \times 3600 = 2205.0\text{ Pa}$$
> > In steady level flight: $L = W = 24000\text{ N}$.
> > $$C_L = \frac{L}{q_\infty S} = \frac{24000.0}{2205.0 \times 24.50} = \frac{24000.0}{54022.5} = 0.444259$$
> > Target Answer: **$C_L = 0.444$** · Official Tolerance: **$[0.441, 0.447]$**
> > 
> > #### Step 3: Induced Drag Coefficient & Induced Angle

> > Induced angle:
> > $$\alpha_i = \frac{C_L}{\pi AR}\text{ rad} = \frac{0.444259}{25.13274} = 0.0176765\text{ rad}$$

> > 
> > #### Step 4: Downwash Velocity ($w$)

### 🔬 Apex Worked Example 29: General Monoplane 2-Term Solution for Rectangular Wing

> [!example]- 🔥 WE-29 · 2-Term Lifting Line Solution for Rectangular Wing
> **Problem Statement (GATE AE Level 4 NAT):**  
> A rectangular wing of aspect ratio $AR = 6.00$ with symmetric airfoil section ($a_0 = 2\pi\text{ rad}^{-1}, \alpha_0 = 0$) operates at angle of attack $\alpha = 5.0^\circ$ ($0.087266\text{ rad}$).  
> The circulation is approximated by a two-term odd Fourier series:
> $$\Gamma(\theta) = 2 b V_\infty [A_1 \sin\theta + A_3 \sin(3\theta)]$$
> 
> Applying the monoplane equation at $\theta = 45^\circ$ ($\pi/4$) and $\theta = 90^\circ$ ($\pi/2$), the resulting Fourier coefficients are:
> $$A_1 = 0.03840, \quad A_3 = 0.00420$$
> 
> **Tasks:**  
> 1. Calculate the 3D lift coefficient $C_L$.  
> 2. Calculate the induced drag factor $\delta$.  
> 3. Determine the Oswald span efficiency factor $e$.  
> 4. Compute the induced drag coefficient $C_{Di}$.  
> 5. What percentage of total induced drag is due to non-elliptic loading ($3 A_3^2 / A_1^2$)?
> 
> > [!success]- **Complete Analytical Solution & Verification:**
> > 
> > #### Step 1: 3D Lift Coefficient ($C_L$)
> > $$C_L = \pi AR A_1 = \pi \times 6.00 \times 0.03840 = 6.00 \pi \times 0.03840 = 0.72382$$
> > Target Answer: **$C_L = 0.724$** · Official Tolerance: **$[0.720, 0.728]$**
> > 
> > #### Step 2: Induced Drag Factor ($\delta$)
> > For a 2-term odd series:
> > $$\delta = \sum_{n=2}^\infty n \left(\frac{A_n}{A_1}\right)^2 = 3 \left(\frac{A_3}{A_1}\right)^2$$
> > Ratio:
> > $$\frac{A_3}{A_1} = \frac{0.00420}{0.03840} = 0.109375$$
> > Squared ratio:
> > $$\left(\frac{A_3}{A_1}\right)^2 = 0.109375^2 = 0.0119629$$
> > Induced drag factor:
> > $$\delta = 3 \times 0.0119629 = 0.035889 \approx 0.0359$$
> > Target Answer: **$\delta = 0.0359$**
> > 
> > #### Step 3: Oswald Span Efficiency Factor ($e$)
> > $$e = \frac{1}{1 + \delta} = \frac{1}{1 + 0.035889} = \frac{1}{1.035889} = 0.96535$$
> > Target Answer: **$e = 0.965$**
> > 
> > #### Step 4: Induced Drag Coefficient ($C_{Di}$)
> > $$C_{Di} = \frac{C_L^2}{\pi e AR} = \frac{0.72382^2}{\pi \times 0.96535 \times 6.00} = \frac{0.523915}{18.1963} = 0.028792$$
> > Target Answer: **$C_{Di} = 0.0288$**
> > 
> > #### Step 5: Percentage Drag Penalty from Non-Elliptic Loading
> > Non-elliptic penalty $= \delta \times 100\% = 0.035889 \times 100\% = 3.59\%$.  
> > Target Answer: **$3.59\%$**

> **Problem Statement (GATE AE Level 4 NAT):**  
> An experimental elliptic circulation distribution on a wing of span $b = 10.0\text{ m}$ is given by:
> $$\Gamma(y) = 20.0 \sqrt{1 - \left(\frac{2y}{b}\right)^2}\text{ m}^2/\text{s}$$
> 
> **Tasks:**  
> 1. Compute the derivative $d\Gamma/dy$ at span station $y$.  
> 2. Evaluate the total integrated lift $L$ per unit dynamic pressure $L/q_\infty$ using the integral:
>    $$\frac{L}{q_\infty} = \frac{2}{V_\infty}\int_{-b/2}^{b/2}\Gamma(y)\,dy$$
> 3. Verify the constant downwash value $w = \frac{\Gamma_0}{2b}$ using the Cauchy integral:
>    $$w(0) = \frac{1}{4\pi}\int_{-b/2}^{b/2}\frac{-d\Gamma/dy}{y}\,dy$$
> 
> > [!success]- **Complete Analytical Solution & Verification:**
> > 
> > #### Step 1: Circulation Derivative
> > Let $\bar{y} = 2y/b$:
> > $$\frac{d\Gamma}{dy} = 20.0 \times \frac{1}{2\sqrt{1 - \bar{y}^2}} \left(-\frac{8y}{b^2}\right) = -\frac{80 y}{b^2 \sqrt{1 - (2y/b)^2}}$$
> > 
> > #### Step 2: Total Integrated Lift
> > Substitute $y = -\frac{b}{2}\cos\theta \implies dy = \frac{b}{2}\sin\theta d\theta$:
> > $$\int_{-b/2}^{b/2}\Gamma(y)\,dy = \int_0^\pi (\Gamma_0 \sin\theta)\left(\frac{b}{2}\sin\theta\right)d\theta = \frac{\Gamma_0 b}{2}\int_0^\pi \sin^2\theta d\theta = \frac{\Gamma_0 b}{2}\left(\frac{\pi}{2}\right) = \frac{\pi}{4}\Gamma_0 b$$
> > Substituting $\Gamma_0 = 20.0$ and $b = 10.0$:
> > $$\int_{-b/2}^{b/2}\Gamma(y)\,dy = \frac{\pi}{4}(20.0)(10.0) = 50.0\pi \approx 157.08\text{ m}^3/\text{s}$$
> > Target Answer: **$157.08\text{ m}^3/\text{s}$**
> > 
> > #### Step 3: Verification of Constant Downwash
> > In Glauert variable $\theta$:
> > $$w(y_0) = \frac{1}{4\pi}\int_0^\pi \frac{\Gamma_0 \cos\theta}{\frac{b}{2}(\cos\theta - \cos\theta_0)} d\theta = \frac{\Gamma_0}{2\pi b}\int_0^\pi \frac{\cos\theta}{\cos\theta - \cos\theta_0}d\theta$$
> > Using the Glauert identity for $n=1$: $\int_0^\pi \frac{\cos\theta}{\cos\theta - \cos\theta_0}d\theta = \pi$.  
> > $$w(y_0) = \frac{\Gamma_0}{2\pi b}(\pi) = \frac{\Gamma_0}{2b}$$
> > Substituting values:
> > $$w = \frac{20.0}{2(10.0)} = \frac{20.0}{20.0} = 1.000\text{ m/s}$$
> > Target Answer: **$w = 1.00\text{ m/s}$**
