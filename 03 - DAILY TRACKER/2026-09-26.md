### Step 1 — The Master Quantitative Shortcuts Table (30 min)

| Topic | Key Formula / Shortcut | High-Yield Application |
|---|---|---|
| **Successive Percentage Change** | $\boxed{\text{Net \%} = a + b + \frac{ab}{100}}$ | Price increases by $20\%$ then drops by $10\% \implies 20 - 10 - \frac{200}{100} = +8\%$. |
| **Average Speed (Equal Distance)** | $\boxed{V_{\text{avg}} = \frac{2 V_1 V_2}{V_1 + V_2}}$ | Travel at $60\text{ km/h}$ and return at $40\text{ km/h} \implies \frac{2(60)(40)}{100} = \mathbf{48\text{ km/h}}$ (NOT 50!). |
| **Work & Time / Pipes** | $\boxed{\frac{1}{T_{\text{total}}} = \frac{1}{A} + \frac{1}{B} - \frac{1}{C_{\text{drain}}}}$ | $A$ takes 6 days, $B$ takes 12 days $\implies T = \frac{6 \times 12}{6 + 12} = \frac{72}{18} = \mathbf{4\text{ days}}$. |
| **Mixtures & Alligation** | $\boxed{\frac{Q_{\text{cheap}}}{Q_{\text{dear}}} = \frac{C_{\text{dear}} - C_{\text{mean}}}{C_{\text{mean}} - C_{\text{cheap}}}}$ | Mixing liquids of concentrations $20\%$ and $50\%$ to get $30\% \implies \frac{50-30}{30-20} = \mathbf{2:1}$. |
| **Permutations & Combinations** | $^n P_r = \frac{n!}{(n-r)!}, \quad ^n C_r = \frac{n!}{r!(n-r)!}$ | Arranging items in a circle $\implies (n-1)!$. |
| **Probability & Independence** | $P(A \cup B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B)$<br>$P(A|B) = \frac{P(A \cap B)}{P(B)}$ | For independent events: $P(A \cap B) = P(A) \cdot P(B)$. |

### Step 2 — Spatial Aptitude Core Patterns (20 min)

Since 2021, GATE includes mandatory **Spatial Aptitude** questions:

1. **2D & 3D Transformations:**
   - **Mirror Reflection:** Inverts horizontal coordinates across vertical axis ($x \to -x$).
   - **Rotation:** $90^\circ$ clockwise transformation maps $(x, y) \to (y, -x)$.
2. **Paper Folding & Punching:**
   - Trace the reverse symmetry path: unfold step-by-step applying mirror reflection across the fold line!
3. **Cube & Solid Nets:**
   - On a standard 6-square cube net, opposite faces are separated by exactly **one intermediate square** (they can never share an edge or vertex).

### Step 3 — D5 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** A person travels from city A to city B at a speed of $60\text{ km/h}$ and returns from city B to city A along the same route at $40\text{ km/h}$. The average speed for the entire round trip is:
> **(A)** $50\text{ km/h}$ **(B)** $48\text{ km/h}$ **(C)** $45\text{ km/h}$ **(D)** $52\text{ km/h}$

> **Q2 (2 marks):** Pipe A can fill a tank in 12 hours, while Pipe B can empty the same tank in 18 hours. If both pipes are opened simultaneously when the tank is empty, the time taken (in hours) to fill the tank completely is:
> **(A)** 30 **(B)** 36 **(C)** 24 **(D)** 15

> **Q3 (2 marks):** In how many different ways can the letters of the word **"ENGINE"** be arranged such that the two 'E's are always together?
> **(A)** 120 **(B)** 60 **(C)** 240 **(D)** 720

> **Q4 (1 mark):** In a standard 2D unfold (net) of a 6-faced cube, two opposite faces can:
> **(A)** Share a common corner vertex **(B)** Share a common edge **(C)** Never touch each other on the net **(D)** Be adjacent

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A bag contains 4 red balls and 6 blue balls. Two balls are drawn at random one after another without replacement. The probability that both drawn balls are red is ___ (round off to 2 decimal places).

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D5 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) 48 km/h**
> $V_{\text{avg}} = \frac{2 V_1 V_2}{V_1 + V_2} = \frac{2(60)(40)}{60 + 40} = \frac{4800}{100} = \mathbf{48\text{ km/h}}$.
>
> **Q2: (B) 36**
> Net filling rate: $\frac{1}{T} = \frac{1}{12} - \frac{1}{18} = \frac{3 - 2}{36} = \frac{1}{36} \implies T = \mathbf{36\text{ hours}}$.
>
> **Q3: (B) 60**
> Treat the two 'E's as a single unit $[EE]$.
> We now arrange 5 units: $[EE], N, G, I, N$.
> The letter 'N' appears 2 times.
> Number of arrangements $= \frac{5!}{2!} = \frac{120}{2} = \mathbf{60}$.
>
> **Q4: (C) Never touch each other on the net**
> Opposite faces on a folded 3D cube can never share a boundary edge or vertex in the flattened 2D net.
>
> **Q5: 0.13**
> Total balls $= 4 + 6 = 10$.
> $P(\text{Both Red}) = \frac{4}{10} \times \frac{3}{9} = \frac{2}{5} \times \frac{1}{3} = \frac{2}{15} \approx \mathbf{0.1333 \approx 0.13}$.
