## ⏰ HOUR 1 (0:00 – 1:00): 51-MARK POWER SCAN & SPEED SHORTCUTS

### Step 1 — Engineering Mathematics Speed Invariants (20 min)
1. **Eigenvalues & Matrix Algebra:**
   - $\text{Tr}(A) = \sum \lambda_i, \ \det(A) = \prod \lambda_i, \ A^{-1} \implies 1/\lambda_i, \ A^k \implies \lambda_i^k$.
   - Cayley-Hamilton Theorem: Matrix satisfies its own characteristic polynomial $P(A) = 0$.
2. **Vector Calculus Integrals:**
   - Divergence Theorem: $\iint_S \vec{F}\cdot\hat{n}\,dS = \iiint_V (\nabla\cdot\vec{F})\,dV$.
   - Stokes' Theorem: $\oint_C \vec{F}\cdot d\vec{r} = \iint_S (\nabla\times\vec{F})\cdot\hat{n}\,dS$.
3. **Differential Equations & Probability:**
   - Integrating factor for linear ODE $\frac{dy}{dx} + P(x)y = Q(x)$: $\text{IF} = e^{\int P dx}$.
   - Standard Normal $Z$: $P(Z > 0) = 0.50, \ P(-1 < Z < 1) \approx 0.6826$.

### Step 2 — Flight Mechanics & Space Dynamics Invariants (20 min)
1. **Drag Polars & Level Flight:**
   - Drag Polar: $C_D = C_{D0} + K C_L^2$.
   - Min Thrust Speed: $V_{\text{md}} = \sqrt{\frac{2 W}{\rho S \sqrt{C_{D0}/K}}}, \ (L/D)_{\max} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{K C_{D0}}}$.
   - Min Power Speed: $V_{\text{mp}} = \frac{V_{\text{md}}}{3^{1/4}} \approx 0.76 V_{\text{md}}$.
2. **Maneuver & Stability:**
   - Coordinated Turn: $n = \frac{1}{\cos\phi}, \ R = \frac{V^2}{g\sqrt{n^2 - 1}}, \ \omega = \frac{g\sqrt{n^2 - 1}}{V}$.
   - Static Margin: $\text{SM} = \frac{x_{\text{NP}} - x_{\text{cg}}}{\bar{c}} = -\frac{dC_m}{dC_L}$.
3. **Orbital Mechanics:**
   - Specific Energy: $\mathcal{E} = -\frac{\mu}{2a} = \frac{v^2}{2} - \frac{\mu}{r}$.
   - Velocity: $v = \sqrt{\mu\left(\frac{2}{r} - \frac{1}{a}\right)}, \ v_{\text{circ}} = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{r}}, \ v_{\text{esc}} = \sqrt{\frac{2\mu}{r}}$.
   - Period: $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{a^3}{\mu}}$.

### Step 3 — Aerospace Propulsion & Rocketry Invariants (20 min)
1. **Brayton Cycle & Gas Turbines:**
   - Thermal Efficiency: $\eta_{\text{thermal}} = 1 - \frac{1}{r_p^{(\gamma-1)/\gamma}}$.
   - Uninstalled Net Thrust: $F = \dot{m}_a [(1+f)V_e - V_0] + (p_e - p_a)A_e$.
   - Specific Fuel Consumption: $\text{TSFC} = \frac{\dot{m}_f}{F}$.
2. **Compressors & Turbines:**
   - Compressor Temperature Exit: $T_{02} = T_{01}(r_p)^{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma}}$.
   - Turbine-Compressor Power Balance: $\dot{m}_a c_p (T_{03} - T_{04}) = \dot{m}_a c_p (T_{02} - T_{01})$.
3. **Rocket Propulsion:**
   - Thrust: $F = \dot{m} V_e + (p_e - p_a)A_e = \dot{m} c = C_F P_c A_t$.
   - Characteristic Velocity: $c^* = \frac{P_c A_t}{\dot{m}}$.
   - Tsiolkovsky Equation: $\Delta v = I_{\text{sp}} g_0 \ln\left(\frac{m_0}{m_f}\right) = c \ln\left(\frac{m_0}{m_f}\right)$.

### Step 3 — D4 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** If a $3\times 3$ matrix $A$ has determinant $8$, the determinant of $2A$ is:
> **(A)** $16$ **(B)** $32$ **(C)** $64$ **(D)** $128$

> **Q2 (1 mark):** In steady level flight of an aircraft, moving the center of gravity aft towards the neutral point causes the static margin to:
> **(A)** Increase **(B)** Decrease **(C)** Remain constant **(D)** Become infinite

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A satellite in a circular orbit of radius $r = 8000\text{ km}$ around Earth ($\mu = 3.986\times 10^5\text{ km}^3/\text{s}^2$) has circular velocity $v$ (in $\text{km/s}$):
> **(A)** $6.82\text{ km/s}$ **(B)** $7.06\text{ km/s}$ **(C)** $7.90\text{ km/s}$ **(D)** $8.45\text{ km/s}$

> **Q4 (2 marks):** An ideal turbojet compressor with $r_p = 16.0$ and $\gamma = 1.40$ has inlet total temperature $T_{01} = 300\text{ K}$. The compressor exit total temperature $T_{02}$ (in Kelvin) is:
> **(A)** $550.0\text{ K}$ **(B)** $662.6\text{ K}$ **(C)** $720.0\text{ K}$ **(D)** $810.5\text{ K}$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A rocket engine has chamber pressure $P_c = 4.5\text{ MPa}$, throat area $A_t = 0.010\text{ m}^2$, and characteristic velocity $c^* = 1500\text{ m/s}$. The propellant mass flow rate $\dot{m}$ (in $\text{kg/s}$) is ___ $\text{kg/s}$.

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D4 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (C) 64**
> For $n\times n$ matrix, $\det(kA) = k^n \det(A) = 2^3 (8) = 8 \times 8 = \mathbf{64}$.
>
> **Q2: (B) Decrease**
> $\text{SM} = \frac{x_{\text{NP}} - x_{\text{cg}}}{\bar{c}}$. As $x_{\text{cg}} \to x_{\text{NP}}$, $\text{SM} \to 0$.
>
> **Q3: (B) 7.06 km/s**
> $v = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{r}} = \sqrt{\frac{3.986\times 10^5}{8000}} = \sqrt{49.825} \approx \mathbf{7.0587\text{ km/s} \approx 7.06\text{ km/s}}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) 662.6 K**
> $T_{02} = T_{01}(r_p)^{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma}} = 300(16)^{\frac{0.40}{1.40}} = 300(16)^{0.2857} = 300(2.2085) = \mathbf{662.56\text{ K} \approx 662.6\text{ K}}$.
>
> **Q5: 30.0**
> $\dot{m} = \frac{P_c A_t}{c^*} = \frac{(4.5\times 10^6\text{ Pa})(0.010\text{ m}^2)}{1500\text{ m/s}} = \frac{45,000}{1500} = \mathbf{30.0\text{ kg/s}}$.
