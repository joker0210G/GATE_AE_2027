### Step 2 — Euler Column Buckling Theory (20 min)
Open `04 - Column Buckling`.

```
                    EULER COLUMN BUCKLING (ELASTIC INSTABILITY)
                                  P
                                  ▼
                            ┌─────┴─────┐
                            │           │
                            │     ╭─╮   │
                            │    │   │  │  Lateral Buckling Deflection w(x)
                            │     ╰─╯   │
                            │           │
                            └─────┬─────┘
                                  ▲
                                  P
```

1. **The Euler Critical Buckling Load:**
   For a slender, pin-ended column under axial compressive load $P$:
   $$E I \frac{d^2 w}{dx^2} + P w = 0 \implies w(x) = C_1 \sin\left(\sqrt{\frac{P}{EI}}x\right) + C_2 \cos\left(\sqrt{\frac{P}{EI}}x\right)$$
   Applying boundary conditions ($w(0) = 0, w(L) = 0$) gives the fundamental buckling load:

   $$\boxed{P_{\text{cr}} = \frac{\pi^2 E I_{\min}}{L_e^2}}$$
   - $E$: Young's modulus ($\text{Pa}$).
   - $I_{\min}$: **MINIMUM second moment of area** of the cross-section ($\text{m}^4$).
     *(A column ALWAYS buckles about its WEAKEST principal axis: $I_{\min} = \min(I_x, I_y)$).*
   - $L_e$: Effective length of the column ($\text{m}$).

2. **Critical Buckling Stress ($\sigma_{\text{cr}}$) & Slenderness Ratio ($\lambda$):**
   $$\boxed{\sigma_{\text{cr}} \equiv \frac{P_{\text{cr}}}{A} = \frac{\pi^2 E I_{\min}}{A L_e^2} = \frac{\pi^2 E}{(L_e / r_{\min})^2} = \frac{\pi^2 E}{\lambda^2}}$$
   - **Radius of Gyration ($r_{\min}$):** $\mathbf{r_{\min} \equiv \sqrt{\frac{I_{\min}}{A}}}$.
   - **Slenderness Ratio ($\lambda$):** $\mathbf{\lambda \equiv \frac{L_e}{r_{\min}}}$.

### Step 3 — The 4 Standard End Boundary Conditions ($L_e$) (20 min)

```
                    THE 4 FUNDAMENTAL END SUPPORT CONDITIONS
  End Conditions              Effective Length (L_e)    Critical Load (P_cr)          Load Factor
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1. Both Ends Pinned (Hinged) L_e = L                  π² E I / L²                   1.0 P_0
  2. Fixed–Free (Cantilever)   L_e = 2 L                π² E I / (4 L²)               0.25 P_0 (Weakest!)
  3. Both Ends Fixed           L_e = 0.5 L = L/2        4 π² E I / L²                 4.0 P_0  (Strongest!)
  4. Fixed–Pinned (Hinged)     L_e ≈ 0.707 L = L/√2     2 π² E I / L²                 2.0 P_0
```

### Step 4 — Validity Limits of Euler's Formula (15 min)

- Euler's formula assumes **purely elastic buckling** without prior plastic yielding:
  $$\sigma_{\text{cr}} \le \sigma_{\text{yield}} \implies \frac{\pi^2 E}{\lambda^2} \le \sigma_{\text{yield}}$$
  $$\boxed{\lambda \ge \lambda_{\text{critical}} \equiv \pi \sqrt{\frac{E}{\sigma_{\text{yield}}}}}$$
  - For structural steel ($E = 200\text{ GPa}, \sigma_y = 250\text{ MPa}$): $\lambda_{\text{critical}} \approx \pi \sqrt{\frac{200\times 10^3}{250}} = \pi \sqrt{800} \approx \mathbf{88.8}$.
  - If $\lambda < \lambda_{\text{critical}}$, the column fails by **inelastic/plastic yielding** before reaching Euler buckling.

---

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Maximum vs. Minimum Moment of Inertia**
> Always use $\mathbf{I_{\min}}$, NOT $I_{\max}$! For a rectangle $b \times h$ ($b < h$), $I_{\min} = \frac{h b^3}{12}$, NOT $\frac{b h^3}{12}$!
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Fixed-Free vs. Fixed-Fixed Load Factor**
> Fixed-Fixed is $\mathbf{16\times}$ stronger than Fixed-Free: $\frac{P_{\text{cr,fixed-fixed}}}{P_{\text{cr,fixed-free}}} = \frac{4 \pi^2 EI / L^2}{\pi^2 EI / (4L^2)} = 16$!

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A steel rod ($E = 200\text{ GPa}$) of solid circular cross-section with diameter $d = 20\text{ mm}$ and length $L = 1.0\text{ m}$ is pinned at both ends. The Euler critical buckling load $P_{\text{cr}}$ (in $\text{kN}$) is:
> **(A)** $7.75\text{ kN}$ **(B)** $15.50\text{ kN}$ **(C)** $31.00\text{ kN}$ **(D)** $62.00\text{ kN}$

> **Q4 (2 marks):** A column of length $L = 3.0\text{ m}$ with $E = 210\text{ GPa}$ has cross-sectional area $A = 1200\text{ mm}^2$ and minimum radius of gyration $r_{\min} = 25\text{ mm}$. If one end is fixed and the other pinned ($L_e = 0.707 L$), the slenderness ratio $\lambda$ is:
> **(A)** $60.0$ **(B)** $84.8$ **(C)** $100.0$ **(D)** $120.0$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A cantilever column (fixed at base, free at top) of length $L = 2.0\text{ m}$ has flexural rigidity $EI = 8.0\times 10^4\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2$. The Euler critical load $P_{\text{cr}}$ (in $\text{kN}$) is ___ $\text{kN}$ (round off to 2 decimal places).

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D4 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) 40(20³) / 12**
> Weakest axis $I_{\min}$ governs buckling.
>
> **Q2: (B) 4**
> $P_{\text{cr,fixed}} = \frac{4 \pi^2 EI}{L^2} = 4 P_{\text{cr,pinned}}$.
>
> **Q3: (B) 15.50 kN**
> $I = \frac{\pi(0.020^4)}{64} = \frac{\pi(1.6\times 10^{-7})}{64} = 2.50\pi\times 10^{-9} = 7.854\times 10^{-9}\text{ m}^4$.
> $P_{\text{cr}} = \frac{\pi^2(200\times 10^9)(7.854\times 10^{-9})}{1.0^2} = \pi^2(1570.8) \approx 15,503\text{ N} = \mathbf{15.50\text{ kN}}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) 84.8**
> $L_e = 0.707(3000\text{ mm}) = 2121.3\text{ mm}$.
> $\lambda = \frac{L_e}{r_{\min}} = \frac{2121.3}{25} \approx \mathbf{84.85 \approx 84.8}$.
>
> **Q5: 49.35**
> $L_e = 2 L = 4.0\text{ m}$.
> $P_{\text{cr}} = \frac{\pi^2(8.0\times 10^4)}{4.0^2} = \frac{789568.35}{16} = 49,348\text{ N} = \mathbf{49.35\text{ kN}}$.
