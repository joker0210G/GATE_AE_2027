### Step 2 — Potential Flow Governing Equation & Superposition (15 min)

For flow that is both **Incompressible** ($\nabla \cdot \vec{V} = 0$) and **Irrotational** ($\vec{V} = \nabla \phi$):

$$\nabla \cdot (\nabla \phi) = 0 \implies \boxed{\nabla^2 \phi = \frac{\partial^2 \phi}{\partial x^2} + \frac{\partial^2 \phi}{\partial y^2} = 0} \quad \text{and} \quad \boxed{\nabla^2 \psi = 0}$$

- **The Principle of Superposition:**
  Because Laplace's equation is **linear**, any linear combination of valid elementary solutions is also a valid potential flow solution:
  $$\phi_{\text{total}} = \sum_{i=1}^N c_i \phi_i, \qquad \psi_{\text{total}} = \sum_{i=1}^N c_i \psi_i$$

---

### Step 3 — The Four Elementary Potential Flows (Master Table) (25 min)

> 🔴 **MEMORIZE ALL 4 ELEMENTARY FLOWS IN BOTH CARTESIAN AND POLAR COORDINATES.**

| Elementary Flow | Defining Parameter | Velocity Potential ($\phi$) | Stream Function ($\psi$) | Velocity Components ($v_r, v_\theta$) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Uniform Flow** | Speed $V_\infty$, angle $\alpha$ | $\phi = V_\infty(x\cos\alpha + y\sin\alpha)$<br>$\phi = V_\infty r\cos(\theta - \alpha)$ | $\psi = V_\infty(y\cos\alpha - x\sin\alpha)$<br>$\psi = V_\infty r\sin(\theta - \alpha)$ | $v_r = V_\infty\cos(\theta - \alpha)$<br>$v_\theta = -V_\infty\sin(\theta - \alpha)$ |
| **2. Source ($+\Lambda$) / Sink ($-\Lambda$)** | Volumetric source strength $\Lambda = 2\pi q$ | $\phi = \dfrac{\Lambda}{2\pi}\ln r$ | $\psi = \dfrac{\Lambda}{2\pi}\theta$ | $v_r = \dfrac{\Lambda}{2\pi r}$<br>$v_\theta = 0$ |
| **3. Doublet** | Strength $\kappa = 2\pi R^2 V_\infty$ (aligned with $+x$) | $\phi = \dfrac{\kappa}{2\pi}\dfrac{\cos\theta}{r} = \dfrac{\kappa}{2\pi}\dfrac{x}{x^2+y^2}$ | $\psi = -\dfrac{\kappa}{2\pi}\dfrac{\sin\theta}{r} = -\dfrac{\kappa}{2\pi}\dfrac{y}{x^2+y^2}$ | $v_r = -\dfrac{\kappa}{2\pi r^2}\cos\theta$<br>$v_\theta = -\dfrac{\kappa}{2\pi r^2}\sin\theta$ |
| **4. Point Vortex** | Circulation $\Gamma$ (clockwise $-\Gamma$) | $\phi = -\dfrac{\Gamma}{2\pi}\theta$ | $\psi = \dfrac{\Gamma}{2\pi}\ln r$ | $v_r = 0$<br>$v_\theta = -\dfrac{\Gamma}{2\pi r}$ |

---

### Step 4 — Velocity, Pressure & Bernoulli's Equation (10 min)

Once $\phi$ or $\psi$ is obtained by superposition:
1. **Compute Velocity Field:** $u = \frac{\partial \phi}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial \psi}{\partial y}$, $v = \frac{\partial \phi}{\partial y} = -\frac{\partial \psi}{\partial x} \implies V = \sqrt{u^2 + v^2}$.
2. **Apply Bernoulli's Equation (Valid EVERYWHERE in Irrotational Incompressible Flow):**
   $$p + \frac{1}{2}\rho V^2 = p_\infty + \frac{1}{2}\rho V_\infty^2 = p_0 \quad (\text{Total Pressure is constant everywhere!})$$
3. **Pressure Coefficient ($C_p$):**
   $$\boxed{C_p \equiv \frac{p - p_\infty}{\frac{1}{2}\rho_\infty V_\infty^2} = 1 - \left(\frac{V}{V_\infty}\right)^2}$$
   - At Stagnation Point ($V = 0$): $\mathbf{C_p = +1.0}$ (Maximum possible pressure in incompressible flow).
   - In regions of high velocity ($V > V_\infty$): $C_p < 0$ (**Suction region**).

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Doublet Stream Function Sign**
> $\psi = -\frac{\kappa}{2\pi}\frac{\sin\theta}{r}$. Do not drop the negative sign!
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Clockwise vs. Counter-Clockwise Vortex**
> In standard aerodynamic convention, circulation $\Gamma$ producing positive lift is defined **clockwise**, which corresponds to $v_\theta = -\frac{\Gamma}{2\pi r}$ (negative $v_\theta$).

### Step 3 — D1 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** The stream function for a 2D line source of strength $\Lambda$ located at the origin is:
> **(A)** $\frac{\Lambda}{2\pi}\ln r$ **(B)** $\frac{\Lambda}{2\pi}\theta$ **(C)** $-\frac{\Lambda}{2\pi}\theta$ **(D)** $\frac{\Lambda}{2\pi r}$

> **Q2 (2 marks):** A uniform flow with $V_\infty = 20\text{ m/s}$ in the $+x$ direction is combined with a line source of strength $\Lambda = 40\pi\text{ m}^2\text{/s}$ at the origin. The distance of the stagnation point from the origin is:
> **(A)** $0.5\text{ m}$ **(B)** $1.0\text{ m}$ **(C)** $2.0\text{ m}$ **(D)** $4.0\text{ m}$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** At a point on an aerodynamic body where the local flow velocity is $1.5 V_\infty$, the pressure coefficient $C_p$ is:
> **(A)** $-1.25$ **(B)** $-0.50$ **(C)** $+0.25$ **(D)** $-2.25$

> **Q4 (1 mark):** A 2D doublet is formed by:
> **(A)** Bringing a source and a sink of equal strength together as their distance $d \to 0$ while product $\Lambda d = \text{const}$
> **(B)** Two equal sources placed at opposite ends of a diameter
> **(C)** A vortex and a source combined at the origin
> **(D)** Two counter-rotating vortices

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** For a source of strength $\Lambda = 10\text{ m}^2\text{/s}$ placed at $(1, 0)$ and a sink of equal strength $\Lambda = 10\text{ m}^2\text{/s}$ placed at $(-1, 0)$, the flow velocity magnitude at the origin $(0, 0)$ is ___ $\text{m/s}$ (round off to 2 decimal places).

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D1 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) (Λ/2π) θ**
> For a 2D source: $\phi = \frac{\Lambda}{2\pi}\ln r$ and $\psi = \mathbf{\frac{\Lambda}{2\pi}\theta}$.
>
> **Q2: (B) 1.0 m**
> On the negative $x$-axis, the velocity is $u = V_\infty + \frac{\Lambda}{2\pi x} = 0 \implies x_s = -\frac{\Lambda}{2\pi V_\infty} = -\frac{40\pi}{2\pi(20)} = -1.0\text{ m}$.
> Distance $= |x_s| = \mathbf{1.0\text{ m}}$.
>
> **Q3: (A) -1.25**
> $C_p = 1 - \left(\frac{V}{V_\infty}\right)^2 = 1 - (1.5)^2 = 1 - 2.25 = \mathbf{-1.25}$.
>
> **Q4: (A)**
> A doublet is the limiting case of a source-sink pair as distance $d \to 0$ with strength $\kappa = \lim_{d\to0}(\Lambda d) = \text{constant}$.
>
> **Q5: 3.18**
> Source at $(1, 0)$ pushes fluid in $-x$ direction at origin: $u_1 = -\frac{\Lambda}{2\pi r_1} = -\frac{10}{2\pi(1)} = -\frac{5}{\pi}$.
> Sink at $(-1, 0)$ pulls fluid in $-x$ direction at origin: $u_2 = -\frac{\Lambda}{2\pi r_2} = -\frac{10}{2\pi(1)} = -\frac{5}{\pi}$.
> Total velocity $u = u_1 + u_2 = -\frac{10}{\pi} \approx -3.183\text{ m/s} \implies |\vec{V}| \approx \mathbf{3.18\text{ m/s}}$.
