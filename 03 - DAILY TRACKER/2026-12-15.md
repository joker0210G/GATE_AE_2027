### Step 2 — Transverse Shear Stress in Solid Beams (20 min)
Open `05 - Thin Walled Sections and Aircraft Loads`.

```
                    TRANSVERSE SHEAR STRESS DISTRIBUTION
                 Cross Section                         Shear Stress Profile τ(y)
             ┌──────────────────┐  y = +h/2             ──► τ = 0 (Free Top)
             │   Area A'        │                        ╱
             ├── ── ── ── ── ── ┤ y = y_1               │
             │   Neutral Axis   │  y = 0               ────► τ_max = 1.5 τ_avg (Max at NA)
             │                  │                       │
             └──────────────────┘  y = −h/2             ──► τ = 0 (Free Bottom)
```

1. **The Jourawski Transverse Shear Formula:**
   $$\boxed{\tau(y) = \frac{V Q(y)}{I b(y)}}$$
   - $V$: Transverse shear force at the cross-section ($\text{N}$).
   - $Q(y) \equiv \int_y^{y_{\max}} \eta \, dA = A' \bar{y}'$: First moment of the area above level $y$ about the neutral axis ($\text{m}^3$ or $\text{mm}^3$).
   - $I$: Second moment of area about the neutral axis ($\text{m}^4$ or $\text{mm}^4$).
   - $b(y)$: Width of the cross-section at level $y$ ($\text{m}$ or $\text{mm}$).

2. **Standard Cross-Sections:**
   - **Solid Rectangle ($b \times h$):**
     $$\tau(y) = \frac{6V}{b h^3}\left(\frac{h^2}{4} - y^2\right) \implies \boxed{\tau_{\max} = \frac{3}{2}\frac{V}{A} = 1.5 \tau_{\text{avg}}} \quad (\text{at } y = 0)$$
   - **Solid Circle (Diameter $d$):**
     $$\boxed{\tau_{\max} = \frac{4}{3}\frac{V}{A} \approx 1.333 \tau_{\text{avg}}} \quad (\text{at } y = 0)$$
   - **Symmetric I-Beam:**
     - Web carries **$>90\text{--}95\%$** of the total transverse shear force: $\mathbf{V_{\text{web}} \approx V}$.
     - Average web shear stress: $\mathbf{\tau_{\text{web,avg}} \approx \frac{V}{t_w h_w}}$.

### Step 3 — Shear Flow ($q$) in Open Thin-Walled Sections (25 min)

Shear flow $q$ is the shear force per unit length along the wall contour ($s$):

$$\boxed{q(s) \equiv \tau(s) \cdot t(s) = \frac{V Q(s)}{I} \quad (\text{Units: N/m or N/mm})}$$

```
                    SHEAR FLOW IN THIN-WALLED OPEN C-CHANNEL
                         s_1 ──► q_flange(s) = Linear (0 at free tip)
                        ┌────────────────────────┐
                        │
                        │ ▲
                        │ │ q_web(s) = Parabolic (Max at Neutral Axis)
                        │ │
                        │
                        └────────────────────────┘
                         ◄── s_2 q_flange(s) = Linear (0 at free tip)
```

1. **General Shear Flow Equation for Open Sections:**
   For a thin-walled section with coordinate contour $s$ measured from a free edge:

   $$\boxed{q(s) = q_0 - \left(\frac{V_y I_y - V_x I_{xy}}{I_x I_y - I_{xy}^2}\right)\int_0^s y(s) \, t \, ds - \left(\frac{V_x I_x - V_y I_{xy}}{I_x I_y - I_{xy}^2}\right)\int_0^s x(s) \, t \, ds}$$

2. **Symmetrical Section ($I_{xy} = 0$) under Vertical Shear $V_y$ ($V_x = 0$):**
   $$\boxed{q(s) = q_0 - \frac{V_y}{I_x}\int_0^s y(s) \, t \, ds}$$

3. **Key Boundary & Variation Rules:**
   - **Free Edge Rule:** At any open end of a cross-section, the shear flow is **STRICTLY ZERO**: $\mathbf{q_0 = 0}$.
   - **Flanges parallel to $x$-axis ($y = \text{const}$):**
     $$q_{\text{flange}}(s) = -\frac{V_y y_{\text{flange}} t}{I_x} s \propto s \quad (\mathbf{\text{Varies strictly LINEARLY}})$$
   - **Webs parallel to $y$-axis ($y = \text{variable}$):**
     $$q_{\text{web}}(s) = q_{\text{flange,end}} - \frac{V_y t}{I_x}\int (h/2 - s) ds \quad (\mathbf{\text{Varies PARABOLICALLY}})$$

### Step 4 — Traps & Self-Check (10 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Shear Stress vs. Shear Flow at Flange-Web Junction**
> While shear flow $q$ is continuous around corners, shear stress $\mathbf{\tau = q/t}$ jumps abruptly if the flange thickness $t_f$ differs from the web thickness $t_w$!
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Maximum Parabolic Shear Flow Location**
> In a vertical web, the maximum shear flow occurs **EXACTLY AT THE INTERSECTION WITH THE NEUTRAL AXIS** ($y = 0$).

### Step 3 — D1 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** In a rectangular cross-section beam of depth $h$ subjected to a vertical shear force $V$, the maximum transverse shear stress occurs at:
> **(A)** The top surface ($y = +h/2$) **(B)** The bottom surface ($y = -h/2$) **(C)** The neutral axis ($y = 0$) **(D)** $y = h/4$

> **Q2 (1 mark):** For a solid circular cross-section beam subjected to vertical shear force $V$, the ratio of maximum shear stress $\tau_{\max}$ to average shear stress $\tau_{\text{avg}}$ is:
> **(A)** $1.25$ **(B)** $1.33$ **(C)** $1.50$ **(D)** $2.00$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A rectangular cantilever beam of width $b = 50\text{ mm}$ and depth $h = 100\text{ mm}$ carries a concentrated load $V = 10.0\text{ kN}$ at its free tip. The maximum transverse shear stress developed in the beam (in $\text{MPa}$) is:
> **(A)** $2.0\text{ MPa}$ **(B)** $3.0\text{ MPa}$ **(C)** $4.0\text{ MPa}$ **(D)** $5.0\text{ MPa}$

> **Q4 (2 marks):** A thin-walled C-channel has web height $h = 120\text{ mm}$, flange width $b = 40\text{ mm}$, thickness $t = 2.0\text{ mm}$, and second moment of area $I_x = 3.6\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4$. When subjected to vertical shear $V_y = 12.0\text{ kN}$, the shear flow $q$ at the junction between top flange and web (in $\text{N/mm}$) is:
> **(A)** $8.0\text{ N/mm}$ **(B)** $12.0\text{ N/mm}$ **(C)** $16.0\text{ N/mm}$ **(D)** $24.0\text{ N/mm}$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A solid square beam of side length $a = 60\text{ mm}$ carries a transverse shear force $V = 14.4\text{ kN}$. The maximum shear stress $\tau_{\max}$ (in $\text{MPa}$) is ___ $\text{MPa}$.

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D1 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (C) The neutral axis (y = 0)**
> Parabolic profile with peak at neutral axis.
>
> **Q2: (B) 1.33**
> $\tau_{\max} = \frac{4}{3}\tau_{\text{avg}} \approx 1.333$.
>
> **Q3: (B) 3.0 MPa**
> $A = 50 \times 100 = 5000\text{ mm}^2$.
> $\tau_{\text{avg}} = \frac{10000\text{ N}}{5000\text{ mm}^2} = 2.0\text{ MPa}$.
> $\tau_{\max} = 1.5 \tau_{\text{avg}} = 1.5(2.0) = \mathbf{3.0\text{ MPa}}$.
>
> **Q4: (C) 16.0 N/mm**
> Flange area $A' = b \cdot t = 40 \times 2.0 = 80\text{ mm}^2$.
> Flange centroid from NA: $y = h/2 = 60\text{ mm}$.
> $Q = A' y = 80 \times 60 = 4800\text{ mm}^3$.
> $q = \frac{V_y Q}{I_x} = \frac{(12000\text{ N})(4800\text{ mm}^3)}{3.6\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4} = \frac{5.76\times 10^7}{3.6\times 10^6} = \mathbf{16.0\text{ N/mm}}$.
>
> **Q5: 6.0**
> $A = 60^2 = 3600\text{ mm}^2$. $\tau_{\text{avg}} = \frac{14400}{3600} = 4.0\text{ MPa}$.
> $\tau_{\max} = 1.5(4.0) = \mathbf{6.0\text{ MPa}}$.
