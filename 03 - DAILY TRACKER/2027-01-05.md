## ⏰ HOUR 1 (0:00 – 1:00): 38-MARK APPLICATION SCAN & INVARIANTS

### Step 1 — Flight Mechanics & Space Dynamics Invariants (30 min)
1. **Thrust & Power Required:**
   - Thrust Required: $T_R = D = \frac{1}{2}\rho V_\infty^2 S C_{D0} + \frac{2 K W^2}{\rho V_\infty^2 S}$.
   - Minimum Drag Condition: $C_{Di} = C_{D0} \implies (L/D)_{\max} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{K C_{D0}}}, \ V_{\text{md}} = \sqrt{\frac{2 W}{\rho S \sqrt{C_{D0}/K}}}$.
   - Minimum Power Condition (Propeller): $C_{Di} = 3 C_{D0} \implies (C_L^{3/2}/C_D)_{\max} = \frac{3^{3/4}}{4 K^{3/4} C_{D0}^{1/4}}, \ V_{\text{mp}} = \frac{V_{\text{md}}}{3^{1/4}} \approx 0.76 V_{\text{md}}$.
2. **Climb & Gliding:**
   - Rate of climb: $R/C = \frac{T - D}{W} V_\infty = \frac{P_A - P_R}{W}$.
   - Max Glide Range: $R_{\text{glide}} = h (L/D)_{\max}$ (Independent of aircraft weight!).
3. **Maneuver & Stability:**
   - Coordinated Turn: $n = \frac{1}{\cos\phi}, \ R = \frac{V^2}{g\sqrt{n^2 - 1}}, \ \omega = \frac{g\sqrt{n^2 - 1}}{V}$.
   - Static Margin: $\text{SM} = \frac{x_{\text{NP}} - x_{\text{cg}}}{\bar{c}} = -\frac{dC_m}{dC_L}$.
4. **Orbital Mechanics:**
   - Specific energy: $\mathcal{E} = -\frac{\mu}{2a} = \frac{v^2}{2} - \frac{\mu}{r}, \ v = \sqrt{\mu\left(\frac{2}{r} - \frac{1}{a}\right)}$.
   - Orbital period: $T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{a^3}{\mu}}$.
   - Hohmann Transfer: $\Delta v_{\text{total}} = |v_{t1} - v_{c1}| + |v_{c2} - v_{t2}|$.

### Step 2 — Propulsion & Rocketry Invariants (30 min)
1. **Gas Dynamics & Nozzles:**
   - Isentropic Stagnation: $\frac{T_0}{T} = 1 + \frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2, \ \frac{p_0}{p} = (1 + \frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2)^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}}$.
   - Critical Pressure Ratio (Choking): $\frac{p^*}{p_0} = \left(\frac{2}{\gamma+1}\right)^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}} = 0.5283$ (for $\gamma = 1.4$).
2. **Air-Breathing Engines:**
   - Brayton Cycle: $\eta_{\text{thermal}} = 1 - \frac{1}{r_p^{(\gamma-1)/\gamma}}$.
   - Net Uninstalled Thrust: $F = \dot{m}_a [(1+f)V_e - V_0] + (p_e - p_a)A_e$.
   - Compressor/Turbine: $T_{02} = T_{01}(r_p)^{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma}}, \ W_{\text{turbine}} = W_{\text{compressor}} \implies \dot{m}_a c_p (T_{03} - T_{04}) = \dot{m}_a c_p (T_{02} - T_{01})$.
3. **Rocket Propulsion:**
   - Rocket Thrust: $F = \dot{m} V_e + (p_e - p_a)A_e = \dot{m} c = C_F P_c A_t$.
   - Characteristic Velocity: $c^* = \frac{P_c A_t}{\dot{m}}$.
   - Tsiolkovsky Equation: $\Delta v = I_{\text{sp}} g_0 \ln\left(\frac{m_0}{m_f}\right) = c \ln\left(\frac{m_0}{m_f}\right)$.

### Step 3 — D1 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** In steady unaccelerated level flight of a jet aircraft, minimum thrust required corresponds to flight at:
> **(A)** $(C_L/C_D)_{\max}$ **(B)** $(C_L^{1/2}/C_D)_{\max}$ **(C)** $(C_L^{3/2}/C_D)_{\max}$ **(D)** $(C_L^2/C_D)_{\max}$

> **Q2 (1 mark):** In an ideal converging-diverging nozzle, if the pressure ratio $p_0/p_a$ is sufficient to choke the throat, the Mach number at the throat is:
> **(A)** $0.5$ **(B)** $0.8$ **(C)** $1.0$ **(D)** Greater than $1.0$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** An aircraft in a coordinated level turn has bank angle $\phi = 60^\circ$ and airspeed $V = 140\text{ m/s}$ ($g = 9.81\text{ m/s}^2$). The turn rate $\omega$ (in $\text{rad/s}$) is:
> **(A)** $0.061\text{ rad/s}$ **(B)** $0.121\text{ rad/s}$ **(C)** $0.173\text{ rad/s}$ **(D)** $0.242\text{ rad/s}$

> **Q4 (2 marks):** A satellite in a circular orbit around Earth ($\mu = 3.986\times 10^5\text{ km}^3/\text{s}^2$) has orbital speed $v = 7.50\text{ km/s}$. The radius of the orbit $r$ (in $\text{km}$) is:
> **(A)** $6678\text{ km}$ **(B)** $7086\text{ km}$ **(C)** $8420\text{ km}$ **(D)** $9120\text{ km}$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A rocket engine produces thrust $F = 100.0\text{ kN}$ at chamber pressure $P_c = 5.0\text{ MPa}$ and throat area $A_t = 0.0125\text{ m}^2$. The thrust coefficient $C_F$ is ___ (round off to 1 decimal place).

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D1 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (A) (C_L / C_D)_max**
> Minimum drag occurs at maximum lift-to-drag ratio $(L/D)_{\max}$.
>
> **Q2: (C) 1.0**
> Choked sonic throat condition.
>
> **Q3: (B) 0.121 rad/s**
> $\sqrt{n^2 - 1} = \tan(60^\circ) = \sqrt{3} \approx 1.732$.
> $\omega = \frac{g\sqrt{n^2 - 1}}{V} = \frac{9.81 \times 1.732}{140} = \frac{16.99}{140} \approx \mathbf{0.1214\text{ rad/s} \approx 0.121\text{ rad/s}}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) 7086 km**
> $r = \frac{\mu}{v^2} = \frac{3.986\times 10^5}{7.50^2} = \frac{398600}{56.25} \approx \mathbf{7086.2\text{ km} \approx 7086\text{ km}}$.
>
> **Q5: 1.6**
> $C_F = \frac{F}{P_c A_t} = \frac{100,000\text{ N}}{(5.0\times 10^6\text{ Pa})(0.0125\text{ m}^2)} = \frac{100,000}{62,500} = \mathbf{1.60 \approx 1.6}$.
