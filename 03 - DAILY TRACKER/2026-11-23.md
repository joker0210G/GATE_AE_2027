#### 🔑 Key Formulas & Invariants for Week 4:
1. **Rocket Thrust & Specific Impulse:**
   - Thrust Equation: $\mathbf{F = \dot{m} v_e + (p_e - p_a)A_e = \dot{m} c}$.
   - Effective Exhaust Velocity: $\mathbf{c \equiv v_e + \frac{p_e - p_a}{\dot{m}}A_e}$.
   - Specific Impulse: $\mathbf{I_{\text{sp}} \equiv \frac{F}{\dot{m} g_0} = \frac{c}{g_0}}$ ($\text{seconds}$).
2. **Characteristic Velocity ($c^*$) & Thrust Coefficient ($C_F$):**
   - Characteristic Velocity (Chamber performance): $\mathbf{c^* \equiv \frac{p_0 A_t}{\dot{m}} = \frac{\sqrt{\gamma R T_0}}{\gamma \sqrt{\left(\frac{2}{\gamma+1}\right)^{\frac{\gamma+1}{\gamma-1}}}}}$.
   - Thrust Coefficient (Nozzle performance): $\mathbf{C_F \equiv \frac{F}{p_0 A_t}}$.
   - Fundamental Link: $\mathbf{c = c^* \cdot C_F \implies I_{\text{sp}} = \frac{c^* C_F}{g_0}}$.
3. **The Tsiolkovsky Rocket Equation & Multi-Staging:**
   - Single Stage: $\mathbf{\Delta v = c \ln\left(\frac{m_0}{m_f}\right) = I_{\text{sp}} g_0 \ln\left(\frac{m_0}{m_f}\right)}$.
   - Multi-Stage Rockets: Payload ratio $\lambda \equiv m_L / m_0$, Structural coefficient $\epsilon \equiv m_s / (m_s + m_p)$.
   - Multi-Stage Total $\Delta v$: $\mathbf{\Delta v_{\text{total}} = \sum_{i=1}^N c_i \ln\left(\frac{1 + \lambda_i}{\epsilon_i + \lambda_i}\right)}$.
