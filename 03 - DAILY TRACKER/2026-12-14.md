#### 🔑 Key Formulas & Invariants for Week 3:
1. **Shear Stress & Shear Flow in Solid / Open Beams:**
   - Solid beam shear stress: $\mathbf{\tau(y) = \frac{V Q}{I b}}$ where $Q \equiv \int_y^{y_{\max}} \eta \, dA = A' \bar{y}'$.
   - Thin-walled open section shear flow: $\mathbf{q(s) = q_0 - \frac{V_y}{I_x}\int_0^s y \, t \, ds - \frac{V_x}{I_y}\int_0^s x \, t \, ds}$.
   - Free boundary condition: $q = 0$ at all free open edges.
2. **Shear Center ($e_x, e_y$):**
   - The point through which the resultant shear force must act to produce **PURE BENDING WITHOUT TORSION / TWISTING**.
   - Symmetry property: The shear center **ALWAYS LIES ON ANY AXIS OF SYMMETRY**.
   - C-Channel section ($b \times h \times t$): $\mathbf{e = \frac{b^2 h^2 t}{4 I_x} = \frac{3 b}{6 + h/b}}$.
   - Intersecting thin-walled elements (Angle, T-section, Cruciform): Shear center lies **EXACTLY AT THE INTERSECTION POINT** ($\mathbf{e = 0}$).
   - Thin semicircular slit tube of radius $r$: $\mathbf{e = \frac{4 r}{\pi}}$.
3. **Torsion of Thin-Walled Closed Tubes (Bredt-Batho Theory):**
   - Constant shear flow around cell: $\mathbf{q = \frac{T}{2 A_m}}$ ($A_m$ = Enclosed area by median line).
   - Maximum shear stress: $\mathbf{\tau_{\max} = \frac{q}{t_{\min}} = \frac{T}{2 A_m t_{\min}}}$.
   - Rate of twist: $\mathbf{\theta = \frac{d\phi}{dz} = \frac{T}{4 A_m^2 G} \oint \frac{ds}{t} = \frac{q}{2 A_m G}\oint \frac{ds}{t}}$.
   - Torsional constant: $\mathbf{J = \frac{4 A_m^2}{\oint \frac{ds}{t}}}$.
4. **Euler Column Buckling:**
   - Critical Buckling Load: $\mathbf{P_{\text{cr}} = \frac{\pi^2 E I_{\min}}{L_e^2}}$.
   - Critical Buckling Stress: $\mathbf{\sigma_{\text{cr}} = \frac{\pi^2 E}{\lambda^2}}$ where slenderness ratio $\mathbf{\lambda \equiv \frac{L_e}{r_{\min}} = \frac{L_e}{\sqrt{I_{\min}/A}}}$.
   - Effective Lengths ($L_e$):
     - Both ends pinned: $\mathbf{L_e = L}$
     - One fixed, one free (cantilever column): $\mathbf{L_e = 2 L}$ ($P_{\text{cr}} = \frac{\pi^2 EI}{4 L^2}$)
     - Both ends fixed: $\mathbf{L_e = 0.5 L}$ ($P_{\text{cr}} = \frac{4 \pi^2 EI}{L^2} = 4\times$ pinned!)
     - One fixed, one pinned: $\mathbf{L_e \approx 0.707 L = \frac{L}{\sqrt{2}}}$ ($P_{\text{cr}} \approx \frac{2 \pi^2 EI}{L^2}$).
