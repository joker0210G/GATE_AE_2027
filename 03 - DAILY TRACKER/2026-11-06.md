### Step 2 — Intakes / Inlets & Diffusers Aerothermodynamics (20 min)
Open `02 - Aircraft Engine Types and Performance`.

The intake decelerates incoming freestream air ($V_0, M_0$) to compressor face condition ($M_2 \approx 0.4\text{ to } 0.5$) with maximum stagnation pressure recovery:

```
                    SUBSONIC & SUPERSONIC INTAKES
        1. Subsonic Intake (Diverging Duct dA > 0):
           Freestream M_0 < 1 ──► Compressor Face M_2 ≈ 0.4–0.5

        2. Supersonic Pitot Intake (M_0 > 1):
           Normal Shock Ahead ──► Subsonic Diffuser (Large Δp_0 Loss!)

        3. Supersonic Multi-Shock Spike Intake (M_0 > 1):
           Multiple Oblique Shocks ──► Weak Normal Shock ──► Subsonic Diffuser (High π_d)
```

1. **Ram Total Pressure Recovery Ratio ($\pi_d$):**
   $$\boxed{\pi_d \equiv \frac{p_{02}}{p_{00}} \le 1.0}$$
   - For an ideal isentropic intake: $\pi_d = 1.0 \implies p_{02} = p_{00}$.
2. **Intake Isentropic Efficiency ($\eta_d$):**
   $$\boxed{\eta_d \equiv \frac{h_{02s} - h_0}{h_{02} - h_0} = \frac{T_0\left(\pi_d^{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma}} - 1\right)}{T_0\left(\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M_0^2\right)} = \frac{\pi_d^{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma}} - 1}{\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M_0^2}}$$
   $$\implies \boxed{\pi_d = \left(1 + \eta_d \frac{\gamma-1}{2}M_0^2\right)^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}} \cdot \frac{1}{\left(1 + \frac{\gamma-1}{2}M_0^2\right)^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}}}}$$
3. **Stagnation Temperature across Intake:**
   $$\mathbf{T_{02} = T_{00} = T_0\left(1 + \frac{\gamma-1}{2}M_0^2\right) \quad (\text{Strictly Constant in ANY Adiabatic Intake!})}$$

### Step 3 — Gas Turbine Combustor Aerothermodynamics (25 min)
Open `03 - Gas Turbine Combustors`.

```
                    COMBUSTOR AIRFLOW ARCHITECTURE
          Compressor Discharge Air (ṁ_a, T_03, p_03)
                             │
     ┌───────────────────────┼───────────────────────┐
     ▼                       ▼                       ▼
  PRIMARY ZONE            SECONDARY ZONE          DILUTION ZONE
(15–20% Airflow)         (15–20% Airflow)        (60–70% Airflow)
• Fuel injection         • Drops local temp      • Lowers mixed gas
• Flame holder vortex    • Completes burning       temp to Turbine
• Stoichiometric φ ≈ 1.0   of CO & UHC             Inlet Temp (TIT T_04)
```

1. **Combustor Energy Balance (Fuel-to-Air Ratio $f \equiv \dot{m}_f / \dot{m}_a$):**
   $$\dot{m}_a h_{03} + \dot{m}_f \text{LHV} \cdot \eta_b = (\dot{m}_a + \dot{m}_f) h_{04}$$
   $$\boxed{f \equiv \frac{\dot{m}_f}{\dot{m}_a} = \frac{c_{pg} T_{04} - c_{pa} T_{03}}{\text{LHV}\cdot\eta_b - c_{pg} T_{04}} \approx \frac{c_p(T_{04} - T_{03})}{\text{LHV}\cdot\eta_b}}$$
   - $\text{LHV}$: Lower Heating Value of aviation kerosene ($\approx 43.0 \times 10^6\text{ J/kg} = 43\text{ MJ/kg}$).
   - $\eta_b$: Combustor combustion efficiency ($\approx 0.98\text{ to } 0.99$).
   - Typical values: $f \approx 0.015\text{ to } 0.030$ ($A/F = 1/f \approx 33\text{ to } 66$).
2. **Equivalence Ratio ($\phi$):**
   $$\boxed{\phi \equiv \frac{(f)_{\text{actual}}}{(f)_{\text{stoichiometric}}} = \frac{(A/F)_{\text{stoich}}}{(A/F)_{\text{actual}}}}$$
   - $\phi = 1.0$: Stoichiometric ($f_{\text{stoich}} \approx 0.068 \implies A/F \approx 14.7$).
   - $\phi < 1.0$: Lean mixture (Overall gas turbine engine operates very lean $\phi_{\text{overall}} \approx 0.3$).
   - $\phi > 1.0$: Rich mixture.
3. **Total Pressure Loss in Combustor ($\Delta p_{0b} / p_{03}$):**
   $$\frac{\Delta p_{0b}}{p_{03}} \approx 2\%\text{ to } 5\%$$
   - **Rayleigh (Thermal) Loss:** Fundamental total pressure drop caused by heat addition to a moving compressible stream ($q > 0 \implies \Delta p_0 < 0$).
   - **Frictional / Viscous Loss:** Turbulence, liner skin friction, and swirlers.

### Step 4 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Combustor Temperature Invariant**
> Total temperature $T_{04} > T_{03}$ INCREASES across the burner, but stagnation pressure $p_{04} < p_{03}$ **ALWAYS DECREASES**!
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Mass Flow with Fuel Addition**
> Turbine mass flow rate is $\dot{m}_{\text{turbine}} = \dot{m}_a + \dot{m}_f = \dot{m}_a(1 + f)$. Do not neglect $(1+f)$ in high-precision GATE 2-mark questions!

### Step 3 — D4 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** In a gas turbine combustor, the primary purpose of the dilution zone is to:
> **(A)** Provide stoichiometric conditions for ignition
> **(B)** Mix cooling air with combustion gases to lower temperature to the permissible turbine inlet temperature
> **(C)** Increase stagnation pressure
> **(D)** Accelerate the flow to supersonic speed

> **Q2 (1 mark):** Across an adiabatic supersonic intake with shock waves, the stagnation temperature $T_0$ and stagnation pressure $p_0$ behave as:
> **(A)** $T_0$ increases, $p_0$ decreases
> **(B)** $T_0$ remains constant, $p_0$ decreases
> **(C)** Both $T_0$ and $p_0$ remain constant
> **(D)** Both $T_0$ and $p_0$ decrease

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A turbojet combustor receives air at $T_{03} = 650\text{ K}$ and discharges gas at $T_{04} = 1450\text{ K}$. If $c_p = 1.05\text{ kJ/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}$, fuel lower heating value $\text{LHV} = 42\text{ MJ/kg}$, and combustion efficiency $\eta_b = 0.99$, the fuel-to-air ratio $f$ is:
> **(A)** $0.0102$ **(B)** $0.0152$ **(C)** $0.0202$ **(D)** $0.0252$

> **Q4 (2 marks):** An aircraft flies at Mach $M_0 = 2.0$ where ambient temperature is $T_0 = 225\text{ K}$ ($\gamma = 1.4$). The total temperature at the compressor entry $T_{02}$ (in $\text{K}$) is:
> **(A)** $225\text{ K}$ **(B)** $315\text{ K}$ **(C)** $405\text{ K}$ **(D)** $450\text{ K}$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A combustor burns fuel with stoichiometric air-to-fuel ratio $(A/F)_{\text{stoich}} = 14.7$. If the engine operates with an actual airflow $\dot{m}_a = 40.0\text{ kg/s}$ and fuel flow $\dot{m}_f = 0.80\text{ kg/s}$, the equivalence ratio $\phi$ is ___ (round off to 3 decimal places).

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D4 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) Mix cooling air to lower temperature to TIT**
> Dilution air (~60% of total airflow) brings gas temperature down to metallurgically safe limits for turbine blades.
>
> **Q2: (B) T_0 remains constant, p_0 decreases**
> Adiabatic flow preserves $T_0$ ($h_0 = \text{const}$), while shock wave irreversibility destroys stagnation pressure ($\Delta s > 0 \implies \Delta p_0 < 0$).
>
> **Q3: (C) 0.0202**
> $f \approx \frac{c_p(T_{04} - T_{03})}{\text{LHV}\cdot\eta_b} = \frac{1050(1450 - 650)}{42 \times 10^6 \times 0.99} = \frac{1050(800)}{41.58 \times 10^6} = \frac{840000}{41580000} \approx \mathbf{0.02020 \approx 0.0202}$.
>
> **Q4: (C) 405 K**
> $T_{02} = T_{00} = T_0(1 + 0.2 M_0^2) = 225(1 + 0.2(4)) = 225(1.8) = \mathbf{405\text{ K}}$.
>
> **Q5: 0.294**
> Actual $A/F = \frac{40.0}{0.80} = 50.0$.
> $\phi = \frac{(A/F)_{\text{stoich}}}{(A/F)_{\text{actual}}} = \frac{14.7}{50.0} = \mathbf{0.294}$.
