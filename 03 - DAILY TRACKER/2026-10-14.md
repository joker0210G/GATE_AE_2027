### Step 2 — Coordinated Level Turn Dynamics (20 min)
Open `03.6b - Turning Flight Performance`.

```
                    COORDINATED LEVEL TURN FORCE BALANCE
                                 L ^  (Tilted by Bank Angle φ)
                                   │ /
                                   │/  L cos φ = W
                                   ┼──────────────► L sin φ = m V² / R (Centripetal)
                                   │
                                   ▼ W (Weight)
                Load Factor: n = L / W = 1 / cos φ ⟹ cos φ = 1 / n
                tan φ = (L sin φ) / (L cos φ) = (m V² / R) / W = V² / (g R)
                Since cos φ = 1/n ⟹ tan φ = √(n² − 1)
```

#### 🔑 The 4 Golden Turn Equations:
1. **Load Factor ($n$):**
   $$\boxed{n = \frac{1}{\cos\phi}}$$
   *(For $\phi = 60^\circ \implies \cos(60^\circ) = 0.5 \implies \mathbf{n = 2.0\text{ g}}$).*
2. **Bank Angle Relation:**
   $$\boxed{\tan\phi = \frac{V^2}{g R} = \sqrt{n^2 - 1}}$$
3. **Turn Radius ($R$):**
   $$\boxed{R = \frac{V^2}{g \tan\phi} = \frac{V^2}{g \sqrt{n^2 - 1}}}$$
4. **Turn Rate ($\omega = \dot{\psi}$ in rad/s):**
   $$\boxed{\omega = \frac{V}{R} = \frac{g \tan\phi}{V} = \frac{g \sqrt{n^2 - 1}}{V}}$$

---

### Step 3 — Turn Optimization & Corner Velocity ($V^*$) (20 min)

To achieve maximum turn performance (Minimum turn radius $R_{\min}$ and Maximum turn rate $\omega_{\max}$), an aircraft must simultaneously operate at its aerodynamic limit ($C_{L,\max}$) and structural limit ($n_{\max}$).

```
                             THE CORNER SPEED V*
         Load Factor n
                n_max ┌──────────┐ (Corner Speed V*)
                      │       ▲  │ (R_min & ω_max achieved HERE!)
                      │    ╭──╯  │
                      │  ╭─╯     │  n = (1/2 ρ V² S C_L,max) / W
                  1 g ├─╯        │
                      └──────────┴────────────────► Velocity V
                                 V*
```

1. **Corner Velocity / Maneuvering Speed ($V^*$):**
   $$\boxed{V^* = \sqrt{\frac{2 n_{\max} (W/S)}{\rho C_{L,\max}}} = V_{\text{stall}} \sqrt{n_{\max}}}$$
2. **Minimum Turn Radius ($R_{\min}$):**
   $$\boxed{R_{\min} = \frac{(V^*)^2}{g \sqrt{n_{\max}^2 - 1}} = \frac{2 (W/S)}{\rho g C_{L,\max}} \frac{n_{\max}}{\sqrt{n_{\max}^2 - 1}}}$$
3. **Maximum Turn Rate ($\omega_{\max}$):**
   $$\boxed{\omega_{\max} = \frac{g \sqrt{n_{\max}^2 - 1}}{V^*} = g \sqrt{\frac{\rho C_{L,\max}}{2 (W/S)}} \frac{\sqrt{n_{\max}^2 - 1}}{\sqrt{n_{\max}}}}$$

### Step 4 — High-Lift Devices: Flaps vs. Slats (10 min)
Open `02.3 - High-Lift Devices`.

```
                    EFFECTS OF HIGH-LIFT DEVICES ON C_L-α
          C_L ^
              │                ╭───╮ Leading-Edge Slats (Delays stall, shifts α_stall right)
              │             ╭──╯   ╰──
              │   Flaps  ╭──╯ ╭───╮ Clean Wing
              │  ╭───────╯ ╭──╯   ╰──
              │  │      ╭──╯
              │  │   ╭──╯
              └──┴───┴────────────────► α
```

| High-Lift Device | Working Mechanism | Effect on $C_{L,\max}$ | Effect on $\alpha_{\text{stall}}$ | Effect on $\alpha_{L=0}$ | Effect on $C_{m,c/4}$ |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Trailing-Edge Flaps**<br>*(Plain, Split, Slotted, Fowler)* | Increases effective camber & wing area (Fowler) | **Large Increase ($\uparrow\uparrow$)** | **Decreases ($\downarrow$)** (Stalls earlier in $\alpha$) | **Shifts Negative ($\Delta\alpha_{L=0} < 0$)** | **Strong Nose-Down ($\Delta C_m < 0$)** |
| **Leading-Edge Slats / Slots** | Delays boundary layer separation via energizing jet | **Large Increase ($\uparrow\uparrow$)** | **Increases ($\uparrow\uparrow$)** (Stalls at higher $\alpha$) | **Unchanged ($\Delta\alpha_{L=0} \approx 0$)** | **Negligible change** |

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Turn Radius at High Load Factors**
> As $n \to \infty$, $\frac{n}{\sqrt{n^2-1}} \to 1.0$. Thus $R_{\min} \approx \frac{2(W/S)}{\rho g C_{L,\max}}$. Lower wing loading $W/S$ gives tighter turns!
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Flap Pitching Moment Direction**
> Deploying trailing-edge flaps creates a strong **NOSE-DOWN (Negative)** pitching moment $\Delta C_{m,c/4} < 0$, requiring up-elevator trim.

### Step 3 — D2 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** In a coordinated level turn at a bank angle $\phi = 60^\circ$, the load factor $n$ is:
> **(A)** $1.15$ **(B)** $1.41$ **(C)** $2.00$ **(D)** $2.50$

> **Q2 (2 marks):** An aircraft flying at $V = 150\text{ m/s}$ performs a coordinated level turn with a load factor $n = 3.0$. Taking $g = 9.81\text{ m/s}^2$, the turn radius $R$ (in meters) is:
> **(A)** $542\text{ m}$ **(B)** $811\text{ m}$ **(C)** $1146\text{ m}$ **(D)** $1532\text{ m}$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** An aircraft executes a coordinated level turn with bank angle $\phi = 45^\circ$ at a velocity $V = 120\text{ m/s}$ ($g = 9.81\text{ m/s}^2$). The rate of turn $\omega$ (in $\text{deg/s}$) is:
> **(A)** $2.34\text{ deg/s}$ **(B)** $4.68\text{ deg/s}$ **(C)** $6.75\text{ deg/s}$ **(D)** $9.36\text{ deg/s}$

> **Q4 (1 mark):** The deployment of trailing-edge flaps on a clean wing results in:
> **(A)** Increased stall angle and nose-up pitching moment
> **(B)** Increased zero-lift angle of attack and reduced drag
> **(C)** Shift of zero-lift angle of attack to a more negative value and nose-down pitching moment
> **(D)** Constant lift curve with reduced skin friction

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A fighter aircraft with wing loading $W/S = 3000\text{ N/m}^2$ and $C_{L,\max} = 1.5$ operates in air of density $\rho = 1.0\text{ kg/m}^3$. If the structural limit load factor is $n_{\max} = 9.0$, the corner speed $V^*$ (in $\text{m/s}$) is ___.

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D2 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (C) 2.00**
> $n = \frac{1}{\cos(60^\circ)} = \frac{1}{0.5} = \mathbf{2.00}$.
>
> **Q2: (B) 811 m**
> $R = \frac{V^2}{g\sqrt{n^2-1}} = \frac{150^2}{9.81\sqrt{3^2 - 1}} = \frac{22500}{9.81\sqrt{8}} = \frac{22500}{9.81(2.8284)} = \frac{22500}{27.747} \approx \mathbf{810.9\text{ m} \approx 811\text{ m}}$.
>
> **Q3: (B) 4.68 deg/s**
> $\omega = \frac{g\tan(45^\circ)}{V} = \frac{9.81(1)}{120} = 0.08175\text{ rad/s}$.
> In deg/s: $\omega = 0.08175 \times \frac{180}{\pi} \approx \mathbf{4.68\text{ deg/s}}$.
>
> **Q4: (C)**
> Flaps increase camber $\implies \Delta\alpha_{L=0} < 0$ (more negative) and produce a nose-down moment $\Delta C_{m,c/4} < 0$.
>
> **Q5: 189.7**
> $V^* = \sqrt{\frac{2 n_{\max} (W/S)}{\rho C_{L,\max}}} = \sqrt{\frac{2(9.0)(3000)}{1.0(1.5)}} = \sqrt{\frac{54000}{1.5}} = \sqrt{36000} \approx \mathbf{189.7\text{ m/s}}$.
