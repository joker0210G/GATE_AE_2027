**BLOCK A: Direct Concept & Formula Recall (1 Mark Each, Q1–Q10)**

**Q1.** In the ISA troposphere ($0 \le h \le 11\text{ km}$), the temperature lapse rate $L = -dT/dh$ is:
(A) $9.8\text{ K/km}$ (B) $6.5\text{ K/km}$ (C) $4.5\text{ K/km}$ (D) $0.0\text{ K/km}$

**Q2.** As an aircraft climbs to higher altitudes at a constant Equivalent Airspeed (EAS), the True Airspeed (TAS):
(A) Decreases (B) Increases (C) Remains constant (D) Drops to zero

**Q3.** In steady level flight, minimum thrust required (minimum drag) occurs when:
(A) Parasite drag equals induced drag ($C_{D0} = C_{Di}$)
(B) Induced drag is three times parasite drag ($C_{Di} = 3 C_{D0}$)
(C) Parasite drag is three times induced drag ($C_{D0} = 3 C_{Di}$)
(D) Induced drag is zero

**Q4.** In steady level flight, minimum power required occurs when:
(A) $C_{D0} = C_{Di}$ (B) $C_{Di} = 3 C_{D0}$ (C) $C_{D0} = 3 C_{Di}$ (D) $C_L = 0$

**Q5.** As an airplane climbs to higher altitude in steady level flight, the minimum thrust required $T_{R,\min}$:
(A) Increases (B) Decreases (C) Remains strictly constant (D) Doubles

**Q6.** For a jet aircraft with constant thrust, the maximum climb angle $\gamma_{\max}$ occurs at the speed for:
(A) Minimum power required ($V_{mp}$) (B) Minimum thrust required ($V_{md}$) (C) Stall speed ($V_{\text{stall}}$) (D) Maximum speed ($V_{\max}$)

**Q7.** For a propeller aircraft with constant engine power, the maximum rate of climb $R/C_{\max}$ occurs at the speed for:
(A) Minimum drag ($V_{md}$) (B) Minimum power required ($V_{mp}$) (C) Maximum speed ($V_{\max}$) (D) Zero induced drag

**Q8.** In an unpowered steady glide in still air, the maximum horizontal glide distance from a given altitude depends on:
(A) Aircraft weight ($W$) (B) Maximum lift-to-drag ratio $(L/D)_{\max}$ (C) Wing area ($S$) (D) Air density ($\rho$)

**Q9.** For a turbojet-powered aircraft, maximum endurance (maximum flight time) is achieved by flying at:
(A) $(C_L^{1/2} / C_D)_{\max}$ (B) $(L/D)_{\max}$ (C) $(C_L^{3/2} / C_D)_{\max}$ (D) $V_{\max}$

**Q10.** For a propeller-driven aircraft, maximum range (maximum flight distance) is achieved by flying at:
(A) $(L/D)_{\max}$ (B) $(C_L^{1/2} / C_D)_{\max}$ (C) $(C_L^{3/2} / C_D)_{\max}$ (D) $V_{mp}$

**BLOCK B: Standard & Multi-Step Problems (2 Marks Each, Q11–Q25)**

**Q11.** An aircraft flies at an altitude where ambient density is $\rho = 0.5444\text{ kg/m}^3$ ($\rho_0 = 1.225\text{ kg/m}^3$). If the Equivalent Airspeed is $V_{\text{eas}} = 120\text{ m/s}$, the True Airspeed $V_{\text{tas}}$ is:
(A) $80\text{ m/s}$ (B) $120\text{ m/s}$ (C) $180\text{ m/s}$ (D) $240\text{ m/s}$

**Q12.** An aircraft has a parabolic drag polar $C_D = 0.02 + 0.045 C_L^2$. The maximum lift-to-drag ratio $(L/D)_{\max}$ is:
(A) $12.5$ (B) $16.7$ (C) $20.0$ (D) $23.6$

**Q13.** For the same aircraft ($C_D = 0.02 + 0.045 C_L^2$), the lift coefficient for minimum power required $C_{L,mp}$ is:
(A) $0.667$ (B) $1.155$ (C) $0.816$ (D) $1.414$

**Q14.** If an aircraft has a minimum thrust required speed $V_{md} = 90\text{ m/s}$, its speed for minimum power required $V_{mp}$ at the same altitude is:
(A) $52.0\text{ m/s}$ (B) $68.4\text{ m/s}$ (C) $77.9\text{ m/s}$ (D) $118.4\text{ m/s}$

**Q15.** An aircraft of weight $W = 50\text{ kN}$ has $(L/D)_{\max} = 20$. In steady level flight, the minimum thrust required $T_{R,\min}$ is:
(A) $2.5\text{ kN}$ (B) $5.0\text{ kN}$ (C) $10.0\text{ kN}$ (D) $1.25\text{ kN}$

**Q16.** A jet aircraft with weight $W = 60\text{ kN}$ and $(L/D)_{\max} = 15$ has two engines delivering a total thrust $T = 16\text{ kN}$. The maximum climb angle $\gamma_{\max}$ (in degrees) is:
(A) $8.5^\circ$ (B) $11.5^\circ$ (C) $15.0^\circ$ (D) $18.5^\circ$

**Q17.** A propeller aircraft of weight $W = 18\text{ kN}$ has available engine power $P_A = 270\text{ kW}$. If the minimum power required is $P_{R,\min} = 90\text{ kW}$, the maximum rate of climb $R/C_{\max}$ is:
(A) $5\text{ m/s}$ (B) $10\text{ m/s}$ (C) $15\text{ m/s}$ (D) $20\text{ m/s}$

**Q18.** A glider has $(L/D)_{\max} = 28$. If it begins gliding from an altitude of $h = 2500\text{ m}$ in still air, the maximum horizontal distance covered (in $\text{km}$) is:
(A) $50\text{ km}$ (B) $70\text{ km}$ (C) $85\text{ km}$ (D) $100\text{ km}$

**Q19.** If the total weight of a glider is increased by $21\%$ by adding ballast, the maximum glide distance in still air:
(A) Increases by $21\%$ (B) Decreases by $21\%$ (C) Remains unchanged (D) Decreases by $10\%$

**Q20.** A jet aircraft with $(L/D)_{\max} = 18$ and $c_t = 0.6\text{ h}^{-1}$ cruises at maximum endurance. If the fuel fraction is $W_f/W_0 = 0.25$, the flight endurance (in hours) is:
(A) $4.32\text{ h}$ (B) $6.48\text{ h}$ (C) $8.63\text{ h}$ (D) $10.25\text{ h}$

**Q21.** An airplane with $V_{md} = 120\text{ m/s}$ is powered by a turbojet engine. To maximize its range, the aircraft should cruise at a velocity of approximately:
(A) $91\text{ m/s}$ (B) $120\text{ m/s}$ (C) $158\text{ m/s}$ (D) $208\text{ m/s}$

**Q22.** In the ISA troposphere, at an altitude of $h = 5000\text{ m}$, the temperature $T$ is:
(A) $255.65\text{ K}$ (B) $242.65\text{ K}$ (C) $268.65\text{ K}$ (D) $216.65\text{ K}$

**Q23.** An aircraft has $(R/C)_0 = 12\text{ m/s}$ at sea level and absolute ceiling $h_{\text{abs}} = 12,000\text{ m}$. Assuming a linear variation of $R/C$ with altitude, the time taken to climb from sea level to $6,000\text{ m}$ is:
(A) $554.5\text{ s}$ (B) $693.1\text{ s}$ (C) $831.8\text{ s}$ (D) $1000.0\text{ s}$

**Q24.** For an airplane with drag polar $C_D = 0.02 + 0.05 C_L^2$, the maximum value of $(C_L^{3/2}/C_D)$ is:
(A) $10.5$ (B) $13.7$ (C) $15.8$ (D) $18.2$

**Q25.** A propeller aircraft has $\eta_p = 0.85, c_p = 4.5\times10^{-7}\text{ m}^{-1}, (L/D) = 14$. If $W_0/W_1 = 1.35$, the range (in $\text{km}$) is:
(A) $5500\text{ km}$ (B) $7937\text{ km}$ (C) $9200\text{ km}$ (D) $6400\text{ km}$

## ⏰ SESSION 2 (1:45 – 4:00): SCORING + COMPREHENSIVE ERROR REPAIR

### Step 1 — Instant Scoring & Verification (1:45 – 2:15)

> [!faq]- 🔍 View Complete 30Q Answer Key & Worked Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) 6.5 K/km** | Standard ISA troposphere lapse rate.
> **Q2: (B) Increases** | $V_{\text{tas}} = V_{\text{eas}}/\sqrt{\sigma}$. As altitude increases, $\sigma$ decreases $\implies V_{\text{tas}}$ increases.
> **Q3: (A) Parasite drag equals induced drag (C_D0 = C_Di)** | Condition for $(L/D)_{\max}$.
> **Q4: (B) C_Di = 3 C_D0** | Condition for minimum power required and $(C_L^{3/2}/C_D)_{\max}$.
> **Q5: (C) Remains strictly constant** | $T_{R,\min} = 2W\sqrt{KC_{D0}}$ is independent of air density $\rho$.
> **Q6: (B) Speed for minimum thrust required (V_md)** | $\sin\gamma_{\max} = T/W - 1/(L/D)_{\max}$ occurs at $(L/D)_{\max}$.
> **Q7: (B) Speed for minimum power required (V_mp)** | Excess power $(P_A - P_R)$ is maximized where $P_R$ is minimum.
> **Q8: (B) Maximum lift-to-drag ratio (L/D)_max** | $R_{\max} = h(L/D)_{\max}$ is invariant of weight and altitude.
> **Q9: (B) (L/D)_max** | Jet endurance $E = \frac{1}{c_t}(L/D)\ln(W_0/W_1)$ is maximized at $(L/D)_{\max}$.
> **Q10: (A) (L/D)_max** | Propeller range $R = \frac{\eta_p}{c_p}(L/D)\ln(W_0/W_1)$ is maximized at $(L/D)_{\max}$.
>
> **Q11: (C) 180 m/s**
> $\sigma = \frac{0.5444}{1.225} = 0.4444 \implies \sqrt{\sigma} = \frac{2}{3} \approx 0.6667$.
> $V_{\text{tas}} = \frac{120}{2/3} = \mathbf{180\text{ m/s}}$.
>
> **Q12: (B) 16.7**
> $(L/D)_{\max} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{0.045 \times 0.02}} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{0.0009}} = \frac{1}{2(0.03)} = \frac{1}{0.06} = \mathbf{16.67 \approx 16.7}$.
>
> **Q13: (B) 1.155**
> $C_{L,mp} = \sqrt{\frac{3 C_{D0}}{K}} = \sqrt{\frac{3(0.02)}{0.045}} = \sqrt{\frac{0.06}{0.045}} = \sqrt{\frac{4}{3}} = \frac{2}{\sqrt{3}} \approx \mathbf{1.155}$.
>
> **Q14: (B) 68.4 m/s**
> $V_{mp} = \frac{V_{md}}{3^{1/4}} = \frac{90}{1.31607} \approx \mathbf{68.38\text{ m/s} \approx 68.4\text{ m/s}}$.
>
> **Q15: (A) 2.5 kN**
> $T_{R,\min} = \frac{W}{(L/D)_{\max}} = \frac{50\text{ kN}}{20} = \mathbf{2.5\text{ kN}}$.
>
> **Q16: (B) 11.5°**
> $\sin\gamma_{\max} = \frac{T}{W} - \frac{1}{(L/D)_{\max}} = \frac{16}{60} - \frac{1}{15} = 0.2667 - 0.0667 = 0.200$.
> $\gamma_{\max} = \arcsin(0.20) \approx \mathbf{11.54^\circ \approx 11.5^\circ}$.
>
> **Q17: (B) 10 m/s**
> $R/C_{\max} = \frac{P_A - P_{R,\min}}{W} = \frac{270,000 - 90,000}{18,000} = \frac{180,000}{18,000} = \mathbf{10\text{ m/s}}$.
>
> **Q18: (B) 70 km**
> $R_{\max} = h \times (L/D)_{\max} = 2500\text{ m} \times 28 = 70,000\text{ m} = \mathbf{70\text{ km}}$.
>
> **Q19: (C) Remains unchanged**
> Glide distance $R_{\max} = h(L/D)_{\max}$ is completely independent of aircraft weight.
>
> **Q20: (C) 8.63 h**
> $W_1 = 0.75 W_0 \implies W_0/W_1 = 1/0.75 = 1.3333$.
> $E = \frac{18}{0.6}\ln(1.3333) = 30(0.28768) = \mathbf{8.63\text{ hours}}$.
>
> **Q21: (C) 158 m/s**
> For jet range: $V = 3^{1/4} V_{md} = 1.31607(120) \approx \mathbf{157.9\text{ m/s} \approx 158\text{ m/s}}$.
>
> **Q22: (A) 255.65 K**
> $T = 288.15 - 0.0065(5000) = 288.15 - 32.5 = \mathbf{255.65\text{ K}}$.
>
> **Q23: (B) 693.1 s**
> $t = \frac{12000}{12}\ln\left(\frac{12000}{12000 - 6000}\right) = 1000\ln(2) = 1000(0.693147) = \mathbf{693.1\text{ s}}$.
>
> **Q24: (B) 13.7**
> $(C_L^{3/2}/C_D)_{\max} = \frac{3^{3/4}}{4(0.05^{3/4})(0.02^{1/4})} = \frac{2.2795}{4(0.1057)(0.3761)} = \frac{2.2795}{4(0.03975)} = \frac{2.2795}{0.159} \approx \mathbf{13.69 \approx 13.7}$.
>
> **Q25: (B) 7937 km**
> $R = \frac{0.85}{4.5\times10^{-7}}(14)\ln(1.35) = (1.8889\times10^6)(14)(0.3001) = 26.444\times10^6(0.3001) \approx 7,936,700\text{ m} \approx \mathbf{7937\text{ km}}$.
>
> **Q26: 0.380**
> $\frac{p}{p_0} = \left(\frac{240}{288.15}\right)^{5.2561} = (0.8329)^{5.2561} \approx \mathbf{0.380}$.
>
> **Q27: 67.3**
> $C_{L,md} = \sqrt{\frac{0.02}{0.04}} = \sqrt{0.5} \approx 0.7071$.
> $V_{md} = \sqrt{\frac{2(30000)}{1.225 \times 15 \times 0.7071}} = \sqrt{\frac{60000}{12.993}} = \sqrt{4617.9} \approx \mathbf{67.95\text{ m/s} \approx 68.0\text{ m/s}}$.
>
> **Q28: 1.70**
> $(L/D)_{\max} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{0.04 \times 0.02}} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{0.0008}} = \frac{1}{2(0.02828)} = \frac{1}{0.05657} \approx 17.68$.
> $T_{R,\min} = \frac{30\text{ kN}}{17.68} \approx \mathbf{1.70\text{ kN}}$.
>
> **Q29: 2.17**
> $(L/D)_{\max} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{0.03 \times 0.012}} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{0.00036}} = \frac{1}{2(0.01897)} = \frac{1}{0.03795} \approx 26.35$.
> $\theta_{\min} = \arctan(1/26.35) = \arctan(0.03795) \approx \mathbf{2.17^\circ}$.
>
> **Q30: 7.64**
> $W_0/W_1 = 1 / 0.70 \approx 1.4286$.
> $E = \frac{15}{0.7}\ln(1.4286) = 21.4286(0.35667) = \mathbf{7.64\text{ hours}}$.
