**BLOCK A: Direct Concept & Formula Recall (1 Mark Each, Q1–Q10)**

**Q1.** In a single-spool turbojet engine, the work extracted by the turbine in steady operation is matched to the work required by the:
(A) Exhaust nozzle (B) Compressor (C) Intake diffuser (D) Propulsive jet

**Q2.** As flight speed $V_0$ increases at constant jet exit velocity $V_9$, the propulsive efficiency $\eta_p = \frac{2 V_0}{V_9 + V_0}$ of a turbojet engine:
(A) Decreases (B) Increases (C) Remains constant (D) Becomes zero

**Q3.** The bypass ratio $\alpha$ of a turbofan engine is defined as the ratio of:
(A) Core airflow to total airflow
(B) Fan airflow to core airflow ($\dot{m}_{\text{fan}} / \dot{m}_{\text{core}}$)
(C) Fuel mass flow to air mass flow
(D) Turbine exit area to nozzle area

**Q4.** According to Lighthill's acoustic noise law, the jet noise power emitted by an aircraft engine scales with jet velocity $V_j$ as:
(A) $V_j^2$ (B) $V_j^4$ (C) $V_j^6$ (D) $V_j^8$

**Q5.** In a turboprop engine, the fraction of available gas enthalpy extracted by the turbine system to drive the propeller shaft is approximately:
(A) $10\%\text{ to } 20\%$ (B) $30\%\text{ to } 40\%$ (C) $50\%\text{ to } 60\%$ (D) $80\%\text{ to } 90\%$

**Q6.** The standard empirical conversion for Equivalent Shaft Horsepower (ESHP) is:
(A) $\text{ESHP} = \text{SHP} + 2.5 F_{\text{jet}}$
(B) $\text{ESHP} = \text{SHP} + \frac{F_{\text{jet}}}{2.5}$
(C) $\text{ESHP} = \text{SHP} \times F_{\text{jet}}$
(D) $\text{ESHP} = \frac{\text{SHP}}{2.5}$

**Q7.** Conventional turboprop aircraft are restricted to maximum cruise speeds below Mach $0.65\text{--}0.70$ primarily because:
(A) Propeller blade tips encounter supersonic compressibility shock waves
(B) Core combustor experiences flameout
(C) Turbine blade cooling fails
(D) Engine weight becomes excessive

**Q8.** When the afterburner of a turbojet engine is ignited, the exhaust nozzle throat area $A_t$ must:
(A) Decrease (B) Increase (C) Remain unchanged (D) Vibrate

**Q9.** The thermal efficiency $\eta_{\text{th}}$ of an ideal ramjet engine operating with a calorically perfect gas depends solely upon the:
(A) Turbine inlet temperature (B) Flight Mach number $M_0$ (C) Fuel lower heating value (D) Ambient pressure

**Q10.** At zero flight speed ($M_0 = 0$), the net thrust produced by a standalone ramjet engine is:
(A) Maximum (B) Equal to gross thrust (C) Zero (D) Negative

**BLOCK B: Standard & Multi-Step Problems (2 Marks Each, Q11–Q25)**

**Q11.** A turbojet turbine receives gas at $T_{04} = 1400\text{ K}$ ($c_{pg} = 1150\text{ J/(kg K)}$). The compressor temperature rise is $T_{03} - T_{02} = 280\text{ K}$ ($c_{pa} = 1005\text{ J/(kg K)}$). With $f = 0.020$ and $\eta_m = 1.0$, the turbine exit temperature $T_{05}$ is:
(A) $1120.0\text{ K}$ (B) $1160.0\text{ K}$ (C) $1200.0\text{ K}$ (D) $1240.0\text{ K}$

**Q12.** A turbojet cruises at $V_0 = 200\text{ m/s}$ with airflow $\dot{m}_a = 50\text{ kg/s}$, jet exit speed $V_9 = 650\text{ m/s}$, and fuel-to-air ratio $f = 0.020$ (fully expanded $p_9 = p_0$). The net thrust $F_n$ is:
(A) $18.5\text{ kN}$ (B) $23.15\text{ kN}$ (C) $27.5\text{ kN}$ (D) $32.5\text{ kN}$

**Q13.** An engine produces net thrust $F_n = 25\text{ kN}$ at flight speed $V_0 = 220\text{ m/s}$ with jet velocity $V_9 = 580\text{ m/s}$. The propulsive efficiency $\eta_p$ is:
(A) $45.0\%$ (B) $55.0\%$ (C) $65.0\%$ (D) $75.0\%$

**Q14.** A turbojet produces $F_n = 30.0\text{ kN}$ while consuming fuel at $\dot{m}_f = 0.60\text{ kg/s}$. The Thrust Specific Fuel Consumption (TSFC) in $\text{g/(kN}\cdot\text{s)}$ is:
(A) $15.0$ (B) $20.0$ (C) $25.0$ (D) $30.0$

**Q15.** A turbofan engine has core airflow $\dot{m}_{\text{core}} = 25\text{ kg/s}$ and bypass ratio $\alpha = 6.0$. The total mass flow rate $\dot{m}_{\text{total}}$ is:
(A) $125\text{ kg/s}$ (B) $150\text{ kg/s}$ (C) $175\text{ kg/s}$ (D) $200\text{ kg/s}$

**Q16.** An unmixed turbofan has $\dot{m}_{\text{core}} = 20\text{ kg/s}, \alpha = 5.0, V_0 = 200\text{ m/s}, V_{9,\text{core}} = 520\text{ m/s}, V_{9,\text{fan}} = 320\text{ m/s}$ (neglect $f$). The net thrust $F_n$ is:
(A) $14.4\text{ kN}$ (B) $18.4\text{ kN}$ (C) $22.4\text{ kN}$ (D) $26.4\text{ kN}$

**Q17.** In a turbofan, the fan temperature rise is $\Delta T_{\text{fan}} = 35\text{ K}$ ($c_{pa} = 1005\text{ J/(kg K)}$). The bypass ratio is $\alpha = 6.0$. For $c_{pg} = 1150\text{ J/(kg K)}, f = 0.02$, and $\eta_m = 1.0$, the temperature drop across the LP turbine $\Delta T_{\text{LPT}}$ is:
(A) $120.5\text{ K}$ (B) $155.0\text{ K}$ (C) $179.9\text{ K}$ (D) $210.0\text{ K}$

**Q18.** A turboprop engine produces shaft power $P_{\text{shaft}} = 2000\text{ kW}$ at flight velocity $V_0 = 150\text{ m/s}$ with propeller efficiency $\eta_{\text{prop}} = 0.82$. The propeller thrust $F_{\text{prop}}$ is:
(A) $8.20\text{ kN}$ (B) $10.93\text{ kN}$ (C) $13.33\text{ kN}$ (D) $16.40\text{ kN}$

**Q19.** For the same turboprop ($P_{\text{shaft}} = 2000\text{ kW}, \eta_{\text{prop}} = 0.82, V_0 = 150\text{ m/s}$), if residual jet thrust is $F_{\text{jet}} = 2.50\text{ kN}$, the total propulsive thrust $F_{\text{total}}$ is:
(A) $10.93\text{ kN}$ (B) $13.43\text{ kN}$ (C) $15.93\text{ kN}$ (D) $18.43\text{ kN}$

**Q20.** A turboprop engine produces $\text{SHP} = 3000\text{ hp}$ and residual jet thrust $F_{\text{jet}} = 600\text{ lbf}$. The Equivalent Shaft Horsepower ($\text{ESHP}$) is:
(A) $3240\text{ ESHP}$ (B) $3400\text{ ESHP}$ (C) $3600\text{ ESHP}$ (D) $4500\text{ ESHP}$

**Q21.** A turbojet has turbine exit temperature $T_{05} = 950\text{ K}$. When afterburning is turned on, the gas temperature reaches $T_{07} = 2137.5\text{ K}$. The thrust augmentation ratio $F_{\text{wet}} / F_{\text{dry}}$ is approximately:
(A) $1.25$ (B) $1.50$ (C) $1.75$ (D) $2.25$

**Q22.** An afterburning engine has a dry nozzle throat area $A_{t,\text{dry}} = 0.20\text{ m}^2$ with $T_{05} = 1000\text{ K}$ and reheat temperature $T_{07} = 2250\text{ K}$. The required wet throat area $A_{t,\text{wet}}$ is:
(A) $0.25\text{ m}^2$ (B) $0.30\text{ m}^2$ (C) $0.35\text{ m}^2$ (D) $0.45\text{ m}^2$

**Q23.** An ideal ramjet engine operates at flight Mach $M_0 = 2.50$ with $\gamma = 1.4$. The thermal efficiency $\eta_{\text{th}}$ is:
(A) $44.4\%$ (B) $55.6\%$ (C) $62.5\%$ (D) $71.4\%$

**Q24.** An aircraft flies at $M_0 = 3.0$ where ambient temperature is $T_0 = 216.65\text{ K}$ ($\gamma = 1.4$). The total temperature in the ramjet combustor $T_{02}$ is:
(A) $433.3\text{ K}$ (B) $606.6\text{ K}$ (C) $758.3\text{ K}$ (D) $866.6\text{ K}$

**Q25.** A turbofan with bypass ratio $\alpha = 8.0$ consumes fuel at $\dot{m}_f = 0.40\text{ kg/s}$ producing total net thrust $F_n = 40\text{ kN}$. The overall engine TSFC is:
(A) $5.0\text{ g/(kN}\cdot\text{s)}$ (B) $10.0\text{ g/(kN}\cdot\text{s)}$ (C) $15.0\text{ g/(kN}\cdot\text{s)}$ (D) $20.0\text{ g/(kN}\cdot\text{s)}$

## ⏰ SESSION 2 (1:45 – 4:00): SCORING + COMPREHENSIVE ERROR REPAIR

### Step 1 — Instant Scoring & Verification (1:45 – 2:15)

> [!faq]- 🔍 View Complete 30Q Answer Key & Worked Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) Compressor** | Turbine work powers the compressor ($\dot{W}_t \eta_m = \dot{W}_c$).
> **Q2: (B) Increases** | $\eta_p = \frac{2}{1 + V_9/V_0}$. As $V_0 \uparrow$, $\eta_p \uparrow$.
> **Q3: (B) Fan airflow to core airflow** | $\alpha \equiv \dot{m}_{\text{fan}} / \dot{m}_{\text{core}}$.
> **Q4: (D) V_j⁸** | Lighthill's eighth-power acoustic law.
> **Q5: (D) 80% to 90%** | Power turbine extracts 80–90% of enthalpy for propeller.
> **Q6: (B) ESHP = SHP + F_jet / 2.5** | Standard imperial conversion.
> **Q7: (A)** | Propeller blade tip Mach number exceeds $1.0$.
> **Q8: (B) Increase** | Nozzle throat opens ($A_t \propto \sqrt{T_0}$) to prevent upstream surge.
> **Q9: (B) Flight Mach number M_0** | $\eta_{\text{th}} = \frac{0.2 M_0^2}{1 + 0.2 M_0^2}$.
> **Q10: (C) Zero** | Ramjet has zero static compression and zero static thrust.
>
> **Q11: (B) 1160.0 K**
> $T_{05} = 1400 - \frac{1005(280)}{1.02(1150)(1.0)} = 1400 - \frac{281400}{1173} = 1400 - 239.90 = \mathbf{1160.1\text{ K} \approx 1160.0\text{ K}}$.
>
> **Q12: (B) 23.15 kN**
> $F_n = 50[(1.02)(650) - 200] = 50[663 - 200] = 50(463) = 23,150\text{ N} = \mathbf{23.15\text{ kN}}$.
>
> **Q13: (B) 55.0%**
> $\eta_p = \frac{2(220)}{580 + 220} = \frac{440}{800} = 0.55 = \mathbf{55.0\%}$.
>
> **Q14: (B) 20.0 g/(kN·s)**
> $\text{TSFC} = \frac{0.60\text{ kg/s}}{30.0\text{ kN}} = 0.020\text{ kg/(kN}\cdot\text{s)} = \mathbf{20.0\text{ g/(kN}\cdot\text{s)}}$.
>
> **Q15: (C) 175 kg/s**
> $\dot{m}_{\text{total}} = 25(1 + 6) = 25(7) = \mathbf{175\text{ kg/s}}$.
>
> **Q16: (B) 18.4 kN**
> $\dot{m}_{\text{fan}} = 5(20) = 100\text{ kg/s}$.
> $F_{\text{core}} = 20(520 - 200) = 20(320) = 6400\text{ N}$.
> $F_{\text{fan}} = 100(320 - 200) = 100(120) = 12000\text{ N}$.
> $F_n = 6400 + 12000 = 18,400\text{ N} = \mathbf{18.4\text{ kN}}$.
>
> **Q17: (C) 179.9 K**
> $\Delta T_{\text{LPT}} = \frac{\alpha c_{pa} \Delta T_{\text{fan}}}{(1+f) c_{pg} \eta_m} = \frac{6.0 \times 1005 \times 35}{1.02 \times 1150 \times 1.0} = \frac{211050}{1173} \approx \mathbf{179.92\text{ K} \approx 179.9\text{ K}}$.
>
> **Q18: (B) 10.93 kN**
> $F_{\text{prop}} = \frac{2000 \times 10^3 \times 0.82}{150} = \frac{1,640,000}{150} \approx 10,933\text{ N} = \mathbf{10.93\text{ kN}}$.
>
> **Q19: (B) 13.43 kN**
> $F_{\text{total}} = 10.93 + 2.50 = \mathbf{13.43\text{ kN}}$.
>
> **Q20: (A) 3240 ESHP**
> $\text{ESHP} = 3000 + \frac{600}{2.5} = 3000 + 240 = \mathbf{3240\text{ ESHP}}$.
>
> **Q21: (B) 1.50**
> $\frac{F_{\text{wet}}}{F_{\text{dry}}} \approx \sqrt{\frac{2137.5}{950}} = \sqrt{2.25} = \mathbf{1.50}$.
>
> **Q22: (B) 0.30 m²**
> $A_{t,\text{wet}} = 0.20\sqrt{\frac{2250}{1000}} = 0.20\sqrt{2.25} = 0.20(1.50) = \mathbf{0.30\text{ m}^2}$.
>
> **Q23: (B) 55.6%**
> $\eta_{\text{th}} = \frac{0.2(2.5^2)}{1 + 0.2(2.5^2)} = \frac{0.2(6.25)}{1 + 0.2(6.25)} = \frac{1.25}{2.25} = \frac{5}{9} \approx \mathbf{0.5556 = 55.6\%}$.
>
> **Q24: (B) 606.6 K**
> $T_{02} = 216.65(1 + 0.2(9)) = 216.65(2.8) = \mathbf{606.62\text{ K} \approx 606.6\text{ K}}$.
>
> **Q25: (B) 10.0 g/(kN·s)**
> $\text{TSFC} = \frac{0.40\text{ kg/s}}{40\text{ kN}} = \frac{400\text{ g/s}}{40\text{ kN}} = \mathbf{10.0\text{ g/(kN}\cdot\text{s)}}$.
>
> **Q26: 18.62**
> $F_n = 40.0[(1.022)(700) - 250] = 40.0[715.4 - 250] = 40.0(465.4) = 18,616\text{ N} = \mathbf{18.62\text{ kN}}$.
>
> **Q27: 425**
> $0.64 = \frac{2(200)}{V_9 + 200} = \frac{400}{V_9 + 200} \implies V_9 + 200 = \frac{400}{0.64} = 625 \implies V_9 = 625 - 200 = \mathbf{425\text{ m/s}}$.
>
> **Q28: 2600**
> $P_{\text{equiv}} = 2100 + \frac{3.0 \times 140}{0.84} = 2100 + \frac{420}{0.84} = 2100 + 500 = \mathbf{2600\text{ kW}}$.
>
> **Q29: 75.0**
> $F_{\text{wet}} = F_{\text{dry}}\sqrt{\frac{T_{07}}{T_{05}}} = 50.0\sqrt{\frac{2160}{960}} = 50.0\sqrt{2.25} = 50.0(1.50) = \mathbf{75.0\text{ kN}}$.
>
> **Q30: 2.24**
> $\frac{0.2 M_0^2}{1 + 0.2 M_0^2} = 0.50 \implies 0.2 M_0^2 = 0.50(1 + 0.2 M_0^2) = 0.50 + 0.1 M_0^2 \implies 0.1 M_0^2 = 0.50 \implies M_0^2 = 5.0 \implies M_0 = \sqrt{5.0} \approx \mathbf{2.236 \approx 2.24}$.
