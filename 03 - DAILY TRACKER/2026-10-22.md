#### 🔑 The 12 Rigid-Body State Variables:
1. **Translational Velocities (along body axes):**
   - $u$: Axial / Surge velocity (along $x_b$).
   - $v$: Side / Sway velocity (along $y_b$).
   - $w$: Normal / Heave velocity (along $z_b$).
2. **Angular Velocity Rates:**
   - $p$: Roll rate (about $x_b$, positive right wing down).
   - $q$: Pitch rate (about $y_b$, positive nose up).
   - $r$: Yaw rate (about $z_b$, positive nose right).
3. **Euler Orientation Angles (3-2-1 Sequence: Yaw $\psi \to$ Pitch $\theta \to$ Roll $\phi$):**
   - $\psi$: Heading / Yaw angle.
   - $\theta$: Pitch attitude angle.
   - $\phi$: Bank / Roll attitude angle.
4. **Forces & Moments:**
   - Total Aerodynamic Forces: $X, Y, Z$.
   - Total Aerodynamic Moments: $L$ (Rolling), $M$ (Pitching), $N$ (Yawing).

### Step 3 — Rigid-Body Rotational Equations of Motion (20 min)
Open `05.2 - Angular Momentum Balance`.

Using Newton's Second Law for rigid bodies with plane of symmetry $x_b$-$z_b$ ($I_{xy} = I_{yz} = 0$, $I_{xz} \ne 0$):

$$\boxed{\begin{aligned}
L &= I_{xx}\dot{p} - I_{xz}\dot{r} + q r (I_{zz} - I_{yy}) - p q I_{xz} \\
M &= I_{yy}\dot{q} + p r (I_{xx} - I_{zz}) + (p^2 - r^2)I_{xz} \\
N &= I_{zz}\dot{r} - I_{xz}\dot{p} + p q (I_{yy} - I_{xx}) + q r I_{xz}
\end{aligned}}$$

- **Inertia Cross-Coupling Terms:** The gyroscopic terms $q r(I_{zz} - I_{yy})$ and $p r(I_{xx} - I_{zz})$ become critical in high-speed rolling maneuvers (Inertial pitch-roll coupling!).

### Step 4 — Small Disturbance Linearization & The Decoupling Theorem (20 min)

```
              THE FUNDAMENTAL 6-DOF SYSTEM DECOUPLING
                    Linearized 6-DOF Aircraft System
                                   │
                 ┌─────────────────┴─────────────────┐
                 ▼                                   ▼
     LONGITUDINAL DYNAMICS               LATERAL-DIRECTIONAL DYNAMICS
        (Symmetric Motion)                   (Asymmetric Motion)
   • States: [Δu, Δw, Δq, Δθ]           • States: [Δv, Δp, Δr, Δφ]
     (or [Δu, Δα, Δq, Δθ])                (or [Δβ, Δp, Δr, Δφ])
   • Controls: Elevator (δ_e), Thrust   • Controls: Aileron (δ_a), Rudder (δ_r)
   • Modes: Short Period, Phugoid       • Modes: Roll Subsidence, Spiral, Dutch Roll
```

1. **Longitudinal Subsystem (3-DOF In-Plane):**
   $$\begin{bmatrix} \dot{u} \\ \dot{w} \\ \dot{q} \\ \dot{\theta} \end{bmatrix} = \mathbf{A}_{\text{long}} \begin{bmatrix} u \\ w \\ q \\ \theta \end{bmatrix} + \mathbf{B}_{\text{long}} \begin{bmatrix} \delta_e \\ \delta_T \end{bmatrix}$$
   - Independent of lateral states ($v, p, r, \phi$).

2. **Lateral-Directional Subsystem (3-DOF Out-of-Plane):**
   $$\begin{bmatrix} \dot{v} \\ \dot{p} \\ \dot{r} \\ \dot{\phi} \end{bmatrix} = \mathbf{A}_{\text{lat}} \begin{bmatrix} v \\ p \\ r \\ \phi \end{bmatrix} + \mathbf{B}_{\text{lat}} \begin{bmatrix} \delta_a \\ \delta_r \end{bmatrix}$$
   - Independent of longitudinal states ($u, w, q, \theta$).

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Symmetric vs. Asymmetric State Classification**
> - Longitudinal States: $\Delta u, \Delta w, \Delta q, \Delta\theta$ (or $\Delta\alpha$).
> - Lateral-Directional States: $\Delta v, \Delta p, \Delta r, \Delta\phi$ (or $\Delta\beta$).
> - Never mix them in state-space derivations!
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Body Axes Sign Convention**
> Downward is $+z_b$. Therefore, weight in body axis points in $+z_b$ direction ($W = mg$ along $+z_b$ in level flight).

### Step 3 — D3 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** In standard aircraft flight dynamics, the body-fixed $z$-axis ($z_b$) points:
> **(A)** Forward along the fuselage **(B)** Towards the right wingtip **(C)** Downwards perpendicular to the $x_b$-$y_b$ plane **(D)** Upwards

> **Q2 (1 mark):** Which of the following sets represents purely longitudinal perturbation state variables?
> **(A)** $[\Delta u, \Delta v, \Delta p, \Delta\phi]$
> **(B)** $[\Delta u, \Delta w, \Delta q, \Delta\theta]$
> **(C)** $[\Delta v, \Delta p, \Delta r, \Delta\psi]$
> **(D)** $[\Delta w, \Delta p, \Delta q, \Delta r]$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** An aircraft has principal moments of inertia $I_{xx} = 20,000\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m}^2$ and $I_{zz} = 60,000\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m}^2$ ($I_{xz} = 0$). In a high-speed roll at $p = 3.0\text{ rad/s}$ with a yaw rate $r = 0.5\text{ rad/s}$, the gyroscopic pitching moment induced on the aircraft is:
> **(A)** $+60,000\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$ **(B)** $-60,000\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$ **(C)** $+120,000\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$ **(D)** $0\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$

> **Q4 (1 mark):** The standard Euler angle rotation sequence used to transform from earth-fixed inertial axes to aircraft body-fixed axes is:
> **(A)** Roll $\to$ Pitch $\to$ Yaw (1-2-3)
> **(B)** Yaw $\to$ Pitch $\to$ Roll (3-2-1: $\psi \to \theta \to \phi$)
> **(C)** Pitch $\to$ Roll $\to$ Yaw (2-1-3)
> **(D)** Yaw $\to$ Roll $\to$ Pitch (3-1-2)

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** An aircraft in pure pitch oscillation has $p = r = 0, I_{yy} = 50,000\text{ kg}\cdot\text{m}^2$. If the pitch rate is increasing at $\dot{q} = 0.40\text{ rad/s}^2$, the total pitching moment $M$ (in $\text{kN}\cdot\text{m}$) acting on the aircraft is ___ $\text{kN}\cdot\text{m}$.

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D3 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (C) Downwards perpendicular to the x_b-y_b plane**
> Standard aerospace body axes: $x$ nose forward, $y$ right wingtip, $z$ down.
>
> **Q2: (B) [Δu, Δw, Δq, Δθ]**
> Longitudinal symmetric states comprise axial velocity $u$, heave velocity $w$ (or $\alpha$), pitch rate $q$, and pitch attitude $\theta$.
>
> **Q3: (B) -60,000 N·m**
> $M = p r (I_{xx} - I_{zz}) = (3.0)(0.5)(20,000 - 60,000) = 1.5(-40,000) = \mathbf{-60,000\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) Yaw → Pitch → Roll (3-2-1: ψ → θ → φ)**
> Standard aerospace transformation sequence is 3-2-1 (Yaw $\psi$, Pitch $\theta$, Roll $\phi$).
>
> **Q5: 20**
> $M = I_{yy}\dot{q} = 50,000 \times 0.40 = 20,000\text{ N}\cdot\text{m} = \mathbf{20\text{ kN}\cdot\text{m}}$.
