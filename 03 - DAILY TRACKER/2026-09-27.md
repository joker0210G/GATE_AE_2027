**Q12.** A supersonic wind tunnel operates with test-section Mach number $M = 2.0$ at static pressure $p = 20\text{ kPa}$ and static temperature $T = 200\text{ K}$ ($\gamma = 1.4$). The reservoir stagnation pressure $p_0$ is:

## ⏰ SESSION 2 (1:45 – 4:00): SCORING + COMPREHENSIVE ERROR REPAIR

### Step 1 — Instant Scoring & Verification (1:45 – 2:15)

> [!faq]- 🔍 View Complete 30Q Answer Key & Worked Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) 2.59**
> Blasius solution gives $\delta^*/x = 1.72/\sqrt{Re_x}, \theta/x = 0.664/\sqrt{Re_x} \implies H = 1.72/0.664 \approx \mathbf{2.59}$.
>
> **Q2: (C) Zero**
> Separation occurs where wall velocity gradient vanishes: $\tau_w = \mu(\partial u/\partial y)_{y=0} = \mathbf{0}$.
>
> **Q3: (C) Remains constant**
> Across normal shocks, the flow is adiabatic ($q = 0$) $\implies T_{02} = T_{01}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) Tangential component (V_t)**
> No pressure gradient acts tangential to the shock $\implies V_{t1} = V_{t2}$.
>
> **Q5: (C) Remains constant**
> Prandtl-Meyer expansion is strictly isentropic ($\Delta s = 0 \implies p_{02} = p_{01}$).
>
> **Q6: (B) 0.50c**
> Linearized supersonic flow has uniform loading $\implies x_{ac} = \mathbf{0.50c}$ (Mid-chord).
>
> **Q7: (B) 40% of chord**
> 2nd digit $4$ represents max camber location at $40\%$ chord ($0.4c$).
>
> **Q8: (B) 40 km/h**
> $V_{\text{avg}} = \frac{2(30)(60)}{30+60} = \frac{3600}{90} = \mathbf{40\text{ km/h}}$.
>
> **Q9: (C) Some engineers may be musicians**
> Standard Venn diagram logical deduction.
>
> **Q10: (B) 4**
> A cube has 6 faces; the longest linear chain in a valid cube net contains at most 4 squares.
>
> **Q11: (B) 1 / (√2 − 1)**
> $D_1 = C_F(L/2) \cdot \frac{1}{2}\rho U^2 (L/2) \propto \sqrt{L/2}$.
> Total drag $D_{\text{total}} \propto \sqrt{L} \implies D_2 = D_{\text{total}} - D_1 \propto \sqrt{L} - \sqrt{L/2} = \sqrt{L/2}(\sqrt{2} - 1)$.
> Ratio $D_1 / D_2 = \frac{\sqrt{L/2}}{\sqrt{L/2}(\sqrt{2}-1)} = \mathbf{\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}-1}} \approx 2.414$.
>
> **Q12: (A) 156.5 kPa**
> $p_0 = p(1 + 0.2 M^2)^{3.5} = 20(1 + 0.2(4))^{3.5} = 20(1.8)^{3.5} = 20(7.824) = \mathbf{156.48\text{ kPa}}$.
>
> **Q13: (A) 135 kPa**
> $\frac{p_2}{p_1} = \frac{7(2^2) - 1}{6} = \frac{27}{6} = 4.5 \implies p_2 = 30 \times 4.5 = \mathbf{135\text{ kPa}}$.
>
> **Q14: (B) 0.701**
> $M_{n1} = 3\sin(30^\circ) = 1.5$.
> $M_{n2}^2 = \frac{2 + 0.4(1.5^2)}{2.8(1.5^2) - 0.4} = \frac{2 + 0.9}{6.3 - 0.4} = \frac{2.9}{5.9} \approx 0.4915 \implies M_{n2} = \sqrt{0.4915} \approx \mathbf{0.701}$.
>
> **Q15: (A) 2.27**
> $M_2 = \frac{M_{n2}}{\sin(\beta - \theta)} = \frac{0.701}{\sin(30^\circ - 12^\circ)} = \frac{0.701}{\sin(18^\circ)} = \frac{0.701}{0.3090} \approx \mathbf{2.268 \approx 2.27}$.
>
> **Q16: (B) 2.0**
> $\nu(M_2) = 11.9^\circ + 14.5^\circ = 26.4^\circ \implies M_2 = \mathbf{2.0}$.
>
> **Q17: (B) 0.121**
> $\alpha = 3 \times \frac{\pi}{180} = 0.05236\text{ rad}$.
> $C_L = \frac{4\alpha}{\sqrt{2^2 - 1}} = \frac{4(0.05236)}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{0.20944}{1.732} \approx \mathbf{0.1209 \approx 0.121}$.
>
> **Q18: (B) 0.0063**
> $C_{D,\text{wave}} = C_L \alpha = (0.1209)(0.05236) \approx \mathbf{0.00633}$.
>
> **Q19: (B) 15**
> $\frac{1}{T} = \frac{1}{6} - \frac{1}{10} = \frac{5 - 3}{30} = \frac{2}{30} = \frac{1}{15} \implies T = \mathbf{15\text{ hours}}$.
>
> **Q20: (B) +4%**
> $\text{Net \%} = 30 - 20 + \frac{30(-20)}{100} = 10 - 6 = \mathbf{+4\%}$.
>
> **Q21: (B) 50**
> Case 1 (2 men, 1 woman): $^5C_2 \times ^4C_1 = 10 \times 4 = 40$.
> Case 2 (3 men, 0 women): $^5C_3 \times ^4C_0 = 10 \times 1 = 10$.
> Total $= 40 + 10 = \mathbf{50}$.
>
> **Q22: (A) 1/6**
> Sums $\ge 10$: $(4,6),(5,5),(5,6),(6,4),(6,5),(6,6) \implies 6$ outcomes.
> Probability $= 6 / 36 = \mathbf{1/6}$.
>
> **Q23: (B) 2/15 δ**
> $\theta = \int_0^\delta (2\eta - \eta^2)(1 - 2\eta + \eta^2) d\eta \cdot \delta = \delta \int_0^1 (2\eta - 5\eta^2 + 4\eta^3 - \eta^4) d\eta = \delta [1 - 5/3 + 1 - 1/5] = \delta [2 - 28/15] = \mathbf{\frac{2}{15}\delta}$.
>
> **Q24: (A) 2.50**
> $\delta^* = \delta \int_0^1 (1 - 2\eta + \eta^2) d\eta = \delta [1 - 1 + 1/3] = \delta/3$.
> $H = \frac{\delta^*}{\theta} = \frac{\delta/3}{2\delta/15} = \frac{15}{6} = \mathbf{2.50}$.
>
> **Q25: (B) 2.2**
> From Rayleigh Pitot formula table: for $M_1 \approx 2.2$, $p_{02}/p_1 \approx 6.0$.
>
> **Q26: 2.50**
> $Re_x = \frac{30(0.5)}{1.5\times10^{-5}} = 10^6 \implies \sqrt{Re_x} = 1000$.
> $\delta = \frac{5.0(0.5\text{ m})}{1000} = 2.5\times10^{-3}\text{ m} = \mathbf{2.50\text{ mm}}$.
>
> **Q27: 7.13**
> $\frac{p_2}{p_1} = \frac{7(2.5^2) - 1}{6} = \frac{7(6.25) - 1}{6} = \frac{43.75 - 1}{6} = \frac{42.75}{6} = \mathbf{7.125 \approx 7.13}$.
>
> **Q28: 0.018**
> $C_{D,\text{wave}} = \frac{4(t/c)^2}{\sqrt{M_\infty^2 - 1}} = \frac{4(0.0875^2)}{\sqrt{4 - 1}} = \frac{4(0.007656)}{1.732} = \frac{0.030625}{1.732} \approx \mathbf{0.0177 \approx 0.018}$.
>
> **Q29: 604.8**
> Word: "AERODYNAMICS" (12 letters: A:2, E:1, R:1, O:1, D:1, Y:1, N:1, M:1, I:1, C:1, S:1).
> Vowels: A, E, O, A, I (5 vowels: A appears 2 times).
> Consonants: R, D, Y, N, M, C, S (7 consonants).
> Treat vowels as single block: 8 entities arranged in $8!$ ways.
> Vowels arranged internally in $5! / 2! = 60$ ways.
> Total ways $= 8! \times 60 = 40320 \times 60 = 2,419,200$.
> Divided by $1000 \implies \mathbf{2419.2}$ (or if exact int: $2419200$).
>
> **Q30: 30**
> Relative speed $= 72 - 36 = 36\text{ km/h} = 36 \times \frac{5}{18} = 10\text{ m/s}$.
> Total distance $= 180 + 120 = 300\text{ m}$.
> Time $= 300 / 10 = \mathbf{30\text{ seconds}}$.
