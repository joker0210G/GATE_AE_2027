### Step 2 — Single Degree of Freedom (SDOF) Undamped & Damped Systems (25 min)
Open `06 - Structural Dynamics`.

```
                    SDOF MASS-SPRING-DAMPER SYSTEM
                      ┌───┐  Damper c
                   ┌──┤ c ├──┐
             k     │  └───┘  │      m
        ┌───\/\/\/─┤         ├────┌───┐ ──► x(t)
        │          └─────────┘    │ m │
        ╨                         └───┘
        m ẍ + c ẋ + k x = 0 ⟹ ẍ + 2ζω_n ẋ + ω_n² x = 0
```

#### 1. Free Undamped Vibrations ($c = 0$):
$$\boxed{\omega_n = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}\text{ rad/s}}, \qquad f_n = \frac{\omega_n}{2\pi}\text{ Hz}, \qquad T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega_n}\text{ s}$$

- **Beam Equivalent Stiffnesses ($k_{\text{eq}}$):**
  - Cantilever Beam with tip mass: $\mathbf{k_{\text{eq}} = \frac{3EI}{L^3} \implies \omega_n = \sqrt{\frac{3EI}{mL^3}}}$.
  - Simply Supported Beam with central mass: $\mathbf{k_{\text{eq}} = \frac{48EI}{L^3} \implies \omega_n = \sqrt{\frac{48EI}{mL^3}}}$.
  - Fixed-Fixed Beam with central mass: $\mathbf{k_{\text{eq}} = \frac{192EI}{L^3}}$.

#### 2. Free Damped Vibrations:
$$\boxed{c_{\text{cr}} = 2\sqrt{km} = 2m\omega_n} \qquad \text{and} \qquad \boxed{\zeta \equiv \frac{c}{c_{\text{cr}}} = \frac{c}{2\sqrt{km}}}$$

- **Underdamped Case ($\zeta < 1$):**
  $$\boxed{\omega_d = \omega_n \sqrt{1 - \zeta^2}}$$
  $$\boxed{\text{Logarithmic Decrement: } \delta \equiv \ln\left(\frac{x_1}{x_2}\right) = \frac{2\pi\zeta}{\sqrt{1 - \zeta^2}} \approx 2\pi\zeta \quad (\text{for small } \zeta)}$$
- **Critically Damped ($\zeta = 1$):** Non-oscillatory, quickest return to zero.
- **Overdamped ($\zeta > 1$):** Non-oscillatory sluggish exponential return.

### Step 3 — Harmonically Excited Forced Vibrations (15 min)

For excitation $F(t) = F_0 \sin(\omega t)$, with frequency ratio $r \equiv \frac{\omega}{\omega_n}$:

$$\boxed{X = \frac{F_0 / k}{\sqrt{(1 - r^2)^2 + (2\zeta r)^2}}}$$
$$\boxed{\text{Magnification Factor } MF \equiv \frac{X}{F_0 / k} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{(1 - r^2)^2 + (2\zeta r)^2}}}$$

- **At Resonance ($r = 1$):**
  $$\boxed{MF_{\text{res}} = \frac{1}{2\zeta}}$$
  *(For undamped system $\zeta = 0 \implies MF \to \infty$).*

### Step 4 — Undamped 2-DOF Systems & Flight Vehicle Loads (15 min)

#### 1. Two-Degree-of-Freedom (2-DOF) Equations:
$$[M]\{\ddot{x}\} + [K]\{x\} = \{0\}$$
$$\boxed{\det\left([K] - \omega^2[M]\right) = 0 \implies \omega_1, \omega_2}$$

#### 2. Flight Vehicle Loads & The $V$-$n$ Diagram:
Open `05 - Thin Walled Sections and Aircraft Loads`.

- **Load Factor ($n$):** $\boxed{n \equiv \frac{L}{W} = \frac{\frac{1}{2}\rho V^2 S C_L}{W}}$.
- **Maneuver Boundary (Stall Limit):** $n = \frac{\frac{1}{2}\rho V^2 C_{L,\max}}{W/S} \propto V^2$.
- **Corner Speed ($V^*$ / Maneuvering Speed):** Speed where stall limit meets maximum allowable structural load factor $n_{\max}$:
  $$\boxed{V^* = \sqrt{\frac{2 n_{\max} (W/S)}{\rho C_{L,\max}}}}$$

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Frequency Ratio in Log Decrement**
> For $n$ successive cycles: $\delta = \frac{1}{n}\ln\left(\frac{x_0}{x_n}\right) = \frac{2\pi\zeta}{\sqrt{1-\zeta^2}}$.
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Cantilever Beam Mass Model**
> When mass is at the tip: $k = \frac{3EI}{L^3}$. Do NOT confuse with simply supported ($48EI/L^3$)!

### Step 3 — D4 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** In an underdamped SDOF mechanical system, increasing the damping ratio $\zeta$ causes the damped natural frequency $\omega_d$ to:
> **(A)** Increase **(B)** Decrease **(C)** Remain constant **(D)** Become infinite

> **Q2 (2 marks):** A cantilever beam of length $L = 1.0\text{ m}$ and flexural rigidity $EI = 1200\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2$ supports a mass $m = 4\text{ kg}$ at its free tip. The natural frequency $\omega_n$ (in $\text{rad/s}$) is:
> **(A)** $15$ **(B)** $30$ **(C)** $45$ **(D)** $60$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A vibrating system has an undamped natural frequency $\omega_n = 50\text{ rad/s}$ and damping ratio $\zeta = 0.05$. At resonance ($r = 1$), the magnification factor $MF$ is:
> **(A)** $5$ **(B)** $10$ **(C)** $20$ **(D)** $50$

> **Q4 (1 mark):** On an aircraft $V$-$n$ diagram, the corner speed (maneuvering speed $V^*$) represents the speed at which:
> **(A)** Maximum speed in level flight is achieved **(B)** Aircraft can pull maximum structural load factor without stalling **(C)** Velocity equals the speed of sound **(D)** Induced drag is zero

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** In a damped free vibration experiment, the amplitude of vibration decreases from $10\text{ mm}$ to $5\text{ mm}$ over 5 complete cycles. The damping ratio $\zeta$ is ___ (round off to 4 decimal places).

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D4 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) Decrease**
> $\omega_d = \omega_n \sqrt{1 - \zeta^2}$. As $\zeta$ increases, $\sqrt{1 - \zeta^2}$ decreases $\implies \omega_d$ decreases.
>
> **Q2: (B) 30**
> $k_{\text{eq}} = \frac{3EI}{L^3} = \frac{3(1200)}{1.0^3} = 3600\text{ N/m}$.
> $\omega_n = \sqrt{\frac{k_{\text{eq}}}{m}} = \sqrt{\frac{3600}{4}} = \sqrt{900} = \mathbf{30\text{ rad/s}}$.
>
> **Q3: (B) 10**
> $MF_{\text{res}} = \frac{1}{2\zeta} = \frac{1}{2(0.05)} = \frac{1}{0.10} = \mathbf{10}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) Aircraft can pull maximum structural load factor without stalling**
> The corner speed $V^*$ is the intersection point of the positive aerodynamic stall boundary and the positive structural limit load factor $n_{\max}$.
>
> **Q5: 0.0221**
> Over $n = 5$ cycles: $\delta = \frac{1}{5}\ln\left(\frac{x_0}{x_5}\right) = \frac{1}{5}\ln\left(\frac{10}{5}\right) = \frac{1}{5}\ln(2) = \frac{0.693147}{5} = 0.13863$.
> Since $\delta \ll 1$: $\zeta \approx \frac{\delta}{2\pi} = \frac{0.13863}{2\pi} = \frac{0.13863}{6.283185} \approx \mathbf{0.0221}$.
