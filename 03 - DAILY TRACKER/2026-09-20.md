**BLOCK A: Direct Concept & Formula Recall (1 Mark Each, Q1–Q10)**

**Q1.** The stream function for a 2D doublet of strength $\kappa$ at the origin in polar coordinates is:
(A) $\frac{\kappa}{2\pi}\frac{\cos\theta}{r}$ (B) $-\frac{\kappa}{2\pi}\frac{\sin\theta}{r}$ (C) $\frac{\kappa}{2\pi}\theta$ (D) $\frac{\kappa}{2\pi}\ln r$

**Q2.** The maximum flow velocity on the surface of a non-lifting circular cylinder in a uniform stream $V_\infty$ is:
(A) $V_\infty$ (B) $1.5 V_\infty$ (C) $2.0 V_\infty$ (D) $4.0 V_\infty$

**Q3.** According to the Kutta-Joukowski theorem, the aerodynamic lift per unit span $L'$ generated by a 2D body with circulation $\Gamma$ in a stream of velocity $V_\infty$ and density $\rho_\infty$ is:
(A) $\frac{1}{2}\rho_\infty V_\infty^2 \Gamma$ (B) $\rho_\infty V_\infty \Gamma$ (C) $\rho_\infty V_\infty^2 \Gamma$ (D) $\frac{\rho_\infty V_\infty}{\Gamma}$

**Q4.** Thin airfoil theory proves that for ANY thin 2D airfoil in incompressible flow, the lift curve slope $dC_L/d\alpha$ is:
(A) $\pi\text{ rad}^{-1}$ (B) $2\pi\text{ rad}^{-1}$ (C) $4\pi\text{ rad}^{-1}$ (D) Dependent on maximum thickness

**Q5.** For a thin cambered airfoil, the pitching moment coefficient about the quarter-chord point $C_{m,c/4}$ is:
(A) Proportional to $\alpha$ (B) Proportional to $\alpha^2$ (C) Independent of angle of attack $\alpha$ (D) Always zero

**Q6.** The Kutta condition for a sharp trailing edge requires that the distributed vortex strength $\gamma(x)$ at $x = c$ must be:
(A) $\infty$ (B) $0$ (C) $2\pi V_\infty$ (D) Undefined

**Q7.** In the NACA 4-digit designation **NACA 4412**, the maximum thickness of the airfoil as a percentage of chord is:
(A) $4\%$ (B) $12\%$ (C) $44\%$ (D) $1.2\%$

**Q8.** When an airfoil starts moving from rest in a viscous fluid, the starting vortex that is shed into the wake has a circulation:
(A) Equal and opposite to the bound vortex circulation (B) In the same direction as the bound vortex (C) Zero (D) Equal to infinity

**Q9.** For a 3D finite wing with an elliptic lift distribution, the downwash velocity $w$ across the span is:
(A) Parabolic (B) Constant (C) Linear (D) Zero

**Q10.** The aspect ratio of a rectangular wing of span $b = 12\text{ m}$ and chord $c = 2\text{ m}$ is:
(A) $6$ (B) $24$ (C) $3$ (D) $12$

**BLOCK B: Standard & Multi-Step Problems (2 Marks Each, Q11–Q20)**

**Q11.** A uniform stream of velocity $V_\infty = 30\text{ m/s}$ flows past a non-lifting circular cylinder. The minimum static pressure coefficient $C_p$ on the cylinder surface is:
(A) $-1.0$ (B) $-2.0$ (C) $-3.0$ (D) $-4.0$

**Q12.** A circular cylinder of radius $R = 0.5\text{ m}$ is placed in a uniform air stream of $V_\infty = 20\text{ m/s}$. To produce a single stagnation point on the bottom of the cylinder surface ($\theta = 270^\circ$), the required circulation $\Gamma$ is:
(A) $20\pi\text{ m}^2\text{/s}$ (B) $40\pi\text{ m}^2\text{/s}$ (C) $10\pi\text{ m}^2\text{/s}$ (D) $80\pi\text{ m}^2\text{/s}$

**Q13.** A thin airfoil with a parabolic camber line of maximum camber $h = 0.03c$ has a zero-lift angle of attack $\alpha_{L=0}$ (in degrees) equal to:
(A) $-1.72^\circ$ (B) $-3.44^\circ$ (C) $+3.44^\circ$ (D) $-0.06^\circ$

**Q14.** For a thin cambered airfoil, the Fourier coefficients are $A_1 = 0.05$ and $A_2 = 0.01$. The pitching moment coefficient about the aerodynamic center $C_{m,ac}$ is:
(A) $-0.0314$ (B) $+0.0314$ (C) $-0.0125$ (D) $-0.0471$

**Q15.** An airfoil in an incompressible flow has a section lift coefficient $C_{L,0} = 0.45$. Using the Prandtl-Glauert rule, the lift coefficient of the airfoil at $M_\infty = 0.6$ is:
(A) $0.5625$ (B) $0.7500$ (C) $0.5000$ (D) $0.6250$

**Q16.** A 3D wing with an elliptic lift distribution has an aspect ratio $AR = 6$ and operates at a lift coefficient $C_L = 0.6$. The induced drag coefficient $C_{Di}$ is:
(A) $0.0191$ (B) $0.0382$ (C) $0.0600$ (D) $0.0095$

**Q17.** A finite wing with aspect ratio $AR = 8$ has a 2D section lift curve slope $a_0 = 2\pi\text{ rad}^{-1}$. Assuming an elliptic lift distribution, the 3D wing lift curve slope $a$ (in $\text{rad}^{-1}$) is:
(A) $4.50$ (B) $5.03$ (C) $6.28$ (D) $3.14$

**Q18.** A circular cylinder of radius $R = 1\text{ m}$ is immersed in a flow with $V_\infty = 10\text{ m/s}$ and circulation $\Gamma = 20\pi\text{ m}^2\text{/s}$. The two stagnation points on the surface are located at angles $\theta_s$ equal to:
(A) $210^\circ, 330^\circ$ (B) $225^\circ, 315^\circ$ (C) $240^\circ, 300^\circ$ (D) $180^\circ, 360^\circ$

**Q19.** A wing has an area $S = 25\text{ m}^2$, span $b = 15\text{ m}$, and Oswald efficiency factor $e = 0.85$. If $C_L = 0.8$, the induced drag coefficient $C_{Di}$ is:
(A) $0.0266$ (B) $0.0452$ (C) $0.0320$ (D) $0.0185$

**Q20.** An untwisted rectangular wing of aspect ratio $AR = 5$ has a section zero-lift angle $\alpha_{L=0} = 0^\circ$ and 2D lift slope $a_0 = 2\pi\text{ rad}^{-1}$. At a geometric angle of attack $\alpha = 7^\circ$, the induced angle of attack $\alpha_i$ (in degrees) is:
(A) $1.0^\circ$ (B) $2.0^\circ$ (C) $2.5^\circ$ (D) $3.5^\circ$

**Q23 (NAT).** A circular cylinder with radius $R = 0.5\text{ m}$ generates a lift per unit span $L' = 1200\text{ N/m}$ in air of density $\rho_\infty = 1.2\text{ kg/m}^3$ at $V_\infty = 25\text{ m/s}$. The circulation $\Gamma$ around the cylinder (in $\text{m}^2\text{/s}$) is ___.

**Q24 (NAT).** An elliptic wing of aspect ratio $AR = 8$ flies at $V_\infty = 80\text{ m/s}$ producing a lift coefficient $C_L = 0.5$. The downwash velocity $w$ at the wing (in $\text{m/s}$) is ___ (round off to 2 decimal places).

**Q25 (NAT).** A thin cambered airfoil has Fourier coefficients $A_1 = 0.08$ and $A_2 = 0.02$. At an operating lift coefficient $C_L = 0.6$, the center of pressure location $x_{cp}$ as a fraction of chord $c$ is ___ (round off to 3 decimal places).

## ⏰ SESSION 2 (1:30 – 4:00): SCORING + COMPREHENSIVE ERROR REPAIR

### Step 1 — Instant Scoring & Verification (1:30 – 2:00)

> [!faq]- 🔍 View Complete 25Q Answer Key & Worked Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) -(κ/2π)(sin θ / r)**
> Doublet stream function in polar coordinates.
>
> **Q2: (C) 2.0 V_inf**
> Surface velocity $v_\theta = -2V_\infty\sin\theta \implies$ Maximum speed is $2V_\infty$ at $\theta = 90^\circ, 270^\circ$.
>
> **Q3: (B) ρ_inf V_inf Γ**
> Kutta-Joukowski Theorem for lift per unit span.
>
> **Q4: (B) 2π rad⁻¹**
> Universal 2D thin airfoil lift curve slope $dC_L/d\alpha = 2\pi\text{ rad}^{-1} \approx 0.1097\text{ deg}^{-1}$.
>
> **Q5: (C) Independent of angle of attack α**
> Thin airfoil theory proves $C_{m,c/4} = \frac{\pi}{4}(A_2 - A_1) = \text{constant}$ for all $\alpha$.
>
> **Q6: (B) 0**
> Kutta condition ensures smooth departure from TE $\implies \gamma(c) = 0$.
>
> **Q7: (B) 12%**
> In NACA 4412, the last two digits $12$ represent maximum thickness of $12\%$ chord.
>
> **Q8: (A) Equal and opposite to the bound vortex circulation**
> By Kelvin's circulation theorem: $\Gamma_{\text{bound}} + \Gamma_{\text{start}} = 0 \implies \Gamma_{\text{start}} = -\Gamma_{\text{bound}}$.
>
> **Q9: (B) Constant**
> Elliptic circulation distribution induces a strictly constant downwash $w = \Gamma_0 / (2b)$ across the span.
>
> **Q10: (A) 6**
> $AR = b / c = 12 / 2 = \mathbf{6}$.
>
> **Q11: (C) -3.0**
> $C_p = 1 - 4\sin^2\theta$. At $\theta = 90^\circ$: $C_p = 1 - 4(1) = \mathbf{-3.0}$.
>
> **Q12: (B) 40π m²/s**
> For single stagnation point: $\Gamma = 4\pi V_\infty R = 4\pi(20)(0.5) = \mathbf{40\pi\text{ m}^2\text{/s}}$.
>
> **Q13: (B) -3.44°**
> $\alpha_{L=0} = -\frac{2h}{c}\text{ rad} = -2(0.03) = -0.06\text{ rad} = -0.06 \times \frac{180}{\pi} = \mathbf{-3.44^\circ}$.
>
> **Q14: (A) -0.0314**
> $C_{m,c/4} = \frac{\pi}{4}(A_2 - A_1) = \frac{\pi}{4}(0.01 - 0.05) = \frac{\pi}{4}(-0.04) = -0.01\pi \approx \mathbf{-0.0314}$.
>
> **Q15: (A) 0.5625**
> $C_L = \frac{C_{L,0}}{\sqrt{1 - M_\infty^2}} = \frac{0.45}{\sqrt{1 - 0.6^2}} = \frac{0.45}{0.80} = \mathbf{0.5625}$.
>
> **Q16: (A) 0.0191**
> $C_{Di} = \frac{C_L^2}{\pi AR} = \frac{0.6^2}{\pi(6)} = \frac{0.36}{6\pi} = \frac{0.06}{\pi} \approx \mathbf{0.0191}$.
>
> **Q17: (B) 5.03**
> $a = \frac{2\pi}{1 + 2\pi/(8\pi)} = \frac{2\pi}{1 + 1/4} = \frac{2\pi}{1.25} = 1.6\pi \approx \mathbf{5.027\text{ rad}^{-1}}$.
>
> **Q18: (A) 210°, 330°**
> $\sin\theta_s = -\frac{\Gamma}{4\pi V_\infty R} = -\frac{20\pi}{4\pi(10)(1)} = -\frac{20}{40} = -0.5 \implies \theta_s = \mathbf{210^\circ, 330^\circ}$.
>
> **Q19: (A) 0.0266**
> $AR = b^2/S = 15^2 / 25 = 225 / 25 = 9$.
> $C_{Di} = \frac{C_L^2}{\pi e AR} = \frac{0.8^2}{\pi(0.85)(9)} = \frac{0.64}{7.65\pi} \approx \mathbf{0.0266}$.
>
> **Q20: (B) 2.0°**
> $\alpha_i = \frac{a_0}{\pi AR + a_0}\alpha = \frac{2\pi}{5\pi + 2\pi}(7^\circ) = \frac{2}{7}(7^\circ) = \mathbf{2.0^\circ}$.
>
> **Q21: 947.5**
> $\alpha = 4^\circ = 4 \times \frac{\pi}{180} = 0.0698\text{ rad}$.
> $C_L = 2\pi\alpha = 2\pi(0.0698) = 0.4387$.
> $L' = C_L \cdot \frac{1}{2}\rho V_\infty^2 c = (0.4387)(0.5)(1.2)(60^2)(2) = (0.4387)(0.6)(3600)(2) = 0.4387(4320) \approx \mathbf{1895.0\text{ N/m}}$... wait:
> $C_L \cdot \frac{1}{2}\rho V^2 c = (0.4386)(0.5 \times 1.2 \times 3600 \times 2) = 0.4386(4320) = \mathbf{1895.0\text{ N/m}}$.
>
> **Q22: 1.10**
> $\alpha - \alpha_{L=0} = 6.0^\circ - (-4.0^\circ) = 10.0^\circ = 10 \times \frac{\pi}{180} = \frac{\pi}{18}\text{ rad} \approx 0.1745\text{ rad}$.
> $C_L = 2\pi(\alpha - \alpha_{L=0}) = 2\pi(0.1745) = \frac{\pi^2}{9} \approx \frac{9.8696}{9} \approx \mathbf{1.10}$.
>
> **Q23: 40**
> $L' = \rho_\infty V_\infty \Gamma \implies \Gamma = \frac{L'}{\rho_\infty V_\infty} = \frac{1200}{(1.2)(25)} = \frac{1200}{30} = \mathbf{40\text{ m}^2\text{/s}}$.
>
> **Q24: 1.59**
> $\alpha_i = \frac{C_L}{\pi AR} = \frac{0.5}{8\pi} = \frac{0.5}{25.1327} \approx 0.01989\text{ rad}$.
> $w = V_\infty \alpha_i = 80(0.01989) \approx \mathbf{1.59\text{ m/s}}$.
>
> **Q25: 0.329**
> $x_{cp} = \frac{c}{4}\left[1 + \frac{\pi}{C_L}(A_1 - A_2)\right] = 0.25c\left[1 + \frac{\pi}{0.6}(0.08 - 0.02)\right] = 0.25c\left[1 + \frac{\pi}{0.6}(0.06)\right] = 0.25c[1 + 0.1\pi] = 0.25c(1 + 0.31416) = 0.25c(1.31416) \approx \mathbf{0.329c}$.
