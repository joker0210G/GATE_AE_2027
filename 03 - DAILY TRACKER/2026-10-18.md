#### 📝 M3 / W2 Flight Mechanics Block 2 Mini-Test (30 Questions)

**BLOCK A: Direct Concept & Formula Recall (1 Mark Each, Q1–Q10)**

**Q1.** During an accelerated maneuver with load factor $n$, the accelerated stall speed $V_{\text{stall,acc}}$ is related to the 1g stall speed $V_{\text{stall}}$ by:
(A) $V_{\text{stall}} \cdot n$ (B) $V_{\text{stall}} \cdot \sqrt{n}$ (C) $V_{\text{stall}} / \sqrt{n}$ (D) $V_{\text{stall}} \cdot n^2$

**Q2.** At the bottom of a vertical loop in a pull-up maneuver of radius $R$, the load factor $n$ is:
(A) $\frac{V^2}{gR}$ (B) $1 + \frac{V^2}{gR}$ (C) $\frac{V^2}{gR} - 1$ (D) $1 - \frac{V^2}{gR}$

**Q3.** In a coordinated level turn with bank angle $\phi = 45^\circ$, the load factor $n$ is:
(A) $1.00$ (B) $1.414$ (C) $2.000$ (D) $0.707$

**Q4.** The deployment of leading-edge slats on an airfoil primary serves to:
(A) Decrease zero-lift angle of attack $\alpha_{L=0}$
(B) Increase the maximum stall angle of attack $\alpha_{\text{stall}}$ by delaying boundary layer separation
(C) Produce a massive nose-down pitching moment
(D) Reduce maximum lift coefficient $C_{L,\max}$

**Q5.** On an aircraft $V$-$n$ diagram, the maneuvering corner speed $V^*$ represents the speed at which:
(A) Minimum turn radius and maximum turn rate are achieved
(B) The aircraft operates at minimum drag
(C) The speed of sound is reached
(D) Dynamic pressure is zero

**Q6.** The design ultimate load factor $n_{\text{ultimate}}$ is related to the limit load factor $n_{\text{limit}}$ by a factor of safety equal to:
(A) $1.25$ (B) $1.50$ (C) $2.00$ (D) $3.00$

**Q7.** For an aircraft to be longitudinally statically stable with positive trim lift, the pitching moment curve must satisfy:
(A) $C_{m0} > 0$ and $C_{m\alpha} < 0$
(B) $C_{m0} < 0$ and $C_{m\alpha} < 0$
(C) $C_{m0} > 0$ and $C_{m\alpha} > 0$
(D) $C_{m0} < 0$ and $C_{m\alpha} > 0$

**Q8.** The horizontal tail volume ratio $V_H$ is defined as:
(A) $\frac{l_t S_t}{\bar{c} S}$ (B) $\frac{l_t S}{\bar{c} S_t}$ (C) $\frac{\bar{c} S_t}{l_t S}$ (D) $\frac{S_t}{S}$

**Q9.** For a conventionally configured aft-tail aircraft, the elevator control power derivative $C_{m\delta_e} \equiv \partial C_m/\partial\delta_e$ is:
(A) Strictly positive (B) Strictly negative (C) Zero (D) Equal to $+1.0$

**Q10.** The stick-free neutral point $x'_{np}$ of an aircraft with unboosted mechanical controls is located:
(A) Aft of the stick-fixed neutral point ($x'_{np} > x_{np}$)
(B) Forward of the stick-fixed neutral point ($x'_{np} < x_{np}$)
(C) Exactly at the center of pressure of the wing
(D) At the trailing edge

**BLOCK B: Standard & Multi-Step Problems (2 Marks Each, Q11–Q25)**

**Q11.** An airplane has a level stall speed $V_{\text{stall}} = 35\text{ m/s}$. During a steep vertical turn pulling a load factor $n = 4.0\text{ g}$, the accelerated stall speed (in $\text{m/s}$) is:
(A) $50\text{ m/s}$ (B) $70\text{ m/s}$ (C) $90\text{ m/s}$ (D) $140\text{ m/s}$

**Q12.** An aircraft flying at $V = 160\text{ m/s}$ executes a vertical pull-up maneuver with radius $R = 400\text{ m}$ ($g = 10\text{ m/s}^2$). The load factor $n$ experienced by the pilot is:
(A) $6.4$ (B) $7.4$ (C) $5.4$ (D) $8.4$

**Q13.** An inverted aircraft at the top of a loop flies at $V = 120\text{ m/s}$ with radius of curvature $R = 360\text{ m}$ ($g = 10\text{ m/s}^2$). The load factor $n$ is:
(A) $3.0\text{ g}$ (B) $4.0\text{ g}$ (C) $5.0\text{ g}$ (D) $2.0\text{ g}$

**Q14.** An aircraft performs a coordinated level turn at a velocity $V = 140\text{ m/s}$ pulling a load factor $n = 2.5\text{ g}$ ($g = 9.81\text{ m/s}^2$). The radius of turn $R$ (in meters) is:
(A) $624\text{ m}$ (B) $872\text{ m}$ (C) $1023\text{ m}$ (D) $1250\text{ m}$

**Q15.** For the same turn ($V = 140\text{ m/s}, n = 2.5\text{ g}, g = 9.81\text{ m/s}^2$), the rate of turn $\omega$ (in $\text{deg/s}$) is:
(A) $5.42\text{ deg/s}$ (B) $7.85\text{ deg/s}$ (C) $9.21\text{ deg/s}$ (D) $11.35\text{ deg/s}$

**Q16.** An aircraft with wing loading $W/S = 2200\text{ N/m}^2$ and $C_{L,\max} = 1.5$ operates in air of density $\rho = 1.1\text{ kg/m}^3$. If the limit load factor is $n_{\max} = 6.0$, the corner speed $V^*$ is:
(A) $100\text{ m/s}$ (B) $126.5\text{ m/s}$ (C) $145.0\text{ m/s}$ (D) $160.2\text{ m/s}$

**Q17.** An aircraft flying at $V = 150\text{ m/s}$ ($W/S = 2000\text{ N/m}^2, a = 5.0\text{ rad}^{-1}, \rho_0 = 1.225\text{ kg/m}^3, K_g = 0.80$) encounters a vertical gust $U_{de} = 6\text{ m/s}$. The incremental gust load factor $\Delta n$ is:
(A) $0.88\text{ g}$ (B) $1.10\text{ g}$ (C) $1.32\text{ g}$ (D) $1.54\text{ g}$

**Q18.** An aircraft has pitching moment curve $C_m = 0.10 - 0.50\alpha$ (with $\alpha$ in radians). The trim angle of attack $\alpha_{\text{trim}}$ (in degrees) is:
(A) $5.73^\circ$ (B) $8.60^\circ$ (C) $11.46^\circ$ (D) $14.32^\circ$

**Q19.** An aircraft has lift curve slope $a = 4.5\text{ rad}^{-1}$ and pitching moment slope $C_{m\alpha} = -0.675\text{ rad}^{-1}$. The stick-fixed static margin $SM$ is:
(A) $10.0\%$ (B) $12.5\%$ (C) $15.0\%$ (D) $17.5\%$

**Q20.** An aircraft has wing area $S = 20\text{ m}^2$, mean chord $\bar{c} = 2.0\text{ m}$, tail area $S_t = 4.0\text{ m}^2$, and tail moment arm $l_t = 5.0\text{ m}$. The horizontal tail volume ratio $V_H$ is:
(A) $0.40$ (B) $0.50$ (C) $0.60$ (D) $0.75$

**Q21.** An aircraft has $x_{ac,wb}/\bar{c} = 0.25, V_H = 0.50, \eta_t = 1.0, a_t/a = 0.80$, and downwash gradient $d\epsilon/d\alpha = 0.35$. The stick-fixed neutral point $x_{np}/\bar{c}$ is:
(A) $0.45$ (B) $0.51$ (C) $0.56$ (D) $0.62$

**Q22.** An aircraft with $C_{m0} = 0.08, C_{m\alpha} = -0.60\text{ rad}^{-1}$, and $C_{m\delta_e} = -1.20\text{ rad}^{-1}$ is trimmed at $\alpha = 0.05\text{ rad}$. The required elevator deflection $\delta_e$ (in radians) is:
(A) $+0.0417\text{ rad}$ (B) $-0.0417\text{ rad}$ (C) $+0.0833\text{ rad}$ (D) $0.0000\text{ rad}$

**Q23.** An elevator has hinge moment derivatives $C_{h\alpha} = -0.006\text{ deg}^{-1}$ and $C_{h\delta_e} = -0.015\text{ deg}^{-1}$. For a tail angle of attack $\alpha_t = +5.0^\circ$, the floating angle $\delta_{e,\text{float}}$ is:
(A) $+2.0^\circ$ (B) $-2.0^\circ$ (C) $+3.0^\circ$ (D) $-3.0^\circ$

**Q24.** If an aircraft's center of gravity is located at $x_{cg}/\bar{c} = 0.32$ and its stick-fixed neutral point is at $x_{np}/\bar{c} = 0.48$, the aircraft is:
(A) Statically unstable with $SM = -16\%$
(B) Statically stable with $SM = +16\%$
(C) Neutrally stable
(D) Statically stable with $SM = +32\%$

**Q25.** A high-performance fighter pulls a $9.0\text{ g}$ coordinated turn at $V = 200\text{ m/s}$ ($g = 10\text{ m/s}^2$). The radius of turn $R$ is approximately:
(A) $224\text{ m}$ (B) $447\text{ m}$ (C) $672\text{ m}$ (D) $894\text{ m}$

## ⏰ SESSION 2 (1:45 – 4:00): SCORING + COMPREHENSIVE ERROR REPAIR

### Step 1 — Instant Scoring & Verification (1:45 – 2:15)

> [!faq]- 🔍 View Complete 30Q Answer Key & Worked Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) V_stall · √n** | $L = nW \implies V_{\text{stall,acc}} = V_{\text{stall}}\sqrt{n}$.
> **Q2: (B) 1 + V² / (gR)** | Bottom of vertical loop: $L - W = mV^2/R \implies n = 1 + V^2/(gR)$.
> **Q3: (B) 1.414** | $n = 1/\cos(45^\circ) = \sqrt{2} \approx 1.414$.
> **Q4: (B)** | Slats energize boundary layer, delaying stall to higher $\alpha_{\text{stall}}$.
> **Q5: (A)** | At corner speed $V^*$, max structural load $n_{\max}$ and max aerodynamic lift $C_{L,\max}$ coincide, yielding $R_{\min}$ and $\omega_{\max}$.
> **Q6: (B) 1.50** | $n_{\text{ultimate}} = 1.50 \times n_{\text{limit}}$.
> **Q7: (A) C_m0 > 0 and C_mα < 0** | Longitudinal static stability and positive trim lift requirement.
> **Q8: (A) (l_t S_t) / (c̄ S)** | Standard definition of horizontal tail volume ratio.
> **Q9: (B) Strictly negative** | Downward elevator deflection ($\delta_e > 0$) produces nose-down pitch $\implies C_{m\delta_e} < 0$.
> **Q10: (B) Forward of the stick-fixed neutral point (x'_np < x_np)** | Floating elevator relieves restoring lift $\implies x'_{np} < x_{np}$.
>
> **Q11: (B) 70 m/s**
> $V_{\text{stall,acc}} = 35\sqrt{4.0} = 35(2) = \mathbf{70\text{ m/s}}$.
>
> **Q12: (B) 7.4**
> $n = 1 + \frac{160^2}{10(400)} = 1 + \frac{25600}{4000} = 1 + 6.4 = \mathbf{7.4\text{ g}}$.
>
> **Q13: (A) 3.0 g**
> Top of loop (inverted): $n = \frac{V^2}{gR} - 1 = \frac{120^2}{10(360)} - 1 = \frac{14400}{3600} - 1 = 4 - 1 = \mathbf{3.0\text{ g}}$.
>
> **Q14: (B) 872 m**
> $R = \frac{140^2}{9.81\sqrt{2.5^2 - 1}} = \frac{19600}{9.81\sqrt{5.25}} = \frac{19600}{9.81(2.2913)} = \frac{19600}{22.477} \approx \mathbf{872.0\text{ m}}$.
>
> **Q15: (C) 9.21 deg/s**
> $\omega = \frac{g\sqrt{n^2-1}}{V} = \frac{9.81(2.2913)}{140} = \frac{22.477}{140} = 0.16055\text{ rad/s}$.
> In deg/s: $0.16055 \times \frac{180}{\pi} \approx \mathbf{9.20\text{ deg/s} \approx 9.21\text{ deg/s}}$.
>
> **Q16: (B) 126.5 m/s**
> $V^* = \sqrt{\frac{2(6.0)(2200)}{1.1(1.5)}} = \sqrt{\frac{26400}{1.65}} = \sqrt{16000} \approx \mathbf{126.49\text{ m/s} \approx 126.5\text{ m/s}}$.
>
> **Q17: (B) 1.10 g**
> $\Delta n = \frac{0.5(1.225)(150)(5.0)(0.80)(6)}{2000} = \frac{2205}{2000} = \mathbf{1.1025\text{ g} \approx 1.10\text{ g}}$.
>
> **Q18: (C) 11.46°**
> $\alpha_{\text{trim}} = -\frac{0.10}{-0.50} = 0.20\text{ rad} = 0.20 \times \frac{180}{\pi} \approx \mathbf{11.46^\circ}$.
>
> **Q19: (C) 15.0%**
> $SM = -\frac{-0.675}{4.5} = 0.15 = \mathbf{15.0\%}$.
>
> **Q20: (B) 0.50**
> $V_H = \frac{5.0 \times 4.0}{2.0 \times 20.0} = \frac{20.0}{40.0} = \mathbf{0.50}$.
>
> **Q21: (B) 0.51**
> $\frac{x_{np}}{\bar{c}} = 0.25 + 0.50(1.0)(0.80)(1 - 0.35) = 0.25 + 0.40(0.65) = 0.25 + 0.26 = \mathbf{0.51}$.
>
> **Q22: (A) +0.0417 rad**
> $\delta_e = -\frac{0.08 + (-0.60)(0.05)}{-1.20} = -\frac{0.08 - 0.03}{-1.20} = -\frac{0.05}{-1.20} = \mathbf{+0.04167\text{ rad}}$.
>
> **Q23: (B) -2.0°**
> $\delta_{e,\text{float}} = -\frac{-0.006}{-0.015}(+5.0^\circ) = -0.4(+5.0^\circ) = \mathbf{-2.0^\circ}$.
>
> **Q24: (B) Statically stable with SM = +16%**
> $SM = \frac{0.48 - 0.32}{\bar{c}} = +0.16 = \mathbf{+16\%}$ (Stable because CG is ahead of NP).
>
> **Q25: (B) 447 m**
> $R = \frac{200^2}{10\sqrt{9^2 - 1}} = \frac{40000}{10\sqrt{80}} = \frac{40000}{10(8.944)} = \frac{40000}{89.44} \approx \mathbf{447.2\text{ m}}$.
>
> **Q26: 509.7**
> $n = 1 + \frac{V^2}{gR} \implies 5.5 = 1 + \frac{150^2}{9.81 R} \implies 4.5 = \frac{22500}{9.81 R} \implies R = \frac{22500}{4.5 \times 9.81} = \frac{5000}{9.81} \approx \mathbf{509.7\text{ m}}$.
>
> **Q27: 9.73**
> $\omega = \frac{g\tan(60^\circ)}{V} = \frac{9.81(\sqrt{3})}{100} = \frac{9.81(1.73205)}{100} = \frac{16.991}{100} = 0.16991\text{ rad/s}$.
> In deg/s: $0.16991 \times \frac{180}{\pi} \approx \mathbf{9.73\text{ deg/s}}$.
>
> **Q28: 13.0**
> $x'_{np}/\bar{c} = 0.54 - 0.06 = 0.48$.
> $SM' = \frac{0.48 - 0.35}{\bar{c}} = 0.13 = \mathbf{13.0\%}$.
>
> **Q29: 2.22**
> $\Delta n = \frac{0.5(1.225)(120)(4.8)(0.85)(7.5)}{1800} = \frac{2249.1}{1800} = 1.2217\text{ g}$.
> $n_{\text{gust}} = 1 + 1.2217 = \mathbf{2.22\text{ g}}$.
>
> **Q30: -0.020**
> $\Delta\delta_e = -\frac{C_{m\alpha}}{C_{m\delta_e}}\Delta\alpha_{\text{trim}} = -\frac{-0.55}{-1.10}(+0.04) = -0.5(+0.04) = \mathbf{-0.020\text{ rad}}$.
