### Step 3 — Aerodynamics & Propulsion Flash Scan (15 min)
Open `06 - FORMULA SHEETS/Aerodynamics - Formulas.md` & `Propulsion - Formulas.md`:
- **Incompressible Potential Flow:** $u = \frac{\partial \phi}{\partial x} = \frac{\partial \psi}{\partial y}$, Circulation $\Gamma = \oint \vec{V}\cdot d\vec{r}$, Kutta-Joukowski lift $L' = \rho_\infty V_\infty \Gamma$.
- **Thin Airfoil Theory:** Symmetric: $C_l = 2\pi\alpha, x_{\text{ac}} = c/4$. Cambered: $C_l = 2\pi(\alpha - \alpha_{L=0})$ with $\alpha_{L=0} = -\frac{1}{\pi}\int_0^\pi \frac{dz}{dx}(\cos\theta_0 - 1)d\theta_0$.
- **Finite Wing:** $C_D = C_{D0} + \frac{C_L^2}{\pi e \text{AR}}$, Induced drag $C_{Di} = \frac{C_L^2}{\pi e \text{AR}}$.
- **Compressible Gas Dynamics:** Isentropic flow $\frac{T_0}{T} = 1 + \frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2, \frac{p_0}{p} = (1 + \frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2)^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}}$, Normal shock $M_2^2 = \frac{2 + (\gamma-1)M_1^2}{2\gamma M_1^2 - (\gamma-1)}$, Prandtl-Meyer function $\nu(M) = \sqrt{\frac{\gamma+1}{\gamma-1}}\tan^{-1}\sqrt{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma+1}(M^2-1)} - \tan^{-1}\sqrt{M^2-1}$.
- **Propulsion & Turbojets:** Brayton cycle $\eta_{\text{thermal}} = 1 - \frac{1}{r_p^{(\gamma-1)/\gamma}}$, Specific thrust $\frac{F}{\dot{m}_a} = (1+f)V_e - V_\infty$, TSFC $= \frac{\dot{m}_f}{F}$, Tsiolkovsky rocket equation $\Delta v = I_{\text{sp}} g_0 \ln\left(\frac{m_0}{m_f}\right)$.

### Step 3 — D1 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** In thin airfoil theory, the theoretical lift curve slope $dC_l/d\alpha$ of any 2D thin airfoil is:
> **(A)** $\pi\text{ rad}^{-1}$ **(B)** $2\pi\text{ rad}^{-1}$ **(C)** $4\pi\text{ rad}^{-1}$ **(D)** Independent of chord

> **Q2 (1 mark):** For an airplane in steady coordinated level turn at bank angle $\phi = 60^\circ$, the load factor $n$ is:
> **(A)** $1.414$ **(B)** $1.732$ **(C)** $2.000$ **(D)** $4.000$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A satellite is in a circular orbit around Earth ($\mu = 3.986\times 10^5\text{ km}^3/\text{s}^2$) at radius $r = 10,000\text{ km}$. The orbital speed $v$ (in $\text{km/s}$) is:
> **(A)** $5.65\text{ km/s}$ **(B)** $6.31\text{ km/s}$ **(C)** $7.90\text{ km/s}$ **(D)** $11.18\text{ km/s}$

> **Q4 (2 marks):** A rocket engine produces thrust $F = 50.0\text{ kN}$ while consuming propellant at $\dot{m} = 20.0\text{ kg/s}$. If $g_0 = 9.81\text{ m/s}^2$, the specific impulse $I_{\text{sp}}$ (in seconds) is:
> **(A)** $204.0\text{ s}$ **(B)** $254.8\text{ s}$ **(C)** $300.0\text{ s}$ **(D)** $350.5\text{ s}$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A pin-ended column of length $L = 2.0\text{ m}$ has flexural rigidity $EI = 4.0\times 10^4\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2$. The Euler critical buckling load $P_{\text{cr}}$ (in $\text{kN}$) is ___ $\text{kN}$ (round off to 2 decimal places).

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D1 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) 2π rad⁻¹**
> Thin airfoil theory fundamental invariant $a_0 = 2\pi$.
>
> **Q2: (C) 2.000**
> $n = \frac{1}{\cos 60^\circ} = \frac{1}{0.5} = \mathbf{2.000}$.
>
> **Q3: (B) 6.31 km/s**
> $v = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{r}} = \sqrt{\frac{3.986\times 10^5}{10,000}} = \sqrt{39.86} \approx \mathbf{6.313\text{ km/s} \approx 6.31\text{ km/s}}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) 254.8 s**
> $I_{\text{sp}} = \frac{F}{\dot{m} g_0} = \frac{50000\text{ N}}{(20.0\text{ kg/s})(9.81\text{ m/s}^2)} = \frac{50000}{196.2} \approx \mathbf{254.84\text{ s} \approx 254.8\text{ s}}$.
>
> **Q5: 98.70**
> $L_e = L = 2.0\text{ m}$.
> $P_{\text{cr}} = \frac{\pi^2(4.0\times 10^4)}{2.0^2} = \frac{394784.18}{4} = 98,696\text{ N} = \mathbf{98.70\text{ kN}}$.
