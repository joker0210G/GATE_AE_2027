### Step 2 — The Ideal Air-Standard Brayton Cycle (20 min)
Open `01 - Thermodynamics and Brayton Cycle`.

```
                    THE IDEAL BRAYTON CYCLE (T-s & P-v DIAGRAMS)
           T ^                                          P ^
             │        3 (TIT T_03)                        │    2        3
             │       ╭───╮  q_in (p = const)              │    ╭────────╮ (p = const)
             │      ╭╯   ╰╮                               │   ╭╯        ╰╮
             │    2 ●     ● 4 (Turbine Exit)              │  1●          ●4 (p = const)
             │     │       │                              │   ╰──────────╯
             │   1 ●───────● 1 (q_out)                    └───────────────────► v
             └───────────────────────► s
```

1. **The 4 Ideal Thermodynamic Processes:**
   - Process 1–2: Reversible adiabatic (Isentropic) compression ($s_2 = s_1$).
   - Process 2–3: Constant pressure heat addition in combustor ($p_3 = p_2$, $q_{\text{in}} = c_p(T_3 - T_2)$).
   - Process 3–4: Reversible adiabatic (Isentropic) expansion in turbine ($s_4 = s_3$).
   - Process 4–1: Constant pressure heat rejection ($p_4 = p_1$, $q_{\text{out}} = c_p(T_4 - T_1)$).
2. **Cycle Pressure Ratio ($r_p$):**
   $$r_p \equiv \frac{p_2}{p_1} = \frac{p_3}{p_4} \implies \frac{T_2}{T_1} = \frac{T_3}{T_4} = r_p^{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma}}$$
3. **Thermal Efficiency of Ideal Brayton Cycle:**
   $$\eta_{\text{th,ideal}} = 1 - \frac{q_{\text{out}}}{q_{\text{in}}} = 1 - \frac{c_p(T_4 - T_1)}{c_p(T_3 - T_2)} = 1 - \frac{T_1(T_4/T_1 - 1)}{T_2(T_3/T_2 - 1)}$$
   $$\boxed{\eta_{\text{th,ideal}} = 1 - \frac{1}{r_p^{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma}}} = 1 - \frac{T_1}{T_2}}$$
   - **Crucial Invariant:** Ideal Brayton thermal efficiency depends **ONLY ON PRESSURE RATIO $r_p$**, and is independent of maximum cycle temperature $T_3$!

---

### Step 3 — Maximum Specific Net Work Output Optimization (25 min)

1. **Specific Net Work Output ($w_{\text{net}}$):**
   $$w_{\text{net}} = w_t - w_c = c_p(T_3 - T_4) - c_p(T_2 - T_1) = c_p T_1\left(\tau - \theta - \frac{\tau}{\theta} + 1\right)$$
   *(where $\tau \equiv \frac{T_3}{T_1}$ is the maximum-to-minimum cycle temperature ratio, and $\theta \equiv \frac{T_2}{T_1} = r_p^{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma}}$ is the compression temperature ratio).*

2. **Condition for Maximum Specific Work:**
   Setting $\frac{dw_{\text{net}}}{d\theta} = 0 \implies -1 + \frac{\tau}{\theta^2} = 0 \implies \theta_{\text{opt}} = \sqrt{\tau}$:
   $$\boxed{T_{2,\text{opt}} = T_{4,\text{opt}} = \sqrt{T_1 T_3}}$$
   $$\boxed{r_{p,\text{opt}} = \left(\frac{T_3}{T_1}\right)^{\frac{\gamma}{2(\gamma-1)}} = \tau^{\frac{\gamma}{2(\gamma-1)}}}$$

3. **Maximum Net Work Formula:**
   $$\boxed{w_{\text{net},\max} = c_p T_1(\sqrt{\tau} - 1)^2 = c_p\left(\sqrt{T_3} - \sqrt{T_1}\right)^2}$$

```
                BRAYTON WORK OUTPUT vs. PRESSURE RATIO
         w_net ^
               │               r_p,opt = (T_3/T_1)^(γ / [2(γ−1)])
               │                    ╭─────╮
               │                  ╭─╯     ╰─╮
               │                ╭─╯         ╰─╮
               │               ╭╯             ╰╮
             0 └───────────────●───────────────●──────► r_p
                              r_p = 1         r_p,max = (T_3/T_1)^(γ/(γ−1))
                              (w_net = 0)     (w_net = 0, T_2 = T_3)
```

### Step 4 — Non-Ideal (Real) Brayton Cycle with Component Efficiencies (15 min)

1. **Compressor Isentropic Efficiency ($\eta_c$):**
   $$\boxed{\eta_c \equiv \frac{h_{2s} - h_1}{h_2 - h_1} = \frac{T_1\left(r_p^{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma}} - 1\right)}{T_2 - T_1} \implies T_2 = T_1\left[1 + \frac{r_p^{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma}} - 1}{\eta_c}\right]}$$
2. **Turbine Isentropic Efficiency ($\eta_t$):**
   $$\boxed{\eta_t \equiv \frac{h_3 - h_4}{h_3 - h_{4s}} = \frac{T_3 - T_4}{T_3\left[1 - \frac{1}{r_p^{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma}}}\right]} \implies T_4 = T_3\left[1 - \eta_t\left(1 - \frac{1}{r_p^{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma}}}\right)\right]}$$
3. **Real Cycle Net Work & Thermal Efficiency:**
   $$w_{\text{net,real}} = c_p(T_3 - T_4) - c_p(T_2 - T_1)$$
   $$\boxed{\eta_{\text{th,real}} = \frac{w_{\text{net,real}}}{q_{\text{in}}} = \frac{c_p(T_3 - T_4) - c_p(T_2 - T_1)}{c_p(T_3 - T_2)}}$$

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Maximum Work vs. Maximum Efficiency Pressure Ratio**
> - Maximum Work occurs at $r_{p,\text{opt}} = \tau^{\frac{\gamma}{2(\gamma-1)}}$.
> - Maximum Efficiency for an ideal cycle occurs at $r_{p,\max} = \tau^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}}$ (where $w_{\text{net}} \to 0$!).
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Isentropic Efficiency Subscripts**
> $\eta_c = \frac{T_{2s} - T_1}{T_2 - T_1} < 1$ (Ideal on TOP), but $\eta_t = \frac{T_3 - T_4}{T_3 - T_{4s}} < 1$ (Actual on TOP)!

### Step 3 — D5 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** The thermal efficiency of an ideal air-standard Brayton cycle depends strictly upon:
> **(A)** Maximum cycle temperature $T_3$
> **(B)** Cycle pressure ratio $r_p$
> **(C)** Mass flow rate of air
> **(D)** Combustor fuel-to-air ratio

> **Q2 (1 mark):** In an ideal Brayton cycle operating between minimum temperature $T_1$ and maximum temperature $T_3$, maximum specific net work output is obtained when compressor discharge temperature $T_2$ equals:
> **(A)** $\frac{T_1 + T_3}{2}$ **(B)** $\sqrt{T_1 T_3}$ **(C)** $\frac{T_1 T_3}{T_1 + T_3}$ **(D)** $T_3 - T_1$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** An ideal Brayton cycle operates with compressor inlet temperature $T_1 = 300\text{ K}$ and turbine inlet temperature $T_3 = 1875\text{ K}$ ($\gamma = 1.4, c_p = 1.005\text{ kJ/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}$). The maximum specific net work output $w_{\text{net},\max}$ (in $\text{kJ/kg}$) is:
> **(A)** $342.5\text{ kJ/kg}$ **(B)** $482.4\text{ kJ/kg}$ **(C)** $678.4\text{ kJ/kg}$ **(D)** $850.0\text{ kJ/kg}$

> **Q4 (2 marks):** An ideal gas turbine operates with pressure ratio $r_p = 8.0$ and $\gamma = 1.4$. The thermal efficiency $\eta_{\text{th}}$ is:
> **(A)** $35.2\%$ **(B)** $44.8\%$ **(C)** $52.4\%$ **(D)** $61.0\%$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** An ideal Brayton cycle operates between $T_{\min} = 290\text{ K}$ and $T_{\max} = 1450\text{ K}$ with $\gamma = 1.4$. The optimum pressure ratio $r_{p,\text{opt}}$ for maximum specific work output is ___ (round off to 2 decimal places).

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D5 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) Cycle pressure ratio r_p**
> $\eta_{\text{th}} = 1 - \frac{1}{r_p^{(\gamma-1)/\gamma}}$.
>
> **Q2: (B) √(T₁ T₃)**
> Differentiating $w_{\text{net}}$ gives the geometric mean: $T_2 = T_4 = \sqrt{T_1 T_3}$.
>
> **Q3: (C) 678.4 kJ/kg**
> $\tau = \frac{1875}{300} = 6.25 \implies \sqrt{\tau} = \sqrt{6.25} = 2.50$.
> $w_{\text{net},\max} = c_p T_1(\sqrt{\tau} - 1)^2 = (1.005)(300)(2.50 - 1)^2 = 301.5(1.5)^2 = 301.5(2.25) \approx \mathbf{678.375\text{ kJ/kg} \approx 678.4\text{ kJ/kg}}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) 44.8%**
> $\eta_{\text{th}} = 1 - \frac{1}{8^{2/7}} = 1 - \frac{1}{8^{0.2857}} = 1 - \frac{1}{1.8114} = 1 - 0.5520 = 0.4480 = \mathbf{44.8\%}$.
>
> **Q5: 16.72**
> $\tau = \frac{1450}{290} = 5.0$.
> $r_{p,\text{opt}} = \tau^{\frac{\gamma}{2(\gamma-1)}} = 5.0^{\frac{1.4}{2(0.4)}} = 5.0^{1.4/0.8} = 5.0^{1.75} \approx \mathbf{16.718 \approx 16.72}$.
