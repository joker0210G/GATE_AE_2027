### Step 2 — Integral Momentum Equation (Reynolds Transport Theorem) (20 min)
Open `01.2b - Conservation of Linear Momentum`.

$$\boxed{\sum \vec{F} = \frac{\partial}{\partial t}\iiint_{\text{CV}} \rho\vec{V}\,d\mathcal{V} + \iint_{\text{CS}} \rho\vec{V}(\vec{V}\cdot\hat{n})\,dA}$$

Where $\sum \vec{F} = \vec{F}_{\text{pressure}} + \vec{F}_{\text{viscous}} + \vec{F}_{\text{gravity}} + \vec{R}_{\text{reaction}}$.

#### 1. 1D Steady Control Volume Momentum Balance:
$$\sum F_x = \dot{m}(V_{x,\text{out}} - V_{x,\text{in}}) = \dot{m}\Delta V_x$$

#### 2. Momentum Flux Correction Factor ($\beta$):
Because velocity profiles $u(y)$ are non-uniform across practical ducts:
$$\boxed{\beta = \frac{1}{A \bar{V}^2} \iint u^2\,dA}$$
- Uniform flow: $\mathbf{\beta = 1.0}$
- Fully developed laminar pipe flow ($u(r) = U_{\max}(1 - r^2/R^2)$): $\mathbf{\beta = \frac{4}{3} \approx 1.33}$
- Fully developed 2D laminar channel flow: $\mathbf{\beta = \frac{6}{5} = 1.20}$
- Turbulent pipe flow ($1/7$-th power law): $\mathbf{\beta \approx 1.02}$

---

### Step 3 — Aerodynamic Profile Drag via Wake Momentum Deficit (20 min)

By applying the momentum balance to a rectangular control volume enclosing an airfoil in a free stream $U_\infty$:

```
               WAKE INTEGRATION CONTROL VOLUME
      ──────────────────────────────────────────────── Free stream U_inf
               ┌───────────────────────┐
       U_inf   │       Airfoil         │  u(y) < U_inf (Wake Deficit)
      ────────►│          ▄▄▄          │──────► 
               │         ▀▀▀▀▀         │
               └───────────────────────┘
      ────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

$$\boxed{D' = \int_{-\infty}^\infty \rho u(y)\left[U_\infty - u(y)\right] dy = \rho_\infty U_\infty^2 \theta}$$

Where $\theta$ is the **Momentum Thickness** of the wake:
$$\boxed{\theta = \int_{-\infty}^\infty \frac{u(y)}{U_\infty}\left(1 - \frac{u(y)}{U_\infty}\right) dy}$$

- **Section Profile Drag Coefficient ($c_d$):**
  $$\boxed{c_d = \frac{D'}{\frac{1}{2}\rho_\infty U_\infty^2 c} = 2 \frac{\theta}{c}}$$
  *(This is the exact formula used in wind tunnel wake survey measurements to calculate airfoil drag without surface pressure taps!)*

### Step 4 — Rocket & Jet Engine Propulsion Thrust (10 min)

Applying momentum conservation across an air-breathing jet engine vs. rocket engine:

$$\boxed{T = \dot{m}_e V_e - \dot{m}_a V_0 + (p_e - p_a)A_e}$$

- **For a Rocket Engine ($\dot{m}_a = 0$):**
  $$T = \dot{m} V_e + (p_e - p_a)A_e = \dot{m} C_j \quad (\text{where } C_j = V_e + \frac{(p_e-p_a)A_e}{\dot{m}} \text{ is effective exhaust velocity})$$

---

### Step 5 — Differential Momentum Equations (Navier-Stokes & Euler) (5 min)

1. **Cauchy Momentum Equation (All Continuous Media):**
   $$\rho \frac{D\vec{V}}{Dt} = -\nabla p + \nabla \cdot \boldsymbol{\tau} + \rho\vec{g}$$
2. **Navier-Stokes Equations (Incompressible Newtonian Fluids):**
   $$\boxed{\rho \frac{D\vec{V}}{Dt} = -\nabla p + \mu \nabla^2 \vec{V} + \rho\vec{g}}$$
3. **Euler Equation (Inviscid Flow, $\mu = 0$):**
   $$\rho \frac{D\vec{V}}{Dt} = -\nabla p + \rho\vec{g}$$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A wake velocity survey downstream of an airfoil of chord $c = 1\text{ m}$ in free stream $U_\infty = 50\text{ m/s}$ yields a momentum thickness $\theta = 0.015\text{ m}$. The section drag coefficient $c_d$ is:
> **(A)** $0.015$ **(B)** $0.030$ **(C)** $0.0075$ **(D)** $0.060$

> **Q4 (1 mark):** For an ideal rocket engine operating in a vacuum ($p_a = 0$), with exhaust area $A_e = 0.2\text{ m}^2$, exit pressure $p_e = 50\text{ kPa}$, mass flow rate $\dot{m} = 20\text{ kg/s}$, and exit velocity $V_e = 3000\text{ m/s}$, the thrust generated is:
> **(A)** $60\text{ kN}$ **(B)** $70\text{ kN}$ **(C)** $50\text{ kN}$ **(D)** $10\text{ kN}$

> **Q5 (1 mark):** In the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations $\rho \frac{D\vec{V}}{Dt} = -\nabla p + \mu \nabla^2 \vec{V} + \rho\vec{g}$, the term $\mu \nabla^2 \vec{V}$ represents:
> **(A)** Pressure force per unit volume **(B)** Viscous shear force per unit volume **(C)** Convective inertia **(D)** Body force

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D4 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) 4/3**
> For parabolic laminar pipe flow $u(r) = U_{\max}(1 - r^2/R^2)$ with $\bar{V} = U_{\max}/2$, integrating $\frac{1}{A \bar{V}^2}\iint u^2 dA = \mathbf{4/3}$.
>
> **Q2: (B) 4000 N**
> $F_x = \dot{m} V_x = (\rho A V) V = \rho A V^2 = (1000)(0.01)(20^2) = 10(400) = \mathbf{4000\text{ N}}$.
>
> **Q3: (B) 0.030**
> $c_d = 2 \frac{\theta}{c} = 2 \frac{0.015}{1.0} = \mathbf{0.030}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) 70 kN**
> $T = \dot{m} V_e + (p_e - p_a)A_e = 20(3000) + (50000 - 0)(0.2) = 60,000 + 10,000 = 70,000\text{ N} = \mathbf{70\text{ kN}}$.
>
> **Q5: (B) Viscous shear force per unit volume**
> $\mu \nabla^2 \vec{V}$ represents the net viscous diffusion force per unit volume acting on the fluid parcel.
