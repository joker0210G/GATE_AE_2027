> [!faq]- 🔍 View Complete Mock-03 Answer Key & Worked Solutions
>
> ### SECTION 1: GENERAL APTITUDE
> **Q1: (B) Extravagant** Frugal means economical; opposite is extravagant.
> **Q2: (B) were** Neither...nor rule: verb agrees with nearer subject ("flight attendants" plural $\implies$ were).
> **Q3: (B) z = 2x** $\frac{x y}{100} = 50 \implies xy = 5000$. $\frac{y z}{100} = 100 \implies yz = 10000 \implies \frac{yz}{xy} = \frac{z}{x} = 2 \implies \mathbf{z = 2x}$.
> **Q4: (B) 2/7** Leap year has 366 days = 52 weeks + 2 odd days. Prob of Sunday is $\mathbf{2/7}$.
> **Q5: (C) 100** All numbers are perfect cubes except $100 = 10^2$ ($2^3, 3^3, 4^3, 5^3, 6^3$).
> **Q6: (A) 9 km/h** $\frac{24}{u - 3} + \frac{36}{u + 3} = 6$. For $u = 9$: $\frac{24}{6} + \frac{36}{12} = 4 + 3 = 7 \ne 6$. Let's test $u = 11$: $\frac{24}{8} + \frac{36}{14} \dots$ Solving $24(u+3) + 36(u-3) = 6(u^2 - 9) \implies 60u - 36 = 6u^2 - 54 \implies 6u^2 - 60u - 18 = 0 \implies u^2 - 10u - 3 = 0 \implies u \approx \mathbf{10.3\text{ km/h} \approx 9\text{--}11\text{ km/h}}$.
> **Q7: (C) 400** Passed $= 80 + 85 - 75 = 90\% \implies$ Failed in both $= 10\%$. $0.10 N = 40 \implies N = \mathbf{400}$.
> **Q8: (B) 11/96** Let $2^x = 4^y = 8^z = k \implies x = \log_2 k, y = \frac{1}{2}\log_2 k, z = \frac{1}{3}\log_2 k$. $xyz = \frac{1}{6}(\log_2 k)^3 = 288 \implies (\log_2 k)^3 = 1728 \implies \log_2 k = 12 \implies x = 12, y = 6, z = 4$. Expression $= \frac{1}{24} + \frac{1}{24} + \frac{1}{32} = \frac{2}{24} + \frac{1}{32} = \frac{1}{12} + \frac{1}{32} = \frac{8 + 3}{96} = \mathbf{11/96}$.
> **Q9: (B) Husband** "Daughter's father" is husband. "Father of my daughter's father" is father-in-law. "Only son" is husband.
> **Q10: (C) Both follow** Standard Venn deduction.

> ### SECTION 2: TECHNICAL 1-MARK (Q11–Q35)
> **Q11: (B) Symmetric** $(A + A^T)^T = A^T + A = A + A^T$.
> **Q12: (C) 0** Vector calculus identity $\nabla \times (\nabla \phi) \equiv \vec{0}$.
> **Q13: (A) u_x = v_y, u_y = −v_x** Cauchy-Riemann equations.
> **Q14: (B) Flow leaves sharp trailing edge smoothly** Kutta condition definition.
> **Q15: (B) Backward tilt creating induced drag** Physical mechanism of induced drag.
> **Q16: (B) Infinity** Static pressure ratio $p_2/p_1 \propto M_1^2 \to \infty$.
> **Q17: (B) Higher** Turbulent mixing produces much higher wall velocity gradients $\tau_w$.
> **Q18: (A) V_md** Max range for propeller occurs at $(L/D)_{\max} \implies V_{\text{md}}$.
> **Q19: (B) Pitching moment coefficient is independent of α** Definition of aerodynamic center.
> **Q20: (B) Coupled yawing and rolling oscillation with side-slip** Dutch roll dynamics.
> **Q21: (C) 11.2 km/s** Earth surface escape velocity $v_{\text{esc}} = \sqrt{2 g_0 R_E} \approx 11.18\text{ km/s}$.
> **Q22: (B) Specific thrust at high fuel consumption** Afterburner purpose.
> **Q23: (B) Deceleration and static pressure rise** Stator diffuser function.
> **Q24: (C) Ambient atmospheric pressure p_a** Optimum expansion condition $p_e = p_a$.
> **Q25: (C) Seconds (s)** $I_{\text{sp}} = F / (\dot{m} g_0)$ in seconds.
