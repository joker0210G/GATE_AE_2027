### Step 2 — 2D Stress Transformation Equations (20 min)
Open `03 - Stress Transformations and Failure Criteria`.

Consider a cut plane inclined at counter-clockwise angle $\theta$ from the vertical $y$-face (or whose outward normal is rotated by $\theta$ from the $x$-axis):

```
                    2D STRESS TRANSFORMATION AT ANGLE θ
                            y ▲
                              │   n (Outward Normal)
                              │  /  Inclined at angle θ
                              │ /
                         ┌────┴────┐
                         │    •    │──────► x
                         └────┬────┘
                              │
```

1. **General Transformation Equations:**
   $$\boxed{\sigma_{x'} = \frac{\sigma_x + \sigma_y}{2} + \frac{\sigma_x - \sigma_y}{2}\cos 2\theta + \tau_{xy}\sin 2\theta}$$
   $$\boxed{\sigma_{y'} = \frac{\sigma_x + \sigma_y}{2} - \frac{\sigma_x - \sigma_y}{2}\cos 2\theta - \tau_{xy}\sin 2\theta}$$
   $$\boxed{\tau_{x'y'} = -\frac{\sigma_x - \sigma_y}{2}\sin 2\theta + \tau_{xy}\cos 2\theta}$$

2. **The Two Fundamental Stress Invariants:**
   - **First Invariant ($I_1$):** Sum of orthogonal normal stresses is invariant:
     $$\mathbf{\sigma_{x'} + \sigma_{y'} = \sigma_x + \sigma_y = \sigma_1 + \sigma_2 = I_1}$$
   - **Second Invariant ($I_2$):** Area/determinant is invariant:
     $$\mathbf{\sigma_x \sigma_y - \tau_{xy}^2 = \sigma_{x'} \sigma_{y'} - \tau_{x'y'}^2 = \sigma_1 \sigma_2 = I_2}$$

---

### Step 3 — Mohr's Circle Geometry & Principal Stresses (20 min)

Eliminating $\theta$ by squaring and adding:
$$\left(\sigma_{x'} - \frac{\sigma_x + \sigma_y}{2}\right)^2 + \tau_{x'y'}^2 = \left(\frac{\sigma_x - \sigma_y}{2}\right)^2 + \tau_{xy}^2 = R^2$$

```
                         MOHR'S CIRCLE OF STRESS
                                τ ▲
                                  │       Radius R
                                  │      ╭───────╮
                                  │     │    •(σ_x, τ_xy)
                                  │    │     │   │
                   ───────────────┼───┼──────C───┼────────► σ
                                  │  σ_2     │  σ_1
                                  │   (Center C = (σ_avg, 0))
                                  │      ╰───────╯
                                  │
```

1. **Circle Parameters:**
   - **Center ($C$):** $\mathbf{C = (\sigma_{\text{avg}}, 0) = \left(\frac{\sigma_x + \sigma_y}{2}, 0\right)}$.
   - **Radius ($R$):** $\mathbf{R = \sqrt{\left(\frac{\sigma_x - \sigma_y}{2}\right)^2 + \tau_{xy}^2}}$.

2. **Principal Stresses ($\sigma_1, \sigma_2$):**
   - On principal planes, **shear stress is ZERO** ($\tau = 0$):
     $$\boxed{\sigma_{1,2} = \sigma_{\text{avg}} \pm R = \frac{\sigma_x + \sigma_y}{2} \pm \sqrt{\left(\frac{\sigma_x - \sigma_y}{2}\right)^2 + \tau_{xy}^2}}$$
   - **Principal Angles ($\theta_p$):**
     $$\boxed{\tan 2\theta_p = \frac{2\tau_{xy}}{\sigma_x - \sigma_y}}$$

3. **Maximum In-Plane Shear Stress ($\tau_{\max,\text{in-plane}}$):**
   - Occurs at $45^\circ$ to principal planes ($\theta_s = \theta_p \pm 45^\circ$):
     $$\boxed{\tau_{\max,\text{in-plane}} = R = \frac{\sigma_1 - \sigma_2}{2} = \sqrt{\left(\frac{\sigma_x - \sigma_y}{2}\right)^2 + \tau_{xy}^2}}$$
   - Normal stress on maximum shear planes: $\mathbf{\sigma_{\text{normal}} = \sigma_{\text{avg}} = \frac{\sigma_x + \sigma_y}{2}}$.

---

### Step 4 — The 3D Absolute Maximum Shear Stress ($\tau_{\max,\text{abs}}$) (15 min)

Order the three 3D principal stresses: $\sigma_1 \ge \sigma_2 \ge \sigma_3$.

$$\boxed{\tau_{\max,\text{abs}} = \frac{\sigma_1 - \sigma_3}{2}}$$

```
              THE 3 REGIMES OF ABSOLUTE MAX SHEAR STRESS IN PLANE STRESS (σ_z = 0)
  Case 1: Opposite Signs (σ_1 > 0, σ_2 < 0) ──► σ_1 > σ_3=0 > σ_2
          τ_max,abs = (σ_1 − σ_2) / 2 = τ_max,in-plane  (In-plane governs!)

  Case 2: Both Tensile (σ_1 > 0, σ_2 > 0)   ──► σ_1 ≥ σ_2 ≥ σ_3=0
          τ_max,abs = (σ_1 − 0) / 2 = σ_1 / 2  (Out-of-plane governs! 🔴 CRITICAL TRAP!)

  Case 3: Both Compressive (σ_1 < 0, σ_2 < 0) ──► σ_1=0 ≥ σ_2 ≥ σ_3=σ_2
          τ_max,abs = (0 − σ_2) / 2 = |σ_2| / 2  (Out-of-plane governs!)
```

---

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: The Out-of-Plane Shear Stress Trap**
> If $\sigma_1 = 100\text{ MPa}$ and $\sigma_2 = 40\text{ MPa}$ in plane stress ($\sigma_3 = 0$), in-plane max shear is $\frac{100-40}{2} = 30\text{ MPa}$. But the ABSOLUTE maximum shear stress is $\mathbf{\frac{100 - 0}{2} = 50\text{ MPa}}$!
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Principal Plane Shear Stress**
> Shear stress on a principal plane is **EXACTLY ZERO**. Never calculate a non-zero shear stress on a principal plane.

### Step 3 — D3 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** In a 2D state of stress, the shear stress on a principal plane is:
> **(A)** Equal to $\sigma_{\text{avg}}$ **(B)** Equal to $R$ **(C)** Strictly zero **(D)** Equal to $\tau_{xy}$

> **Q2 (1 mark):** In a state of plane stress ($\sigma_z = 0$), the in-plane principal stresses are $\sigma_1 = 140\text{ MPa}$ and $\sigma_2 = 60\text{ MPa}$. The absolute maximum shear stress $\tau_{\max,\text{abs}}$ in the material is:
> **(A)** $40.0\text{ MPa}$ **(B)** $60.0\text{ MPa}$ **(C)** $70.0\text{ MPa}$ **(D)** $140.0\text{ MPa}$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A point in a structural member is subjected to $\sigma_x = 90\text{ MPa}, \sigma_y = 10\text{ MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy} = 30\text{ MPa}$. The maximum principal stress $\sigma_1$ (in $\text{MPa}$) is:
> **(A)** $90.0\text{ MPa}$ **(B)** $100.0\text{ MPa}$ **(C)** $110.0\text{ MPa}$ **(D)** $120.0\text{ MPa}$

> **Q4 (2 marks):** For the stress state in Q3 ($\sigma_x = 90\text{ MPa}, \sigma_y = 10\text{ MPa}, \tau_{xy} = 30\text{ MPa}$), the orientation of the principal plane $\theta_p$ (counter-clockwise from the $x$-axis) is:
> **(A)** $18.4^\circ$ **(B)** $22.5^\circ$ **(C)** $36.9^\circ$ **(D)** $45.0^\circ$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A thin plate in plane stress is subjected to $\sigma_x = 100\text{ MPa}, \sigma_y = -60\text{ MPa}$, and $\tau_{xy} = 60\text{ MPa}$. The maximum in-plane shear stress $\tau_{\max,\text{in-plane}}$ (in $\text{MPa}$) is ___ $\text{MPa}$.

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D3 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (C) Strictly zero**
> Principal planes are defined as the planes of zero shear stress.
>
> **Q2: (C) 70.0 MPa**
> Since $\sigma_1 = 140\text{ MPa}, \sigma_2 = 60\text{ MPa}$, and $\sigma_3 = 0$, the ordered 3D principal stresses are $140 \ge 60 \ge 0$.
> $\tau_{\max,\text{abs}} = \frac{\sigma_1 - \sigma_3}{2} = \frac{140 - 0}{2} = \mathbf{70.0\text{ MPa}}$.
>
> **Q3: (B) 100.0 MPa**
> $\sigma_{\text{avg}} = \frac{90 + 10}{2} = 50\text{ MPa}$.
> $R = \sqrt{\left(\frac{90 - 10}{2}\right)^2 + 30^2} = \sqrt{40^2 + 30^2} = \sqrt{1600 + 900} = \sqrt{2500} = 50\text{ MPa}$.
> $\sigma_1 = \sigma_{\text{avg}} + R = 50 + 50 = \mathbf{100.0\text{ MPa}}$.
>
> **Q4: (A) 18.4°**
> $\tan 2\theta_p = \frac{2\tau_{xy}}{\sigma_x - \sigma_y} = \frac{2(30)}{90 - 10} = \frac{60}{80} = 0.75$.
> $2\theta_p = \arctan(0.75) \approx 36.87^\circ \implies \mathbf{\theta_p \approx 18.43^\circ \approx 18.4^\circ}$.
>
> **Q5: 100**
> $R = \sqrt{\left(\frac{100 - (-60)}{2}\right)^2 + 60^2} = \sqrt{80^2 + 60^2} = \sqrt{6400 + 3600} = \sqrt{10000} = \mathbf{100\text{ MPa}}$.
