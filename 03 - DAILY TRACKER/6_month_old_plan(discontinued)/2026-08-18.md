**Group C — Polynomial Ratio at $\infty$:**
$$\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{P_m(x)}{Q_n(x)} = \begin{cases}0 & m < n \\ \frac{\text{leading coeff}_P}{\text{leading coeff}_Q} & m = n \\ \pm\infty & m > n\end{cases}$$

**Growth hierarchy at $\infty$ (tattoo this):**
$$\ln x \ll x^a \ll e^x \ll x^x \quad (a>0)$$

### Step 3 — 7 Indeterminate Forms & Resolution Roadmap (10 min)

The 7 forms: $\frac{0}{0}$, $\frac{\infty}{\infty}$, $0\cdot\infty$, $\infty-\infty$, $1^\infty$, $0^0$, $\infty^0$

**Resolution algorithm:**
```
Is it 0/0 or ∞/∞?
  → YES: L'Hôpital OR Taylor Series (Taylor is faster!)
  → NO:
    Is it 0·∞? → Convert to 0/0 or ∞/∞ first
    Is it ∞−∞? → Rationalise / combine fractions
    Is it 1^∞, 0^0, ∞^0? → Use 1^∞ formula: e^{lim g(f−1)}
```

### Step 4 — 5 Speed Tricks (10 min)
Read and internalise the shortcuts from your note:

**⚡ Trick 1 — $1^\infty$ Instant Formula (MOST TESTED):**
$$\lim[f(x)]^{g(x)} = e^{\lim g(x)[f(x)-1]} \quad \text{when }f\to1, g\to\infty$$
*Example:* $\lim_{x\to0}(1+3x)^{2/x} = e^{\lim\frac{2}{x}\cdot 3x} = e^6$

**⚡ Trick 2 — Taylor > L'Hôpital for $\frac{0}{0}$:**
Always try Taylor expansion first for limits as $x\to0$. Faster by 10x.
Key series to know cold:
$$\sin x = x - \frac{x^3}{6} + \cdots \quad \cos x = 1 - \frac{x^2}{2} + \cdots \quad e^x = 1+x+\frac{x^2}{2}+\cdots \quad \ln(1+x)=x-\frac{x^2}{2}+\cdots$$

**⚡ Trick 3 — $\frac{\sin(mx)}{x}$ in 3 seconds:**
$$\frac{\sin(5x)}{3x} = \frac{5}{3}\cdot\frac{\sin(5x)}{5x}\to\frac{5}{3}\cdot1=\frac{5}{3}$$

**⚡ Trick 4 — $\infty-\infty$ Rationalisation:**
$$\lim_{x\to\infty}(\sqrt{x^2+ax}-x) = \frac{a}{2}$$

**⚡ Trick 5 — L'Hôpital Rules to NEVER Break:**
1. Only for $\frac{0}{0}$ or $\frac{\infty}{\infty}$ — check the form FIRST
2. Differentiate numerator and denominator SEPARATELY (not quotient rule)
3. If result oscillates → switch to Squeeze Theorem

### Step 5 — 5 Traps (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1:** Applying L'Hôpital to $\frac{\cos x}{x}$ — form is $\frac{1}{0}$, NOT $\frac{0}{0}$ → L'Hôpital INVALID
> 🔴 **Trap 2:** $\lim_{x\to\infty}\frac{3x^3+5}{2x^3-7}=\frac{3}{2}$ NOT $\frac{3+5}{2-7}=-\frac{8}{5}$
> 🔴 **Trap 3:** $0^0$ is indeterminate — write as $e^{x\ln x}$ and evaluate $\lim x\ln x$ first
> 🔴 **Trap 4:** $\sin(1/x)$ at $x\to0$ oscillates — use Squeeze, NOT L'Hôpital
> 🔴 **Trap 5:** LHL and RHL must BOTH be finite for limit to exist — $\pm\infty$ means limit DNE

### D1 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions, 10 min

> **Q1 (1 mark):** $\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{\tan(3x)}{\sin(5x)}=$
> **(A)** $\frac{3}{5}$ **(B)** $\frac{5}{3}$ **(C)** $\frac{15}{1}$ **(D)** 1

> **Q2 (2 marks):** $\lim_{x\to0}(1+2x)^{5/x}=$
> **(A)** $e^5$ **(B)** $e^{10}$ **(C)** $e^{2/5}$ **(D)** $e^2$

> **Q3 (1 mark NAT):** $\lim_{x\to0}\dfrac{e^{3x}-1-3x}{x^2}=$ ___

> **Q4 (2 marks):** $\lim_{x\to\infty}(\sqrt{x^2+8x+3}-x)=$
> **(A)** 4 **(B)** 8 **(C)** 3 **(D)** ∞

> **Q5 (1 mark):** $\lim_{x\to0}x^2\cos(1/x)=$
> **(A)** 0 **(B)** 1 **(C)** DNE **(D)** ∞

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D1 Mini Quiz Answers
>
> **Q1: (A) 3/5**
> $\frac{\tan(3x)}{\sin(5x)} = \frac{\tan(3x)}{3x}\cdot\frac{3x}{5x}\cdot\frac{5x}{\sin(5x)} \to 1\cdot\frac{3}{5}\cdot1 = \frac{3}{5}$
>
> **Q2: (B) $e^{10}$**
> $1^\infty$ form: $\lim\frac{5}{x}\cdot[(1+2x)-1] = \lim\frac{5}{x}\cdot2x = 10 \Rightarrow e^{10}$
>
> **Q3: 9/2**
> Taylor: $e^{3x}=1+3x+\frac{9x^2}{2}+\cdots \Rightarrow \frac{e^{3x}-1-3x}{x^2}=\frac{9x^2/2}{x^2}=\frac{9}{2}$
>
> **Q4: (A) 4**
> $\sqrt{x^2+8x+3}-x = \frac{8x+3}{\sqrt{x^2+8x+3}+x}\to\frac{8x}{2x}=4$
> (Using $\sqrt{x^2+ax}-x\to a/2$: here $a=8\Rightarrow4$)
>
> **Q5: (A) 0**
> $-x^2\le x^2\cos(1/x)\le x^2$. Both bounds $\to0$. By Squeeze, limit $=0$.
