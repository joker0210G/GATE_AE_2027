### Step 2 — The 3 Mandatory Criteria for Longitudinal Static Stability (15 min)
Open `04.3 - Longitudinal Static Stability`.

```
                    LONGITUDINAL STATIC STABILITY (C_m vs α)
          C_m ^
              │  ╭───────── C_m0 > 0 (Positive Intercept)
              │  │  \
              │  │   \   Statically Stable Aircraft: Slope C_mα < 0
              │  │    \
          0 ──┼──┴─────\───────► α
              │         \ ▲ Trim Angle α_trim = −C_m0 / C_mα > 0
              │          \
```

#### 🔑 The 3 Invariant Stability Criteria:
1. $\boxed{C_{m0} > 0}$ (Positive zero-lift pitching moment — ensures positive trim lift).
2. $\boxed{C_{m\alpha} \equiv \frac{\partial C_m}{\partial \alpha} < 0}$ (Negative slope — creates a restoring pitch-down moment when angle of attack $\alpha$ increases).
3. $\boxed{C_{L,\text{trim}} > 0 \text{ at } C_m = 0}$ (Aircraft must be able to support its weight at trim).

- **Trim Angle of Attack:**
  $$C_m = C_{m0} + C_{m\alpha}\alpha = 0 \implies \boxed{\alpha_{\text{trim}} = -\frac{C_{m0}}{C_{m\alpha}} > 0}$$

### Step 3 — Component Contributions & The Horizontal Tail (20 min)

```
                    AIRCRAFT PITCHING MOMENT CONTRIBUTIONS
                         Wing-Body AC           CG                  Tail AC
                              │                 │                      │
                              ▼                 ▼                      ▼
                       ───────┬─────────────────┼──────────────────────┬──────
                              │◄── x_cg−x_ac ──►│◄──────── l_t ───────►│
                             L_wb                                     L_t
```

1. **Wing-Body Contribution:**
   $$C_{m,cg,wb} = C_{mac,wb} + C_{L,wb}\left(\frac{x_{cg} - x_{ac,wb}}{\bar{c}}\right)$$
   $$\frac{\partial C_{m,cg,wb}}{\partial \alpha} = a_{wb}\left(\frac{x_{cg} - x_{ac,wb}}{\bar{c}}\right)$$
   *(Since CG is almost always behind the wing aerodynamic center ($x_{cg} > x_{ac,wb}$), the wing-body is inherently **DESTABILIZING** ($\frac{\partial C_m}{\partial \alpha} > 0$)!).*

2. **Horizontal Tail Contribution:**
   - **Horizontal Tail Volume Ratio ($V_H$):**
     $$\boxed{V_H \equiv \frac{l_t S_t}{\bar{c} S}}$$
   - **Downwash Angle ($\epsilon$):** Induced by the wing vortices, reducing tail angle of attack:
     $$\alpha_t = \alpha\left(1 - \frac{d\epsilon}{d\alpha}\right) + i_t - \epsilon_0$$
   - **Tail Pitching Moment Derivative:**
     $$\frac{\partial C_{m,cg,t}}{\partial \alpha} = -V_H \eta_t a_t \left(1 - \frac{d\epsilon}{d\alpha}\right) \quad (\mathbf{Strongly\ STABILIZING\ / \ Negative!})$$
     *(where $\eta_t \equiv \frac{q_t}{q_\infty} \approx 0.9\text{--}1.0$ is tail dynamic pressure ratio).*

3. **Total Aircraft Pitching Moment Slope ($C_{m\alpha}$):**
   $$\boxed{C_{m\alpha} = a_{wb}\left(\frac{x_{cg} - x_{ac,wb}}{\bar{c}}\right) - V_H \eta_t a_t \left(1 - \frac{d\epsilon}{d\alpha}\right)}$$

---

### Step 4 — Stick-Fixed Neutral Point ($x_{np}$) & Static Margin ($SM$) (15 min)

1. **Stick-Fixed Neutral Point ($x_{np}$ or $h_n$):**
   - The specific CG location where total aircraft static stability is neutral ($C_{m\alpha} = 0$):
     $$\boxed{\frac{x_{np}}{\bar{c}} = \frac{x_{ac,wb}}{\bar{c}} + V_H \eta_t \frac{a_t}{a}\left(1 - \frac{d\epsilon}{d\alpha}\right)}$$
2. **Stick-Fixed Static Margin ($SM$ or $K_n$):**
   - The fundamental non-dimensional measure of longitudinal stability:
     $$\boxed{SM \equiv \frac{x_{np} - x_{cg}}{\bar{c}} = -\frac{C_{m\alpha}}{a}}$$
   - **CG Position Rules:**
     - $\mathbf{x_{cg} < x_{np}} \implies \mathbf{SM > 0} \implies \mathbf{C_{m\alpha} < 0}$ (**Statically Stable!** CG is AHEAD of Neutral Point).
     - $\mathbf{x_{cg} = x_{np}} \implies \mathbf{SM = 0} \implies \mathbf{C_{m\alpha} = 0}$ (**Neutrally Stable**).
     - $\mathbf{x_{cg} > x_{np}} \implies \mathbf{SM < 0} \implies \mathbf{C_{m\alpha} > 0}$ (**Statically Unstable!** CG is BEHIND Neutral Point).

---

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Static Margin Sign & CG Movement**
> Moving the CG aft (rearward) **DECREASES** the static margin and makes the aircraft less stable!
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Wing-Body vs. Tail Pitching Contributions**
> - The wing-body makes $C_{m\alpha}$ *positive* (destabilizing).
> - The horizontal tail makes $C_{m\alpha}$ *negative* (stabilizing). The tail MUST be large enough ($V_H$) to overcome the wing-body!

### Step 3 — D4 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** For an aircraft to possess longitudinal static stability and be trimmable at positive lift, the pitching moment curve must have:
> **(A)** $C_{m0} < 0$ and $C_{m\alpha} < 0$
> **(B)** $C_{m0} > 0$ and $C_{m\alpha} < 0$
> **(C)** $C_{m0} > 0$ and $C_{m\alpha} > 0$
> **(D)** $C_{m0} < 0$ and $C_{m\alpha} > 0$

> **Q2 (2 marks):** An aircraft has $C_m = 0.12 - 0.80\alpha$ (with $\alpha$ in radians). The trim angle of attack $\alpha_{\text{trim}}$ (in degrees) is:
> **(A)** $4.30^\circ$ **(B)** $8.59^\circ$ **(C)** $12.45^\circ$ **(D)** $15.00^\circ$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** An aircraft has total lift curve slope $a = 5.0\text{ rad}^{-1}$. If its pitching moment derivative is $C_{m\alpha} = -0.75\text{ rad}^{-1}$, the static margin $SM$ is:
> **(A)** $7.5\%$ **(B)** $12.5\%$ **(C)** $15.0\%$ **(D)** $20.0\%$

> **Q4 (1 mark):** If the center of gravity of an airplane is shifted rearward (aft):
> **(A)** Longitudinal static stability increases
> **(B)** Longitudinal static stability decreases
> **(C)** Neutral point moves forward
> **(D)** Tail volume ratio increases

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** An aircraft has $x_{ac,wb}/\bar{c} = 0.24$, wing area $S = 25\text{ m}^2$, mean chord $\bar{c} = 2.0\text{ m}$, horizontal tail area $S_t = 5.0\text{ m}^2$, tail arm $l_t = 6.0\text{ m}$, tail efficiency $\eta_t = 0.95$, $a_t/a = 0.85$, and downwash gradient $d\epsilon/d\alpha = 0.30$. The stick-fixed neutral point location $x_{np}/\bar{c}$ is ___ (round off to 3 decimal places).

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D4 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) C_m0 > 0 and C_mα < 0**
> $C_{m0} > 0$ allows trim at $\alpha > 0$, while $C_{m\alpha} < 0$ ensures a restorative pitching moment.
>
> **Q2: (B) 8.59°**
> $\alpha_{\text{trim}} = -\frac{C_{m0}}{C_{m\alpha}} = -\frac{0.12}{-0.80} = 0.15\text{ rad}$.
> In degrees: $\alpha_{\text{trim}} = 0.15 \times \frac{180}{\pi} = \frac{27}{\pi} \approx \mathbf{8.594^\circ \approx 8.59^\circ}$.
>
> **Q3: (C) 15.0%**
> $SM = -\frac{C_{m\alpha}}{a} = -\frac{-0.75}{5.0} = 0.15 = \mathbf{15.0\%\bar{c}}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) Longitudinal static stability decreases**
> As $x_{cg}$ increases (moves aft), $SM = \frac{x_{np} - x_{cg}}{\bar{c}}$ decreases $\implies C_{m\alpha}$ becomes less negative.
>
> **Q5: 0.579**
> $V_H = \frac{l_t S_t}{\bar{c} S} = \frac{6.0 \times 5.0}{2.0 \times 25.0} = \frac{30.0}{50.0} = 0.60$.
> $\frac{x_{np}}{\bar{c}} = 0.24 + 0.60(0.95)(0.85)(1 - 0.30) = 0.24 + 0.60(0.95)(0.85)(0.70) = 0.24 + 0.33915 = \mathbf{0.57915 \approx 0.579}$.
