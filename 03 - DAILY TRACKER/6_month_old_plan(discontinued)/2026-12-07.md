#### 🔑 Key Formulas & Invariants for Week 2:
1. **Symmetric Bending (Euler-Bernoulli Beam Theory):**
   - Flexure Formula: $\mathbf{\frac{M}{I} = \frac{\sigma}{y} = \frac{E}{R} \implies \sigma = \frac{M y}{I}}$.
   - Section Modulus: $Z \equiv \frac{I}{y_{\max}} \implies \sigma_{\max} = \frac{M}{Z}$.
   - Beam Curvature: $\mathbf{\kappa \equiv \frac{1}{R} = \frac{d^2 w}{dx^2} = -\frac{M}{EI}}$.
2. **Deflection Calculation Methods:**
   - Macaulay's Method: Singularity functions for discontinuous loading.
   - Moment-Area Theorems (Mohr's Theorems):
     - First Theorem (Slope): $\mathbf{\theta_{B/A} = \int_A^B \frac{M}{EI} \, dx = \frac{\text{Area of } M/EI \text{ diagram}}{1}}$.
     - Second Theorem (Deflection): $\mathbf{\delta_{B/A} = \int_A^B \frac{M x}{EI} \, dx = \frac{\text{Area} \cdot \bar{x}}{1}}$.
3. **Second Moments of Area & Product of Inertia ($I_{xy}$):**
   - $I_x = \int y^2 \, dA, \quad I_y = \int x^2 \, dA, \quad I_{xy} = \int x y \, dA$.
   - **Symmetry Rule:** If a cross-section has at least **ONE axis of symmetry**, the product of inertia is **EXACTLY ZERO**: $\mathbf{I_{xy} = 0}$!
   - Principal Axes Orientation: $\mathbf{\tan 2\theta = -\frac{2 I_{xy}}{I_x - I_y}}$.
4. **Unsymmetrical Bending Formula ($I_{xy} \ne 0$):**
   $$\boxed{\sigma_z = \left(\frac{M_x I_y - M_y I_{xy}}{I_x I_y - I_{xy}^2}\right) y + \left(\frac{M_y I_x - M_x I_{xy}}{I_x I_y - I_{xy}^2}\right) x}$$
   - **Neutral Axis Orientation ($\tan\alpha_{\text{NA}}$):**
     Setting $\sigma_z = 0$:
     $$\boxed{\tan\alpha_{\text{NA}} \equiv \frac{y}{x} = -\frac{M_y I_x - M_x I_{xy}}{M_x I_y - M_y I_{xy}}}$$
