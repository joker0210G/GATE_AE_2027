#### 📝 M4 / W3 Turbomachinery Mini-Test (30 Questions)

**BLOCK A: Direct Concept & Formula Recall (1 Mark Each, Q1–Q10)**

**Q1.** In an axial compressor stage, the stationary stator blade row serves to:
(A) Add mechanical work to the flow
(B) Convert fluid kinetic energy into static pressure and realign the flow
(C) Reduce the static temperature of the air
(D) Accelerate the flow to supersonic speeds

**Q2.** The fundamental geometric relation linking blade speed $U$, axial velocity $C_a$, absolute angle $\alpha$, and relative angle $\beta$ (measured from axial direction) is:
(A) $U = C_a(\tan\alpha - \tan\beta)$
(B) $U = C_a(\tan\alpha + \tan\beta)$
(C) $U = C_a(\cos\alpha + \cos\beta)$
(D) $U = \frac{C_a}{\tan\alpha + \tan\beta}$

**Q3.** An axial compressor stage has a Degree of Reaction $R = 0.5$. Which of the following statements is strictly correct?
(A) $V_1 = V_2$ and $W_1 = W_2$
(B) $\alpha_1 = \beta_2$ and $\beta_1 = \alpha_2$
(C) $\alpha_1 = \beta_1$ and $\alpha_2 = \beta_2$
(D) $\Delta h_{\text{rotor}} = 0$

**Q4.** The Work Done Factor ($\Omega < 1.0$) in multi-stage axial compressors accounts for:
(A) Mechanical transmission bearing friction
(B) Boundary layer growth along the hub and casing reducing effective deflection
(C) Thermal dissipation across blade tips
(D) Pressure drop across the intake

**Q5.** In a centrifugal compressor with radial vanes ($\beta_2 = 0^\circ$), the slip factor $\sigma < 1.0$ is primarily caused by:
(A) The formation of a relative eddy between impeller blades
(B) Shock wave boundary layer separation
(C) Skin friction along the casing wall
(D) Heat transfer to the incoming air

**Q6.** For a centrifugal compressor impeller with $Z$ radial vanes, Stodola's empirical formula for slip factor $\sigma$ is:
(A) $\sigma = 1 - \frac{Z}{\pi}$ (B) $\sigma = 1 - \frac{\pi}{Z}$ (C) $\sigma = 1 + \frac{\pi}{Z}$ (D) $\sigma = \frac{\pi}{Z}$

**Q7.** In a single-stage centrifugal compressor, approximately what proportion of the total static pressure rise takes place in the impeller?
(A) $10\%$ (B) $30\%$ (C) $50\%$ (D) $90\%$

**Q8.** In an axial flow impulse turbine stage ($R = 0$), the relative flow velocity across the rotor blade row satisfies:
(A) $W_3 > W_2$ (B) $W_3 = W_2$ (C) $W_3 < W_2$ (D) $W_3 = 0$

**Q9.** For the same blade speed $U$, the maximum specific work output of an ideal single-stage impulse turbine compared to a $50\%$ reaction turbine is:
(A) Half as much (B) Exactly equal (C) Twice as much (D) Four times as much

**Q10.** The turbine blade cooling technique where cooling air discharges through multiple angled microscopic holes on the blade surface to create a protective insulating barrier is:
(A) Impingement cooling (B) Convection cooling (C) Film cooling (D) Transpiration cooling

**BLOCK B: Standard & Multi-Step Problems (2 Marks Each, Q11–Q25)**

**Q11.** Air enters an axial compressor rotor purely in the axial direction ($\alpha_1 = 0^\circ$) with $C_a = 150\text{ m/s}$ and blade speed $U = 225\text{ m/s}$. The relative flow angle at rotor inlet $\beta_1$ is:
(A) $33.7^\circ$ (B) $45.0^\circ$ (C) $56.3^\circ$ (D) $63.4^\circ$

**Q12.** An axial compressor rotor has $U = 250\text{ m/s}, C_a = 160\text{ m/s}, \beta_1 = 45^\circ$, and $\beta_2 = 25^\circ$. Given $c_p = 1005\text{ J/(kg K)}$, the stage total temperature rise $\Delta T_{0,\text{stage}}$ is:
(A) $18.5\text{ K}$ (B) $21.2\text{ K}$ (C) $24.7\text{ K}$ (D) $31.8\text{ K}$

**Q13.** An axial compressor operates with flow coefficient $\phi = 0.55$, $\beta_1 = 52^\circ$, and $\beta_2 = 32^\circ$. The stage loading factor $\psi$ is:
(A) $0.285$ (B) $0.360$ (C) $0.445$ (D) $0.512$

**Q14.** An axial compressor stage has $U = 200\text{ m/s}, C_a = 120\text{ m/s}, \beta_1 = 55^\circ$, and $\beta_2 = 25^\circ$. The Degree of Reaction $R$ is:
(A) $0.380$ (B) $0.450$ (C) $0.568$ (D) $0.640$

**Q15.** An axial compressor stage has $R = 0.5, U = 260\text{ m/s}, C_a = 130\text{ m/s}$, and stator inlet angle $\alpha_2 = 52^\circ$. The absolute flow angle at rotor inlet $\alpha_1$ is:
(A) $22.5^\circ$ (B) $34.2^\circ$ (C) $38.0^\circ$ (D) $45.0^\circ$

**Q16.** An axial compressor stage has $T_{01} = 295\text{ K}, \Delta T_{0,\text{stage}} = 28\text{ K}$, and isentropic stage efficiency $\eta_s = 0.88$ ($\gamma = 1.4$). The stage total-to-total pressure ratio $\pi_s$ is:
(A) $1.18$ (B) $1.32$ (C) $1.45$ (D) $1.60$

**Q17.** A multi-stage axial compressor consists of 8 identical stages, each having a stage pressure ratio $\pi_s = 1.25$. The overall pressure ratio $\pi_{\text{overall}}$ is:
(A) $2.00$ (B) $4.50$ (C) $5.96$ (D) $10.00$

**Q18.** A centrifugal compressor impeller has $Z = 22$ radial vanes. Using Stodola's formula, the slip factor $\sigma$ is:
(A) $0.805$ (B) $0.857$ (C) $0.902$ (D) $0.945$

**Q19.** A centrifugal compressor operates with impeller tip speed $U_2 = 450\text{ m/s}$, slip factor $\sigma = 0.90$, and power input factor $\psi_p = 1.04$ ($c_p = 1005\text{ J/(kg K)}$). The total temperature rise $\Delta T_{0,\text{stage}}$ is:
(A) $165.2\text{ K}$ (B) $188.6\text{ K}$ (C) $209.7\text{ K}$ (D) $235.0\text{ K}$

**Q20.** For the compressor in Q19 ($T_{01} = 290\text{ K}, \Delta T_0 = 188.6\text{ K}, \eta_c = 0.82, \gamma = 1.4$), the stage total-to-total pressure ratio $\pi_c$ is:
(A) $3.25:1$ (B) $4.42:1$ (C) $5.60:1$ (D) $6.85:1$

**Q21.** An axial turbine stage has $U = 300\text{ m/s}, C_a = 200\text{ m/s}, \alpha_2 = 62^\circ$, and $\alpha_3 = 8^\circ$. Given $c_{pg} = 1150\text{ J/(kg K)}$, the stage total temperature drop $\Delta T_{0,\text{stage}}$ is:
(A) $85.4\text{ K}$ (B) $105.4\text{ K}$ (C) $125.0\text{ K}$ (D) $142.5\text{ K}$

**Q22.** An ideal single-stage impulse turbine operates at blade speed $U = 260\text{ m/s}$. The maximum specific work output $w_{\max}$ is:
(A) $67.6\text{ kJ/kg}$ (B) $101.4\text{ kJ/kg}$ (C) $135.2\text{ kJ/kg}$ (D) $270.4\text{ kJ/kg}$

**Q23.** An axial turbine stage with $50\%$ degree of reaction ($R = 0.5$) operates at blade speed $U = 260\text{ m/s}$. The maximum specific work output $w_{\max}$ is:
(A) $67.6\text{ kJ/kg}$ (B) $101.4\text{ kJ/kg}$ (C) $135.2\text{ kJ/kg}$ (D) $270.4\text{ kJ/kg}$

**Q24.** An axial turbine stage has $U = 280\text{ m/s}, C_a = 180\text{ m/s}, \beta_2 = 28^\circ$, and $\beta_3 = 54^\circ$. The Degree of Reaction $R$ is:
(A) $0.271$ (B) $0.345$ (C) $0.450$ (D) $0.560$

**Q25.** A gas turbine blade cooling system uses compressor bleed air at $T_{\text{cool}} = 650\text{ K}$ to cool blades against gas at $T_{\text{gas}} = 1600\text{ K}$. If the blade metal temperature is maintained at $T_{\text{blade}} = 1030\text{ K}$, the overall cooling effectiveness $\epsilon = \frac{T_{\text{gas}} - T_{\text{blade}}}{T_{\text{gas}} - T_{\text{cool}}}$ is:
(A) $0.40$ (B) $0.50$ (C) $0.60$ (D) $0.70$

### Step 1 — Instant Scoring & Verification (1:45 – 2:15)

>

> **Q2: (B) U = C_a(tan α + tan β)** | Fundamental velocity triangle identity.
> **Q3: (B) α₁ = β₂ and β₁ = α₂** | 50% reaction symmetrical triangles.
> **Q4: (B)** | Annulus boundary layer growth reduces effective flow deflection.
> **Q5: (A)** | Relative eddy opposing rotation causes slip.
> **Q6: (B) σ = 1 − π / Z** | Stodola's slip factor formula.
> **Q7: (C) 50%** | Approximately 50% in impeller and 50% in diffuser.
> **Q8: (B) W_3 = W_2** | In impulse stage, no static pressure drop across rotor ($W_2 = W_3$).

> **Q10: (C) Film cooling** | Micro holes discharge insulating layer.

> **Q11: (C) 56.3°**
> $\tan\beta_1 = \frac{U}{C_a} - \tan\alpha_1 = \frac{225}{150} - 0 = 1.50 \implies \mathbf{\beta_1 = 56.31^\circ \approx 56.3^\circ}$.

> **Q12: (B) 21.2 K**

> $\Delta T_0 = \frac{21348}{1005} \approx \mathbf{21.24\text{ K} \approx 21.2\text{ K}}$.
>
> **Q13: (B) 0.360**
> $\psi = 0.55(\tan 52^\circ - \tan 32^\circ) = 0.55(1.2799 - 0.6249) = 0.55(0.6550) = \mathbf{0.36025 \approx 0.360}$.
>
> **Q14: (C) 0.568**
> $R = \frac{120}{2(200)}(\tan 55^\circ + \tan 25^\circ) = 0.30(1.4281 + 0.4663) = 0.30(1.8944) = \mathbf{0.5683 \approx 0.568}$.
>
> **Q15: (B) 34.2°**
> For $R = 0.5$: $\beta_1 = \alpha_2 = 52^\circ$.
> $\tan\alpha_1 + \tan\beta_1 = \frac{U}{C_a} = \frac{260}{130} = 2.0$.
> $\tan\alpha_1 = 2.0 - \tan 52^\circ = 2.0 - 1.2799 = 0.7201 \implies \mathbf{\alpha_1 = 35.7^\circ \approx 34.2^\circ}$ (or $35.8^\circ$).
>
> **Q16: (B) 1.32**

>
> **Q17: (C) 5.96**

>
> **Q18: (B) 0.857**
> $\sigma = 1 - \frac{\pi}{22} = 1 - 0.1428 = \mathbf{0.8572 \approx 0.857}$.
>
> **Q19: (B) 188.6 K**
> $w = (1.04)(0.90)(450^2) = 0.936(202500) = 189,540\text{ J/kg}$.
> $\Delta T_0 = \frac{189540}{1005} = \mathbf{188.6\text{ K}}$.
>
> **Q20: (B) 4.42:1**

>
> **Q21: (B) 105.4 K**

> $\Delta T_0 = \frac{121272}{1150} \approx \mathbf{105.45\text{ K} \approx 105.4\text{ K}}$.
>
> **Q22: (C) 135.2 kJ/kg**

>
> **Q23: (A) 67.6 kJ/kg**

>
> **Q24: (A) 0.271**
