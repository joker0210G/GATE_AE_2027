### Step 2 — The 3 Flow Lines & Their Governing Differential Equations (20 min)
Open `01.1a`, `01.1b`, and `01.1c`.

```
                    FLOW LINE TAXONOMY
   ┌──────────────────────┬──────────────────────┬──────────────────────┐
   │      STREAMLINE      │       PATHLINE       │      STREAKLINE      │
   ├──────────────────────┼──────────────────────┼──────────────────────┤
   │ Instantaneous tangent│ Trajectory traced by │ Instantaneous locus  │
   │ to velocity vector V │ a single fluid parcel│ of particles passing │
   │ at a frozen instant t│ over time            │ a fixed dye injector │
   └──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┘
```

1. **Streamline Equation (Frozen Time $t$):**
   $$\boxed{\frac{dx}{u} = \frac{dy}{v} = \frac{dz}{w} \iff u\,dy - v\,dx = 0}$$
   In polar coordinates $(r, \theta)$: $\frac{dr}{v_r} = \frac{r d\theta}{v_\theta}$.
2. **Pathline Equation (Time Tracking):**
   $$\boxed{\frac{dx}{dt} = u(x, y, z, t), \quad \frac{dy}{dt} = v(x, y, z, t), \quad \frac{dz}{dt} = w(x, y, z, t)}$$
   Integrate with initial condition $(x_0, y_0, z_0)$ at $t = t_0$, then eliminate time parameter $t$ to find spatial path $y = f(x)$.
3. **Streakline Equation (Dye/Smoke Filament):**
   - Locus of positions at observation time $t$ of all particles injected at point $(x_0, y_0)$ at past times $\tau \le t$.
   - **3-Step Derivation:** (1) Find pathline parameterized by release time $\tau$; (2) Freeze observation time $t$; (3) Eliminate $\tau$ between $x(t, \tau)$ and $y(t, \tau)$.

> 🔑 **The Equivalence Theorem:** In **STEADY FLOW**, Streamlines $\equiv$ Pathlines $\equiv$ Streaklines! They diverge ONLY in unsteady flows.

### Step 3 — Streamline Curvature & Physics of Aerodynamic Lift (20 min)
Open `01.1a - Streamlines and Streamline Curvature`.

In natural streamline coordinates $(s, n)$ where $s$ is along the streamline and $n$ is along the normal (pointing toward center of curvature):

$$\boxed{\frac{\partial p}{\partial n} = \rho \frac{V^2}{R}}$$

- $R =$ local radius of curvature of the streamline.
- **Physical Law:** Pressure **INCREASES in the direction away from the center of curvature** (or decreases toward the center of curvature).

```
                      Streamline Curvature Physics
                            High Pressure (p > p_inf)
                                 │    ▲
                                 │    │  n (Normal outward)
                           ──────┴────┴─────── Streamline (Radius R)
                                   ▼
                            Center of Curvature (Low Pressure / Suction)
```

- **Mechanism of Airfoil Lift:**
  - **Upper Surface (Convex):** Center of curvature is inside the airfoil $\implies$ pressure decreases towards the surface $\implies$ **Suction peak ($p < p_\infty$)** $\implies$ LIFT!
  - **Lower Surface (Concave):** Center of curvature is outside $\implies$ pressure increases towards the surface $\implies$ **Compression ($p > p_\infty$)**.

### Step 4 — Optical Flow Visualization Techniques for Compressible Flow (10 min)
Open `01.1b - Streaklines and Flow Visualization`.

Based on the **Gladstone-Dale Law** for optical refractive index of gases ($n - 1 = K\rho$):

| Optical Method | Physical Property Sensed | Optical Feature / Principle | Primary Aerodynamic Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Interferometry** | **Density ($\rho$) directly** | Fringe shift $\propto \Delta\rho$ (phase difference) | Isopycnal mapping / Boundary layer profiles |
| **Schlieren Technique** | **First Derivative of Density ($\nabla\rho$)** | Light deflection blocked by **Knife Edge** | Shock waves, Expansion fans, Boundary layers |
| **Shadowgraph** | **Second Derivative of Density ($\nabla^2\rho$)** | Illumination non-uniformity $\propto \frac{\partial^2\rho}{\partial x^2} + \frac{\partial^2\rho}{\partial y^2}$ | Strong shocks, Explosions, Wake turbulence |

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Streamline vs Pathline in Unsteady Flow**
> For $\vec{V} = (1 + 2t)\hat{i} + x\hat{j}$: Streamlines treat $t$ as a CONSTANT ($dy/dx = x/(1+2t)$), while Pathlines integrate $dx/dt = 1 + 2t \implies x(t) = t + t^2 + x_0$. They are completely different!
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Schlieren vs Shadowgraph Sensitivity**
> Schlieren senses $\nabla\rho$ (first spatial derivative); Shadowgraph senses $\nabla^2\rho$ (second spatial derivative).

### Step 3 — D2 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** In an unsteady 2D flow field, the streamline, pathline, and streakline passing through a given point:
> **(A)** Are always identical **(B)** In general, are three distinct curves **(C)** Are always orthogonal **(D)** Are concentric circles

> **Q2 (2 marks):** For the velocity field $\vec{V} = (2x)\hat{i} - (2y)\hat{j}$, the equation of the streamline passing through the point $(2, 4)$ is:
> **(A)** $y = 2x$ **(B)** $xy = 8$ **(C)** $x^2 + y^2 = 20$ **(D)** $y = x^2$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** Air ($\rho = 1.2\text{ kg/m}^3$) flows over the curved upper surface of an airfoil at $V = 150\text{ m/s}$ along a streamline with local radius of curvature $R = 5\text{ m}$. The normal pressure gradient $\frac{\partial p}{\partial n}$ towards the center of curvature is:
> **(A)** $5400\text{ Pa/m}$ **(B)** $-5400\text{ Pa/m}$ **(C)** $2700\text{ Pa/m}$ **(D)** $1080\text{ Pa/m}$

> **Q4 (1 mark):** The Schlieren optical visualization system detects spatial variations of:
> **(A)** Density ($\rho$) **(B)** Gradient of density ($\nabla\rho$) **(C)** Laplacian of density ($\nabla^2\rho$) **(D)** Temperature only

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A fluid particle moves in a 2D velocity field $u = 2t, v = 3$. If the particle was at $(0, 0)$ at $t = 0$, the $x$-coordinate of its position when $y = 6$ is ___.

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D2 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) In general, are three distinct curves**
> In unsteady flow ($\frac{\partial\vec{V}}{\partial t} \neq 0$), streamlines, pathlines, and streaklines diverge into three separate curves.
>
> **Q2: (B) xy = 8**
> Streamline: $\frac{dx}{2x} = \frac{dy}{-2y} \implies \frac{dx}{x} = -\frac{dy}{y} \implies \ln x + \ln y = \ln C \implies xy = C$.
> At $(2, 4)$: $C = 2(4) = 8 \implies \mathbf{xy = 8}$.
>
> **Q3: (A) 5400 Pa/m**
> $\frac{\partial p}{\partial n} = \rho \frac{V^2}{R} = 1.2 \frac{150^2}{5} = 1.2 \frac{22500}{5} = 1.2(4500) = \mathbf{5400\text{ Pa/m}}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) Gradient of density (∇ρ)**
> Schlieren uses a knife edge to block refracted light rays proportional to the first spatial gradient of refractive index / density ($\nabla\rho$).
>
> **Q5: 4**
> $\frac{dy}{dt} = 3 \implies y(t) = 3t$. When $y = 6 \implies 3t = 6 \implies t = 2\text{ s}$.
> $\frac{dx}{dt} = 2t \implies x(t) = \int_0^t 2\tau d\tau = t^2$.
> At $t = 2\text{ s}$: $x = 2^2 = \mathbf{4}$.
