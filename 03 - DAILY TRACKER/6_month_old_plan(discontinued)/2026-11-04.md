### Step 2 — The Hugoniot Area-Velocity Relation (20 min)
Open `02 - Aircraft Engine Types and Performance`.

From continuity ($\frac{d\rho}{\rho} + \frac{dA}{A} + \frac{dV}{V} = 0$), Euler momentum ($dp = -\rho V dV$), and speed of sound ($a^2 = dp/d\rho$):

$$\boxed{\frac{dA}{A} = (M^2 - 1)\frac{dV}{V}}$$

```
               THE HUGONIOT AREA-VELOCITY DUALITY
              Regime          Duct Shape     Area Change (dA)  Velocity Change (dV)
              ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
              Subsonic (M < 1)   Converging    dA < 0            dV > 0 (Accelerates)
                                 Diverging     dA > 0            dV < 0 (Diffuses)
              ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
              Sonic (M = 1)      Throat        dA = 0 (Minimum)  Maximum Subsonic V
              ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
              Supersonic (M > 1) Converging    dA < 0            dV < 0 (Diffuses)
                                 Diverging     dA > 0            dV > 0 (Accelerates)
              ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

- **Fundamental Invariant:** To accelerate a flow smoothly from subsonic ($M < 1$) to supersonic ($M > 1$), the duct geometry MUST be **Converging-Diverging (CD Nozzle / de Laval Nozzle)**, and sonic speed ($M = 1$) can occur **ONLY AT THE MINIMUM CROSS-SECTIONAL AREA (THE THROAT $A = A^*$)**!

---

### Step 3 — Critical State & Sonic Throat Properties (20 min)

Substituting $M = 1$ into isentropic stagnation relations for $\gamma = 1.4$:

1. **Critical Temperature Ratio:**
   $$\boxed{\frac{T^*}{T_0} = \frac{2}{\gamma + 1} = \frac{2}{2.4} = \frac{5}{6} \approx \mathbf{0.8333}}$$
2. **Critical Pressure Ratio (Choking Threshold):**
   $$\boxed{\frac{p^*}{p_0} = \left(\frac{2}{\gamma + 1}\right)^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma - 1}} = \left(\frac{2}{2.4}\right)^{3.5} = (0.8333)^{3.5} \approx \mathbf{0.52828 \approx 0.528}}$$
   *(For a nozzle with upstream stagnation pressure $p_0$, the throat chokes as soon as the static pressure at the throat drops to $\le 0.528 p_0$!).*
3. **Critical Density Ratio:**
   $$\boxed{\frac{\rho^*}{\rho_0} = \left(\frac{2}{\gamma + 1}\right)^{\frac{1}{\gamma - 1}} = (0.8333)^{2.5} \approx \mathbf{0.6339}}$$

---

### Step 4 — Mach-Area Relation & Choked Mass Flow Formula (15 min)

1. **The Mach-Area Relation ($A/A^*$):**
   $$\boxed{\left(\frac{A}{A^*}\right)^2 = \frac{1}{M^2}\left[\frac{2}{\gamma+1}\left(1 + \frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2\right)\right]^{\frac{\gamma+1}{\gamma-1}}}$$
   - For every area ratio $A/A^* > 1$, there exist **TWO unique isentropic solutions**:
     - One subsonic solution ($M < 1$).
     - One supersonic solution ($M > 1$).

2. **The Master Choked Mass Flow Rate Formula:**
   Evaluating mass flow at the sonic throat where $M=1, A=A^*, V=a^*=\sqrt{\gamma R T^*}, \rho=\rho^*$:
   $$\dot{m} = \rho^* A^* V^* = \left[\rho_0\left(\frac{2}{\gamma+1}\right)^{\frac{1}{\gamma-1}}\right] A^* \sqrt{\gamma R T_0\left(\frac{2}{\gamma+1}\right)}$$
   $$\boxed{\dot{m}_{\max} = \frac{p_0 A^*}{\sqrt{T_0}}\sqrt{\frac{\gamma}{R}\left(\frac{2}{\gamma+1}\right)^{\frac{\gamma+1}{\gamma-1}}}}$$
   - **For Air ($\gamma = 1.4, R = 287\text{ J/(kg K)}$):**
     $$\boxed{\dot{m}_{\max} \approx 0.040418 \frac{p_0 A^*}{\sqrt{T_0}} \quad (p_0\text{ in Pa, } A^*\text{ in m}^2, \ T_0\text{ in K, } \dot{m}\text{ in kg/s})}$$
   - **Crucial Invariants:**
     - $\dot{m}_{\max} \propto p_0$ (Directly proportional to reservoir stagnation pressure).
     - $\dot{m}_{\max} \propto A^*$ (Directly proportional to throat area).
     - $\dot{m}_{\max} \propto \frac{1}{\sqrt{T_0}}$ (**Inversely proportional to the square root of stagnation temperature!**).

---

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Downstream Pressure Effect on Choked Mass Flow**
> Once a nozzle is choked ($p_b \le p^*$), lowering the back pressure $p_b$ further **DOES NOT INCREASE MASS FLOW RATE $\dot{m}$**! Mass flow is completely frozen at $\dot{m}_{\max}$ governed solely by $p_0, T_0, A^*$.
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Subsonic vs. Supersonic Area Changes**
> In a rocket engine diverging nozzle ($M > 1$), increasing area $dA > 0$ accelerates the gas ($dV > 0$) and decreases pressure ($dp < 0$). Do not apply subsonic intuition!

### Step 3 — D2 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** In a supersonic flow ($M > 1$), expanding the cross-sectional area of the duct ($dA > 0$) causes the flow velocity and static pressure to:
> **(A)** Velocity increases, pressure decreases
> **(B)** Velocity decreases, pressure increases
> **(C)** Velocity decreases, pressure decreases
> **(D)** Both remain unchanged

> **Q2 (1 mark):** Air ($\gamma = 1.4$) expands from a stagnation pressure $p_0 = 500\text{ kPa}$ through a converging nozzle into the atmosphere ($p_a = 100\text{ kPa}$). The static pressure at the exit plane of the nozzle is:
> **(A)** $100.0\text{ kPa}$ **(B)** $264.1\text{ kPa}$ **(C)** $350.0\text{ kPa}$ **(D)** $500.0\text{ kPa}$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A choked converging nozzle operates with reservoir stagnation conditions $p_0 = 300\text{ kPa}, T_0 = 400\text{ K}$. If the reservoir temperature is increased to $T_0 = 900\text{ K}$ while keeping $p_0$ constant, the mass flow rate will:
> **(A)** Increase by a factor of $1.5$
> **(B)** Decrease by a factor of $1.5$ (Multiply by $2/3$)
> **(C)** Increase by a factor of $2.25$
> **(D)** Remain unchanged

> **Q4 (2 marks):** A converging-diverging nozzle has a throat area $A^* = 0.02\text{ m}^2$. Air ($\gamma = 1.4, R = 287\text{ J/(kg K)}$) enters at $p_0 = 800\text{ kPa}$ and $T_0 = 600\text{ K}$. The maximum choked mass flow rate $\dot{m}_{\max}$ (in $\text{kg/s}$) is approximately:
> **(A)** $13.2\text{ kg/s}$ **(B)** $26.4\text{ kg/s}$ **(C)** $52.8\text{ kg/s}$ **(D)** $79.2\text{ kg/s}$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A rocket combustion chamber supplies gas ($\gamma = 1.25, R = 350\text{ J/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}$) at stagnation temperature $T_0 = 3200\text{ K}$ to a choked throat. The critical sonic velocity $V^*$ at the throat (in $\text{m/s}$) is ___ (round off to the nearest integer).

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D2 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (A) Velocity increases, pressure decreases**
> From $\frac{dA}{A} = (M^2 - 1)\frac{dV}{V}$, for $M > 1$, $dA > 0 \implies dV > 0$. From Euler equation $dp = -\rho V dV \implies dp < 0$.
>
> **Q2: (B) 264.1 kPa**
> Choking threshold: $p^* = 0.52828 p_0 = 0.52828(500) = 264.14\text{ kPa}$. Since $p_a = 100\text{ kPa} < p^* = 264.14\text{ kPa}$, the nozzle is choked and the exit static pressure stays pinned at $p_e = p^* = \mathbf{264.1\text{ kPa}}$!
>
> **Q3: (B) Decrease by a factor of 1.5 (Multiply by 2/3)**
> $\dot{m} \propto \frac{1}{\sqrt{T_0}} \implies \frac{\dot{m}_2}{\dot{m}_1} = \sqrt{\frac{400}{900}} = \sqrt{\frac{4}{9}} = \frac{2}{3} \approx 0.667$.
>
> **Q4: (B) 26.4 kg/s**
> $\dot{m}_{\max} \approx 0.04042 \frac{p_0 A^*}{\sqrt{T_0}} = 0.04042 \frac{(800,000)(0.02)}{\sqrt{600}} = 0.04042 \frac{16,000}{24.495} = 0.04042(653.2) \approx \mathbf{26.40\text{ kg/s}}$.
>
> **Q5: 1673**
> $T^* = \frac{2}{\gamma + 1} T_0 = \frac{2}{2.25}(3200) = 2844.44\text{ K}$.
> $V^* = \sqrt{\gamma R T^*} = \sqrt{1.25 \times 350 \times 2844.44} = \sqrt{1,244,444} \approx \mathbf{1115.5\text{ m/s}}$... wait:
> $\sqrt{1.25 \times 350 \times 2844.44} = \sqrt{437.5 \times 2844.44} = \sqrt{1,244,444} = 1115.54\text{ m/s} \approx \mathbf{1116\text{ m/s}}$.
