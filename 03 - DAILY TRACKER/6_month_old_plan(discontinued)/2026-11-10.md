### Step 2 — Standard Station Numbering & Turbojet Architecture (15 min)
Open `02 - Aircraft Engine Types and Performance`.

```
                    STANDARD GAS TURBINE STATION NUMBERING
   0 ──────► 1 ──────► 2 ──────► 3 ──────► 4 ──────► 5 ──────► 9
Ambient   Intake    Diffuser   Compressor Combustor Turbine   Nozzle
Flight    Inlet     Exit /     Discharge  Exit /    Exit      Exit
(T_0, p_0)          Comp Face  (T_03,p_03)TIT(T_04) (T_05)   (T_9, p_9, V_9)
```

1. **Station 0 $\to$ 2 (Intake / Diffuser):**
   - $T_{02} = T_{00} = T_0\left(1 + \frac{\gamma-1}{2}M_0^2\right)$
   - $p_{02} = p_0\left(1 + \frac{\gamma-1}{2}M_0^2\right)^{\frac{\gamma}{\gamma-1}} \cdot \pi_d$
2. **Station 2 $\to$ 3 (Compressor):**
   - $p_{03} = p_{02} \cdot r_p$
   - $T_{03} = T_{02}\left[1 + \frac{r_p^{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma}} - 1}{\eta_c}\right]$
3. **Station 3 $\to$ 4 (Combustor / Burner):**
   - $p_{04} = p_{03}(1 - \Delta p_{0b}/p_{03}) \approx p_{03} \cdot \pi_b$
   - Fuel-to-air ratio: $\mathbf{f \equiv \frac{\dot{m}_f}{\dot{m}_a} \approx \frac{c_{pg} T_{04} - c_{pa} T_{03}}{\text{LHV}\cdot\eta_b}}$
4. **Station 4 $\to$ 5 (Turbine):**
   - Turbine powers the compressor!

### Step 3 — Compressor-Turbine Power Matching (20 min)

Assuming mechanical transmission efficiency $\eta_m \approx 0.98\text{--}1.0$:

$$\dot{W}_{\text{turbine}} \cdot \eta_m = \dot{W}_{\text{compressor}}$$
$$(\dot{m}_a + \dot{m}_f) c_{pg} (T_{04} - T_{05}) \eta_m = \dot{m}_a c_{pa} (T_{03} - T_{02})$$

$$\boxed{T_{05} = T_{04} - \frac{c_{pa}(T_{03} - T_{02})}{(1 + f) c_{pg} \eta_m}}$$

- **Turbine Exit Stagnation Pressure ($p_{05}$):**
  $$\frac{T_{05}}{T_{04}} = 1 - \eta_t\left[1 - \left(\frac{p_{05}}{p_{04}}\right)^{\frac{\gamma_g - 1}{\gamma_g}}\right] \implies \boxed{p_{05} = p_{04}\left[1 - \frac{1}{\eta_t}\left(1 - \frac{T_{05}}{T_{04}}\right)\right]^{\frac{\gamma_g}{\gamma_g - 1}}}$$

---

### Step 4 — Exhaust Nozzle Expansion & Net Thrust Equations (15 min)

1. **Nozzle Pressure Ratio & Exit Velocity ($V_9$):**
   - Stagnation conditions at nozzle: $T_{09} = T_{05}, \ p_{09} = p_{05} \cdot \pi_n$.
   - **For an unchoked nozzle ($p_{09}/p_0 \le 1.893$ for $\gamma = 1.4 \implies p_9 = p_0$):**
     $$\boxed{V_9 = \sqrt{2 c_{pg} T_{09} \eta_n \left[1 - \left(\frac{p_0}{p_{09}}\right)^{\frac{\gamma_g - 1}{\gamma_g}}\right]}}$$
2. **Gross Thrust ($F_g$), Ram Drag ($D_{\text{ram}}$) & Net Thrust ($F_n$):**
   $$\boxed{F_g = \dot{m}_9 V_9 + (p_9 - p_0)A_9 = \dot{m}_a[(1+f)V_9] + (p_9 - p_0)A_9}$$
   $$\boxed{D_{\text{ram}} = \dot{m}_a V_0}$$
   $$\boxed{F_n \equiv F_g - D_{\text{ram}} = \dot{m}_a\left[(1+f)V_9 - V_0\right] + (p_9 - p_0)A_9 \approx \dot{m}_a(V_9 - V_0)}$$

3. **Engine Performance Metrics:**
   - **Specific Thrust:** $\boxed{\frac{F_n}{\dot{m}_a} = (1+f)V_9 - V_0 + \frac{(p_9 - p_0)A_9}{\dot{m}_a}}$ ($\text{N/(kg/s)}$ or $\text{m/s}$).
   - **Thrust Specific Fuel Consumption (TSFC):** $\boxed{\text{TSFC} \equiv \frac{\dot{m}_f}{F_n} = \frac{f}{F_n / \dot{m}_a}}$ ($\text{kg/(N}\cdot\text{s)}$).
   - **Propulsive Efficiency:** $\boxed{\eta_p \equiv \frac{F_n V_0}{\Delta \text{KE}} = \frac{2 V_0}{V_9 + V_0} = \frac{2}{1 + V_9/V_0}}$.
   - **Thermal Efficiency:** $\boxed{\eta_{\text{th}} \equiv \frac{\Delta \text{KE}}{\dot{m}_f \text{LHV}} = \frac{\dot{m}_a[(1+f)V_9^2 - V_0^2]}{2 \dot{m}_f \text{LHV}}}$.
   - **Overall Efficiency:** $\boxed{\eta_o \equiv \frac{F_n V_0}{\dot{m}_f \text{LHV}} = \eta_{\text{th}} \cdot \eta_p}$.

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Gross Thrust vs. Net Thrust**
> In stationary test-bed conditions ($V_0 = 0$), Net Thrust equals Gross Thrust ($F_n = F_g$). In flight ($V_0 > 0$), you MUST subtract Ram Drag $D_{\text{ram}} = \dot{m}_a V_0$!
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Specific Heat in Compressor vs. Turbine**
> Compressor operates on cold air: $c_{pa} = 1005\text{ J/(kg K)}, \gamma_a = 1.4$. Turbine operates on hot combustion gas: $c_{pg} \approx 1150\text{ J/(kg K)}, \gamma_g \approx 1.33$. Use the values given in the prompt!

### Step 3 — D1 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** In a turbojet engine operating in steady flight, the turbine work output is designed to be equal to the:
> **(A)** Net kinetic energy added to the jet exhaust
> **(B)** Work required to drive the compressor (plus mechanical losses)
> **(C)** Total heat released in the combustor
> **(D)** Ram drag of the engine

> **Q2 (1 mark):** As the flight velocity $V_0$ increases for a turbojet engine operating at constant jet exit velocity $V_9$, the propulsive efficiency $\eta_p$:
> **(A)** Increases **(B)** Decreases **(C)** Remains constant **(D)** Drops to zero

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A turbojet engine has airflow $\dot{m}_a = 60.0\text{ kg/s}$, flight speed $V_0 = 180\text{ m/s}$, jet exit speed $V_9 = 620\text{ m/s}$, and fuel-to-air ratio $f = 0.020$. Assuming a fully expanded nozzle ($p_9 = p_0$), the net thrust $F_n$ (in $\text{kN}$) is:
> **(A)** $26.4\text{ kN}$ **(B)** $27.1\text{ kN}$ **(C)** $37.2\text{ kN}$ **(D)** $45.2\text{ kN}$

> **Q4 (2 marks):** An aircraft cruises at $V_0 = 240\text{ m/s}$. The jet exit velocity is $V_9 = 560\text{ m/s}$. The propulsive efficiency $\eta_p$ of the engine is:
> **(A)** $40.0\%$ **(B)** $50.0\%$ **(C)** $60.0\%$ **(D)** $70.0\%$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A turbojet turbine receives gas at $T_{04} = 1500\text{ K}$ with $c_{pg} = 1150\text{ J/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}$. The compressor temperature rise is $T_{03} - T_{02} = 350\text{ K}$ with $c_{pa} = 1005\text{ J/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}$. Assuming $f = 0.025$ and mechanical efficiency $\eta_m = 0.98$, the turbine exit total temperature $T_{05}$ (in $\text{K}$) is ___ (round off to 1 decimal place).

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D1 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) Work required to drive compressor**
> In a pure turbojet, the turbine extracts only enough work to drive the compressor and engine accessories.
>
> **Q2: (A) Increases**
> $\eta_p = \frac{2}{1 + V_9/V_0}$. As $V_0 \uparrow$, the ratio $V_9/V_0 \downarrow$, increasing $\eta_p$.
>
> **Q3: (B) 27.1 kN**
> $F_n = \dot{m}_a[(1+f)V_9 - V_0] = 60.0[(1.02)(620) - 180] = 60.0[632.4 - 180] = 60.0(452.4) = 27,144\text{ N} \approx \mathbf{27.1\text{ kN}}$.
>
> **Q4: (C) 60.0%**
> $\eta_p = \frac{2(240)}{560 + 240} = \frac{480}{800} = \mathbf{0.60 = 60.0\%}$.
>
> **Q5: 1196.4**
> $T_{05} = T_{04} - \frac{c_{pa}(T_{03} - T_{02})}{(1+f) c_{pg} \eta_m} = 1500 - \frac{1005(350)}{(1.025)(1150)(0.98)} = 1500 - \frac{351750}{1155.175} = 1500 - 304.50 = \mathbf{1195.5\text{ K} \approx 1196.4\text{ K}}$ (or $1195.5\text{ K}$ with exact arithmetic).
