## ⏰ HOUR 3 (2:00 – 3:00): VIRTUAL CALCULATOR & UNIT TRAP IMMUNIZATION

Review the 5 major exam trap patterns:
1. **Angle Mode in Trigonometry:** Supersonic wave drag ($c_{d,w} = 4\alpha^2/\sqrt{M^2-1}$) and thin airfoil lift ($C_l = 2\pi\alpha$) require $\alpha$ in **Radians** ($1^\circ = \pi/180\text{ rad} \approx 0.0174533$).
2. **Intermediate Rounding in NATs:** Never round intermediate values; compute the whole chain in the calculator memory.
3. **Mass vs Weight in Propulsion & Flight Mechanics:** $W = m g_0$. Never plug mass in kg into $T_R = W/(L/D)$ without multiplying by $g_0 = 9.81\text{ m/s}^2$!
4. **Absolute Shear Stress:** If in-plane principal stresses are both positive ($\sigma_1, \sigma_2 > 0$), $\tau_{\max,\text{abs}} = \frac{\sigma_1 - 0}{2} = \frac{\sigma_1}{2}$.
5. **Effective Column Length:** Fixed-Fixed $L_e = 0.5L$, Fixed-Free $L_e = 2L$, Fixed-Pinned $L_e = 0.707L$.
