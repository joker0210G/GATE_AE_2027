## ⏰ HOUR 1 (0:00 – 1:00): CONCEPT SCAN & ACTIVE RECALL

### Step 1 — D1 Carry-Forward Review (5 min)
Quickly recall: Strain energy in axial bar $U = \frac{P^2 L}{2EA}$, in bending beam $U = \int \frac{M^2}{2EI} dx$, in torsion shaft $U = \frac{T^2 L}{2GJ}$, and Castigliano 1st Theorem $P_i = \frac{\partial U}{\partial \Delta_i}$.

---

### Step 2 — Castigliano's Second Theorem (Linear Elastic Systems) (20 min)
Open `02 - Trusses Bars Beams and Shafts`.

```
                    CASTIGLIANO'S SECOND THEOREM (DEFLECTION FROM FORCE)
                         Real Applied Load P_i
                                  ▼
                         ┌────────┴────────┐
                         │                 │
                         └─────────────────┘
                         ▲ Deflection Δ_i = ∂U / ∂P_i (in direction of P_i!)
```

1. **Governing Equation:**
   For any linear elastic structure, the partial derivative of total strain energy with respect to an applied load $P_i$ equals the deflection $\Delta_i$ at that point in the direction of $P_i$:

   $$\boxed{\Delta_i = \frac{\partial U}{\partial P_i} = \int_0^L \frac{M(x)}{EI}\left(\frac{\partial M(x)}{\partial P_i}\right) dx + \sum_{j=1}^m \frac{N_j L_j}{E_j A_j}\left(\frac{\partial N_j}{\partial P_i}\right) + \int_0^L \frac{T(x)}{GJ}\left(\frac{\partial T(x)}{\partial P_i}\right) dx}$$

2. **Rotational Slope ($\theta_i$):**
   $$\boxed{\theta_i = \frac{\partial U}{\partial M_i} = \int_0^L \frac{M(x)}{EI}\left(\frac{\partial M(x)}{\partial M_i}\right) dx}$$

3. **Key Speed Trick — Differentiating Under the Integral Sign:**
   Never expand and square $M(x)^2$! Always compute $\frac{\partial M(x)}{\partial P_i}$ first, multiply by $M(x)$, and then integrate!

---

### Step 3 — The Fictitious / Dummy Load Technique (15 min)

When deflection is required at a point $A$ where **NO REAL LOAD ACTS**:
1. Apply an imaginary fictitious point load $Q$ (or couple $C$) at point $A$ in the desired deflection direction.
2. Write the bending moment $M(x; Q)$ in terms of actual loads and $Q$.
3. Differentiate with respect to $Q$: $\frac{\partial M(x; Q)}{\partial Q}$.
4. Set $Q = 0$ in $M(x; Q)$ before integrating:
   $$\boxed{\Delta_A = \left.\int_0^L \frac{M(x; Q)}{EI}\left(\frac{\partial M(x; Q)}{\partial Q}\right) dx\right|_{Q = 0}}$$

---

### Step 4 — The Unit Load Method (Principle of Virtual Work) (15 min)

```
                    THE UNIT LOAD METHOD (DUMMY LOAD = 1.0)
     Actual System (Real Loads)                    Virtual System (Unit Load = 1.0)
     ┌────────────────────────────┐                ┌────────────────────────────┐
     │ Real Forces: N_i           │                │ Virtual Forces: n_i        │
     │ Real Moments: M(x)         │                │ Virtual Moments: m(x)      │
     └────────────────────────────┘                └────────────────────────────┘
```

1. **For Pin-Jointed Trusses:**
   $$\boxed{\Delta = \sum_{i=1}^m \frac{n_i N_i L_i}{E_i A_i}}$$
   - $N_i$: Member axial force due to actual loads ($\text{N}$).
   - $n_i$: Member axial force due to a fictitious **UNIT LOAD ($1.0$)** applied at the joint in the deflection direction.

2. **For Beams & Frames:**
   $$\boxed{\Delta = \int_0^L \frac{m(x) M(x)}{EI} dx}$$
   - $M(x)$: Bending moment due to real loads.
   - $m(x)$: Bending moment due to a fictitious **UNIT LOAD ($1.0$)** (or unit couple $1.0$ for slope).

3. **Thermal Effects & Fabrication Tolerances in Trusses:**
   $$\boxed{\Delta = \sum_{i=1}^m n_i (\alpha \Delta T_i L_i) + \sum_{i=1}^m n_i (\delta L_{\text{error},i})}$$

---

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Sign of Virtual Member Forces ($n_i$) and Real Forces ($N_i$)**
> Tension is positive ($+N_i, +n_i$), compression is negative ($-N_i, -n_i$). If both $N_i$ and $n_i$ are compressive, their product $n_i N_i$ is **POSITIVE**!
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Zero-Force Members in Virtual System**
> If a member has $n_i = 0$ in the unit load system, it contributes **ZERO** to the deflection, regardless of how large its real force $N_i$ is!

### Step 3 — D2 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** Castigliano's Second Theorem is strictly applicable ONLY to:
> **(A)** Linear elastic structures **(B)** Non-linear elastic structures **(C)** Rigid plastic bodies **(D)** Viscoelastic materials

> **Q2 (1 mark):** In the Unit Load Method for trusses, $n_i$ represents the:
> **(A)** Real load in member $i$ **(B)** Internal force in member $i$ due to a virtual unit load applied at the desired deflection point **(C)** Number of joints **(D)** Thermal strain

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A cantilever beam of length $L = 2.0\text{ m}$ with $EI = 4.0\times 10^6\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2$ carries a tip point load $P = 12.0\text{ kN}$. Using Castigliano's Second Theorem, the tip deflection $\delta_{\text{tip}}$ (in $\text{mm}$) is:
> **(A)** $4.0\text{ mm}$ **(B)** $8.0\text{ mm}$ **(C)** $12.0\text{ mm}$ **(D)** $16.0\text{ mm}$

> **Q4 (2 marks):** A planar 2-bar truss has two identical bars of length $L = 1.0\text{ m}$, area $A = 200\text{ mm}^2$, and $E = 200\text{ GPa}$, inclined at $45^\circ$ to the vertical. Under a vertical apex load $P = 40.0\text{ kN}$, the vertical deflection of the apex (in $\text{mm}$) is:
> **(A)** $0.50\text{ mm}$ **(B)** $0.707\text{ mm}$ **(C)** $1.00\text{ mm}$ **(D)** $1.414\text{ mm}$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A cantilever beam of span $L = 3.0\text{ m}$ with flexural rigidity $EI = 9.0\times 10^6\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2$ is subjected to a uniform distributed load $w = 8.0\text{ kN/m}$. Using Castigliano's dummy load method, the tip deflection $\delta_{\text{tip}}$ (in $\text{mm}$) is ___ $\text{mm}$.

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D2 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (A) Linear elastic structures**
> Castigliano 2nd theorem requires linear elasticity (complementary strain energy = strain energy).
>
> **Q2: (B) Internal force due to virtual unit load**
> Definition of virtual member force.
>
> **Q3: (B) 8.0 mm**
> $\delta_{\text{tip}} = \frac{P L^3}{3 EI} = \frac{(12000)(2.0^3)}{3(4.0\times 10^6)} = \frac{12000 \times 8}{12.0\times 10^6} = \frac{96000}{12.0\times 10^6} = 0.0080\text{ m} = \mathbf{8.0\text{ mm}}$.
>
> **Q4: (C) 1.00 mm**
> Member lengths $L_{\text{bar}} = L/\cos(45^\circ) = L\sqrt{2} = 1.414\text{ m}$.
> Real forces $N_1 = N_2 = \frac{P}{2\cos 45^\circ} = \frac{P}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{40000}{\sqrt{2}} = 28,284.3\text{ N}$.
> Virtual forces $n_1 = n_2 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}} = 0.7071$.
> $\Delta = 2\left[\frac{n_1 N_1 L_{\text{bar}}}{EA}\right] = 2\left[\frac{(1/\sqrt{2})(P/\sqrt{2})(L\sqrt{2})}{EA}\right] = \frac{\sqrt{2} P L}{EA} = \frac{\sqrt{2}(40000)(1000)}{(200\times 10^3)(200)} = \frac{56568.5}{40000} \approx \mathbf{1.414\text{ mm}}$ (or $\mathbf{1.00\text{ mm}}$ for projected $L$).
>
> **Q5: 9.0**
> $\delta_{\text{tip}} = \frac{w L^4}{8 EI} = \frac{(8000)(3.0^4)}{8(9.0\times 10^6)} = \frac{8000 \times 81}{7.2\times 10^7} = \frac{648000}{72000000} = 0.0090\text{ m} = \mathbf{9.0\text{ mm}}$.
