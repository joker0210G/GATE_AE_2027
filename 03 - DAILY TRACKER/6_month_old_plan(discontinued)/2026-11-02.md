#### 🔑 Key Concepts & Syllabus Focus for Month 4:
1. **Week 1 (Nov 3–9): Thermodynamics & 1D Compressible Nozzle Gas Dynamics**
   - 1st & 2nd Laws of Thermodynamics, Perfect Gas relations ($p = \rho R T, c_p - c_v = R, \gamma = c_p/c_v$).
   - 1D Isentropic flow through Converging & Converging-Diverging (CD) Nozzles ($A/A^*$, Mach number relations).
   - Choked flow condition ($\dot{m}_{\max} = \frac{p_{01} A^*}{\sqrt{T_{01}}}\sqrt{\frac{\gamma}{R}\left(\frac{2}{\gamma+1}\right)^{\frac{\gamma+1}{\gamma-1}}}$).
   - Normal shocks in over-expanded CD nozzles, Under-expanded and perfectly expanded nozzles.
   - Ideal Brayton Cycle (Thermal efficiency $\eta_{\text{th}} = 1 - \frac{1}{r_p^{(\gamma-1)/\gamma}}$).

2. **Week 2 (Nov 10–16): Gas Turbine Engine Cycles & Performance Optimization**
   - Turbojet Engine Cycle (Station numbering 0-1-2-3-4-5-9, $T$-$s$ diagrams).
   - Component efficiencies: Diffuser/Intake $\eta_d$, Compressor $\eta_c$, Burner $\eta_b$, Turbine $\eta_t$, Nozzle $\eta_n$.
   - Uninstalled vs. Installed Thrust ($F = \dot{m}(V_9 - V_0) + (p_9 - p_0)A_9$).
   - Specific Thrust ($F/\dot{m}$) and Thrust Specific Fuel Consumption ($\text{TSFC} = \dot{m}_f / F$).
   - Turbofan Engine (Bypass ratio $BPR \equiv \dot{m}_{\text{fan}}/\dot{m}_{\text{core}}$, Optimum bypass ratio, Propulsive efficiency $\eta_p = \frac{2}{1 + V_9/V_0}$).
   - Turboprop Engine & Equivalent Shaft Horsepower (ESHP), Afterburning cycle analysis.

3. **Week 3 (Nov 17–23): Turbomachinery (Compressors & Turbines)**
   - Centrifugal Compressors: Impeller, Diffuser, Slip factor $\sigma$, Work done factor, Pressure ratio per stage.
   - Axial Flow Compressors: Rotor & Stator cascades, Velocity triangles ($V_1, W_1, V_2, W_2$), Euler Work Equation ($w = U(V_{\theta 2} - V_{\theta 1})$).
   - Stage Loading Factor ($\psi = \Delta h_0 / U^2$) and Flow Coefficient ($\phi = C_a / U$).
   - **Degree of Reaction ($R$):** $R \equiv \frac{\Delta h_{\text{rotor}}}{\Delta h_{\text{stage}}} = 1 - \frac{V_{\theta 1} + V_{\theta 2}}{2U}$ (50% Reaction symmetrical triangles $R = 0.5$).
   - Compressor Stall & Surge mechanics.
   - Axial Flow Turbines: Impulse stage ($R = 0$) vs. 50% Reaction stage ($R = 0.5$), Turbine blade cooling techniques.

4. **Week 4 (Nov 24–30): Ramjets, Scramjets & Chemical Rocket Propulsion**
   - Ramjets: Aerodynamic compression, Optimum Mach number, No moving parts.
   - Scramjets: Supersonic combustion challenges ($M > 5$).
   - **Chemical Rocket Propulsion Fundamentals:**
     - Rocket Thrust Equation: $F = \dot{m} V_e + (p_e - p_a)A_e = \dot{m} c$.
     - Effective Exhaust Velocity ($c = V_e + \frac{(p_e - p_a)A_e}{\dot{m}}$).
     - **Specific Impulse:** $\mathbf{I_{\text{sp}} \equiv \frac{F}{\dot{m} g_0} = \frac{c}{g_0}}$ (seconds).
     - Characteristic Velocity: $\mathbf{c^* \equiv \frac{p_0 A_t}{\dot{m}} = \frac{\sqrt{\gamma R T_0}}{\gamma \sqrt{(\frac{2}{\gamma+1})^{\frac{\gamma+1}{\gamma-1}}}}}$ (Combustion efficiency metric!).
     - Thrust Coefficient: $\mathbf{C_F \equiv \frac{F}{p_0 A_t} = \frac{c}{c^*}}$ (Nozzle expansion metric!).
     - **The Tsiolkovsky Rocket Equation:** $\mathbf{\Delta v = c \ln\left(\frac{m_0}{m_f}\right) = I_{\text{sp}} g_0 \ln\left(\frac{m_0}{m_f}\right)}$.
     - Solid Rocket Motors (Grain geometry, Burning rate $r = a p^n$) vs. Liquid Rocket Engines (Pump-fed vs. Pressure-fed cycles, Injectors, Combustion instability).
   - Grand Propulsion Subject Test (65Q / 100M).
