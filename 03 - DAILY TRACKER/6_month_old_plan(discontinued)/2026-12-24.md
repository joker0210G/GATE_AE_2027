### Step 2 — Single Degree of Freedom (SDOF) Undamped Vibrations (15 min)
Open `06 - Structural Dynamics`.

```
                    SDOF MASS-SPRING-DAMPER SYSTEM
                             k (Spring)
                      ┌─────/\/\/\/\─────┐
                      │                  │
                Mass  │        m         │ ──► Displacement x(t)
                      │                  │
                      └─────[====]───────┘
                             c (Damper)
```

1. **Free Undamped Motion ($m \ddot{x} + k x = 0$):**
   - Natural Circular Frequency: $\mathbf{\omega_n = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}\text{ (rad/s)}}$.
   - Cyclic Frequency: $\mathbf{f_n = \frac{\omega_n}{2\pi} = \frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}\text{ (Hz)}}$.
   - Time Period: $\mathbf{T_n = \frac{1}{f_n} = \frac{2\pi}{\omega_n} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{m}{k}}\text{ (s)}}$.

### Step 3 — Viscously Damped SDOF Vibrations (20 min)

Governing differential equation:
$$m \ddot{x} + c \dot{x} + k x = 0 \implies \ddot{x} + 2\zeta\omega_n\dot{x} + \omega_n^2 x = 0$$

1. **Critical Damping Coefficient ($c_{\text{cr}}$) & Damping Ratio ($\zeta$):**
   $$\boxed{c_{\text{cr}} = 2\sqrt{k m} = 2 m \omega_n, \qquad \zeta \equiv \frac{c}{c_{\text{cr}}} = \frac{c}{2\sqrt{k m}} = \frac{c}{2 m \omega_n}}$$

2. **Damping Regimes:**
   - **Underdamped ($\zeta < 1$):** Oscillatory decay with damped frequency:
     $$\boxed{\omega_d = \omega_n \sqrt{1 - \zeta^2}}$$
   - **Critically Damped ($\zeta = 1$):** Non-oscillatory, returns to rest fastest ($x(t) = (C_1 + C_2 t)e^{-\omega_n t}$).
   - **Overdamped ($\zeta > 1$):** Non-oscillatory sluggish exponential decay.

3. **Logarithmic Decrement ($\delta$):**
   Rate of decay of vibration amplitude over successive cycles ($x_1, x_2, \dots, x_{n+1}$):
   $$\boxed{\delta \equiv \ln\left(\frac{x_1}{x_2}\right) = \frac{1}{n}\ln\left(\frac{x_1}{x_{n+1}}\right) = \frac{2\pi\zeta}{\sqrt{1 - \zeta^2}} \approx 2\pi\zeta \quad (\text{for small } \zeta < 0.2)}$$

### Step 4 — Forced Harmonically Excited SDOF & 2-DOF Systems (20 min)

#### 1. Harmonically Forced Vibrations ($m \ddot{x} + c \dot{x} + k x = F_0 \sin(\omega t)$):
- Frequency Ratio: $\mathbf{r \equiv \frac{\omega}{\omega_n}}$.
- **Dynamic Magnification Factor (DMF):**
  $$\boxed{\text{DMF} \equiv \frac{X}{F_0 / k} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{(1 - r^2)^2 + (2\zeta r)^2}}}$$
  - At Resonance ($r = 1$): $\mathbf{\text{DMF}_{\text{res}} = \frac{1}{2\zeta}}$.

#### 2. Undamped 2-DOF Systems ($[M]\{\ddot{x}\} + [K]\{x\} = \{0\}$):
$$\boxed{\det([K] - \omega^2 [M]) = 0 \implies \det\begin{pmatrix} k_{11} - \omega^2 m_{11} & k_{12} - \omega^2 m_{12} \\ k_{21} - \omega^2 m_{21} & k_{22} - \omega^2 m_{22} \end{pmatrix} = 0}$$
- Expanding the determinant yields a quadratic in $\omega^2$ with two natural frequencies $\omega_1, \omega_2$ and two normal mode shapes $\{\phi\}_1, \{\phi\}_2$.

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Frequency in rad/s vs. Hz**
> $\omega_n$ is circular frequency in $\text{rad/s}$. Cyclic frequency $f_n$ is in $\text{Hz}$ ($\text{cycles/s}$). $f_n = \omega_n / (2\pi)$!
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Logarithmic Decrement with Multiple Cycles**
> If amplitude drops from $x_0$ to $x_n$ over $n$ cycles, $\delta = \frac{1}{n}\ln(x_0/x_n)$, NOT $\ln(x_0/x_n)$!

### Step 3 — D3 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** The circular natural frequency $\omega_n$ of a single degree of freedom system with mass $m$ and spring stiffness $k$ is:
> **(A)** $\sqrt{m/k}$ **(B)** $\sqrt{k/m}$ **(C)** $\frac{1}{2\pi}\sqrt{k/m}$ **(D)** $2\pi\sqrt{m/k}$

> **Q2 (1 mark):** For an underdamped SDOF system ($\zeta < 1$), the damped natural frequency $\omega_d$ is related to the undamped natural frequency $\omega_n$ by:
> **(A)** $\omega_d = \omega_n \sqrt{1 + \zeta^2}$ **(B)** $\omega_d = \omega_n \sqrt{1 - \zeta^2}$ **(C)** $\omega_d = \omega_n (1 - \zeta)$ **(D)** $\omega_d = \frac{\omega_n}{\sqrt{1 - \zeta^2}}$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A machine of mass $m = 25\text{ kg}$ is mounted on an elastic foundation of stiffness $k = 100\text{ kN/m}$ with viscous damping $c = 200\text{ N}\cdot\text{s/m}$. The damping ratio $\zeta$ of the system is:
> **(A)** $0.0316$ **(B)** $0.0632$ **(C)** $0.1000$ **(D)** $0.2000$

> **Q4 (2 marks):** In a free vibration test of an underdamped system, the initial amplitude is $x_0 = 10.0\text{ mm}$. After $5$ complete cycles, the amplitude decays to $x_5 = 2.0\text{ mm}$. The logarithmic decrement $\delta$ is:
> **(A)** $0.1609$ **(B)** $0.3219$ **(C)** $0.4828$ **(D)** $1.6094$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A 2-DOF undamped system has mass matrix $[M] = \begin{bmatrix} 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}\text{ kg}$ and stiffness matrix $[K] = \begin{bmatrix} 6 & -2 \\ -2 & 2 \end{bmatrix}\text{ N/m}$. The smallest natural circular frequency $\omega_1$ (in $\text{rad/s}$) is ___ $\text{rad/s}$.

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D3 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) √(k/m)**
> Standard SDOF formula.
>
> **Q2: (B) ω_n √(1 − ζ²)**
> Underdamped frequency definition.
>
> **Q3: (B) 0.0632**
> $c_{\text{cr}} = 2\sqrt{k m} = 2\sqrt{(100,000)(25)} = 2\sqrt{2,500,000} = 2(1581.14) = 3162.28\text{ N}\cdot\text{s/m}$.
> $\zeta = \frac{c}{c_{\text{cr}}} = \frac{200}{3162.28} \approx \mathbf{0.06325 \approx 0.0632}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) 0.3219**
> $\delta = \frac{1}{n}\ln\left(\frac{x_0}{x_n}\right) = \frac{1}{5}\ln\left(\frac{10.0}{2.0}\right) = \frac{1}{5}\ln(5) = \frac{1.6094}{5} = \mathbf{0.32188 \approx 0.3219}$.
>
> **Q5: 1.0**
> $\det\begin{pmatrix} 6 - 2\omega^2 & -2 \\ -2 & 2 - \omega^2 \end{pmatrix} = (6 - 2\omega^2)(2 - \omega^2) - 4 = 0$.
> $12 - 6\omega^2 - 4\omega^2 + 2\omega^4 - 4 = 0 \implies 2\omega^4 - 10\omega^2 + 8 = 0 \implies \omega^4 - 5\omega^2 + 4 = 0$.
> Roots for $\omega^2$: $(\omega^2 - 1)(\omega^2 - 4) = 0 \implies \omega_1^2 = 1, \omega_2^2 = 4$.
> $\omega_1 = \sqrt{1} = \mathbf{1.0\text{ rad/s}}$.
