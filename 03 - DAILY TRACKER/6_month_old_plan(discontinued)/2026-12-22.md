### Step 2 — Strain Energy Density ($u_0$) Fundamentals (15 min)
Open `02 - Trusses Bars Beams and Shafts`.

```
                    STRAIN ENERGY DENSITY (AREA UNDER STRESS-STRAIN CURVE)
                            σ ▲
                              │        /
                              │       / Area = u_0 = (1/2) σ ε = σ² / (2E)
                              │      /  (Strain Energy Density)
                              │     /
                              │    /
                              │   /
                   ───────────┼──/──────────► ε
                              │
```

1. **Uniaxial Normal Stress:**
   $$\boxed{u_0 = \frac{1}{2}\sigma \epsilon = \frac{\sigma^2}{2E} = \frac{1}{2}E \epsilon^2 \quad (\text{Units: J/m}^3\text{ or N}\cdot\text{mm/mm}^3)}$$

2. **Pure Shear Stress:**
   $$\boxed{u_0 = \frac{1}{2}\tau \gamma = \frac{\tau^2}{2G} = \frac{1}{2}G \gamma^2}$$

3. **General 3D Triaxial Stress State:**
   $$u_0 = \frac{1}{2E}\left(\sigma_x^2 + \sigma_y^2 + \sigma_z^2 - 2\nu(\sigma_x\sigma_y + \sigma_y\sigma_z + \sigma_z\sigma_x)\right) + \frac{1}{2G}\left(\tau_{xy}^2 + \tau_{yz}^2 + \tau_{zx}^2\right)$$

### Step 3 — Total Strain Energy in Structural Members ($U$) (25 min)

Total strain energy $U = \iiint u_0 \, dV$:

```
                    TOTAL STRAIN ENERGY FORMULATIONS ACROSS MEMBERS
  Loading Mode      Governing Formula                                 Uniform Prismatic Member
  ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1. Axial Load P   U_axial   = ∫_0^L [P(x)² / (2 E A)] dx            P² L / (2 E A)
  2. Bending M      U_bending = ∫_0^L [M(x)² / (2 E I)] dx            ∫ M(x)² / (2 E I) dx
  3. Torsion T      U_torsion = ∫_0^L [T(x)² / (2 G J)] dx            T² L / (2 G J)
  4. Shear Force V  U_shear   = ∫_0^L [f_s V(x)² / (2 G A)] dx        f_s V² L / (2 G A)
```

- **Shear Form Factor ($f_s$):**
  - Rectangular cross-section: $\mathbf{f_s = \frac{6}{5} = 1.20}$.
  - Solid circular cross-section: $\mathbf{f_s = \frac{10}{9} \approx 1.111}$.
  - Thin-walled circular tube: $\mathbf{f_s = 2.0}$.
  - I-beam: $\mathbf{f_s \approx \frac{A}{A_{\text{web}}}}$.

### Step 4 — Castigliano's First Theorem (10 min)

For any elastic structure (linear or non-linear) with total strain energy $U(\Delta_1, \Delta_2, \dots, \Delta_n)$ expressed as a function of generalized displacements:

$$\boxed{P_i = \frac{\partial U}{\partial \Delta_i}, \qquad M_i = \frac{\partial U}{\partial \theta_i}}$$
*(The partial derivative of total strain energy with respect to a generalized displacement $\Delta_i$ yields the corresponding generalized force $P_i$ acting at that point).*

---

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Shear Strain Energy Neglect in Slender Beams**
> In slender beams ($L/h > 10$), bending strain energy dominates ($U_{\text{bending}} \gg U_{\text{shear}}$). Shear strain energy is typically less than $1\text{--}2\%$ of bending energy and can be neglected unless the beam is very deep and short ($L/h < 4$).
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Principle of Superposition for Strain Energy**
> Strain energy is quadratic ($U \propto P^2, M^2$). Therefore, **STRAIN ENERGIES CANNOT BE DIRECTLY ADDED FOR INDIVIDUAL LOADS**: $U(P_1 + P_2) \ne U(P_1) + U(P_2)$! You must sum the internal moments/forces first before squaring: $U = \int \frac{(M_1 + M_2)^2}{2EI} dx$.

### Step 3 — D1 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** The strain energy density $u_0$ for a linear elastic material subjected to uniaxial normal stress $\sigma$ is:
> **(A)** $\frac{\sigma^2}{E}$ **(B)** $\frac{\sigma^2}{2E}$ **(C)** $\frac{\sigma^2}{3E}$ **(D)** $\frac{E \sigma^2}{2}$

> **Q2 (1 mark):** According to Castigliano's First Theorem, the partial derivative of total strain energy $U$ with respect to generalized displacement $\Delta_i$ gives the:
> **(A)** Deflection $\Delta_i$ **(B)** Corresponding generalized load $P_i$ **(C)** Strain energy density **(D)** Flexural rigidity

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A cantilever beam of length $L = 2.0\text{ m}$ with flexural rigidity $EI = 1.0\times 10^6\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2$ is subjected to a tip concentrated load $P = 6.0\text{ kN}$. The total bending strain energy stored in the beam (in Joules) is:
> **(A)** $12.0\text{ J}$ **(B)** $24.0\text{ J}$ **(C)** $48.0\text{ J}$ **(D)** $72.0\text{ J}$

> **Q4 (2 marks):** A solid circular steel shaft ($G = 80\text{ GPa}$) of diameter $d = 40\text{ mm}$ and length $L = 1.5\text{ m}$ transmits a twisting torque $T = 800\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}$. The total torsional strain energy stored in the shaft (in Joules) is:
> **(A)** $12.5\text{ J}$ **(B)** $23.87\text{ J}$ **(C)** $37.5\text{ J}$ **(D)** $47.75\text{ J}$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A simply supported beam of length $L = 4.0\text{ m}$ with $EI = 2.0\times 10^6\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2$ carries a central concentrated load $P = 12.0\text{ kN}$. The total bending strain energy stored in the beam (in Joules) is ___ $\text{J}$.

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D1 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) σ² / (2E)**
> Standard strain energy density formula.
>
> **Q2: (B) Corresponding generalized load P_i**
> $P_i = \partial U / \partial \Delta_i$.
>
> **Q3: (C) 48.0 J**
> $U = \frac{P^2 L^3}{6 EI} = \frac{(6000^2)(2.0^3)}{6(1.0\times 10^6)} = \frac{(3.6\times 10^7)(8)}{6.0\times 10^6} = \frac{2.88\times 10^8}{6.0\times 10^6} = \mathbf{48.0\text{ J}}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) 23.87 J**
> $J = \frac{\pi(0.040^4)}{32} = \frac{\pi(2.56\times 10^{-6})}{32} = 8.0\pi\times 10^{-8} = 2.5133\times 10^{-7}\text{ m}^4$.
> $U = \frac{T^2 L}{2 G J} = \frac{(800^2)(1.5)}{2(80\times 10^9)(2.5133\times 10^{-7})} = \frac{640,000 \times 1.5}{40,212.4} = \frac{960,000}{40212.4} \approx \mathbf{23.87\text{ J}}$.
>
> **Q5: 48.0**
> For simply supported beam with center load $P$:
> $U = \frac{P^2 L^3}{96 EI} = \frac{(12000^2)(4^3)}{96(2.0\times 10^6)} = \frac{(1.44\times 10^8)(64)}{1.92\times 10^8} = \frac{9.216\times 10^9}{1.92\times 10^8} = \mathbf{48.0\text{ J}}$.
