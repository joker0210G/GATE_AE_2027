### Step 2 — Conservation of Energy & Stagnation Temperature (15 min)
Open `01.2c - Conservation of Energy`.

1. **Steady Flow Energy Equation (SFEE per unit mass):**
   $$q - w_s = (h_2 - h_1) + \frac{1}{2}(V_2^2 - V_1^2) + g(z_2 - z_1)$$
2. **Total / Stagnation Enthalpy ($h_0$):**
   $$\boxed{h_0 = h + \frac{1}{2}V^2}$$
   In adiabatic flow with no shaft work ($q = 0, w_s = 0$): $\mathbf{h_0 = \text{constant}}$ along every streamline!
3. **Stagnation Temperature ($T_0$) for Calorically Perfect Gas ($h = c_p T$):**
   $$c_p T_0 = c_p T + \frac{1}{2}V^2 \implies \boxed{T_0 = T\left(1 + \frac{\gamma - 1}{2}M^2\right) = \text{constant}}$$
4. **Adiabatic Wall Recovery Temperature ($T_{aw}$):**
   Due to viscous dissipation in the boundary layer:
   $$\boxed{T_{aw} = T_e\left(1 + r \frac{\gamma - 1}{2}M_e^2\right)}$$
   - **Laminar Boundary Layer Recovery Factor:** $\mathbf{r_{\text{lam}} = \sqrt{Pr} \approx 0.84}$ (for air, $Pr \approx 0.71$)
   - **Turbulent Boundary Layer Recovery Factor:** $\mathbf{r_{\text{turb}} = \sqrt[3]{Pr} \approx 0.89}$

### Step 3 — Dimensional Analysis & Buckingham Pi Theorem (15 min)
Open `01.3 - Dimensional Analysis and Dynamic Similarity`.

Given $n$ physical variables involving $k$ primary fundamental dimensions ($M, L, T, \theta$):
$$\boxed{\text{Number of Dimensionless Groups } N_\Pi = n - k}$$

- **Primary Fundamental Dimensions in Aerodynamics:** Mass ($M$), Length ($L$), Time ($T$), Temperature ($\theta$).

---

### Step 4 — Master Aerodynamic Dimensionless Numbers Table (15 min)

> 🔴 **MEMORIZE ALL 7 DIMENSIONLESS NUMBERS & PHYSICAL FORCE RATIOS.**

| Dimensionless Number | Mathematical Definition | Physical Force Ratio | Primary Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Reynolds Number ($Re$)** | $\dfrac{\rho V L}{\mu} = \dfrac{V L}{\nu}$ | $\dfrac{\text{Inertia Force}}{\text{Viscous Force}}$ | Boundary layers, Aerodynamic drag, Turbulence onset |
| **Mach Number ($M$)** | $\dfrac{V}{a} = \dfrac{V}{\sqrt{\gamma R T}}$ | $\sqrt{\dfrac{\text{Inertia Force}}{\text{Compressibility Force}}}$ | Compressible flow, Shocks, Sonic booms |
| **Froude Number ($Fr$)** | $\dfrac{V}{\sqrt{gL}}$ | $\sqrt{\dfrac{\text{Inertia Force}}{\text{Gravitational Force}}}$ | Seaplane hulls, Free-surface wave drag |
| **Weber Number ($We$)** | $\dfrac{\rho V^2 L}{\sigma}$ | $\dfrac{\text{Inertia Force}}{\text{Surface Tension Force}}$ | Fuel spray atomization, Droplet breakup |
| **Pressure Coefficient ($C_p$)** | $\dfrac{p - p_\infty}{\frac{1}{2}\rho_\infty V_\infty^2}$ | $\dfrac{\text{Static Pressure Force}}{\text{Dynamic Pressure Force}}$ | Airfoil surface pressure distributions |
| **Prandtl Number ($Pr$)** | $\dfrac{\mu c_p}{k} = \dfrac{\nu}{\alpha}$ | $\dfrac{\text{Momentum Diffusivity}}{\text{Thermal Diffusivity}}$ | Aerodynamic kinetic heating, Heat transfer |
| **Knudsen Number ($Kn$)** | $\dfrac{\lambda}{L} = \sqrt{\dfrac{\gamma\pi}{2}}\dfrac{M}{Re}$ | $\dfrac{\text{Mean Free Path}}{\text{Characteristic Body Length}}$ | Continuum breakdown threshold ($Kn < 0.01$) |

### Step 5 — Wind Tunnel Dynamic Similarity & Scaling Laws (10 min)
Open `01.3a - Dynamic Similarity and Wind Tunnel Model Testing`.

To achieve **complete dynamic similarity** between a scaled wind tunnel model ($m$) and full-scale prototype ($p$):
$$\boxed{Re_m = Re_p \qquad \text{and} \qquad M_m = M_p}$$

#### 1. Incompressible Wind Tunnel Force Scaling:
When $Re_m = Re_p \implies \frac{\rho_m V_m L_m}{\mu_m} = \frac{\rho_p V_p L_p}{\mu_p}$:
$$\boxed{F_m = F_p \cdot \left(\frac{\rho_m}{\rho_p}\right)\left(\frac{V_m}{V_p}\right)^2\left(\frac{L_m}{L_p}\right)^2}$$

#### 2. The Reynolds-Mach Scaling Conflict & Pressurized Tunnel Solution:
For scale factor $\lambda_L = L_m / L_p < 1$ in identical air ($T_m = T_p \implies V_m = V_p$):
Matching $Re$ requires:
$$\rho_m = \rho_p \cdot \left(\frac{L_p}{L_m}\right) \implies \boxed{p_m = p_p \cdot \left(\frac{L_p}{L_m}\right)}$$
*(To test a $1:10$ scale model at the same Mach number and Reynolds number, the wind tunnel must be **pressurized to 10 atmospheres**!)*

### Step 3 — D5 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** The number of independent dimensionless $\Pi$-groups required to express the aerodynamic lift $L$ on a wing of chord $c$ and span $b$ flying at speed $V$ in air of density $\rho$, viscosity $\mu$, and speed of sound $a$ is:
> **(A)** $3$ **(B)** $4$ **(C)** $5$ **(D)** $2$

> **Q2 (2 marks):** Air ($\gamma = 1.4$) flows at Mach $M = 3.0$ with a static temperature $T = 200\text{ K}$. The stagnation temperature $T_0$ is:
> **(A)** $560\text{ K}$ **(B)** $480\text{ K}$ **(C)** $360\text{ K}$ **(D)** $600\text{ K}$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A $1:10$ scale model of an aircraft is tested in a wind tunnel using the same air at the same temperature. To achieve dynamic similarity at low speed (incompressible, matching $Re$), the model test speed $V_m$ must be related to prototype speed $V_p$ by:
> **(A)** $V_m = 0.1 V_p$ **(B)** $V_m = V_p$ **(C)** $V_m = 10 V_p$ **(D)** $V_m = 100 V_p$

> **Q4 (1 mark):** The Prandtl number $Pr$ represents the ratio of:
> **(A)** Inertia force to viscous force **(B)** Kinematic viscosity to thermal diffusivity **(C)** Dynamic pressure to static pressure **(D)** Boundary layer thickness to chord

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** The lift force measured on a $1:4$ scale model in a wind tunnel is $200\text{ N}$ when operated at dynamically similar conditions ($Re_m = Re_p$) with the same fluid density and velocity ($V_m = 4 V_p$). The lift force on the full-scale prototype is ___ $\text{N}$.

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D5 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) 4**
> Total variables: $n = 7$ ($L, c, b, V, \rho, \mu, a$).
> Fundamental dimensions: $k = 3$ ($M, L, T$).
> Number of $\Pi$-groups: $N_\Pi = n - k = 7 - 3 = \mathbf{4}$ ($C_L, \text{Aspect Ratio } b/c, Re, M$).
>
> **Q2: (A) 560 K**
> $T_0 = T\left[1 + \frac{\gamma-1}{2}M^2\right] = 200[1 + 0.2(3.0^2)] = 200[1 + 0.2(9)] = 200[1 + 1.8] = 200(2.8) = \mathbf{560\text{ K}}$.
>
> **Q3: (C) V_m = 10 V_p**
> $Re_m = Re_p \implies \frac{\rho V_m L_m}{\mu} = \frac{\rho V_p L_p}{\mu} \implies V_m L_m = V_p L_p \implies V_m = V_p \left(\frac{L_p}{L_m}\right) = V_p(10) = \mathbf{10 V_p}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) Kinematic viscosity to thermal diffusivity**
> $Pr = \frac{\nu}{\alpha} = \frac{\mu c_p}{k}$ (Ratio of momentum diffusivity to thermal diffusivity).
>
> **Q5: 200**
> Dynamic pressure and force scaling:
> $F = C_L \cdot \frac{1}{2}\rho V^2 A \propto \rho V^2 L^2$.
> $\frac{F_m}{F_p} = \left(\frac{\rho_m}{\rho_p}\right)\left(\frac{V_m}{V_p}\right)^2\left(\frac{L_m}{L_p}\right)^2 = (1) \cdot (4)^2 \cdot \left(\frac{1}{4}\right)^2 = 16 \cdot \frac{1}{16} = 1$.
> $F_p = F_m = \mathbf{200\text{ N}}$.
