### Step 1 — Engineering Mathematics Top Invariants (20 min)
1. **Eigenvalues & Invariants:** $\text{Tr}(A) = \sum \lambda_i$, $\det(A) = \prod \lambda_i$, $A \vec{x} = \lambda \vec{x} \implies A^k \vec{x} = \lambda^k \vec{x}$.
2. **Vector Integral Theorems:**
   - Gauss Divergence: $\iint_S \vec{F}\cdot\hat{n}\,dS = \iiint_V (\nabla\cdot\vec{F})\,dV$.
   - Stokes' Theorem: $\oint_C \vec{F}\cdot d\vec{r} = \iint_S (\nabla\times\vec{F})\cdot\hat{n}\,dS$.
   - Green's Theorem: $\oint_C (P\,dx + Q\,dy) = \iint_R \left(\frac{\partial Q}{\partial x} - \frac{\partial P}{\partial y}\right) dA$.
3. **Differential Equations:**
   - Exact differential equation: $\frac{\partial M}{\partial y} = \frac{\partial N}{\partial x} \implies \int M dx + \int (\text{terms in } N \text{ without } x) dy = C$.

### Step 2 — Aerodynamics Top Invariants (25 min)
1. **Thin Airfoil Theory:**
   - Symmetric: $C_l = 2\pi\alpha, C_{m,c/4} = 0, x_{\text{ac}} = c/4$.
   - Cambered: $C_l = 2\pi(\alpha - \alpha_{L=0}), C_{m,c/4} = \frac{\pi}{4}(A_2 - A_1)$.
2. **Finite Wing & Induced Drag:**
   - $C_D = C_{D0} + C_{Di} = C_{D0} + \frac{C_L^2}{\pi e \text{AR}}$.
   - Downwash: $w = \frac{V_\infty C_L}{\pi \text{AR}}$ (for elliptical wing).
3. **Compressible Flow & Shocks:**
   - Oblique Shock: $\tan\theta = 2\cot\beta \left[\frac{M_1^2 \sin^2\beta - 1}{M_1^2(\gamma + \cos 2\beta) + 2}\right]$.
   - Supersonic Flat Plate: $c_l = \frac{4\alpha}{\sqrt{M_\infty^2 - 1}}, c_{d,w} = \frac{4\alpha^2}{\sqrt{M_\infty^2 - 1}}$.
4. **Boundary Layer Theory:**
   - Laminar: $\delta/x = \frac{5.0}{\sqrt{\text{Re}_x}}, C_f = \frac{0.664}{\sqrt{\text{Re}_x}}, C_D = \frac{1.328}{\sqrt{\text{Re}_L}}$.
   - Turbulent: $\delta/x = \frac{0.37}{\text{Re}_x^{1/5}}, C_f = \frac{0.0592}{\text{Re}_x^{1/5}}, C_D = \frac{0.074}{\text{Re}_L^{1/5}}$.

### Step 3 — D4 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** The trace of a $2\times 2$ matrix with determinant $6$ and eigenvalues $\lambda_1, \lambda_2$ such that $\lambda_1 + \lambda_2 = 5$ is:
> **(A)** $1$ **(B)** $5$ **(C)** $6$ **(D)** $11$

> **Q2 (1 mark):** For a cambered thin airfoil, the zero-lift pitching moment coefficient about the quarter-chord $C_{m,c/4}$ is:
> **(A)** Always zero **(B)** Generally non-zero and independent of angle of attack **(C)** Linearly proportional to $\alpha$ **(D)** Positive for positive camber

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A finite wing of aspect ratio $\text{AR} = 8.0$ and span efficiency $e = 0.95$ flies at lift coefficient $C_L = 0.80$. The induced drag coefficient $C_{Di}$ is:
> **(A)** $0.0160$ **(B)** $0.0268$ **(C)** $0.0350$ **(D)** $0.0536$

> **Q4 (2 marks):** Air flows over a flat plate of length $L = 1.0\text{ m}$ at $\text{Re}_L = 10^5$ (laminar everywhere). The total skin friction drag coefficient $C_D$ is:
> **(A)** $0.00133$ **(B)** $0.00210$ **(C)** $0.00420$ **(D)** $0.00840$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** An oblique shock wave has upstream Mach number $M_1 = 3.0$ and wave angle $\beta = 30^\circ$. The upstream normal Mach number $M_{n1}$ is ___ .

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D4 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) 5**
> $\text{Tr}(A) = \lambda_1 + \lambda_2 = \mathbf{5}$.
>
> **Q2: (B) Generally non-zero and independent of angle of attack**
> For cambered thin airfoils, $C_{m,c/4} = \frac{\pi}{4}(A_2 - A_1) = \text{const} \ne 0$.
>
> **Q3: (B) 0.0268**
> $C_{Di} = \frac{C_L^2}{\pi e \text{AR}} = \frac{0.80^2}{\pi(0.95)(8.0)} = \frac{0.64}{23.876} \approx \mathbf{0.0268}$.
>
> **Q4: (C) 0.00420**
> $C_D = \frac{1.328}{\sqrt{\text{Re}_L}} = \frac{1.328}{\sqrt{100,000}} = \frac{1.328}{316.228} \approx \mathbf{0.00420}$.
>
> **Q5: 1.5**
> $M_{n1} = M_1 \sin\beta = 3.0 \sin(30^\circ) = 3.0(0.5) = \mathbf{1.50}$.
