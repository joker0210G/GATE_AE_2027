**BLOCK A: Direct Concept & Formula Recall (1 Mark Each, Q1–Q10)**

**Q1.** In the Euler-Bernoulli theory of bending, the transverse shear strain $\gamma_{xy}$ across the beam cross-section is assumed to be:
(A) Maximum at the neutral axis (B) Linearly varying (C) Strictly zero (D) Equal to $\epsilon_x$

**Q2.** For a rectangular beam of width $b$ and depth $h$, the section modulus $Z$ is:
(A) $\frac{b h^3}{12}$ (B) $\frac{b h^2}{6}$ (C) $\frac{b^2 h}{6}$ (D) $\frac{b h^2}{12}$

**Q3.** The beam curvature $\kappa = 1/R$ is related to the applied bending moment $M$ and flexural rigidity $EI$ by:
(A) $\kappa = \frac{M}{EI}$ (B) $\kappa = -\frac{M}{EI}$ (C) $\kappa = \frac{EI}{M}$ (D) $\kappa = -\frac{EI}{M}$

**Q4.** According to Mohr's Second Moment-Area Theorem, the tangential deviation $t_{B/A}$ of point B from the tangent at point A is:
(A) The area of the $M/EI$ diagram between A and B
(B) The first moment of the $M/EI$ diagram area between A and B about point B
(C) The first moment of the $M/EI$ diagram area between A and B about point A
(D) The maximum bending moment divided by $EI$

**Q5.** A cantilever beam of length $L$ and rigidity $EI$ carries a uniform distributed load $w$ over its entire span. The maximum tip deflection is:
(A) $\frac{w L^4}{24EI}$ (B) $\frac{w L^4}{8EI}$ (C) $\frac{w L^3}{6EI}$ (D) $\frac{5 w L^4}{384EI}$

**Q6.** The product of inertia $I_{xy}$ about the centroidal axes of a cross-section is GUARANTEED to be zero if:
(A) The section is rectangular only
(B) The section possesses at least one axis of geometric symmetry
(C) The cross-sectional area is small
(D) The material is isotropic

**Q7.** For an equal-leg structural angle section (L-section) referred to standard horizontal and vertical centroidal axes, the product of inertia $I_{xy}$ is:
(A) Zero (B) Always non-zero (C) Equal to $I_x + I_y$ (D) Negative always

**Q8.** In a beam subjected to unsymmetrical bending under vertical moment $M_x$ ($M_y = 0$) with $I_{xy} \ne 0$, the Neutral Axis:
(A) Coincides with the horizontal $x$-axis
(B) Is inclined to the horizontal $x$-axis at angle $\tan\alpha_{\text{NA}} = \frac{I_{xy}}{I_y}$
(C) Is perpendicular to the vertical $y$-axis
(D) Coincides with the principal axis of maximum inertia

**Q9.** The maximum bending stress in an unsymmetrical beam occurs at:
(A) The centroid $(0,0)$
(B) The point on the perimeter furthest from the Neutral Axis
(C) The point of maximum shear flow
(D) The shear center

**Q10.** The ratio of the center deflection of a fixed-fixed beam to a simply supported beam under an identical center point load $P$ is:
(A) $1/2$ (B) $1/3$ (C) $1/4$ (D) $1/5$

**BLOCK B: Standard & Multi-Step Problems (2 Marks Each, Q11–Q25)**

**Q11.** A rectangular timber beam of width $b = 100\text{ mm}$ and depth $h = 200\text{ mm}$ is subjected to a bending moment $M = 20\text{ kN}\cdot\text{m}$. The maximum bending stress $\sigma_{\max}$ (in $\text{MPa}$) is:
(A) $15.0\text{ MPa}$ (B) $30.0\text{ MPa}$ (C) $45.0\text{ MPa}$ (D) $60.0\text{ MPa}$

**Q12.** A solid circular steel shaft of diameter $d = 50\text{ mm}$ ($E = 200\text{ GPa}$) is subjected to a pure bending moment $M = 1.227\text{ kN}\cdot\text{m}$. The radius of curvature of the bent shaft (in meters) is:
(A) $50.0\text{ m}$ (B) $100.0\text{ m}$ (C) $150.0\text{ m}$ (D) $200.0\text{ m}$

**Q13.** A cantilever beam of span $L = 3.0\text{ m}$ carries a point load $P = 10.0\text{ kN}$ at its free tip. If $EI = 4.5\times 10^6\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2$, the tip slope $\theta_{\text{tip}}$ (in radians) is:
(A) $0.005\text{ rad}$ (B) $0.010\text{ rad}$ (C) $0.015\text{ rad}$ (D) $0.020\text{ rad}$

**Q14.** For the same cantilever beam in Q13 ($L = 3.0\text{ m}, P = 10.0\text{ kN}, EI = 4.5\times 10^6\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2$), the maximum tip deflection $\delta_{\text{tip}}$ (in $\text{mm}$) is:
(A) $10.0\text{ mm}$ (B) $15.0\text{ mm}$ (C) $20.0\text{ mm}$ (D) $25.0\text{ mm}$

**Q15.** A simply supported beam of length $L = 6.0\text{ m}$ carries a uniform distributed load $w = 8.0\text{ kN/m}$ over its entire span. If $EI = 1.8\times 10^7\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2$, the midspan deflection $\delta_{\text{mid}}$ (in $\text{mm}$) is:
(A) $5.0\text{ mm}$ (B) $7.5\text{ mm}$ (C) $10.0\text{ mm}$ (D) $12.5\text{ mm}$

**Q16.** A thin-walled equal Z-section has flanges of width $b = 50\text{ mm}$, web height $h = 120\text{ mm}$, and thickness $t = 2.0\text{ mm}$. The product of inertia $I_{xy}$ about the centroidal axes (in $\text{mm}^4$) is:
(A) $1.5\times 10^5\text{ mm}^4$ (B) $3.0\times 10^5\text{ mm}^4$ (C) $4.5\times 10^5\text{ mm}^4$ (D) $6.0\times 10^5\text{ mm}^4$

**Q17.** A cross-section has $I_x = 16.0\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4, I_y = 6.0\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4$, and $I_{xy} = -5.0\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4$. The principal axes angle $\theta_p$ (in degrees) is:
(A) $-22.5^\circ$ (B) $22.5^\circ$ (C) $-45.0^\circ$ (D) $45.0^\circ$

**Q18.** For the section in Q17 ($I_x = 16\times 10^6, I_y = 6\times 10^6, I_{xy} = -5\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4$), the maximum principal second moment of area $I_1$ (in $10^6\text{ mm}^4$) is:
(A) $14.07\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4$ (B) $16.07\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4$ (C) $18.07\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4$ (D) $20.07\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4$

**Q19.** A beam with $I_x = 15.0\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4, I_y = 5.0\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4$, and $I_{xy} = 2.5\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4$ is subjected to pure bending moment $M_x = 20.0\text{ kN}\cdot\text{m}$ ($M_y = 0$). The slope of the Neutral Axis $\tan\alpha_{\text{NA}}$ is:
(A) $0.25$ (B) $0.50$ (C) $0.75$ (D) $1.00$

**Q20.** For the same beam in Q19 ($I_x = 15\times 10^6, I_y = 5\times 10^6, I_{xy} = 2.5\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4, M_x = 20\text{ kN}\cdot\text{m}$), the bending stress $\sigma_z$ at $(x = 20\text{ mm}, y = 40\text{ mm})$ (in $\text{MPa}$) is:
(A) $29.1\text{ MPa}$ (B) $43.6\text{ MPa}$ (C) $58.2\text{ MPa}$ (D) $72.7\text{ MPa}$

**Q21.** A symmetrical rectangular beam with $I_x = 30.0\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4, I_y = 10.0\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4$, and $I_{xy} = 0$ is subjected to biaxial bending with $M_x = 15.0\text{ kN}\cdot\text{m}$ and $M_y = 5.0\text{ kN}\cdot\text{m}$. The inclination of the Neutral Axis $\alpha_{\text{NA}}$ with the $x$-axis is:

**Q22.** A cantilever of length $L$ carries a concentrated moment $M_0$ at the free end. Using Moment-Area theorem, the maximum tip deflection is:

**Q23.** An I-beam with $I_x = 4.0\times 10^7\text{ mm}^4$ and depth $H = 240\text{ mm}$ is bent to a radius of curvature $R = 80.0\text{ m}$ ($E = 200\text{ GPa}$). The maximum bending moment $M$ carried by the beam (in $\text{kN}\cdot\text{m}$) is:

## ⏰ SESSION 2 (1:45 – 4:00): SCORING + COMPREHENSIVE ERROR REPAIR

### Step 1 — Instant Scoring & Verification (1:45 – 2:15)

> [!faq]- 🔍 View Complete 30Q Answer Key & Worked Solutions
>
> **Q1: (C) Strictly zero** Euler-Bernoulli assumption $\gamma_{xy} = 0$.
> **Q2: (B) b h² / 6** Rectangle section modulus.
> **Q3: (B) κ = −M / EI** Curvature relation with sagging convention.
> **Q4: (B) First moment about point B** Mohr's 2nd Moment-Area Theorem.
> **Q5: (B) w L⁴ / (8 EI)** Cantilever under full UDL tip deflection.
> **Q6: (B) At least one axis of geometric symmetry** Symmetry theorem for $I_{xy}$.
> **Q7: (B) Always non-zero** L-angle has no Cartesian axis of symmetry.
> **Q8: (B) tan α_NA = I_xy / I_y** Neutral axis slope under pure $M_x$.
> **Q9: (B) Furthest from the Neutral Axis** Maximum stress location rule.
> **Q10: (C) 1/4** Fixed-fixed deflection $\frac{P L^3}{192 EI} = \frac{1}{4}\frac{P L^3}{48 EI}$.
>
> **Q11: (B) 30.0 MPa**
> $Z = \frac{b h^2}{6} = \frac{100(200^2)}{6} = \frac{4,000,000}{6} = 666,666.7\text{ mm}^3$.
> $\sigma_{\max} = \frac{20\times 10^6}{666666.7} = \mathbf{30.0\text{ MPa}}$.
>
> **Q12: (B) 100.0 m**
> $I = \frac{\pi(0.050^4)}{64} = 3.06796\times 10^{-7}\text{ m}^4$.
> $R = \frac{EI}{M} = \frac{(200\times 10^9)(3.06796\times 10^{-7})}{1227} = \frac{61359.2}{1227} = \mathbf{100.0\text{ m}}$.
>
> **Q13: (B) 0.010 rad**
> $\theta_{\text{tip}} = \frac{P L^2}{2EI} = \frac{(10000)(3^2)}{2(4.5\times 10^6)} = \frac{90000}{9.0\times 10^6} = \mathbf{0.010\text{ rad}}$.
>
> **Q14: (C) 20.0 mm**
> $\delta_{\text{tip}} = \frac{P L^3}{3EI} = \frac{(10000)(3^3)}{3(4.5\times 10^6)} = \frac{270000}{13.5\times 10^6} = 0.020\text{ m} = \mathbf{20.0\text{ mm}}$.
>
> **Q15: (B) 7.5 mm**
> $\delta_{\text{mid}} = \frac{5 w L^4}{384 EI} = \frac{5(8000)(6^4)}{384(1.8\times 10^7)} = \frac{40000 \times 1296}{6.912\times 10^9} = \frac{51,840,000}{6.912\times 10^9} = 0.0075\text{ m} = \mathbf{7.5\text{ mm}}$.
>
> **Q16: (B) 3.0 × 10⁵ mm⁴**
> $I_{xy} = \frac{b^2 h t}{2} = \frac{(50^2)(120)(2.0)}{2} = (2500)(120) = \mathbf{300,000\text{ mm}^4 = 3.0\times 10^5\text{ mm}^4}$.
>
> **Q17: (B) 22.5°**
> $\tan 2\theta_p = \frac{2 I_{xy}}{I_y - I_x} = \frac{2(-5.0\times 10^6)}{6.0\times 10^6 - 16.0\times 10^6} = \frac{-10.0}{-10.0} = 1.0 \implies 2\theta_p = 45^\circ \implies \mathbf{\theta_p = 22.5^\circ}$.
>
> **Q18: (C) 18.07 × 10⁶ mm⁴**
> $I_{\text{avg}} = \frac{16+6}{2}\times 10^6 = 11.0\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4$.
> $R = \sqrt{(\frac{16-6}{2})^2 + (-5)^2}\times 10^6 = \sqrt{5^2 + 25}\times 10^6 = \sqrt{50}\times 10^6 \approx 7.071\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4$.
> $I_1 = 11.0 + 7.071 = \mathbf{18.071\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4 \approx 18.07\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4}$.
>
> **Q19: (B) 0.50**
> $\tan\alpha_{\text{NA}} = \frac{I_{xy}}{I_y} = \frac{2.5\times 10^6}{5.0\times 10^6} = \mathbf{0.50}$.
>
> **Q20: (B) 43.6 MPa**
> $\text{Denom} = (15)(5) - 2.5^2 = 75 - 6.25 = 68.75\times 10^{12}\text{ mm}^8$.
> $\sigma_z = \frac{20\times 10^6}{68.75\times 10^{12}}[5.0\times 10^6(40) - 2.5\times 10^6(20)] = \frac{20}{68.75}[200 - 50] = \frac{20 \times 150}{68.75} = \frac{3000}{68.75} \approx \mathbf{43.64\text{ MPa} \approx 43.6\text{ MPa}}$.
>
> **Q21: (B) −45.0°**
> $\tan\alpha_{\text{NA}} = -\frac{M_y I_x}{M_x I_y} = -\frac{(5.0)(30.0)}{(15.0)(10.0)} = -\frac{150}{150} = -1.0 \implies \mathbf{\alpha_{\text{NA}} = -45.0^\circ}$.
>
> **Q22: (B) M_0 L² / (2 EI)**
> Direct derivation from Moment-Area theorem.
>
> **Q23: (B) 100.0 kN·m**
> $M = \frac{EI}{R} = \frac{(200\times 10^9\text{ N/m}^2)(4.0\times 10^{-5}\text{ m}^4)}{80.0\text{ m}} = \frac{8.0\times 10^6}{80.0} = 100,000\text{ N}\cdot\text{m} = \mathbf{100.0\text{ kN}\cdot\text{m}}$.
>
> **Q24: (C) 4 P L² / 81 EI**
> Standard formula $\theta_A = \frac{P b (L^2 - b^2)}{6 E I L}$ with $b = 2L/3 \implies \theta_A = \frac{P(2L/3)(L^2 - 4L^2/9)}{6 E I L} = \frac{2P L(5L^2/9)}{18 E I L} = \mathbf{\frac{4 P L^2}{81 EI}}$.
>
> **Q25: (B) 0.875**
> Coefficient of $y = \frac{M_x I_y - M_y I_{xy}}{I_x I_y - I_{xy}^2} = \frac{(10\times 10^6)(5\times 10^6) - (5\times 10^6)(3\times 10^6)}{40\times 10^{12}} = \frac{50 - 15}{40} = \frac{35}{40} = \mathbf{0.875\text{ N/mm}^3}$.
>
> **Q26: 3.3**
> $\delta_{\text{mid}} = \frac{P L^3}{48 EI} = \frac{(32000)(4^3)}{48(1.28\times 10^7)} = \frac{32000 \times 64}{6.144\times 10^8} = \frac{2,048,000}{614,400,000} = 0.003333\text{ m} = \mathbf{3.3\text{ mm}}$.
>
> **Q27: 4.0**
> $\delta_{\text{tip}} = \frac{w L^4}{8 EI} = \frac{(12000)(2^4)}{8(6.0\times 10^6)} = \frac{12000 \times 16}{4.8\times 10^7} = \frac{192000}{48000000} = 0.0040\text{ m} = \mathbf{4.0\text{ mm}}$.
>
> **Q28: 9.61**
> $I_{\text{avg}} = \frac{8+4}{2} = 6.0\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4$. $R = \sqrt{2^2 + 3^2}\times 10^6 = \sqrt{13}\times 10^6 \approx 3.6055\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4$.
> $I_1 = 6.0 + 3.6055 = \mathbf{9.6055\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4 \approx 9.61\times 10^6\text{ mm}^4}$.
>
> **Q29: 26.57**
> $\tan\alpha_{\text{NA}} = \frac{I_{xy}}{I_y} = \frac{4.0\times 10^6}{8.0\times 10^6} = 0.50 \implies \mathbf{\alpha_{\text{NA}} = \arctan(0.50) \approx 26.57^\circ}$.
>
> **Q30: 48.0**
> $Z = \frac{b h^2}{6} = \frac{50(100^2)}{6} = \frac{500,000}{6} = 83,333.3\text{ mm}^3$.
> $\sigma_{\max} = \frac{4.0\times 10^6}{83333.3} = \mathbf{48.0\text{ MPa}}$.
