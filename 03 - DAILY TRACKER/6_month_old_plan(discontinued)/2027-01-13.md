> ### SECTION 1: GENERAL APTITUDE
> **Q1: (A) consistent** "Consistent with" is the standard collocation.

> **Q3: (B) 7 / 6** $0.40(A + B) = 0.60(A - B) \implies 2(A + B) = 3(A - B) \implies 2A + 2B = 3A - 3B \implies A = 5B$. Expression: $\frac{2(5B) - 3B}{5B + B} = \frac{7B}{6B} = \mathbf{7/6}$.
> **Q4: (A) 7 / 13** 26 red cards + 2 black queens = 28 cards out of $52 \implies \frac{28}{52} = \mathbf{7/13}$.

> **Q6: (D) 24 km** Downstream speed $= 6 + 2 = 8\text{ km/h}$, Upstream speed $= 6 - 2 = 4\text{ km/h}$. $\frac{d}{4} - \frac{d}{8} = 3 \implies \frac{d}{8} = 3 \implies d = \mathbf{24\text{ km}}$.
> **Q7: (A) ₹4460** $P(1 + r)^3 = 6690, P(1 + r)^6 = 10035 \implies (1 + r)^3 = \frac{10035}{6690} = 1.50 \implies P = \frac{6690}{1.50} = \mathbf{₹4460}$.
> **Q8: (B) 3** $x = \sqrt{6 + x} \implies x^2 - x - 6 = 0 \implies (x - 3)(x + 2) = 0 \implies x = \mathbf{3}$.
> **Q9: (B) 33** Total $= 22 + 12 - 1 = \mathbf{33\text{ boys}}$.
> **Q10: (C) Both follow** Standard syllogism deduction.
