### Step 2 — Prandtl-Meyer Centered Expansion Fan (20 min)
Open `05.3 - Prandtl-Meyer Expansion Waves`.

When a supersonic flow ($M_1 > 1$) turns around a convex corner of angle $\theta$, it expands smoothly through a continuous fan of Mach lines:

```
                  PRANDTL-MEYER EXPANSION FAN GEOMETRY
                                Forward Mach Line (μ_1 = arcsin(1/M_1))
                                ╲
         V_1 (Mach M_1)          ╲
       ───────────────────────────╲──────┐ Rear Mach Line (μ_2 = arcsin(1/M_2))
                                   ╲     │╲
                                    ╲    │ ╲  V_2 (Mach M_2 > M_1)
                                     ╲   │  ╲ (Deflected by angle θ)
       ───────────────────────────────┴──┴───► (p_2 < p_1, T_2 < T_1)
                                          θ
```

#### 🔑 The 4 Fundamental Invariants of Prandtl-Meyer Expansion:
1. **Flow is STRICTLY ISENTROPIC:** $\boxed{\Delta s = 0 \iff s_2 = s_1}$.
2. **Stagnation Pressure is CONSTANT:** $\boxed{p_{02} = p_{01}}$.
3. **Stagnation Temperature is CONSTANT:** $\boxed{T_{02} = T_{01}}$.
4. **Static Flow Properties:**
   - Mach number **INCREASES** ($M_2 > M_1$).
   - Static Pressure **DROPS** ($p_2 < p_1$).
   - Static Temperature & Density **DROP** ($T_2 < T_1, \rho_2 < \rho_1$).

---

### Step 3 — The Prandtl-Meyer Function $\nu(M)$ (15 min)

The turning angle $\theta$ required to accelerate from Mach $1.0$ to Mach $M$ is given by the **Prandtl-Meyer Function $\nu(M)$**:

$$\boxed{\nu(M) = \sqrt{\frac{\gamma + 1}{\gamma - 1}}\arctan\sqrt{\frac{\gamma - 1}{\gamma + 1}(M^2 - 1)} - \arctan\sqrt{M^2 - 1}}$$

- For air ($\gamma = 1.4$):
  $$\boxed{\nu(M) = \sqrt{6}\arctan\sqrt{\frac{M^2 - 1}{6}} - \arctan\sqrt{M^2 - 1}}$$
- **Turning Formula:**
  $$\boxed{\nu(M_2) = \nu(M_1) + \theta}$$
- **Maximum Turning Angle ($M \to \infty$):**
  $$\nu_{\max} = \left(\sqrt{\frac{\gamma + 1}{\gamma - 1}} - 1\right)\frac{\pi}{2} = (\sqrt{6} - 1)\frac{\pi}{2}\text{ rad} \approx \mathbf{130.45^\circ}$$

### Step 4 — Supersonic Airfoil Theory (Ackeret Linearized Theory) (15 min)

For a thin 2D airfoil at angle of attack $\alpha$ in supersonic flow ($M_\infty > 1$):

```
              SUPERSONIC FLAT PLATE SHOCK-EXPANSION FIELD
                     Expansion Fan (Top LE)       Trailing Shock (Top TE)
                           ╲  ╲  ╲                     ╱
                     ───────╲──╲──╲───────────────────╱───── Upper Surface (Suction)
                                    \   α
                     ───────╱──╱──╱───────────────────╲───── Lower Surface (Pressure)
                           ╱  ╱  ╱                     ╲
                       Oblique Shock (Bottom LE)   Trailing Expansion (Bottom TE)
```

1. **Ackeret's Linearized Pressure Coefficient:**
   $$\boxed{C_p = \pm \frac{2\theta}{\sqrt{M_\infty^2 - 1}}}$$
   ($+$ for compression surfaces, $-$ for expansion surfaces).
2. **Supersonic Flat Plate Lift & Wave Drag:**
   $$\boxed{C_L = \frac{4\alpha}{\sqrt{M_\infty^2 - 1}}}$$
   $$\boxed{C_{D,\text{wave}} = C_L \alpha = \frac{4\alpha^2}{\sqrt{M_\infty^2 - 1}}}$$
3. **Aerodynamic Center Location:**
   $$\boxed{x_{ac} = 0.50c = \text{Mid-Chord (50% Chord)}} \quad (\text{Shifted back from 25% in subsonic!})$$
4. **Symmetric Diamond-Wedge Airfoil ($t/c$ thickness ratio):**
   $$\boxed{C_{D,\text{wave}} = \frac{4\alpha^2}{\sqrt{M_\infty^2 - 1}} + \frac{4(t/c)^2}{\sqrt{M_\infty^2 - 1}}}$$

---

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Supersonic vs. Subsonic Aerodynamic Center**
> - Subsonic thin airfoil: $x_{ac} = \mathbf{0.25c}$ (Quarter-chord).
> - Supersonic thin airfoil: $x_{ac} = \mathbf{0.50c}$ (Mid-chord). *This aft shift creates significant nose-down stability trim changes in supersonic transition!*
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Isentropic Total Pressure Rule**
> Remember that $p_{02} = p_{01}$ across a Prandtl-Meyer expansion fan, whereas $p_{02} < p_{01}$ across an oblique or normal shock wave.

### Step 3 — D4 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** Across a centered Prandtl-Meyer expansion fan, the stagnation pressure $p_0$:

> **Q2 (1 mark):** In supersonic linearized thin airfoil theory, the aerodynamic center of an airfoil is located at:
> **(A)** $0.25c$ (Quarter-chord) **(B)** $0.50c$ (Mid-chord) **(C)** $0.75c$ (Three-quarter chord) **(D)** Leading edge

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A flat plate is placed at an angle of attack $\alpha = 4^\circ$ in a supersonic air stream at $M_\infty = \sqrt{5} \approx 2.236$. The section lift coefficient $C_L$ is:
> **(A)** $0.070$ **(B)** $0.139$ **(C)** $0.279$ **(D)** $0.035$

> **Q4 (2 marks):** For the same flat plate ($M_\infty = \sqrt{5}, \alpha = 4^\circ$), the supersonic wave drag coefficient $C_{D,\text{wave}}$ is:
> **(A)** $0.0049$ **(B)** $0.0097$ **(C)** $0.0194$ **(D)** $0.0024$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A supersonic flow at $M_1 = 2.0$ has a Prandtl-Meyer function value $\nu(M_1) = 26.38^\circ$. If the flow expands around a convex corner of angle $\theta = 12.82^\circ$, the value of $\nu(M_2)$ downstream (in degrees) is ___$^\circ$.

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D4 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (C) Remains strictly constant**
> Because a Prandtl-Meyer expansion fan consists of an infinite number of infinitesimal isentropic Mach waves, entropy is strictly conserved ($\Delta s = 0 \implies p_{02} = p_{01}$).
>
> **Q2: (B) 0.50c (Mid-chord)**
> In supersonic linearized flow, the pressure distribution is uniform over the upper and lower surfaces, placing the center of pressure and aerodynamic center at the midpoint of the chord ($x_{ac} = 0.50c$).
>
> **Q3: (B) 0.139**
> $\alpha = 4 \times \frac{\pi}{180} = \frac{\pi}{45}\text{ rad} \approx 0.06981\text{ rad}$.
> $C_L = \frac{4\alpha}{\sqrt{M_\infty^2 - 1}} = \frac{4(0.06981)}{\sqrt{5 - 1}} = \frac{0.27925}{\sqrt{4}} = \frac{0.27925}{2} \approx \mathbf{0.1396 \approx 0.140}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) 0.0097**
> $C_{D,\text{wave}} = C_L \alpha = (0.1396)(0.06981) \approx \mathbf{0.00975}$.
>
> **Q5: 39.2**
> $\nu(M_2) = \nu(M_1) + \theta = 26.38^\circ + 12.82^\circ = \mathbf{39.20^\circ}$.
