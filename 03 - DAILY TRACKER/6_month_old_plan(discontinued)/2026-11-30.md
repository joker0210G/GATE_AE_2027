### The 4-Week Month 5 Campaign Map:
1. **Week 1 (Dec 1–7): Stress-Strain, Elasticity & Failure Theories:**
   - 3D State of Stress, Equilibrium equations ($\sigma_{ij,j} + X_i = 0$), Generalized Hooke's Law in 3D.
   - Plane Stress ($\sigma_z = \tau_{xz} = \tau_{yz} = 0$) and Plane Strain ($\epsilon_z = \gamma_{xz} = \gamma_{yz} = 0$).
   - Mohr's Circle in 2D and 3D, Principal stresses and maximum shear stress $\tau_{\max} = \frac{\sigma_1 - \sigma_3}{2}$.
   - Yield Criteria: Tresca (Maximum Shear Stress), von Mises (Distortion Energy Theory).
2. **Week 2 (Dec 8–14): Bending of Beams, Deflections & Unsymmetrical Bending:**
   - Pure bending theory, Euler-Bernoulli beam equation ($EI \frac{d^4 w}{dx^4} = q(x)$).
   - Unsymmetrical Bending of Beams with non-zero product of inertia $I_{xy}$:
     $$\sigma_z = \frac{M_x I_y - M_y I_{xy}}{I_x I_y - I_{xy}^2} y + \frac{M_y I_x - M_x I_{xy}}{I_x I_y - I_{xy}^2} x$$
   - Principle axes of inertia and Neutral Axis orientation $\tan\theta_{\text{NA}}$.
3. **Week 3 (Dec 15–21): Shear Flow, Shear Center & Thin-Walled Beams:**
   - Shear flow in open thin-walled sections ($q(s) = q_0 - \frac{V_y}{I_x} \int y \, t \, ds$).
   - Shear Center ($e_x, e_y$) determination for open sections (Channels, Angles, Semicircles).
   - Closed single-cell and multi-cell thin-walled sections under torsion (Bredt-Batho theory: $q = T / (2 A_m), \theta = \frac{T L}{4 A_m^2 G} \oint \frac{ds}{t}$).
   - Column Buckling: Euler critical load ($P_{\text{cr}} = \frac{\pi^2 E I}{L_e^2}$), Slenderness ratio, Johnson's parabolic formula for short columns.
4. **Week 4 (Dec 22–28): Energy Methods & Grand Structures Subject Test:**
   - Strain Energy formulations for Axial, Bending, Shear, and Torsion.
   - Castigliano's First Theorem ($\frac{\partial U}{\partial \Delta_i} = P_i$) and Second Theorem ($\frac{\partial U^*}{\partial P_i} = \Delta_i$).
   - Principle of Virtual Work and Unit Load Method for statically indeterminate structures.
