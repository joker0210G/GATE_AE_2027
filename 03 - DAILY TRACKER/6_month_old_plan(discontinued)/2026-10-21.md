### Step 2 — Lateral Static Stability Criterion ($C_{l\beta} < 0$) (15 min)
Open `04.5b - Lateral Static Stability`.

1. **Rolling Moment Coefficient ($C_l$):**
   $$C_l \equiv \frac{L_{\text{roll}}}{q_\infty S b}$$
   - Positive rolling moment ($L_{\text{roll}} > 0$): Rolls aircraft to the **RIGHT** (Right wing down).
2. **Lateral Static Stability Criterion (Dihedral Effect):**
   $$\boxed{C_{l\beta} \equiv \frac{\partial C_l}{\partial \beta} < 0}$$
   - **Physics:** When a disturbance causes a sideslip to the right ($\beta > 0$), a laterally stable aircraft must generate a **negative (left wing down) rolling moment** $C_l < 0$ to level the wings and restore symmetry.

### Step 3 — The 4 Core Contributors to Dihedral Effect ($C_{l\beta} < 0$) (25 min)
Open `04.5b2 - Wing Dihedral` and `04.5b3 - Wing Sweep and Position`.

```
                    THE 4 CONTRIBUTORS TO DIHEDRAL EFFECT
       1. Geometric Dihedral (Γ)            2. Wing Sweepback (Λ)
          Advancing wing: +Δα = β Γ            Advancing wing: V_eff = V cos(Λ − β)
          Retreating wing: −Δα = −β Γ          Retreating wing: V_eff = V cos(Λ + β)
          ▲ C_lβ,dihedral ≈ −(a/6) Γ           ▲ C_lβ,sweep ∝ −C_L sin(2Λ)

       3. Wing Vertical Position            4. Vertical Tail Offset (z_v)
          High Wing: Favors ΔC_lβ < 0          Side force Y_v above CG
          (Equivalent to +2° to +3° Dihedral)  creates restoring left roll:
          Low Wing: Destabilizing!             ▲ C_lβ,v = −V_v (z_v / l_v) η_v a_v
```

#### 🔑 Mathematical & Design Summary:

| Contributing Factor | Mechanism & Key Formula | Typical Effect on $C_{l\beta}$ | Notes & Trade-Offs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Geometric Dihedral ($\Gamma$)** | $\boxed{C_{l\beta,\text{dihedral}} \approx -\frac{a}{6}\Gamma}$ | **Negative ($\Delta C_{l\beta} < 0$)** | $+1^\circ$ dihedral gives $\Delta C_{l\beta} \approx -0.0002\text{ deg}^{-1}$ |
| **Wing Sweepback ($\Lambda$)** | $\boxed{C_{l\beta,\text{sweep}} \approx -\frac{1}{2} C_L \sin(2\Lambda)}$ | **Negative ($\Delta C_{l\beta} < 0$)** | **Proportional to $C_L$!** (High-AoA creates huge dihedral effect). Swept wings often need **Anhedral ($\Gamma < 0$)**! |
| **High Wing Position** | Fuselage cross-flow pushes air upward under advancing wing | **Negative ($\Delta C_{l\beta} < 0$)** | Equivalent to $+2^\circ \text{ to } +3^\circ$ dihedral |
| **Low Wing Position** | Cross-flow spills over top of advancing wing | **Positive / Destabilizing ($\Delta C_{l\beta} > 0$)** | Requires $+3^\circ \text{ to } +6^\circ$ geometric dihedral |
| **Vertical Tail Offset ($z_v$)** | Side force at height $z_v > 0$ produces left roll | **Negative ($\Delta C_{l\beta} < 0$)** | Proportional to tail height $z_v$ |

### Step 4 — Aileron Control Power, Adverse Yaw & Aileron Reversal (15 min)
Open `02.4a - Roll Dynamics and Control`.

1. **Aileron Control Power Derivative ($C_{l\delta_a}$):**
   - Aileron deflection $\delta_a \equiv \frac{\delta_L - \delta_R}{2}$: Right aileron down, left aileron up produces roll to the right.
   - $\boxed{C_{l\delta_a} < 0 \text{ or } > 0}$ (Sign depends on deflection convention).
2. **Adverse Yaw:**
   - The downward-deflected aileron increases lift and camber, which **increases induced drag** on that wing.
   - The upward-deflected aileron reduces induced drag.
   - **Result:** Aircraft yaws to the *left* when rolling to the *right*!
   - **Solutions:** Frise ailerons (protruding lower lip adds profile drag to up-aileron), Differential aileron gearing (up-aileron deflects more than down-aileron).
3. **Aileron Reversal:**
   - At high speeds, the aerodynamic nose-down twisting moment produced by a down-deflected aileron twists the flexible wing nose-down, reducing total wing lift and reversing control action.

---

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Swept Wing Dihedral Dependence on $C_L$**
> $C_{l\beta,\text{sweep}} \propto -C_L \sin(2\Lambda)$. At high lift ($C_L \uparrow$, landing/climb), swept-wing aircraft develop massive dihedral effect, which can trigger severe Dutch roll instability unless anhedral is applied!
> 🔴 **Trap 2: High Wing vs. Low Wing Dihedral Requirements**
> High-wing aircraft naturally have stabilizing cross-flow (need little to no dihedral, or even anhedral). Low-wing aircraft MUST have positive geometric dihedral!

### Step 3 — D2 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** An aircraft possesses lateral static stability if its rolling moment derivative satisfies:
> **(A)** $C_{l\beta} > 0$ **(B)** $C_{l\beta} < 0$ **(C)** $C_{l\beta} = 0$ **(D)** $C_{l\alpha} < 0$

> **Q2 (1 mark):** A high-wing aircraft configuration compared to a low-wing configuration provides:
> **(A)** A destabilizing lateral effect ($\Delta C_{l\beta} > 0$)
> **(B)** A stabilizing lateral dihedral effect ($\Delta C_{l\beta} < 0$)
> **(C)** Zero change in lateral stability
> **(D)** An increase in zero-lift drag

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A straight wing aircraft with lift curve slope $a = 5.0\text{ rad}^{-1}$ has a geometric dihedral angle $\Gamma = 0.06\text{ rad}$. The contribution of dihedral to the lateral stability derivative $C_{l\beta}$ is:
> **(A)** $-0.025\text{ rad}^{-1}$ **(B)** $-0.050\text{ rad}^{-1}$ **(C)** $-0.075\text{ rad}^{-1}$ **(D)** $-0.100\text{ rad}^{-1}$

> **Q4 (1 mark):** Swept-back wings contribute to dihedral effect ($C_{l\beta} < 0$) with a magnitude that is directly proportional to:
> **(A)** Mach number only **(B)** Aircraft lift coefficient $C_L$ **(C)** Reynolds number **(D)** Wing thickness ratio

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** An aircraft has a wing swept back by $\Lambda = 30^\circ$. At a cruise condition where $C_L = 0.60$, the sweep contribution to lateral stability $C_{l\beta,\text{sweep}}$ (in $\text{rad}^{-1}$) is ___ (round off to 3 decimal places).

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D2 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) C_lβ < 0**
> Positive sideslip $\beta > 0$ must create negative roll $C_l < 0$ to restore wings level.
>
> **Q2: (B) A stabilizing lateral dihedral effect (ΔC_lβ < 0)**
> Fuselage cross-flow increases effective AoA under the windward wing on a high-wing aircraft.
>
> **Q3: (B) -0.050 rad⁻¹**
> $C_{l\beta,\text{dihedral}} \approx -\frac{a}{6}\Gamma = -\frac{5.0}{6}(0.06) = -5.0(0.01) = \mathbf{-0.050\text{ rad}^{-1}}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) Aircraft lift coefficient C_L**
> $C_{l\beta,\text{sweep}} \approx -\frac{1}{2} C_L \sin(2\Lambda) \propto C_L$.
>
> **Q5: -0.260**
> $C_{l\beta,\text{sweep}} \approx -\frac{1}{2} C_L \sin(2\Lambda) = -\frac{1}{2}(0.60)\sin(60^\circ) = -0.30(0.866025) \approx \mathbf{-0.260\text{ rad}^{-1}}$.
