### Step 2 — The Longitudinal 4th-Order Characteristic Equation (15 min)
Open `07.2 - Decoupling of Longitudinal Dynamics`.

The characteristic equation of the linearized longitudinal 4-state system factors into two distinct second-order complex conjugate pairs:

$$\Delta_{\text{long}}(s) = s^4 + A s^3 + B s^2 + C s + D = 0$$
$$\boxed{(s^2 + 2\zeta_{\text{sp}}\omega_{n,\text{sp}}s + \omega_{n,\text{sp}}^2)(s^2 + 2\zeta_{\text{ph}}\omega_{n,\text{ph}}s + \omega_{n,\text{ph}}^2) = 0}$$

```
                 COMPLEX S-PLANE: LONGITUDINAL ROOTS
                        Im ^
                           │   x (Short Period Mode — High Frequency)
                           │
                           │ x (Phugoid Mode — Low Frequency, Near Imag Axis)
                     ──────┼────────────────────────► Re
                           │ x (Phugoid Mode)
                           │
                           │   x (Short Period Mode)
```

### Step 3 — Short Period vs. Phugoid Mode Master Comparison (25 min)

> 🔴 **THE DEFINITIVE GATE FLIGHT DYNAMICS TABLE. MEMORIZE COLD!**

| Mode Parameter | Short Period Mode ($\text{SP}$) | Phugoid Mode ($\text{PH}$) |
|---|---|---|
| **Physical Frequency ($\omega_n$)** | **High** ($\omega_{n,\text{sp}} \approx 1.0\text{ to } 10.0\text{ rad/s}$) | **Very Low** ($\omega_{n,\text{ph}} \approx 0.05\text{ to } 0.2\text{ rad/s}$) |
| **Damping Ratio ($\zeta$)** | **Moderate to Heavy** ($\zeta_{\text{sp}} \approx 0.3\text{ to } 0.8$) | **Very Light** ($\zeta_{\text{ph}} \approx 0.04\text{ to } 0.10$) |
| **Time Period ($T = 2\pi/\omega_d$)** | **Short** ($T \approx 1\text{ to } 5\text{ seconds}$) | **Long** ($T \approx 30\text{ to } 120\text{ seconds}$) |
| **Motion Characteristics** | Rapid pitching & angle of attack oscillations ($\theta, \alpha$) at **CONSTANT AIRSPEED ($u \approx 0$)** | Slow energy interchange between kinetic ($u$) and potential ($h$) at **CONSTANT ANGLE OF ATTACK ($\alpha \approx 0$)** |
| **Governing Physical Derivatives** | $M_\alpha$ (Pitch stiffness), $M_q$ (Pitch damping), $Z_\alpha$ | Gravity $g$, Airspeed $V_\infty$, Drag polar $(L/D)$ |
| **Approximation Formula** | $\boxed{\omega_{n,\text{sp}} \approx \sqrt{-M_\alpha}}$<br>$\boxed{\zeta_{\text{sp}} \approx -\frac{M_q + M_{\dot{\alpha}} + Z_\alpha/U_0}{2\omega_{n,\text{sp}}}}$ | $\boxed{\omega_{n,\text{ph}} = \frac{\sqrt{2} g}{V_\infty}}$<br>$\boxed{\zeta_{\text{ph}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}(L/D)} \approx \frac{0.707}{L/D}}$ |
| **Pilot Controllability** | Highly dangerous if poorly damped (Pilot Induced Oscillations - PIO) | Easily controlled by pilot due to long period |

---

### Step 4 — Lanchester Phugoid Approximations Deep Dive (10 min)

1. **Phugoid Natural Frequency:**
   $$\boxed{\omega_{n,\text{ph}} = \sqrt{2}\frac{g}{V_\infty}} \implies \boxed{T_{\text{ph}} = \frac{2\pi}{\omega_{n,\text{ph}}} = \frac{2\pi V_\infty}{\sqrt{2}g} \approx 0.45 V_\infty\text{ seconds}}$$
   *(Flying faster increases the Phugoid period!).*
2. **Phugoid Damping Ratio:**
   $$\boxed{\zeta_{\text{ph}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\frac{1}{L/D} \approx \frac{0.707}{L/D}}$$
   *(High-efficiency gliders and transports with high $(L/D)$ have **LOWER PHUGOID DAMPING** and oscillate for much longer!).*

---

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Constant Variables in Modes**
> - Short Period: **Airspeed is CONSTANT** ($u \approx 0$).
> - Phugoid: **Angle of Attack is CONSTANT** ($\alpha \approx 0$).
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Phugoid Damping vs $L/D$**
> $\zeta_{\text{ph}} \propto \frac{1}{L/D}$. Increasing aerodynamic efficiency $(L/D)$ *decreases* Phugoid damping!

### Step 3 — D4 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** In the short period mode of longitudinal dynamic stability, which of the following variables remains nearly constant?
> **(A)** Pitch attitude $\theta$ **(B)** Angle of attack $\alpha$ **(C)** Forward airspeed $u$ **(D)** Pitch rate $q$

> **Q2 (1 mark):** In the phugoid mode of longitudinal dynamic stability, which of the following variables remains nearly constant?
> **(A)** Forward airspeed $u$ **(B)** Altitude $h$ **(C)** Angle of attack $\alpha$ **(D)** Kinetic energy

> **Q3 (2 marks):** An aircraft cruises at True Airspeed $V_\infty = 245.25\text{ m/s}$ in level flight ($g = 9.81\text{ m/s}^2$). Using Lanchester's approximation, the Phugoid natural frequency $\omega_{n,\text{ph}}$ (in $\text{rad/s}$) is:
> **(A)** $0.028\text{ rad/s}$ **(B)** $0.057\text{ rad/s}$ **(C)** $0.113\text{ rad/s}$ **(D)** $0.226\text{ rad/s}$

> **Q4 (2 marks):** A high-performance sailplane has an aerodynamic lift-to-drag ratio $(L/D) = 35.35$. Using Lanchester's formula, the damping ratio of the Phugoid mode $\zeta_{\text{ph}}$ is:
> **(A)** $0.020$ **(B)** $0.050$ **(C)** $0.100$ **(D)** $0.200$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** An aircraft has a dimensional pitching moment derivative $M_\alpha = -25.0\text{ s}^{-2}$. Neglecting heave damping coupling, the short period undamped natural frequency $\omega_{n,\text{sp}}$ (in $\text{rad/s}$) is ___.

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D4 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (C) Forward airspeed u**
> The short period mode is a rapid pitching oscillation occurring too quickly for airspeed $u$ to change.
>
> **Q2: (C) Angle of attack α**
> The phugoid mode is a slow trajectory oscillation where pitch attitude tracks flight path angle $\theta \approx \gamma \implies \alpha \approx 0$.
>
> **Q3: (B) 0.057 rad/s**
> $\omega_{n,\text{ph}} = \frac{\sqrt{2}g}{V_\infty} = \frac{\sqrt{2}(9.81)}{245.25} = \frac{13.873}{245.25} \approx \mathbf{0.05657\text{ rad/s} \approx 0.057\text{ rad/s}}$.
>
> **Q4: (A) 0.020**
> $\zeta_{\text{ph}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}(L/D)} = \frac{1}{1.4142 \times 35.35} = \frac{1}{50.0} = \mathbf{0.020}$.
>
> **Q5: 5.0**
> $\omega_{n,\text{sp}} \approx \sqrt{-M_\alpha} = \sqrt{-(-25.0)} = \sqrt{25.0} = \mathbf{5.0\text{ rad/s}}$.
