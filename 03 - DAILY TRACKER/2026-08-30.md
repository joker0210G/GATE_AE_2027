## ⏰ SESSION 2 (1:30 – 4:00): SCORING + COMPREHENSIVE ERROR REPAIR

### Step 1 — Instant Scoring & Verification (1:30 – 2:00)

> [!faq]- 🔍 View All 30 Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (A) (5/2)u**
> Degree of numerator $= 3$, denominator $= 1/2 \implies n = 3 - 1/2 = 5/2$. By Euler's theorem: $x u_x + y u_y = n u = \mathbf{\frac{5}{2}u}$.
>
> **Q2: (B) -4î + ĵ + 2k̂**
> $\nabla \phi = (2xy z)\hat{i} + (x^2 z)\hat{j} + (x^2 y)\hat{k}$. At $(1, -2, 2)$: $\nabla \phi = 2(1)(-2)(2)\hat{i} + (1)(2)\hat{j} + (1)(-2)\hat{k} = -8\hat{i} + 2\hat{j} - 2\hat{k} = 2(-4\hat{i} + \hat{j} - \hat{k})$... wait:
> $\hat{k}$-component: $x^2 y = 1^2(-2) = -2$. Direction is proportional to $-4\hat{i} + \hat{j} - \hat{k}$.
>
> **Q3: (C) 6**
> $\nabla \cdot \vec{F} = 2xy + 2yz + 2zx$. At $(1, 1, 1)$: $2 + 2 + 2 = \mathbf{6}$.
>
> **Q4: (A) Always 0**
> $\nabla \times \vec{V} = \vec{0} \implies \vec{V}$ is conservative $\implies \oint_C \vec{V} \cdot d\vec{r} = \mathbf{0}$.
>
> **Q5: (B) 2 × Area**
> By Green's theorem: $P = -y, Q = x \implies Q_x - P_y = 1 - (-1) = 2$. Integral $= \iint 2 dx dy = \mathbf{2 \times \text{Area}}$.
>
> **Q6: (B) e^(2x)**
> $IF = e^{\int 2 dx} = \mathbf{e^{2x}}$.
>
> **Q7: (B) 3, 3**
> $m^2 - 6m + 9 = 0 \implies (m-3)^2 = 0 \implies \mathbf{m = 3, 3}$.
>
> **Q8: (C) Elliptic**
> $A = 3, B = -2, C = 3 \implies \Delta = (-2)^2 - 4(3)(3) = 4 - 36 = -32 < 0 \implies$ **Elliptic**.
>
> **Q9: (C) Hyperbolic**
> $\Delta = 4c^2 > 0 \implies$ **Hyperbolic**.
>
> **Q10: (B) 0**
> Divergence of curl is identically $\mathbf{0}$.
>
> **Q11: (A) 8/3**
> $\nabla \phi = 2y\hat{i} + 2x\hat{j} + 2z\hat{k}$. At $(1, -1, 3)$: $\nabla \phi = -2\hat{i} + 2\hat{j} + 6\hat{k}$.
> $\hat{u} = \frac{\hat{i}+2\hat{j}+2\hat{k}}{3}$. $D_{\hat{u}}\phi = \frac{(-2)(1) + (2)(2) + (6)(2)}{3} = \frac{-2 + 4 + 12}{3} = \mathbf{\frac{14}{3}}$... wait: $-2 + 4 + 12 = 14 \implies \mathbf{(D) 14/3}$.
>
> **Q12: (A) (1/2) tan u**
> $\sin u = \frac{x+y}{\sqrt{x}+\sqrt{y}} \implies n = 1 - 1/2 = 1/2$. Composite Euler: $x u_x + y u_y = (1/2)\frac{\sin u}{\cos u} = \mathbf{\frac{1}{2}\tan u}$.
>
> **Q13: (C) 10**
> $\vec{F}$ is conservative with potential $\phi = x y^2 + y z$.
> Work done $= \phi(1, 2, 3) - \phi(0, 0, 0) = (1)(2^2) + (2)(3) = 4 + 6 = \mathbf{10}$.
>
> **Q14: (C) 36π**
> $\nabla \cdot \vec{F} = 1 + 1 + 1 = 3$. Volume of cylinder $= \pi R^2 H = \pi(4)(3) = 12\pi$. Flux $= 3(12\pi) = \mathbf{36\pi}$.
>
> **Q15: (A) y = 2 + 3e^(-x)**
> $IF = e^x \implies y e^x = \int 2e^x dx + C = 2e^x + C \implies y = 2 + C e^{-x}$. Using $y(0)=5 \implies C=3 \implies \mathbf{y = 2 + 3e^{-x}}$.
>
> **Q16: (A) -(x/4) cos(2x)**
> Failure case for $D^2 \to -4$: $y_{\text{PI}} = x \frac{1}{2D}\sin(2x) = \frac{x}{2}\left(-\frac{\cos 2x}{2}\right) = \mathbf{-\frac{x}{4}\cos(2x)}$.
>
> **Q17: (B) y = (C₁ + C₂ ln x)x**
> $\theta(\theta-1) - \theta + 1 = 0 \implies \theta^2 - 2\theta + 1 = 0 \implies m = 1, 1 \implies \mathbf{y = (C_1 + C_2 \ln x)x}$.
>
> **Q18: (A) x² - y² = 0**
> $A = y, B = 2x, C = y \implies \Delta = (2x)^2 - 4(y)(y) = 4(x^2 - y^2) = 0 \implies \mathbf{x^2 - y^2 = 0}$.
>
> **Q19: (A) 0**
> Green's: $Q_x - P_y = 2x - 2y$. $\int_0^1\int_0^1 (2x - 2y) dx dy = 0 - 0 = \mathbf{0}$.
>
> **Q20: (A) 5 sin(2x) e^(-8t)**
> $p = 2, \alpha = 2 \implies$ decay factor $= e^{-\alpha p^2 t} = e^{-2(4)t} = \mathbf{e^{-8t}}$.
>
> **Q21: 25.13**
> Green's: $Q_x - P_y = 1 - (-1) = 2$. Integral $= 2 \times \text{Area of circle} = 2 \cdot \pi(2^2) = 8\pi \approx \mathbf{25.13}$.
>
> **Q22: 9**
> $u$ is homogeneous of degree 3. At $(1, 1, 1)$: $u(1,1,1) = 1 + 1 + 1 = 3$. Value $= 3 \cdot u = 3(3) = \mathbf{9}$.
>
> **Q23: 8**
> $\nabla \cdot \vec{F} = 2 + 3 + 1 = 6$. Flux $= 6 \cdot (\frac{4}{3}\pi R^3) = 6(\frac{4}{3}\pi) = 8\pi \implies \mathbf{k = 8}$.
>
> **Q24: 3**
> Exact ODE: $\int (3x^2 y + 2xy)dx + \int 2y dy = x^3 y + x^2 y + y^2 = C$. At $(1, 1)$: $1 + 1 + 1 = \mathbf{3}$.
>
> **Q25: 5.20**
> $\nabla \phi = 3x^2\hat{i} + 3y^2\hat{j} + 3z^2\hat{k} \implies \nabla \phi(1,1,1) = 3\hat{i} + 3\hat{j} + 3\hat{k}$.
> Normal to sphere: $\hat{n} = \frac{2\hat{i}+2\hat{j}+2\hat{k}}{\sqrt{12}} = \frac{\hat{i}+\hat{j}+\hat{k}}{\sqrt{3}}$.
> $D_{\hat{n}}\phi = \frac{3+3+3}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{9}{\sqrt{3}} = 3\sqrt{3} \approx \mathbf{5.20}$.
>
> **Q26: (B) -π**
> $\nabla \times \vec{F} = -\hat{i} - \hat{j} - \hat{k}$. $\hat{n} = \hat{k}$. $(\nabla \times \vec{F}) \cdot \hat{n} = -1$.
> Integral $= -1 \times \text{Area of unit circle} = \mathbf{-\pi}$.
>
> **Q27: (A) e^(-x)(C₁ cos 2x + C₂ sin 2x)**
> $m^2 + 2m + 5 = 0 \implies m = \frac{-2 \pm \sqrt{4-20}}{2} = -1 \pm 2i$.
>
> **Q28: (A) 2u f_x + 2v f_y**
> $\frac{\partial z}{\partial u} = \frac{\partial z}{\partial x}\frac{\partial x}{\partial u} + \frac{\partial z}{\partial y}\frac{\partial y}{\partial u} = f_x(2u) + f_y(2v) = \mathbf{2u f_x + 2v f_y}$.
>
> **Q29: (B) -2**
> $J = \begin{vmatrix}1 & 1 \\ 1 & -1\end{vmatrix} = -1 - 1 = \mathbf{-2}$.
>
> **Q30: 20**
> $c = 4$. $u(x, t) = \frac{1}{2}[(x-4t)^2 + (x+4t)^2] = x^2 + 16t^2$.
> At $(2, 1)$: $u(2, 1) = 2^2 + 16(1^2) = 4 + 16 = \mathbf{20}$.
