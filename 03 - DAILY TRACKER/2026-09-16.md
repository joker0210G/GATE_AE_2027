### Step 2 — Non-Lifting Circular Cylinder (Uniform Flow + Doublet) (20 min)

Superimposing a uniform flow $V_\infty$ and a doublet of strength $\kappa = 2\pi R^2 V_\infty$ at the origin:

$$\boxed{\psi = V_\infty r\sin\theta\left(1 - \frac{R^2}{r^2}\right)} \qquad \boxed{\phi = V_\infty r\cos\theta\left(1 + \frac{R^2}{r^2}\right)}$$

- The circle $r = R$ has $\psi = 0$ (acts as a solid circular cylinder surface).

#### 1. Flow Velocity Field on Cylinder Surface ($r = R$):
$$v_r = \left.\frac{1}{r}\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial\theta}\right|_{r=R} = 0 \quad (\text{No-penetration boundary condition})$$
$$\boxed{v_\theta = \left.-\frac{\partial\psi}{\partial r}\right|_{r=R} = -2V_\infty\sin\theta}$$

#### 2. Surface Pressure Coefficient ($C_p$):
$$\boxed{C_p(\theta) = 1 - \left(\frac{v_\theta}{V_\infty}\right)^2 = 1 - 4\sin^2\theta}$$

```
                NON-LIFTING CYLINDER C_p DISTRIBUTION
                    Top: θ = 90° (V = 2 V_inf, C_p = -3.0)
                             ▲ Suction Peak
                             │
       Stagnation θ = 180°   ┌───┐   Stagnation θ = 0°
       (V = 0, C_p = +1.0) ──│ O │── (V = 0, C_p = +1.0)
                             └───┘
                             │
                             ▼ Suction Peak
                   Bottom: θ = 270° (V = 2 V_inf, C_p = -3.0)
```

- **Symmetry & D'Alembert's Paradox:**
  - Forward-backward symmetry $\implies$ **Zero Net Drag ($D' = 0$)**!
  - Top-bottom symmetry $\implies$ **Zero Net Lift ($L' = 0$)**!
  - *D'Alembert's Paradox:* Inviscid, unseparated potential flow predicts zero drag on any closed 2D/3D body.

### Step 3 — Lifting Circular Cylinder (Uniform Flow + Doublet + Vortex) (20 min)

Superimposing a clockwise vortex of strength $\Gamma$ to the cylinder flow:

$$\psi = V_\infty r\sin\theta\left(1 - \frac{R^2}{r^2}\right) + \frac{\Gamma}{2\pi}\ln\left(\frac{r}{R}\right)$$

#### 1. Surface Tangential Velocity ($r = R$):
$$\boxed{v_\theta = -2V_\infty\sin\theta - \frac{\Gamma}{2\pi R}}$$

#### 2. Stagnation Points Location on Surface ($v_\theta = 0$):
$$-2V_\infty\sin\theta_s - \frac{\Gamma}{2\pi R} = 0 \implies \boxed{\sin\theta_s = -\frac{\Gamma}{4\pi V_\infty R}}$$

#### 🔑 The 4 Stagnation Point Regimes (HIGH YIELD IN GATE!):

| Vortex Circulation ($\Gamma$) | Stagnation Point Behavior | Physical Geometry |
|---|---|---|
| **$\Gamma = 0$** | $\sin\theta_s = 0 \implies \theta_s = 0^\circ, 180^\circ$ | 2 points on horizontal equator (Non-lifting) |
| **$\Gamma < 4\pi V_\infty R$** | $\sin\theta_s = -\frac{\Gamma}{4\pi V_\infty R} \in (-1, 0)$ | **2 distinct points in lower half** ($\theta_s$ in 3rd & 4th quadrants) |
| **$\Gamma = 4\pi V_\infty R$** | $\sin\theta_s = -1 \implies \mathbf{\theta_s = 270^\circ}$ | **1 single point merged at the very bottom** |
| **$\Gamma > 4\pi V_\infty R$** | $|\sin\theta_s| > 1$ (No surface points) | Stagnation point leaves cylinder, floats in fluid below at $r_s = \frac{\Gamma}{4\pi V_\infty} + \sqrt{\left(\frac{\Gamma}{4\pi V_\infty}\right)^2 - R^2}$ |

---

### Step 4 — The Kutta-Joukowski Theorem (10 min)

Integrating the surface pressure $p(\theta) = p_\infty + \frac{1}{2}\rho_\infty(V_\infty^2 - v_\theta^2)$ around the lifting cylinder perimeter yields the **Kutta-Joukowski Theorem**:

$$\boxed{L' = \rho_\infty V_\infty \Gamma} \qquad \text{and} \qquad \boxed{D' = 0}$$

- **$L'$:** Aerodynamic lift force per unit span ($\text{N/m}$).
- **Direction:** Perpendicular to the free-stream velocity vector $\vec{V}_\infty$ (pointing up for clockwise circulation $\Gamma$).
- **Magnus Effect:** The physical generation of lift on a spinning cylinder or sphere due to the induced circulation $\Gamma$.

---

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Stagnation Point Angle Coordinates**
> Remember that for positive lift (clockwise $\Gamma$), the stagnation points shift into the **lower half** ($\theta \in [180^\circ, 360^\circ]$), so $\sin\theta_s$ is NEGATIVE.
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Maximum Surface Velocity on Non-Lifting Cylinder**
> Surface velocity is $2V_\infty$ (NOT $V_\infty$). The maximum suction pressure coefficient is $C_p = 1 - 2^2 = \mathbf{-3.0}$ (NOT $-1.0$).

### Step 3 — D2 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** On the surface of a non-lifting circular cylinder of radius $R$ in uniform flow $V_\infty$, the maximum velocity occurs at angles $\theta$ equal to:
> **(A)** $0^\circ$ and $180^\circ$ **(B)** $90^\circ$ and $270^\circ$ **(C)** $45^\circ$ and $135^\circ$ **(D)** $60^\circ$ and $120^\circ$

> **Q2 (2 marks):** A circular cylinder of radius $R = 1\text{ m}$ is placed in a uniform air stream of $V_\infty = 25\text{ m/s}$. The minimum circulation $\Gamma$ required to produce a single stagnation point on the cylinder surface is:
> **(A)** $25\pi\text{ m}^2\text{/s}$ **(B)** $50\pi\text{ m}^2\text{/s}$ **(C)** $100\pi\text{ m}^2\text{/s}$ **(D)** $200\pi\text{ m}^2\text{/s}$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** An airplane wing has a span of $b = 10\text{ m}$ and generates a total lift of $L = 60\text{ kN}$ at $V_\infty = 100\text{ m/s}$ in sea-level air ($\rho = 1.2\text{ kg/m}^3$). Assuming 2D average circulation, the circulation $\Gamma$ is:
> **(A)** $50\text{ m}^2\text{/s}$ **(B)** $100\text{ m}^2\text{/s}$ **(C)** $500\text{ m}^2\text{/s}$ **(D)** $25\text{ m}^2\text{/s}$

> **Q4 (1 mark):** The pressure coefficient $C_p$ at $\theta = 30^\circ$ on the surface of a non-lifting circular cylinder is:
> **(A)** $+1.0$ **(B)** $0.0$ **(C)** $-1.0$ **(D)** $-3.0$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A rotating cylinder of radius $R = 0.5\text{ m}$ in a uniform flow $V_\infty = 10\text{ m/s}$ has circulation $\Gamma = 10\pi\text{ m}^2\text{/s}$. The acute angle $\alpha$ (in degrees) that the stagnation points make with the negative $x$-axis (below the horizontal) is ___$^\circ$.

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D2 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) 90° and 270°**
> $v_\theta = -2V_\infty\sin\theta$. Maximum speed $|v_\theta| = 2V_\infty$ occurs where $|\sin\theta| = 1 \implies \theta = 90^\circ, 270^\circ$.
>
> **Q2: (C) 100π m²/s**
> For a single stagnation point: $\Gamma = 4\pi V_\infty R = 4\pi(25)(1) = \mathbf{100\pi\text{ m}^2\text{/s}}$.
>
> **Q3: (A) 50 m²/s**
> Lift per unit span: $L' = \frac{L}{b} = \frac{60000}{10} = 6000\text{ N/m}$.
> By Kutta-Joukowski theorem: $L' = \rho_\infty V_\infty \Gamma \implies \Gamma = \frac{L'}{\rho_\infty V_\infty} = \frac{6000}{(1.2)(100)} = \frac{6000}{120} = \mathbf{50\text{ m}^2\text{/s}}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) 0.0**
> $C_p = 1 - 4\sin^2\theta = 1 - 4\sin^2(30^\circ) = 1 - 4(0.5)^2 = 1 - 4(0.25) = 1 - 1 = \mathbf{0.0}$.
>
> **Q5: 30**
> $\sin\theta_s = -\frac{\Gamma}{4\pi V_\infty R} = -\frac{10\pi}{4\pi(10)(0.5)} = -\frac{10}{20} = -0.5$.
> $\theta_s = 210^\circ$ and $330^\circ$.
> $210^\circ = 180^\circ + 30^\circ \implies$ Angle below the negative $x$-axis is $\mathbf{30^\circ}$.
