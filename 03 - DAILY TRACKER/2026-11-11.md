### Step 1 — D1 Carry-Forward Review (5 min)
Quickly recall: Turbojet net thrust $F_n = \dot{m}[(1+f)V_9 - V_0]$, TSFC $\text{TSFC} = \dot{m}_f / F_n$, and propulsive efficiency $\eta_p = \frac{2 V_0}{V_9 + V_0}$.

### Step 2 — Turbofan Engine Architecture & Bypass Ratio (20 min)
Open `02 - Aircraft Engine Types and Performance`.

```
                    TURBOFAN ENGINE ARCHITECTURE (UNMIXED EXHAUST)
                          Fan Duct (Cold Stream ṁ_fan)
                   ┌───────────────────────────────────────► V_9,fan
                   │  ┌─────────────────────────────────┐
     Air Intake ───┼──► Core: Comp ──► Burner ──► Turb ─┼──► V_9,core (Hot Stream ṁ_core)
                   │  └─────────────────────────────────┘
                   └───────────────────────────────────────► V_9,fan
```

1. **Bypass Ratio ($BPR \equiv \alpha$):**
   $$\boxed{\alpha \equiv \frac{\dot{m}_{\text{fan}}}{\dot{m}_{\text{core}}} = \frac{\dot{m}_{\text{bypass}}}{\dot{m}_{\text{core}}}}$$
   - Total Engine Airflow: $\mathbf{\dot{m}_{\text{total}} = \dot{m}_{\text{core}} + \dot{m}_{\text{fan}} = \dot{m}_{\text{core}}(1 + \alpha)}$.
   - Core Flow Fraction: $\frac{\dot{m}_{\text{core}}}{\dot{m}_{\text{total}}} = \frac{1}{1 + \alpha}$.
   - Fan Flow Fraction: $\frac{\dot{m}_{\text{fan}}}{\dot{m}_{\text{total}}} = \frac{\alpha}{1 + \alpha}$.

2. **Classification by Bypass Ratio:**
   - **Low-Bypass Turbofans ($\alpha \approx 0.2\text{ to } 2.0$):** Military combat aircraft; high specific thrust, compact frontal area, supersonic capability.
   - **High-Bypass Turbofans ($\alpha \approx 5.0\text{ to } 12.0$):** Commercial airliners (Boeing 787, Airbus A350); maximum propulsive efficiency, ultra-low TSFC, low jet noise.
   - **Ultra-High Bypass / Geared Turbofans ($\alpha \ge 12.0$):** Modern civil transport (PW1000G, CFM RISE).

---

### Step 3 — Turbofan Thrust Equations & Power Balance (25 min)

1. **Unmixed / Separate Exhaust Turbofan Thrust:**
   $$\boxed{F_n = \dot{m}_{\text{core}}\left[(1+f)V_{9,\text{core}} - V_0\right] + \dot{m}_{\text{fan}}\left[V_{9,\text{fan}} - V_0\right]}$$
   $$\boxed{\frac{F_n}{\dot{m}_{\text{total}}} = \frac{\left[(1+f)V_{9,\text{core}} - V_0\right] + \alpha\left[V_{9,\text{fan}} - V_0\right]}{1 + \alpha}}$$

2. **Mixed Exhaust Turbofan Thrust:**
   Fan air and core gas mix in a common mixer duct at equal stagnation pressure before expanding through a single exhaust nozzle ($V_9$):
   $$\boxed{F_n = \dot{m}_{\text{total}}\left[(1 + f')V_9 - V_0\right] + (p_9 - p_0)A_9 \quad \text{where } f' = \frac{f}{1 + \alpha}}$$

3. **Fan-Turbine Power Matching (Twin-Spool Turbofan):**
   - High-Pressure (HP) Spool: $\dot{W}_{\text{HPT}} \cdot \eta_{m,\text{HP}} = \dot{W}_{\text{HPC}}$.
   - Low-Pressure (LP) Spool (LP Turbine drives Fan & Booster):
     $$\boxed{\dot{m}_{\text{core}}(1+f) c_{pg}(T_{04.5} - T_{05})\eta_{m,\text{LP}} = \dot{m}_{\text{fan}} c_{pa}(T_{02,\text{fan}} - T_{02}) + \dot{m}_{\text{core}} c_{pa}(T_{02.5} - T_{02})}$$

### Step 4 — The Physics of High Bypass Ratio Efficiency (10 min)

```
              WHY HIGH BYPASS TURBOFANS ARE SUPREMELY EFFICIENT
     Net Thrust: F_n = ṁ_total · ΔV
     Kinetic Energy Wasted in Jet Wake: ΔKE_wasted = (1/2) ṁ_total · ΔV²

     To generate the SAME Thrust F_n:
     • Pure Turbojet: Low mass flow ṁ, Huge velocity increment ΔV ──► MASSIVE wasted KE!
     • High BPR Turbofan: Huge mass flow ṁ, Small velocity increment ΔV ──► MINIMAL wasted KE!
       ▲ Propulsive Efficiency: η_p = 2 / (1 + V_9/V_0) ↑
       ▼ TSFC: ṁ_f / F_n ↓ (By 30% to 50%)
       ▼ Jet Noise: Acoustic Power ∝ V_9⁸ ↓
```

---

### Step 5 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Bypass Ratio in Denominators**
> When calculating specific thrust per unit **TOTAL mass flow** ($\dot{m}_{\text{total}}$), divide by $(1+\alpha)$. When calculating per unit **CORE mass flow** ($\dot{m}_{\text{core}}$), do NOT divide by $(1+\alpha)$!
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Acoustic Noise Scaling**
> Jet noise scales as $V_{\text{jet}}^8$! Increasing bypass ratio $\alpha$ reduces $V_{\text{jet}}$, causing an enormous exponential drop in noise pollution.

### Step 3 — D2 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** In a turbofan engine, increasing the bypass ratio $\alpha$ while keeping total thrust constant has which of the following primary thermodynamic effects?
> **(A)** Increases TSFC and decreases propulsive efficiency
> **(B)** Decreases TSFC and increases propulsive efficiency
> **(C)** Increases thermal efficiency and increases jet noise
> **(D)** Decreases compressor pressure ratio

> **Q2 (1 mark):** The acoustic noise power generated by the exhaust jet of an aircraft gas turbine engine is proportional to which power of the jet velocity $V_{\text{jet}}$?
> **(A)** $V_{\text{jet}}^2$ **(B)** $V_{\text{jet}}^4$ **(C)** $V_{\text{jet}}^6$ **(D)** $V_{\text{jet}}^8$

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A turbofan engine with bypass ratio $\alpha = 6.0$ has total mass flow $\dot{m}_{\text{total}} = 280\text{ kg/s}$. The core exhaust velocity is $V_{9,\text{core}} = 550\text{ m/s}$, fan exhaust velocity is $V_{9,\text{fan}} = 320\text{ m/s}$, and flight speed is $V_0 = 220\text{ m/s}$ (neglect $f$). The net thrust $F_n$ (in $\text{kN}$) is:
> **(A)** $27.2\text{ kN}$ **(B)** $37.2\text{ kN}$ **(C)** $47.2\text{ kN}$ **(D)** $57.2\text{ kN}$

> **Q4 (2 marks):** In a turbofan engine, the fan temperature rise is $T_{02,\text{fan}} - T_{02} = 40\text{ K}$ with $c_{pa} = 1005\text{ J/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}$. The bypass ratio is $\alpha = 5.0$. If the LP turbine operates with $c_{pg} = 1150\text{ J/(kg}\cdot\text{K)}, f = 0.02$, and $\eta_m = 1.0$, the temperature drop across the LP turbine $T_{04.5} - T_{05}$ (in $\text{K}$) is:
> **(A)** $85.7\text{ K}$ **(B)** $120.4\text{ K}$ **(C)** $171.4\text{ K}$ **(D)** $200.0\text{ K}$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** A turbofan engine cruise produces net thrust $F_n = 45.0\text{ kN}$ with core airflow $\dot{m}_{\text{core}} = 30.0\text{ kg/s}$ and fuel-to-air ratio $f = 0.018$. The Thrust Specific Fuel Consumption $\text{TSFC}$ (in $\text{g/(kN}\cdot\text{s)}$) is ___ (round off to 1 decimal place).

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D2 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) Decreases TSFC and increases propulsive efficiency**
> High BPR accelerates larger air mass to lower velocity $\Delta V$, boosting $\eta_p$ and reducing TSFC.
>
> **Q2: (D) V_jet⁸**
> Lighthill's eighth-power law: $P_{\text{acoustic}} \propto \rho_0 V_{\text{jet}}^8 D^2 / a_0^5$.
>
> **Q3: (B) 37.2 kN**
> $\dot{m}_{\text{core}} = \frac{280}{1 + 6} = \frac{280}{7} = 40\text{ kg/s}$.
> $\dot{m}_{\text{fan}} = 6 \times 40 = 240\text{ kg/s}$.
> $F_{\text{core}} = 40(550 - 220) = 40(330) = 13,200\text{ N}$.
> $F_{\text{fan}} = 240(320 - 220) = 240(100) = 24,000\text{ N}$.
> $F_n = 13200 + 24000 = 37,200\text{ N} = \mathbf{37.2\text{ kN}}$.
>
> **Q4: (C) 171.4 K**
> $\dot{m}_{\text{core}}(1+f) c_{pg} \Delta T_{\text{LPT}} \eta_m = \dot{m}_{\text{fan}} c_{pa} \Delta T_{\text{fan}}$.
> $\Delta T_{\text{LPT}} = \frac{\alpha c_{pa} \Delta T_{\text{fan}}}{(1+f) c_{pg} \eta_m} = \frac{5.0 \times 1005 \times 40}{(1.02)(1150)(1.0)} = \frac{201,000}{1173} \approx \mathbf{171.35\text{ K} \approx 171.4\text{ K}}$.
>
> **Q5: 12.0**
> $\dot{m}_f = \dot{m}_{\text{core}} \cdot f = 30.0 \times 0.018 = 0.54\text{ kg/s} = 540\text{ g/s}$.
> $\text{TSFC} = \frac{540\text{ g/s}}{45.0\text{ kN}} = \mathbf{12.0\text{ g/(kN}\cdot\text{s)}}$.
