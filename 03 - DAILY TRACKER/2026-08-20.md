### Step 3 — Mean Value Theorems (15 min)

**Rolle's Theorem:**
If $f$ is: (1) continuous on $[a,b]$, (2) differentiable on $(a,b)$, (3) $f(a)=f(b)$
Then $\exists\ c\in(a,b)$ s.t. $f'(c)=0$

**Lagrange's Mean Value Theorem (LMVT):**
If $f$ is continuous on $[a,b]$ and differentiable on $(a,b)$, then:
$$\exists\ c\in(a,b): f'(c)=\frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}$$
> 🎯 Geometric meaning: tangent at $c$ is parallel to the chord $AB$

**Cauchy's Mean Value Theorem (CMVT):**
If $f,g$ continuous on $[a,b]$, diff on $(a,b)$, $g'(x)\neq0$:
$$\exists\ c\in(a,b): \frac{f'(c)}{g'(c)}=\frac{f(b)-f(a)}{g(b)-g(a)}$$

**Key theorem hierarchy:**
$$\text{CMVT}\xrightarrow{g(x)=x}\text{LMVT}\xrightarrow{f(a)=f(b)}\text{Rolle's}$$

> ⚡ **GATE pattern for MVT:** "Verify LMVT for $f(x)$ on $[a,b]$" = Find $c$ from $f'(c)=\frac{f(b)-f(a)}{b-a}$

### Step 4 — Maxima & Minima (15 min)
Open `02.1.5 - Maxima and Minima`.

**Critical Points:** Points where $f'(x)=0$ or $f'(x)$ does not exist.

**First Derivative Test:**
For critical point $x=c$:
- $f'$ changes $+\to-$ → Local **Maximum**
- $f'$ changes $-\to+$ → Local **Minimum**
- $f'$ does NOT change sign → **Inflection point** (neither max nor min)

**Second Derivative Test (faster for GATE):**
- $f'(c)=0$ AND $f''(c)<0$ → Local **Maximum**
- $f'(c)=0$ AND $f''(c)>0$ → Local **Minimum**
- $f'(c)=0$ AND $f''(c)=0$ → **Inconclusive** (use 1st derivative test or higher derivatives)

**Absolute (Global) Extrema on closed $[a,b]$:**
1. Find all critical points in $(a,b)$
2. Evaluate $f$ at all critical points AND at $f(a)$, $f(b)$
3. Largest value = absolute maximum; smallest = absolute minimum

**Applied Optimisation Steps:**
1. Define variables and express quantity to optimise as $f(x)$
2. Find domain (constraints)
3. Find $f'(x)=0$; solve for $x$
4. Verify it's a max/min using 2nd derivative test
5. Answer the question

### Step 5 — Traps (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1:** $f'(c)=0$ does NOT mean $c$ is a maximum or minimum — could be inflection
> 🔴 **Trap 2:** For Rolle's theorem, ALL 3 conditions must hold — especially $f(a)=f(b)$
> 🔴 **Trap 3:** Chain rule: $\frac{d}{dx}\sin(x^2)=\cos(x^2)\cdot2x$, NOT just $\cos(x^2)$
> 🔴 **Trap 4:** Absolute extrema on $[a,b]$: MUST check endpoints, not just critical points
> 🔴 **Trap 5:** If $f''(c)=0$: 2nd derivative test fails → use 1st derivative test instead

### D3 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** $\frac{d}{dx}\left[\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{2x}{1-x^2}\right)\right]=$
> **(A)** $\frac{2}{1+x^2}$ **(B)** $\frac{1}{1+x^2}$ **(C)** $\frac{2}{1-x^2}$ **(D)** $\frac{1}{1-x^2}$

> **Q2 (2 marks):** For $f(x)=x^3-6x^2+9x+1$ on $[0,4]$, the absolute maximum value is:
> **(A)** 1 **(B)** 5 **(C)** 3 **(D)** 9

> **Q3 (1 mark):** For Rolle's theorem to apply to $f(x)=\sin x$ on $[0,\pi]$, which condition is used?
> **(A)** $f(0)\cdot f(\pi)<0$ **(B)** $f(0)=f(\pi)=0$ **(C)** $f'(0)=0$ **(D)** $f(\pi/2)=1$

> **Q4 (2 marks NAT):** By LMVT, the value of $c$ for $f(x)=x^2$ on $[1,3]$ is ___

> **Q5 (1 mark):** $\frac{d}{dx}(x^x)$ at $x=1$ is:
> **(A)** 0 **(B)** 1 **(C)** $e$ **(D)** $\ln x$

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D3 Mini Quiz Answers
>
> **Q1: (A) $\frac{2}{1+x^2}$**
> Let $\theta=\tan^{-1}x$ → $\frac{2\tan\theta}{1-\tan^2\theta}=\tan(2\theta)$
> So $\tan^{-1}\left(\frac{2x}{1-x^2}\right)=2\tan^{-1}x$
> $\frac{d}{dx}[2\tan^{-1}x]=\frac{2}{1+x^2}$
>
> **Q2: (B) 5**
> $f'(x)=3x^2-12x+9=3(x^2-4x+3)=3(x-1)(x-3)=0 \Rightarrow x=1,3$
> $f(0)=1$, $f(1)=1-6+9+1=5$, $f(3)=27-54+27+1=1$, $f(4)=64-96+36+1=5$
> Absolute maximum = **5** (attained at $x=1$ and $x=4$)
>
> **Q3: (B) $f(0)=f(\pi)=0$**
> $\sin0=\sin\pi=0$ — this is the third condition of Rolle's: $f(a)=f(b)$.
>
> **Q4: $c=2$**
> $f'(c)=\frac{f(3)-f(1)}{3-1}=\frac{9-1}{2}=4$
> $f'(x)=2x=4 \Rightarrow x=2 \Rightarrow c=2$ ✓ ($c=2\in(1,3)$)
>
> **Q5: (B) 1**
> $\frac{d}{dx}(x^x)=x^x(\ln x+1)$. At $x=1$: $1^1(\ln1+1)=1\cdot(0+1)=1$
