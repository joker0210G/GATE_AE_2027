### Step 2 — Afterburners (Reheat) Working Principle & Physics (20 min)
Open `02 - Aircraft Engine Types and Performance`.

```
                    AFTERBURNER (REHEAT) ARCHITECTURE
       Turbine Exit Gas             Afterburner Duct              Variable CD Nozzle
     ┌───────────────────┐    ┌──────────────────────────┐    ╭───────────────────╮
     │ T_05 ≈ 1000–1200 K│───►│ Fuel Spray Rings         │───►│ Throat Opens Up:  │──► V_9,wet
     │ (15–16% Unburned O₂)│  │ & Flameholder Gutters    │    │ A_t,wet > A_t,dry │
     └───────────────────┘    │ Reheat Temp T_07 ≈ 2000 K│    ╰───────────────────╯
                              └──────────────────────────┘
```

1. **Why Afterburning is Possible:**
   - Gas turbines operate with overall lean combustion ($\phi \approx 0.3$) to protect turbine blades from melting.
   - Turbine exhaust gas contains $\approx 15\text{ to } 16\%\text{ O}_2$ by mass — ample oxygen to burn additional fuel without extra air intake!
2. **Thrust Augmentation Ratio:**
   Neglecting mass increase ($\dot{m}_f \ll \dot{m}_a$) and pressure terms:
   $$V_9 = \sqrt{2 c_{pg} T_{09} \left[1 - \left(\frac{p_0}{p_{09}}\right)^{\frac{\gamma-1}{\gamma}}\right]}$$
   $$\boxed{\frac{F_{\text{wet}}}{F_{\text{dry}}} \approx \frac{V_{9,\text{wet}}}{V_{9,\text{dry}}} \approx \sqrt{\frac{T_{07}}{T_{05}}} \approx 1.5\text{ to } 1.8 \quad (+50\%\text{ to }+80\%\text{ thrust boost!})}$$
3. **TSFC Penalty:**
   - Because heat addition occurs at low pressure downstream of the turbine, thermal efficiency drops severely $\implies \mathbf{\text{TSFC}_{\text{wet}} \approx 2\text{ to } 3 \times \text{TSFC}_{\text{dry}}}$.
4. **Variable Nozzle Throat Area Sizing (CRITICAL GATE INVARIANT!):**
   - From choked mass flow $\dot{m} = C \frac{p_0 A_t}{\sqrt{T_0}}$, to maintain engine backpressure $p_{05}$ and turbine operation unchanged:
     $$\boxed{\frac{A_{t,\text{wet}}}{A_{t,\text{dry}}} = \sqrt{\frac{T_{07}}{T_{05}}} > 1.0}$$
   - **Rule:** The exhaust nozzle throat area **MUST OPEN UP** during reheat! If it fails to open, the increased backpressure causes compressor stall and catastrophic engine surge.

### Step 3 — Ramjet Engine Aerothermodynamics (25 min)

```
                    RAMJET ENGINE ARCHITECTURE (ZERO MOVING PARTS)
     Supersonic Intake Diffuser       Combustor               Converging-Diverging Nozzle
     ╭───────────────────────╮    ┌───────────────┐          ╭───────────────────────────╮
     │ Supersonic Ram Decel   ├───► Fuel Injection├───► V_9  │ Supersonic Expansion      ├───►
     │ M_0 > 1 ──► M_2 ≈ 0.2 │    │ Subsonic Burn │          │ M > 1                     │
     ╰───────────────────────╯    └───────────────┘          ╰───────────────────────────╯
```

1. **Working Principle:**
   - Entirely dispenses with rotating compressors and turbines. Compression is achieved purely by supersonic aerodynamic ram deceleration in the intake diffuser.
2. **Ideal Ramjet Thermodynamic Cycle:**
   - 0–2: Isentropic ram compression in diffuser ($T_{02} = T_0\left(1 + \frac{\gamma-1}{2}M_0^2\right)$).
   - 2–4: Constant pressure combustion ($q_{\text{in}} = c_p(T_{04} - T_{02})$).
   - 4–9: Isentropic expansion through CD nozzle ($T_{09} = T_{04}$).
3. **Thermal Efficiency of Ideal Ramjet:**
   $$\eta_{\text{th,ramjet}} = 1 - \frac{T_0}{T_{02}} = 1 - \frac{1}{1 + \frac{\gamma-1}{2}M_0^2}$$
   $$\boxed{\eta_{\text{th,ramjet}} = \frac{\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M_0^2}{1 + \frac{\gamma-1}{2}M_0^2}}$$
   - **Key Invariant:** Ideal ramjet thermal efficiency depends **ONLY ON FLIGHT MACH NUMBER $M_0$**!

4. **Ramjet Operational Limits:**
   - **Static ($M_0 = 0$):** $\eta_{\text{th}} = 0$, $F_n = 0$ (Cannot generate static thrust; requires rocket booster!).
   - **Low Supersonic ($M_0 < 1.5\text{--}2.0$):** Ram compression is too weak to compete with turbojets.
   - **Optimum Operating Range:** $\mathbf{M_0 \approx 2.5\text{ to } 4.0}$ (Highest specific thrust and efficiency).
   - **High Hypersonic Limit ($M_0 > 5.0$):** Decelerating flow to subsonic speeds in the combustor causes stagnation temperatures so high that air dissociates, killing combustion efficiency $\implies$ requires supersonic combustion (**Scramjet**).

### Step 4 — Traps & Self-Check (5 min)
> 🔴 **Trap 1: Afterburner Nozzle Throat Area Change**
> $A_t$ MUST **INCREASE** ($A_{t,\text{wet}} > A_{t,\text{dry}}$). If throat area is kept constant, stagnation pressure $p_{05}$ would spike, stalling the upstream turbine and compressor.
> 🔴 **Trap 2: Ramjet Static Starting**
> A ramjet CANNOT self-start from standstill ($M_0 = 0$). It has zero static thrust!

### Step 3 — D4 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (1 mark):** During the operation of an afterburner on a supersonic fighter aircraft, the exhaust nozzle throat area $A_t$ must be:
> **(A)** Decreased **(B)** Increased **(C)** Kept constant **(D)** Closed completely

> **Q2 (1 mark):** The thermal efficiency of an ideal ramjet engine operating with a calorically perfect gas depends solely upon the:
> **(A)** Combustor exit stagnation temperature $T_{04}$
> **(B)** Flight Mach number $M_0$
> **(C)** Fuel-to-air ratio $f$
> **(D)** Nozzle expansion area ratio

> **Q3 (2 marks):** A turbojet engine has turbine exit temperature $T_{05} = 1000\text{ K}$. When the afterburner is ignited, the gas temperature rises to $T_{07} = 1960\text{ K}$. The percentage increase in dry gross thrust ($\Delta F_g / F_{g,\text{dry}}$) is approximately:
> **(A)** $20\%$ **(B)** $40\%$ **(C)** $60\%$ **(D)** $96\%$

> **Q4 (2 marks):** An ideal ramjet engine operates at flight Mach number $M_0 = 2.0$ with $\gamma = 1.4$. The thermal efficiency $\eta_{\text{th}}$ is:
> **(A)** $33.3\%$ **(B)** $44.4\%$ **(C)** $55.6\%$ **(D)** $66.7\%$

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** An afterburning turbojet engine has a dry nozzle throat area $A_{t,\text{dry}} = 0.16\text{ m}^2$ with $T_{05} = 900\text{ K}$. If the reheat temperature is $T_{07} = 2025\text{ K}$, the required wet nozzle throat area $A_{t,\text{wet}}$ (in $\text{m}^2$) to maintain constant upstream turbine operating conditions is ___ $\text{m}^2$.

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D4 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: (B) Increased**
> To maintain constant mass flow $\dot{m}$ and backpressure $p_{05}$ when temperature rises, $A_t \propto \sqrt{T_0} \implies A_t$ must increase.
>
> **Q2: (B) Flight Mach number M_0**
> $\eta_{\text{th,ramjet}} = \frac{\frac{\gamma-1}{2}M_0^2}{1 + \frac{\gamma-1}{2}M_0^2}$.
>
> **Q3: (B) 40%**
> $\frac{F_{\text{wet}}}{F_{\text{dry}}} \approx \sqrt{\frac{T_{07}}{T_{05}}} = \sqrt{\frac{1960}{1000}} = \sqrt{1.96} = 1.40 \implies \Delta F/F = \mathbf{40\%}$.
>
> **Q4: (B) 44.4%**
> $\eta_{\text{th}} = \frac{0.2(2.0^2)}{1 + 0.2(2.0^2)} = \frac{0.2(4)}{1 + 0.2(4)} = \frac{0.8}{1.8} = \frac{4}{9} \approx \mathbf{0.4444 = 44.4\%}$.
>
> **Q5: 0.24**
> $A_{t,\text{wet}} = A_{t,\text{dry}}\sqrt{\frac{T_{07}}{T_{05}}} = 0.16 \sqrt{\frac{2025}{900}} = 0.16 \sqrt{2.25} = 0.16(1.50) = \mathbf{0.24\text{ m}^2}$.
