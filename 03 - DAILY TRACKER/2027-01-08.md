## ⏰ HOUR 1 (0:00 – 1:00): VIRTUAL CALCULATOR SPEED PROTOCOL & UNIT INVARIANTS

### Step 1 — The 5 Golden Rules of the GATE Virtual Calculator (15 min)
1. **Never Round Intermediate Steps:** Store intermediate numbers in calculator memory (`MS`, `M+`, `MR`) or compute compound expressions in a single chain.
2. **Angle Mode Watchdog:** Check whether the calculator is in **Deg** or **Rad** mode before evaluating $\sin, \cos, \tan$ or $\tan^{-1}, \sin^{-1}$! (Thin airfoil and supersonic wave drag formulas require $\alpha$ in **Radians**!).
3. **Logarithm Distinction:** Distinguish between natural log `ln` ($\log_e$) and common log `log` ($\log_{10}$) (Tsiolkovsky rocket equation and logarithmic decrement use **`ln`**!).
4. **Parentheses Hygiene:** Always enclose numerators and denominators in brackets when dividing compound sums/products.
5. **Exact Unit Invariants:**

```
                    MASTER UNIT CONVERSION INVARIANTS
  Quantity               Standard SI Unit      Conversion Multiplier
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Stress / Pressure      Pascal (Pa = N/m²)    1 MPa = 10⁶ Pa = 1 N/mm²
                                               1 GPa = 10⁹ Pa = 10³ N/mm²
                                               1 bar = 10⁵ Pa = 100 kPa
  Angle                  Radians (rad)         1 rad = 180° / π ≈ 57.2958°
                                               1° = π / 180 rad ≈ 0.0174533 rad
  Speed                  m/s                   1 km/h = 5/18 m/s ≈ 0.2778 m/s
                                               1 knot = 0.5144 m/s
```

### Step 3 — D4 Mini Quiz (3:45–4:00) — 5 NAT Questions (Timed 10 min)

> **Q1 (2 marks NAT):** An elliptical wing of aspect ratio $\text{AR} = 7.5$ operates at lift coefficient $C_L = 0.70$. The induced drag coefficient $C_{Di}$ is ___ (round off to 4 decimal places).

> **Q2 (2 marks NAT):** An aircraft in a coordinated level turn at airspeed $V = 160\text{ m/s}$ has bank angle $\phi = 45^\circ$ ($g = 9.81\text{ m/s}^2$). The radius of turn $R$ (in meters) is ___ $\text{m}$ (round off to nearest integer).

> **Q3 (2 marks NAT):** A rocket engine produces thrust $F = 120.0\text{ kN}$ while consuming propellant at $\dot{m} = 45.0\text{ kg/s}$ ($g_0 = 9.81\text{ m/s}^2$). The specific impulse $I_{\text{sp}}$ (in seconds) is ___ $\text{s}$ (round off to 1 decimal place).

> **Q4 (2 marks NAT):** A cantilever beam of length $L = 2.5\text{ m}$ with flexural rigidity $EI = 5.0\times 10^6\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2$ carries a tip load $P = 8.0\text{ kN}$. The tip deflection $\delta_{\text{tip}}$ (in $\text{mm}$) is ___ $\text{mm}$ (round off to 2 decimal places).

> **Q5 (2 marks NAT):** An undamped SDOF system has mass $m = 8.0\text{ kg}$ and spring stiffness $k = 3200\text{ N/m}$. The natural circular frequency $\omega_n$ (in $\text{rad/s}$) is ___ $\text{rad/s}$.

> [!faq]- 🔍 View D4 Mini Quiz Answers & Step-by-Step Solutions
>
> **Q1: 0.0208**
> $C_{Di} = \frac{C_L^2}{\pi \text{AR}} = \frac{0.70^2}{7.5\pi} = \frac{0.49}{23.56194} \approx \mathbf{0.0208}$.
>
> **Q2: 2610**
> $R = \frac{V^2}{g \tan\phi} = \frac{160^2}{9.81 \tan(45^\circ)} = \frac{25600}{9.81(1.0)} = \frac{25600}{9.81} \approx \mathbf{2609.58\text{ m} \approx 2610\text{ m}}$.
>
> **Q3: 271.8**
> $I_{\text{sp}} = \frac{F}{\dot{m} g_0} = \frac{120,000}{(45.0)(9.81)} = \frac{120,000}{441.45} \approx \mathbf{271.83\text{ s} \approx 271.8\text{ s}}$.
>
> **Q4: 8.33**
> $\delta_{\text{tip}} = \frac{P L^3}{3 EI} = \frac{(8000)(2.5^3)}{3(5.0\times 10^6)} = \frac{8000(15.625)}{1.5\times 10^7} = \frac{125000}{1.5\times 10^7} = 0.008333\text{ m} = \mathbf{8.33\text{ mm}}$.
>
> **Q5: 20.0**
> $\omega_n = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}} = \sqrt{\frac{3200}{8.0}} = \sqrt{400} = \mathbf{20.0\text{ rad/s}}$.
